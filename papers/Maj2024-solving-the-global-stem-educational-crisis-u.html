mechanismcognitive gaps in the material raise cognitive load, students fill the gaps with their own incorrect conclusions, and the resulting misconceptions then handicap further learning— “This problem is compounded by cognitive gaps in the materials, which exacerbate the cognitive load and may result in students coming to their own incorrect conclusions called misconceptions, which may handicap further learning.”
