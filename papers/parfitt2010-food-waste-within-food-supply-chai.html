sensefood waste - THE PAPER ENUMERATES THREE COMPETING DEFINITIONS AND SAYS WHICH IT USES, which is rare enough to be the reason this record is worth holding. (1) FAO 1981: wholesome edible material intended for human consumption, discarded, lost, degraded or eaten by pests anywhere in the chain. (2) Stuart 2009: as (1) PLUS edible material fed to animals or diverted as a processing by-product. (3) Smil 2004: as (1) and (2) PLUS OVER-NUTRITION - the gap between energy consumed and energy needed. The paper adopts the first two and states that the second is only usable where nutritional data on animal feed exist— “As definitions (1) and (2) but including over-nutrition—the gap between the energy value of _consumed_ food _per capita_ and the energy value of food _needed per capita_ (Smil 2004 _a_).”
