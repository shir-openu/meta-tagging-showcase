limitationThe theory is not established for high-dimensional settings where variable selection matters. — “We leave a study of asymptotic normality of random forests in high dimensions to future work.”

limitationEmpirically, nominal coverage of the intervals holds only up to moderate dimension. — “Meanwhile, in terms of uncertainty quantification, our method achieves nominal coverage up to d = 10, after which the performance of the confidence intervals starts to decay.”
