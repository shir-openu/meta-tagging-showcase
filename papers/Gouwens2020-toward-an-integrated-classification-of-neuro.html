mechanismthe mapping is bootstrapped rather than computed once - a hundred iterations, each using only seventy per cent of the available genes and of the reference cells - so that a cell's assigned type carries a confidence instead of being a bare label— “Next, we mapped Patch-seq transcriptomes to the reference tree via a bootstrap approach in which only 70% of the available genes and reference cells were used in each of 100 iterations.”
mechanismAMBIGUITY THAT BELONGS TO THE TAXONOMY IS SEPARATED FROM AMBIGUITY THAT BELONGS TO THE MEASUREMENT: a reference matrix records how often each type is confused with each other type in the clean data, and a cell's observed confusion is scored against that expectation rather than against zero— “To distinguish between “expected” and “unexpected” ambiguity in mapping between t-types, we computed the reference mapping probability matrix between t-types (defined as the probability of a cell type being confused with other cell types in the reference dissociated cell data set). We then used Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence between the…
mechanismtwo numerical cut-offs then decide which cells are set aside - a divergence above two, and, among the survivors, a correlation below one half between the cell's expression and the average expression of the types— “Cells assayed via Patch-seq recordings with divergence greater than 2 were considered “inconsistent” cells. For the remaining cells, the correlation between their gene expression and the average gene expression of all the cell types was computed; cells with correlations below 0.5 were also labeled as “inconsistent.””
mechanismthe per-type analyses are then run only on the subset with the highest-confidence mapping, and the stated reason is to limit the effect of mapping ambiguities due to technical issues— “When we performed analyses by t-type, we restricted the data set to the 2,760 cells with highly-consistent mapping to limit the effect mapping ambiguities due to technical issues on our results.”

mechanismcorrespondence between the two classifications is found by spectral co-clustering of the two type sets, with agreement given a stated threshold - at least two thirds of a type's cells falling inside its co-cluster— “To identify sets of t-types and me-types that corresponded closely to each other, we performed spectral co-clustering (Dhillon, 2001) of t-types and me-types (Figure 6B, gray boxes). findingSst neurons respect laminar boundaries, and which layer their axon dominantly targets discriminates among them better than whether or not they send axon to layer 1.— “These results reveal that Sst neurons highly respect laminar boundaries across cortex, they have clear laminar innervation preferences that differ based on their met-type and/or laminar position, and that their dominant laminar target may be a more reliable feature for distinguishing among Sst neurons than the presence or absence of axon in…
findingMost transcriptomic types are electrophysiologically distinguishable from one another, with non-significant pairs largely confined to sibling types on the same branch of the taxonomy.— “Most pairs were significantly better fit by separate distributions for each t-type rather than by a single distribution describing the pair, indicating that most t-types systematically differed from each other in their electrophysiological properties.”
findingElectrophysiology predicts transcriptomic subclass very well but individual t-type poorly, and adding morphology does not improve t-type prediction.— “However, classifier accuracy improved to 57% when electrophysiological features were used together with morphological features (Figure 5D).”
findingCells of the same t-type have similar morpho-electric properties, yet a cell's specific t-type usually cannot be read off those properties.
findingSomata of many GABAergic t-types occupy specific laminar or sublaminar positions, giving an anatomical correlate to purely transcriptomic groupings.
findingIndividual cells' axon depth histograms correlate positively with their t-type average, indicating consistent innervation patterns within a type.
