findingThe review concludes that AI has been widely adopted across education institutions in several distinct technological forms.— “The study ascertained that AI has extensively been adopted and used in education, particularly by education institutions, in different forms.”
findingAcross the reviewed literature, AI raised the efficiency of instructors' administrative work such as grading, feedback and plagiarism checking.— “AI has improved efficiencies in the performance of different administrative tasks that instructors, would require a lot of time to perform in the absence of AI.”
findingThe main learning benefit identified was customisation and personalisation of curriculum and content to individual learners' needs and abilities.— “An important way in which AI has been applied in improving students’ learning is the customization and personalization of curriculum and content in line with the learners needs, abilities, and capabilities [15].”
findingOnly one reviewed study reported a negative effect of AI on education, namely erosion of academic integrity through paper-mill services.— “One study discussed or highlighted the adverse impact of AI, degradation of academic integrity and cheating using paper churning and paper mill services facilitated by AI.”
