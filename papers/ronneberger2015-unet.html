limitationBecause convolutions are unpadded, border pixels are lost at every layer and feature maps must be cropped before concatenation. — “The cropping is necessary due to the loss of border pixels in every convolution.”
limitationWithout the overlap-tile strategy the resolution the network can handle is bounded by GPU memory. — “This tiling strategy is important to apply the network to large images, since otherwise the resolution would be limited by the GPU memory.”
limitationPart of what the network is trained to detect is inserted by the authors: the separation borders between touching cells are synthesised, not annotated. — “and to force the network to learn the small separation borders that we introduce between touching cells (See Figure 3c and d). limitationHow heavily those borders count is set by two chosen constants in the loss. — “In our experiments we set w0 = 10 and σ ≈ 5 pixels”
limitationThe invariances the network has are the ones the augmentation supplied, and the paper says the corpus never contained them. — “This allows the network to learn invariance to such deformations, without the need to see these transformations in the annotated image corpus”
