mechanismsampling ground truths from a database objects and their enclosed points are collected once into a database, then pasted into other point clouds during training, which multiplies the labelled objects per scene and simulates each object in new surroundings— “The major problem we encountered during training was the existence of too few ground truths, which significantly limited the convergence speed and final performance of the network. To solve this problem, we introduced a data augmentation approach. First, we generated a database containing the labels of all ground truths and their associated…
mechanismcollision rejection after sampling pasted objects are removed when they intersect existing ones, so the augmentation cannot produce a scene that could not physically exist— “To avoid physically impossible outcomes, we performed a collision test after sampling the ground truths and removed any sampled objects that collided with other objects.”
mechanismper-object rather than per-scene noise each ground truth and its points are transformed independently instead of the whole cloud being transformed together, so the augmentation varies objects relative to their scene— “To consider noise, we followed the same approach used in VoxelNet, in which each ground truth and its point cloud are independently and randomly transformed, instead of transforming all point clouds with the same parameters.”
