phenomenonauditory temporal masking— “The physiological mechanism of auditory temporal masking was studied by single-unit analysis in the cat's cochlear nucleus and inferior colliculus.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
claim-typemechanism— “The mechanism of forward masking may be due to the postexcitatory inhibition of some slow-adapting neurons and to synaptic inhibition for fast-adapting ones.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
method — evidence— “The recording system for neuronal activity was conventional; single-unit activity was recorded extracellularly by a tungsten microelectrode and stored on magnetic tape for subsequent off-line computation.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
mechanismA faint probe tone drives an abnormally long response latency in collicular neurons, so the probe's discharge arrives at or after the discharge evoked by the following noise burst and is absorbed into it, which produces backward masking— “In the backward masking situation with a masking interval of 0 msec, the latency in response to noise bursts calculated from the beginning of the probe tone becomes 20msec. Accordingly, the unit does not elicit backward masking in the case of a latency of less than 20msec in response to the probe tone.”
builds-on ELLIOT (1962a)— “For monotic conditions of forward and backward masking, the parameters of the stimulating sounds used were the same as those employed by ELLIOT (1962a).” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
extends ELLIOTT (1962a, b) and PICKETT (1959)— “As shown in Fig. 8, the relationship between the amount of masking and the masking interval between the probe tone and the noise burst shows tendencies similar to those in the psychological data of ELLIOTT (1962a, b) and PICKETT (1959), although the curves indicate some differences from neuron to neuron.”
sense**THE DISTINCTION IS PSYCHOLOGICAL AND THE ARGUMENT IS ANATOMICAL.** `From the psychological point of view TWO KINDS of auditory masking are known, that is, SIMULTANEOUS and TEMPORAL masking` - a split made by psychophysics, which this paper then tests by asking where in the cat's auditory pathway each one appears. **A behavioural taxonomy checked against anatomy**, which is the move Carlesimo2004 makes for priming thirty-three years later and mcmahon-olson2007 makes for repetition suppression— “From the psychological point of view two kinds of auditory masking are known, that is, simultaneous and temporal masking.”
sense**AND THE LOCATION IS ESTABLISHED BY AN ABSENCE:** `Backward masking may involve CENTRAL processes because IT WAS NOT DEMONSTRABLE IN THE COCHLEAR NUCLEUS.` Nothing was found at the first relay, therefore the mechanism is above it. **A null result at one recording site doing positive work** - which only holds because the same preparation DID show forward masking there, so the absence is not a failure of the method
sense**IT IS THE PHYSIOLOGY OF marcel1983's INSTRUMENT, AND NEITHER CITES THE OTHER.** marcel1983 uses backward masking to render a prime unreportable and shows that semantic access survives it; this paper, twelve years earlier and in cats, asks WHERE backward masking happens and answers `not in the cochlear nucleus`. **One paper uses the tool, the other locates it.** The corpus holds both, and the pairing is only visible because they sit side by side - the sixth such pair found tonight
controlboth stimulus conditions held constant while the masking measurement was made — held — rules out a change in the stimulus itself explaining the change in masking
controlmasking measured as the difference in probe-tone threshold with and without the noise burst, at each delay — held — rules out the probe threshold itself drifting - the with/without difference at the same delay cancels anything common to both
notebackward masking —distinct-from→ cochlear nucleus — **AN EDGE BUILT ON AN ABSENCE, AND THE AXIS IS DOING THE WORK.** The sentence asserts that backward masking WAS NOT DEMONSTRABLE in the cochlear nucleus - a negative finding, and `no-change` is the only value in the enforced axis vocabulary that can carry it.
