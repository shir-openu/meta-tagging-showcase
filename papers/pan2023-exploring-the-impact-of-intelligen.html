Practical implications. This study delved into the impact of intelligent learning toolsterm·def on students’ independent learning abilities, yielding valuable practical implications for designers and developers of such tools. The findings underscored the significance of interaction qualityterm and information qualityterm·dom in shaping students’ satisfactionterm with and intention to useterm these tools, ultimately influencing their learning abilities. Designers and developers, therefore, need to give particular attention to these factors as they greatly influence students’ positive behavioral intentions towards intelligent learning toolsterm·def. First of all, designers and developers should prioritize the development of a user-friendly communication interface and offer diverse interaction methods. Regarding learner-instructor interactions, timely feedback and guidance from teachers could encourage students to be more engaged in their studies. When it comes to learner-instructor interactions, providing timely feedback and guidance from teachers can stimulate students’ active involvement in their studies. Facilitating learner-learner interactions, such as through discussion boards and exchange rooms, fosters a sense of community among students. As for learnercontent interactions, regular learning reports and personalized resource recommendations can provide students with a comprehensive overview of their learning progress, enabling them to adapt their study plans accordingly. By incorporating these strategies, designers and developers can enhance the usability and effectiveness of intelligent learning toolsterm·def in supporting students’ independent learning journey.

Secondly, equal attention should be given to the information qualityterm·dom of intelligent learning toolsterm·def. Ensuring the accuracy and comprehensibility of the learning content provided by these tools is crucial, as it directly influences students’ satisfactionterm and intention to useterm them. When the learning content aligns with students’ needs and the information is easily accessible when required, they are more inclined to prefer using these tools. Furthermore, resources offers students more options and enables them to study effectively. Developers can leverage big data technology to recommend suitable learning resources to users. Therefore, the development of interaction qualityterm and information qualityterm·dom in intelligent learning toolsterm·def is paramount to enhancing students’ satisfactionterm and intention to useterm them, ultimately leading to the improvement of students’ independent learning abilities.
