phenomenonintention to use intelligent learning tools and independent learning ability— “The purpose of this study was to investigate the influence of interaction quality and information quality of intelligent learning tools on students’ satisfaction and intention to use these tools, as well as to examine the relationship between the intention to use intelligent learning tools and students’ independent learning abilities.”
claim-typemechanism— “From Table 10, it is evidenced that satisfaction with tools could fully mediate the influence of interaction quality on intention to use.”
method — evidence— “The study utilized Smart-PLS 3, a Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM), to analyze data collected from 384 Grade 6 students in China.”
transferfrom information systems research: The information-quality construct and its role as a determinant of satisfaction and use are taken from the DeLone and McLean model of information systems success and applied to primary-school learning tools— “As part of the DeLone and McLean model (D&M) of information systems success, information quality was often used to measure system quality (DeLone and McLean, 2003).”
mechanismWillingness to use the tools raises how often students actually use them, and sustained repeated use over time creates the conditions under which independent learning abilities develop— “The cultivation of independent learning abilities couldn’t be achieved overnight, but students’ willingness to use intelligent learning tools could facilitate their continuous use, which created conditions for the development of independent learning abilities.”
mechanismBecause current tools are built mainly for one-way knowledge delivery (microlectures, reading materials, practice exercises, problem-solving reviews), their interaction features do not directly stimulate usage intention and instead act only by raising satisfaction— “One possible explanation for this result was that current intelligent learning tools primarily focus on knowledge dissemination through learning activities such as watching microlectures, reading learning materials, completing practice exercises, and reviewing problem-solving processes.”
mechanismInteraction with classmates, teachers and the system converts into usage intention only indirectly, by first increasing students' satisfaction with the tool— “Instead, this intention was achieved by enhancing students’ satisfaction with the tools.”
replicates Radha et al. (2020); Prasetyanto et al. (2022)— “These results aligned with previous surveys conducted by Radha et al. (2020) and Prasetyanto et al. (2022), which demonstrated that the use of intelligent learning tools improved students’ independent learning abilities.”
replicates Dai et al. (2020); Alzahrani et al. (2022)— “This finding was consistent with the research conducted by Dai et al. (2020) and Alzahrani et al. (2022), who also reported that the interaction quality had the least impact on students’ willingness to use online tools.”
