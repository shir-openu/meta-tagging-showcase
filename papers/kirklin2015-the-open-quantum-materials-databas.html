mechanisma known blind spot, named by physics rather than by residual the elements whose predicted ground state is wrong are the ones held together by van der Waals forces, which the semi-local functionals omit by construction. The error is attributed to a missing term in the theory rather than to a fitting failure, and the paper says which elements and why— “For all elements in the first group above, the ground-state structure cannot be determined reliably with (semi)-local exchange-correlation functionals used in DFT because of the lack of van der Waals interactions, and can likely be corrected by their inclusion.”
