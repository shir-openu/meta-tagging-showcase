limitationTHE COMMITTEE DECLARES ITS OWN TERM SELECTION ARBITRARY: `these AE terms were CHOSEN SOMEWHAT ARBITRARILY, but by experienced surgeons, and specific grading was decided BASED ON THE OPINIONS AND EXPERIENCE OF OUR COMMITTEE MEMBERS`. No corpus, no frequency count, no vote threshold - stated plainly as the first limitation
limitationAND THE AGREEMENT STUDY IS FUTURE WORK: `some disease-oriented subgroups are conducting clinical trials ... using BOTH the CTCAE and JCOG PC criteria ... AFTER THESE TRIALS ARE COMPLETED, WE WILL EVALUATE THE CONCORDANCE between the grading by the CTCAE and that by the JCOG PC criteria`. The vocabulary is published first and measured later
limitationAND THE SCOPE IS CLOSED BY FIAT, WITH THE REASON GIVEN: `they DO NOT INCLUDE INTRAOPERATIVE COMPLICATIONS ... we considered that incorporating intraoperative complications WOULD DEVIATE TOO MUCH FROM THE ORIGINAL CONCEPT. ANOTHER COMMON CLASSIFICATION MAY BE REQUIRED to define and grade intraoperative complications`. Fidelity to the parent vocabulary chosen over coverage
limitationand the temporal boundary is admitted to be conventional rather than principled: `here, EARLY POSTOPERATIVE GENERALLY INDICATES the time from surgery to the first hospital discharge, BUT IN THEORY, the Clavien-Dindo classification CAN BE APPLIED BROADLY to late postoperative complications after hospital discharge`
limitationTHE SAME CLASSIFICATION IS COUNTED THREE WAYS IN THIS ONE PAPER: `broadly into FOUR MAJOR GROUPS` in the introduction, and `FIVE GRADES of severity (Grade I, II, IIIa, IIIb, IVa, IVb, and V)` in the discussion - a parenthetical that enumerates SEVEN labels for the five it announces. `Grade` and `class` are different units and one word is doing both jobs
limitationTHE THREE VIRTUES ARE CITED, NOT MEASURED: `because it is SIMPLE, REPRODUCIBLE, AND FLEXIBLE [ 12 ]`. Reproducibility is the exact property this paper's own examples put in doubt - the same fever graded differently by different surgeons - and it is supported here by a reference rather than by a concordance study, which the paper elsewhere says is still future work.
limitationTHE COMPARISON THAT JUSTIFIES THE PAPER IS A THREE-CELL TABLE: `AE terms Specified Not specified Specified` and `Grading definitions Defined for each AE Single common definition for all AEs Defined for each AE`. The new instrument's advantage over CTCAE 4.0 is stated as a shared row value, with no measurement of either.
