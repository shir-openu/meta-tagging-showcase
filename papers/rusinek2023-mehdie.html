Object of study — Historical place names in Hebrew, Arabic and Syriac sources, and the problem of deciding when two of them denote the same place.

Research question — Can historical Middle Eastern datasets be linked by matching place names directly between the domain's own languages, rather than by transliterating them into Latin script or routing them through a general-purpose gazetteerterm·dom?

Contribution — Argues, from a worked example, that a general authority fileterm·dom under-covers a specialist domain's own vocabulary, and that intra-domainterm·mth matching beats routing through a central one.
