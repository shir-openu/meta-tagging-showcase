method — evidence— “This review provides a summary of the data that show a role for the IC in audiogenic seizures.”
mechanismGABA is taken back up more efficiently in the ICCN of GEPR-9s, so less GABA remains in the synapse to reach postsynaptic GABA receptors, inhibition is weakened, and seizures start in that nucleus despite the extra GABAergic neurons.— “Evidence is accumulating for the hypothesis that the uptake of GABA is more efficient in the ICCN of GEPR- 9s, and thus less GABA is available to interact with postsynaptic GABA receptors.”
mechanismAlternatively, excitation is enhanced in the ICCN through overexpression of glutamate receptors, which would fit the raised glutamate and aspartate levels measured there.— “Another hypothesis to explain the initiation of seizure activity from the ICCN is that excitation is enhanced in the ICCN of GEPR-9s.”
mechanismSeizure activity initiated in the ICCN is carried out of the colliculus along the bilateral ICCN-to-external-nucleus projection and onward to the superior colliculus and pontine reticular formation.— “Both results from our study of lesions in the coronal plane (Ribak et al. 1994) and anatomical studies that show the ICCN projects bilaterally to the EN (Coleman and Clerici 1987; Saldafia and Merchfin 1992) support the hypothesis that this pathway conveys seizure activity.”
builds-on Faingold et al. (1986a) hypothesis that GABA uptake is more efficient in the ICCN of GEPR-9s— “The observations that the GABA transporter, GAT-1, is localized to GABA neurons in several brain regions (Brecha and Weigman 1994) and that increased numbers of GABA neurons exist in the ICCN of GEPR-9s are consistent with this hypothesis, originally proposed by Faingold et al. (1986a).”
contradicts the disinhibition account carried over from hippocampal dentate gyrus findings (Farias et al. 1992; Buckmaster and Schwartzkroin 1994)— “This hypothesis was tested with an electron microscopic analysis in GEPR-9s, but the data were not consistent with it, because the number of axosomatic synapses per soma in the ICCN of GEPR-9s was not different from that of SD rats (Roberts and Ribak 1988).”
builds-on Faye-Lund and Osen's (1985) description of the rat inferior colliculus— “The IC of rats was elegantly described by Faye-Lund and Osen (1985).”
