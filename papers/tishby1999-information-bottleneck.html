method — evidence— “To prove the convergence of the iterations it is enough to verify that each of the iteration steps minimizes the same functional, independently, and that this functional is bounded from below as a sum of two non–negative terms.”
transferfrom rate distortion theory in communication: the rate-distortion trade-off curve and its cardinality specification, taken from lossy source coding and re-drawn in the information plane as the bottleneck curve— “These curves, analogous to the rate distortion curves, exists for every choice of the cardinality of ˜X.”
mechanismrelevance is supplied by a SECOND VARIABLE with positive mutual information, in place of a chosen distortion function, and the joint distribution of the two is assumed available— “Since the “right” distortion measure is rarely available, the problem of relevant quantization has to be addressed directly, by preserving the relevant information about another variable. The relevance variable, denoted here by Y , must not be independent from the original signal X, namely they have positive mutual information I(X; Y ). It is…
mechanismone Lagrange multiplier carries the whole tradeoff, and its two limits are stated: at zero everything collapses to a single codeword, and as it grows the quantization becomes arbitrarily fine— “At β = 0 our quantization is the most sketchy possible—everything is assigned to a single point—while as β → ∞ we are pushed toward arbitrarily detailed quantization. By varying the (only) parameter β one can explore the tradeoffbetween the preserved meaningful information and compression at various resolutions.”
mechanismthe Markov condition is stated NOT to be a modelling assumption - the compressed variable is not a hidden variable in a model of the data - and the paper names the different condition a model would have required instead— “It is important to notice that this not a modeling assumption and the quantization ˜X is not used as a hidden variable in a model of the data. In the latter, the Markov condition would have been different: Y ← ˜X ← X.”
mechanismthe compressed representation and its representatives are solved for TOGETHER, which is what separates this from rate distortion theory, where choosing representatives is a separate problem— “The crucial quantization is here performed through the conditional distributions p(y|˜x), and the self consistent equations include also the optimization over the representatives, in contrast to rate distortion theory, where the selection of representatives is a separate problem.”
mechanismconvergence of the alternating iterations is argued from each step minimising the same functional independently, that functional being bounded below as a sum of two non-negative terms that the alternation runs over THREE convex sets is stated in Theorem 5, a different span, not in this one— “To prove the convergence of the iterations it is enough to verify that each of the iteration steps minimizes the same functional, independently, and that this functional is bounded from below as a sum of two non–negative terms. The only point to notice is that when p(y|˜x) is fixed we are back to the rate distortion case with fixed distortion…
mechanismraising the multiplier traces convex curves in the information plane, and pairs of curves separate at a finite critical value through a second order phase transition, producing a hierarchy of quantizations
