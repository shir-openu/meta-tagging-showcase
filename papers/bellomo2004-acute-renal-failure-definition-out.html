mechanismAND THE MARKER THE DEFINITION IS BUILT ON IS ADMITTED TO BE WRONG IN EXACTLY THIS CONDITION: `the serum creatinine WILL NOT BE AN ACCURATE REFLECTION OF GFR IN THE NON-STEADY-STATE CONDITION OF ARF. Nonetheless, THE DEGREE TO WHICH SERUM CREATININE CHANGES FROM BASELINE WILL REFLECT THE CHANGE IN GFR.` The absolute value is unreliable and the definition is built on the CHANGE instead— “Like creatinine clearance, the serum creatinine will not be an accurate reflection of GFR in the non-steady-state condition of ARF. Nonetheless, the degree to which serum creatinine changes from baseline will reflect the change in GFR.”

mechanismAND THE DEFINITION IS ADOPTED SO THAT ITS OWN INADEQUACY BECOMES TESTABLE: `one might hypothesize that the UNDERLYING DISEASE PROCESS that results in ARF (e.g. radiocontrast versus sepsis) WILL ALTER THE `CLINICAL MEANING` OF EACH DEGREE of renal dysfunction. However, BY APPLYING THE PRESENT CRITERIA ACROSS THESE VARIOUS AETIOLOGIES, IT WILL BE POSSIBLE TO TEST THIS HYPOTHESIS DIRECTLY.` A committee adopting a criterion it suspects is wrong, in order to find out— “Similarly, one might hypothesize that the underlying disease process that results in ARF (e.g. radiocontrast versus sepsis) will alter the 'clinical meaning' of each degree of renal dysfunction.
