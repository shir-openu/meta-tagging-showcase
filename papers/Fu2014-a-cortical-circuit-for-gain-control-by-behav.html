mechanismLocomotion drives VIP interneurons, which inhibit SST interneurons, thereby disinhibiting neighbouring excitatory cells and raising their visual gain— “The present study reveals that a specific type of GABAergic cortical neuron, the VIP neuron, transmits the signal of locomotion to the cortex and operates through disinhibition of another GABAergic cortical neuron, the SST cell, to enhance the responses of neighboring cortical excitatory neurons”
mechanismAcetylcholine released from basal forebrain depolarises VIP neurons directly via nicotinic receptors, carrying the behavioural-state signal into cortex— “These findings in vivo suggest that acetylcholine (ACh) activates VIP neurons directly through nAChR.”
mechanismPV neurons show mixed locomotion responses because their change is a second-order consequence of SST disinhibition rather than direct locomotor drive— “Because VIP neurons principally inhibit SST neurons, which then inhibit PV neurons, which also receive local excitation, the activity of PV neurons during locomotion would be expected to be heterogeneous, as a secondary effect of relieving the inhibition from SST neurons rather than a direct response to locomotary input.”
mechanismVIP neurons inhibit SST neurons, releasing neighbouring excitatory cells - a disinhibitory circuit— “Our findings that VIP neurons are activated while SST neurons are inhibited during locomotion are in good agreement with the current consensus that VIP neurons mainly innervate SST neurons ( Lee et al., 2013 ), although different effects on SST neurons have also been reported”
mechanismacetylcholine from the nucleus of the diagonal band depolarises VIP neurons directly through nicotinic receptors— “In the presence of tetrodotoxin (TTX), the depolarization induced by ACh was abolished by the nAChR blockers MEC & MLA (12.41±0.98mV vs. 2.61±0.26mV, p <0.01, Supplemental Figure 5B and C ), confirming the existence of direct nicotinic cholinergic responses in VIP neurons.”
mechanismPV heterogeneity is explained as a secondary effect: VIP inhibits SST, SST inhibits PV, and PV also receives local excitation— “Because VIP neurons principally inhibit SST neurons, which then inhibit PV neurons, which also receive local excitation, the activity of PV neurons during locomotion would be expected to be heterogeneous, as a secondary effect of relieving the inhibition from SST neurons rather than a direct response to locomotary input.”
