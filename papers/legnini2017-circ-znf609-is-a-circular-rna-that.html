conceptcircRNAs — covalently closed circular RNAs lacking 5' and 3' ends, once thought rare, now known to come from thousands of genes
conceptIRES — the internal ribosome entry site that permits cap-independent initiation - first described in viruses, then in cellular RNAs
conceptpolysomes — the ribosome complexes circ-ZNF609 is found associated with, which is the evidence that it is translated
conceptmiRNA sponging — the mechanism most often proposed for circRNA function, shown not to generalise
conceptCap-independent translation — translation initiated without the 5' cap - a mechanism first described in VIRAL RNAs and only later found in cellular ones, so the name entered cell biology already carrying a virology history
conceptback-splicing — the circularising reaction that makes a circRNA; the paper proposes it as a route to protein isoforms parallel to, and distinct from, ordinary alternative splicing
conceptalternative splicing — the conventional isoform-generating mechanism that back-splicing is offered as an alternate to
