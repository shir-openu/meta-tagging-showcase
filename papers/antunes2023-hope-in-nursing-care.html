Introduction Hopeterm·dom is an integral part of being human and a dynamic and multidimensional phenomenon with numerous meanings. The concept of hopeterm·dom has been studied extensively in many academic fields, including philosophy, psychology, and medicine, although it only appeared in the nursing literature in the 1980s, which defined the concept for specific populations and inspired the first strategies. Hopeterm·dom has become an important and crucial factor in the care of people with health problems and their families [ 2 ], with three main focuses: (a) hopeterm·dom as an inner power that facilitates transcending the present situation and conceiving a reality-based expectation of a better future for oneself or others; (b) hopeterm·dom as a state of being, characterized by the anticipation of a good and improved state, or a release from perceived entrapment or a human experience [ 1 , 3 ]; (c) hopeterm·dom as a personal experience, centered on personal responsibility and convictions about the world and our chances of achieving what we want [ 4 ].

Scholars agree on describing hopeterm·dom as an idiosyncratic complex process that is essential to life, future-oriented, deliberate, and highly personalized [ 2 , 5 ]. It also entails the presence of substantial goals that are both desired and achievable, as well as the motivation and capacity to make judgments and choices [ 6 ].

Dufault and Martocchio provide the most extensive analysis of hopeterm·dom in the nursing literature [ 7 ]. These positive expectations are influenced by various factors, including self-belief (confidence in one’s abilities and potential), trust in others (confidence in the reliability and support of others), and adherence to a religious doctrine (faith in a higher power or spiritual principles) [ 9 ].

Hopeterm·dom emerges as a forward-looking process that involves a dynamic interplay among cognition, action, emotions, and interpersonal connections. This process is oriented toward the pursuit of future accomplishments that hold personal significance [ 10 ].
