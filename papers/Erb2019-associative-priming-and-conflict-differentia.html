phenomenoncongruency sequence effect in the Stroop task
claim-typemechanism
mechanismCoactivation of competing responses on incongruent trials raises the response threshold, lengthening motoric stopping and thus slowing initiation, with the raised threshold carrying over into the next trial — “On this view, the coactivation of competing responses on incongruent trials results in heightened response thresholds, with longer periods of motoric stopping and, consequently, slower initiation times.”
mechanismOverlap between the current and previous trial's stimulus-response features either helps or hinders the formation of the appropriate stimulus-response pair, and that facilitation or interference shows up as more or less reach curvature — “According to this interpretation, associative-priming effects influence performance by either impeding or facilitating an individual’s ability to form the appropriate stimulus–response pair, resulting in reach movements that feature more or less curvature.”
relation — “In contrast to previous hand-tracking studies (Erb & Marcovitch, 2018a, 2018b; Erb et al., 2016; Erb et al., 2018b), the task used in the present study allowed for a direct comparison of confound-minimized and confound-laden trials.”
relation — “This finding is consistent with a number of previous studies that have failed to observe a CSE in the Stroop task after controlling for alternative explanations (e.g., Schmidt & De Houwer, 2011; Weissman, Jiang, & Egner, 2014).”
relation — “For example, Scherbaum, Dshemuchadse, Fischer, and Goschke (2010, Exp. 2) observed a significant CSE in participants’ hand movements in a mouse-tracking version of the Simon task, even after stimulus repetitions were excluded from the analysis, whereas the present study did not reveal a CSE in reach curvatures in confound-minimized trials.”
differs inrelation — THE AXIS IS WHETHER STIMULUS REPETITIONS ARE EXCLUDED. The disagreement is between two analyses of the same confound, not between two claims about the effect. Q-444. — “even after stimulus repetitions were excluded from the analysis”
replicationuntested
limitationThe study used only a reach-tracking task, so it cannot say whether button-press response times would show the same pattern. — “Given that the present study did not involve both button-press and reach-tracking versions of the same task, it is unclear whether button-press response times in the task would reveal the same pattern of response time effects observed with reaching behavior.”
limitationThe task did not separate stimulus-level from response-level conflict and did not control contingency-learning effects; the authors call for tasks that do. — “Hand-tracking tasks should therefore be developed to investigate the effects of stimulusand responselevel conflict in tasks that control for both associative-priming and contingency-learning effects.”

content-observations: finding · quantity · variable · definition · key-term — grounded in the full text
findingInitiation times showed main effects of both current and previous congruency whether or not associative-priming confounds were present, supporting a threshold adjustment process that is not reducible to priming. — “Thus, initiation times revealed the same pattern of effects (cC < iC < cI < iI) regardless of the presence of associative-priming effects.”
findingReach curvature showed a congruency sequence effect only on confound-laden set repeat trials and not on confound-minimized trials, indicating that the curvature CSE is driven by associative priming rather than conflict adaptation. — “Thus, a CSE was not observed on confound-minimized trials, but a CSE was observed on set repeat trials, which featured substantial associative-priming effects.”
findingThe size of the curvature CSE was itself reliably larger on set repeat than on confound-minimized trials. — “The results of this analysis revealed a significantly larger CSE in curvatures in set repeat than in confound-minimized trials, F(1, 44) = 4.18, p = .047, ηp 2 = .09.”
findingOn confound-minimized trials reach curvature responded only to current congruency, with no carry-over from the previous trial and no interaction. — “Reach curvatures on confound-minimized trials revealed a significant main effect of current congruency, but no main effect of previous congruency or interaction between previous and current congruency.”
findingError rates on confound-minimized trials tracked current congruency alone, mirroring the curvature pattern. — “Error rates on confound-minimized trials revealed a significant main effect of current congruency, but no main effect of previous congruency or interaction between previous and current congruency.”
