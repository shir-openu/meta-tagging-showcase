phenomenoncongruency sequence effect in the Stroop task— “Thus, a CSE was not observed on confound-minimized trials, but a CSE was observed on set repeat trials, which featured substantial associative-priming effects.”
claim-typemechanism— “Thus, by directly evaluating the influence of associative-priming confounds, the present study revealed the strongest evidence to date that decision behavior in tasks involving conflict is fundamentally structured by the functioning of two dissociable processes.”
method — evidence— “We therefore presented 45 adult participants with a reach-tracking version of the Stroop task that featured both confound-minimized and confound-laden trials.”
mechanismCoactivation of competing responses on incongruent trials raises the response threshold, lengthening motoric stopping and thus slowing initiation, with the raised threshold carrying over into the next trial— “On this view, the coactivation of competing responses on incongruent trials results in heightened response thresholds, with longer periods of motoric stopping and, consequently, slower initiation times.”
mechanismOverlap between the current and previous trial's stimulus-response features either helps or hinders the formation of the appropriate stimulus-response pair, and that facilitation or interference shows up as more or less reach curvature— “According to this interpretation, associative-priming effects influence performance by either impeding or facilitating an individual’s ability to form the appropriate stimulus–response pair, resulting in reach movements that feature more or less curvature.”
extends Erb & Marcovitch (2018a, 2018b); Erb et al. (2016, 2018b)— “In contrast to previous hand-tracking studies (Erb & Marcovitch, 2018a, 2018b; Erb et al., 2016; Erb et al., 2018b), the task used in the present study allowed for a direct comparison of confound-minimized and confound-laden trials.”
replicates Schmidt & De Houwer (2011); Weissman, Jiang, & Egner (2014)— “This finding is consistent with a number of previous studies that have failed to observe a CSE in the Stroop task after controlling for alternative explanations (e.g., Schmidt & De Houwer, 2011; Weissman, Jiang, & Egner, 2014).”
contradicts Scherbaum, Dshemuchadse, Fischer, and Goschke (2010, Exp. 2)— “For example, Scherbaum, Dshemuchadse, Fischer, and Goschke (2010, Exp. 2) observed a significant CSE in participants’ hand movements in a mouse-tracking version of the Simon task, even after stimulus repetitions were excluded from the analysis, whereas the present study did not reveal a CSE in reach curvatures in confound-minimized…
sense**PRIMING AS THE CONFOUND RATHER THAN THE PHENOMENON, WHICH THIS CORPUS HAS ALMOST NONE OF:** `The CSE has been at the center of recent debates surrounding the cognitive and neural mechanisms underlying cognitive control and has been attributed to a range of factors, INCLUDING CONFLICT ADAPTATION AND ASSOCIATIVE PRIMING.` Two accounts of one effect, and **the priming account needs no control process at all** - if the current trial repeats the previous trial's stimulus and response, the pairing was already formed. So a CSE is not evidence for cognitive control until the overlap is removed. Every other record I have tagged treats priming as the object of study; here it is the alternative explanation— “The CSE has been at the center of recent debates surrounding the cognitive and neural mechanisms underlying cognitive control and has been attributed to a range of factors, including conflict adaptation and associative priming”
sense**AND THE AUTHORS SAY PLAINLY THAT THE EARLIER WORK - INCLUDING THEIR OWN - LEAVES THE QUESTION OPEN:** `Consequently, the extent to which the observed patterns reflected the proposed processes OR ASSOCIATIVE-PRIMING CONFOUNDS REMAINED UNCLEAR.` The preceding sentence - that earlier tasks did not control for those confounds - is fused in the staged text and cannot be quoted. **This is a whole literature's results placed in doubt by the people who produced some of them**, and it is what motivates the design rather than a limitation conceded at the end— “Consequently, the extent to which the observed patterns reflected the proposed processes or associative-priming confounds remained unclear”
sense**THE DISSOCIATION IS PRODUCED BY A CONTROL RATHER THAN BY A MANIPULATION, WHICH IS THE RARER AND STRONGER FORM:** initiation times showed the effect `on BOTH confound-minimized and confound-laden trials`; reach curvatures showed it `on confound-laden BUT NOT on confound-minimized trials`. **Nothing was added to the design - something was taken away, and one of the two measures lost its effect with it.** A dissociation obtained by subtraction cannot be explained by whatever the added factor also did, which is the usual objection to the added-manipulation kind— “Conversely, reach curvatures exhibited a clear sensitivity to associative-priming effects, revealing a congruency sequence effect on confound-laden but not on confound-minimized trials”
sense**AND THE STROOP TASK APPEARS HERE AS APPARATUS, NOT AS SUBJECT:** a three-response, reach-tracking version, 84 years after Stroop 1935, used to ask about trial-to-trial control rather than about interference. **The corpus now holds the founding paper and a paper that treats it as equipment** - and the pair is worth more than either, because it shows what happens to a paradigm when it stops being a finding and becomes a method— “We therefore presented 45 adult participants with a reach-tracking version of the Stroop task that featured both confound-minimized and confound-laden trials”
controlevery individual trial was VISUALLY INSPECTED and those where the automatic threshold missed part of the movement were identified — held — rules out that the movement measures are corrupted by an automatic threshold that mis-segments the reaching trajectory— “Each individual trial was visually inspected, as in previous work (e.g., Song & Nakayama, 2007); for trials in which the default threshold clearly missed part of the movement or included substantial movement back to the starting point, the thresholds were adjusted manually to ensure that the movement ended at the selected target.”
controla practice block of 24 Stroop trials preceded the experiment — held — rules out that early Stroop performance reflects unfamiliarity rather than conflict— “practice block of 24 trials of the Stroop task before beginning the experiment.”
