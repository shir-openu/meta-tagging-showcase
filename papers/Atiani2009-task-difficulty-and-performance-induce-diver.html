mechanismLowering target SNR raises the attentional effort the animal must invest, and that increased effort drives larger adaptive changes in the receptive field— “As target SNR decreased, distinguishing the target from the reference noise became more difficult, requiring more attention or effort, which, we predicted, could cause larger adaptive changes in the STRF.”
mechanismPulling a tone out of a masker requires simultaneously suppressing the responses driven by the concurrent noise and enhancing sensitivity to the tone— “Hence the process of extracting the tone component of the target necessitated suppression of responses to this concurrent masking noise while simultaneously enhancing the sensitivity to the target tone.”
