phenomenonlong-term subliminal influence on delayed decision-making— “We asked if humans can rapidly integrate and store novel relational information (e.g. “person X is a manager”, see Fig. 1a ) from subliminal messages for later use in a decision-making situation (e.g. “guess the income of X”).”
claim-typeexistence— “This is unprecedented evidence of the longevity and impact of subliminal messages on conscious, rational decision-making.”
method — evidence— “We ran two experiments to test whether subliminal stimulus pairs would affect delayed decision-making ( Figure 1a ).”
object of study — evidence— “We speculated that subliminal messages might affect delayed decision-making especially if messages contain several pieces of novel information that must be relationally bound in long-term memory.”
mechanismmulti-item subliminal displays force relational binding, which recruits the hippocampus; hippocampal plasticity encodes the relation in a single exposure and holds it for the long term— “Relational encoding of multiple stimuli engages hippocampus ( Henke et al., 1997 ; Duss et al. , 2014 ), which learns rapidly and stores relations for long term due to its exceptional plasticity ( McClelland et al. , 1995 ).”
mechanismsingle familiar items are processed by neocortex, which needs many learning trials to lay down a trace, which is why earlier subliminal priming effects decayed within a second or accumulated only slowly— “It is known that the processing of single items engages neocortex ( Henke et al., 1997 ; Duss et al. , 2014 ), which forms long-term memory traces rather slowly over many learning trials ( McClelland et al. , 1995 ).”
mechanismThe proposed route is relational binding in the hippocampal system, which can encode novel relations rapidly and hold them durably— “Relational binding engages the hippocampal memory system, which can rapidly encode and durably store novel relations.”
mechanismThat predicts the opposite of the usual expectation - subliminal effects should last longer when the material is novel and must be bound, not when it is familiar and repeated— “These studies suggest that subliminal messages are only slowly stored and might not be stored at all if they provide novel, unfamiliar information.”
mechanismIn the second experiment the same logic is applied to word meaning: a subliminal German word must be decoded and linked to a foreign word for the later judgement to work— “Hence, the meaning of subliminal German words was decoded, linked to foreign words, and stored to influence lexical–semantic decisions 20 min following subliminal encoding.”
mechanismAwareness is ruled out four ways rather than one: interview, two objective tests, Bayes factors, and a regression on residual awareness— “We further performed a multiple regression analysis to assess whether potential awareness of faces or occupations predicted decision accuracy in the main experiment, and to estimate whether accuracy remained above chance level if the theoretical awareness in both tests is 0 (i.e., whether the intercept is significant).”
mechanismA stricter check: removing every participant whose awareness score deviated from chance in either direction, and confirming the effect survives— “In a final step, we excluded those participants from the analysis of the data from the main experiment, who tended to perform above or below chance level in either of the two awareness tests (12 participants exhibited an awareness score with a binomial probability of P < 20%).”
builds-on Duss et al. (2011) subliminal masking paradigm— “We used a well-established paradigm to render stimuli invisible ( Duss et al. , 2011 ).”
builds-on Greenwald et al. (1995) regression method for estimating awareness-independent subliminal effects— “This method was brought forward by Greenwald et al. (1995) .”
contradicts prevailing views that consciousness is a precondition for information integration, relational learning and decision-making (Tononi, 2004; Shanks, 2010; Newell and Shanks, 2014)— “The finding that subliminally processed information is rapidly integrated and stored to guide delayed decisions challenges prevailing views of the cognitive function of consciousness.”
critiques the view that subliminal effects decay within a second and are therefore harmless— “Subliminal manipulation is often considered harmless because its effects typically decay within a second.”
critiques the assumption that subliminal messages are stored only slowly and not at all when novel— “These studies suggest that subliminal messages are only slowly stored and might not be stored at all if they provide novel, unfamiliar information.”
extends Dienes on using Bayes factors to test whether a null can be accepted— “We, therefore, calculated Bayes factors for the two t -tests following the recommendations provided in Dienes (2008 , 2014 ).”
extends Greenwald et al.'s regression method for separating subliminal influence from residual awareness— “This method was brought forward by Greenwald et al. (1995) .”
sensea non-significant awareness test - as ABSENCE OF EVIDENCE: the test may simply have been too insensitive, which proves nothing— “However, the nonsignificant tests do not reveal whether participants were truly unaware (i.e. whether H0 can be accepted), or whether our tests were not sensitive enough to detect awareness.”
sensea non-significant awareness test - as EVIDENCE OF ABSENCE: a Bayes factor below one third, which is what Experiment 1 obtained— “Both factors were below 1/3, indicating substantial evidence for the null hypothesis, i.e. that participants were not aware of the masked stimuli.”
sensethe same test in Experiment 2 - INCONCLUSIVE: a factor near one, favouring neither hypothesis, and reported rather than glossed— “The resulting factor for the awareness test (mean accuracy = 1.3% above chance level, SE = 1.16%) was 1.14, which suggests that the test was not sensitive enough to reject stimulus awareness.”
sensesubliminal effect - the received view: decays within about a second, which is why it is thought harmless— “Subliminal manipulation is often considered harmless because its effects typically decay within a second.”
sensesubliminal effect - as found here: undiminished at 25 minutes compared with 15, for novel relational material
