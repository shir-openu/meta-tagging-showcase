transferfrom virology: monosynaptic rabies tracing, adapted to single starter cells and to developmental time points — “In addition to providing specific insights regarding the development of connectivity onto Re + interneurons, our findings suggest that monosynaptic tracing using the rabies virus provides an effective method for determining the afferent connectivity of cortical neurons at different developmental time points.”
transferfrom retinogeniculate development: the finding that specific rather than global activity refines connectivity, used as the frame for this result — “These observations emphasize that understanding how brain wiring is achieved will require a case-by-case examination of how different cell types and the cortical microcircuits they contribute to are assembled.”
mechanismDeveloping interneurons discriminate between otherwise similar glutamatergic afferents according to which postsynaptic receptor subtype those afferents engage — “Re + interneurons distinguished between seemingly similar inputs on the basis of the postsynaptic receptors that the afferents impinge on.”
mechanismNR2B-containing NMDA receptors are preferentially allocated to the dendritic postsynaptic sites that receive thalamic input, giving ascending afferents a privileged signalling channel — “Our results extend these observations and suggest that there is a preferential allocation of NR2B-containing NMDARs to the postsynaptic sites in the dendrites of Re + interneurons that receive thalamic input.”
mechanismNMDA receptor activation drives the interneuron's integration into the nascent circuit, so that losing it both stunts the arbor and shifts the balance of afferents toward local cortical sources — “Together these data suggest that activation of NMDARs mediates the proper integration of maturing interneurons in the brain.”
mechanism — “The focal rabies injection together with the sparse electroporation of ventrally generated interneurons further diluted by their dispersion during tangential migration to the cortex 15 allowed us to target one starter cell in each experimental brain”
mechanism — “In accordance with the anatomical tracing, we recorded light-evoked monosynaptic excitatory responses from these neurons in the presence of tetrodotoxin (TTX) to block polysynaptic events and 4-aminopyridine (4AP) to enhance presynaptic vesicular release”

limitationThe complete-intracortical-blockade genotype yielded very few animals, so a second mouse line had to be generated to enlarge the sample. — “In an effort to increase the sample size for this analysis, we generated mice containing a Bhlhb5 Cre allele and loxP -flanked ( fl ) Munc18 ( Stxbp1 ) alleles 24 .”
limitationthe absence of neuromodulatory input is overridden by inference from the authors' own prior work, which says it must appear eventually — “Notably, unlike in other interneuron populations we have investigated, such as VIP- and SST-expressing interneurons ( Supplementary Fig. 3 and data not shown), we did not observe appreciable inputs onto the Re + interneurons from ascending neuromodulatory fibers such as those from the dorsal raphe and the nucleus basalis (although, on the basis of our previous findings 16 , we infer such innervation must ultimately be established).”
limitationinterneuron-to-interneuron connectivity did not change significantly, with only three mutant animals — “In contrast, interneuron afferent connectivity was not significantly changed in Dlx5 / 6-Cre;NR1 fl/fl interneurons (Ctrl: 27% ± 6 versus Dlx5 / 6-Cre;NR1 fl/fl : 42% ± 4, P = 0.103) ( Fig. 7f ).”
limitationthe cross-species claim about the proportion of these interneurons is explicitly a speculation — “The increase in abundance of this population across species is speculated to reflect the larger demand for associative functions in the human brain”
limitationwhether VIP interneurons use intrinsic programs or unidentified activity sources is left open — “These results indicate that Re + neurogliaform interneurons are critically reliant on electrical activity to mature and integrate into cortical circuits, whereas VIP + interneurons may use either intrinsic genetic programs or as-yet-unidentified sources of activity.”

findingContrary to the classical view that layer IV is the main recipient of ascending sensory input, Reelin-expressing interneurons in layers I-III receive direct thalamic afferents during the first postnatal week. — “Although layer IV has been classically regarded as the primary recipient of ascending sensory information carried via thalamic terminals 11 , we found, unexpectedly, that during the first postnatal week, in addition to inputs from intracortical sources, Re + interneurons in layers I–III also receive direct thalamic inputs.”
findingWhisker deprivation left cortical layering and interneuron migration intact but severely truncated the axons and dendrites of Reelin-expressing interneurons. — “While this manipulation did not perturb cortical layering or interneuron migration ( Fig. 2b and Supplementary Fig. 7a,b ), analysis of the morphological maturation of Re + interneurons revealed severely truncated axonal arbors and dendritic trees ( Fig. 2c,e–g and Supplementary Fig. 7c ).”
findingGenetic silencing of thalamic transmission with tetanus toxin light chain reproduced the deprivation phenotype, establishing thalamic activity as necessary for axo-dendritic development. — “These results suggest that thalamic activity is fundamental to the axo-dendritic development of Re + interneurons and are consistent with our previous findings indicating that, in contrast to VIP + subtypes, Re + interneurons are affected by alterations in activity 9 .”
findingBlocking intracortical glutamate release, by contrast, left the morphological development of the same interneurons unaffected. — “These results show that we can substantially reduce the afferent intracortical drive to Re + interneurons without noticeably impairing their development.”
findingThalamic afferents recruit a larger NR2B-containing NMDA receptor component than cortical afferents do, as measured by ifenprodil blockade. — “We found that light-evoked NMDAR-mediated synaptic responses showed a larger NR2B-component in VGlut2-ChR2 than in Emx1-ChR2 mice ( Fig. 4d–f ).”
findingThe maturation requirement is specific to NR2B: NR2A-null and AMPA-receptor-blocked interneurons developed normally. — “These defects were specific to the loss of NR2B-containing receptors, as Re + interneurons developed normally in NR2A −/− ( Grin2a −/− ) mice ( Fig. 6 ), as well as after blockade of AMPA receptors in vivo ( Supplementary Fig. 12 ).”
findingThe NMDA receptor requirement is confined to a narrow window between postnatal days 3 and 6. — “These results indicate that NR2B-containing NMDARs driven by the thalamic inputs are required in Re + interneurons between P3 and P6 for their proper morphological development.”
findingCell-autonomous NMDA receptor deletion rewired the afferent balance, cutting thalamic input roughly fourfold while tripling intracortical pyramidal input, with total input count unchanged. — “Dlx5 / 6-Cre;NR1 fl/fl interneurons showed a severe reduction in thalamic afferent connectivity (Ctrl: 60% ± 6 versus Dlx5 / 6-Cre;NR1 fl/fl : 16% ± 2, P < 0.01) and a concomitant increase in intracortical pyramidal cell innervation (Ctrl: 14% ± 1 versus Dlx5 / 6-Cre;NR1 fl/fl : 42% ± 2, P < 0.0001) ( Fig. 7c,e,f ).”
findingsuperficial Reelin interneurons receive direct thalamic input in the first postnatal week, contrary to the layer IV expectation — “Although layer IV has been classically regarded as the primary recipient of ascending sensory information carried via thalamic terminals 11 , we found, unexpectedly, that during the first postnatal week, in addition to inputs from intracortical sources, Re + interneurons in layers I–III also receive direct thalamic inputs.”
