research question — evidence— “However, the mechanisms by which the formation of these complex cortical circuits is regulated, including the establishment and maintenance of an appropriate balance between thalamic and intracortical inputs, are unknown.”
contribution — evidence— “These results suggest that thalamic inputs are critical in determining the balance between local and long-range connectivity and are fundamental to the proper integration of Reelin-expressing interneurons into nascent cortical circuits.”
transferfrom virology: monosynaptic rabies tracing, adapted to single starter cells and to developmental time points— “In addition to providing specific insights regarding the development of connectivity onto Re + interneurons, our findings suggest that monosynaptic tracing using the rabies virus provides an effective method for determining the afferent connectivity of cortical neurons at different developmental time points.”
transferfrom retinogeniculate development: the finding that specific rather than global activity refines connectivity, used as the frame for this result— “These observations emphasize that understanding how brain wiring is achieved will require a case-by-case examination of how different cell types and the cortical microcircuits they contribute to are assembled.”
mechanismDeveloping interneurons discriminate between otherwise similar glutamatergic afferents according to which postsynaptic receptor subtype those afferents engage— “Re + interneurons distinguished between seemingly similar inputs on the basis of the postsynaptic receptors that the afferents impinge on.”
mechanismNR2B-containing NMDA receptors are preferentially allocated to the dendritic postsynaptic sites that receive thalamic input, giving ascending afferents a privileged signalling channel— “Our results extend these observations and suggest that there is a preferential allocation of NR2B-containing NMDARs to the postsynaptic sites in the dendrites of Re + interneurons that receive thalamic input.”
mechanismNMDA receptor activation drives the interneuron's integration into the nascent circuit, so that losing it both stunts the arbor and shifts the balance of afferents toward local cortical sources— “Together these data suggest that activation of NMDARs mediates the proper integration of maturing interneurons in the brain.”
mechanismsparse electroporation plus focal rabies injection yields one starter cell per brain, so the traced inputs belong to a single identified interneuron— “The focal rabies injection together with the sparse electroporation of ventrally generated interneurons further diluted by their dispersion during tangential migration to the cortex 15 allowed us to target one starter cell in each experimental brain”
mechanismanatomical tracing is confirmed functionally by optogenetic mapping with TTX and 4AP, isolating monosynaptic responses— “In accordance with the anatomical tracing, we recorded light-evoked monosynaptic excitatory responses from these neurons in the presence of tetrodotoxin (TTX) to block polysynaptic events and 4-aminopyridine (4AP) to enhance presynaptic vesicular release”
mechanismthe requirement is timed: removing the receptor at P3 reproduces the defect and at P6 does not, placing the critical window between them— “After electroporating Dlx5/6-Cre ER (estrogen receptor) and CAG-STOP-GFP plasmids in NR1 fl/fl mice at E15.5, we subsequently administered tamoxifen at P3 or P6 and analyzed Re + interneuron morphology at P8. Tamoxifen administration at P3 but not at P6 phenocopied the morphological defects observed in the embryonic Dlx5/6-Cre ablation…
mechanismthe effect is subunit-specific - NR2B-containing receptors are required, NR2A-containing ones are not— “In exploring the mechanisms underlying this observation, we found that thalamocortical synapses use NMDA receptors (NMDARs) containing the NR2B subunit and that cell-autonomous removal of these receptors (but not NR2A-containing receptors) led to severe morphological defects in Re + interneurons.”
mechanismAMPA receptor signalling remains intact and is not sufficient: the cells still fail to develop axons or integrate— “Remarkably, even in the presence of substantial glutamatergic signaling through AMPARs, Dlx5 / 6-Cre;NR1 fl/fl interneurons failed to develop proper axonal processes and to integrate properly into nascent cortical circuits.”
mechanismthe total input count is preserved while its composition flips, so this is reallocation rather than loss— “Furthermore, the total number of presynaptic traced cells was not significantly different between control (190 ± 55) and Dlx5/6-Cre;NR1 fl/fl (150 ± 47; P = 0.646) interneurons ( Fig. 7d ).”
