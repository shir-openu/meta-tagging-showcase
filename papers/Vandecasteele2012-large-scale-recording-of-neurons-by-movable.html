mechanismAny break in the ground path (CSF to ground screw to copper wire to copper mesh to ground pin) leaves the headstage ungrounded, which is why movement artifacts and 50/60 Hz line noise appear in the recording— “the electrical path of the grounding should be checked, as any circuit interruption between the cerebrospinal fluid, the ground screw, the copper wire, the copper mesh flaps and the ground pin on the connector, would result in a large movement artifacts and/or line noise (50 Hz or 60 Hz).”
mechanismA micro-drive protruding above a too-short Faraday cage picks up environmental electrical fields, acting as an antenna and injecting noise into the signal— “If the Faraday cage is not high enough, the protruding micro-drive may act as an antenna.”
mechanismThe copper-mesh cylinder encloses the headgear and shunts environmental fields and whisker-generated charge to ground, which is how it suppresses noise and slow-wave artifacts— “The cylinder also serves as an electrical shield against environmental noise and the slow wave artifacts produced by the charged whiskers in the behaving animal.”
mechanismPlacing the reference over the cerebellum exploits the fact that cerebellar LFP amplitude and midline muscle artifact are both minimal, so the referenced signal carries less contamination— “This site is chosen because cerebellar LFP is the smallest of all cortical regions and muscle artifacts are minimal at this midline location.”
mechanismThe microdrive design is a set of trade-offs rather than an optimum, which is why different drives suit different applications— “As a result of these competing requirements, different drives suite different applications.”
mechanismThe probe shanks must be aligned parallel to the travel axis so that advancing moves them through tissue rather than cutting across it— “This ensures that during advancement, the probe shanks move into brain tissue without 'cutting' through it.”
mechanismInsulation at the bonding area keeps cerebrospinal fluid from short-circuiting the connections— “Before fixing the probe to the drive, add extra insulation to the bonding area of the probe to prevent cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) or humidity from producing short-circuits:”
