findingA style-based generator (borrowing from style-transfer) drives synthesis via a mapping network + AdaIN per layer, giving automatic unsupervised separation of high-level attributes (pose/identity) from stochastic detail (freckles/hair) and scale-specific control. — “an au- tomatically learned, unsupervised separation of high-level attributes (e.g., pose and identity when trained on human faces) and stochastic variation in the generated images (e.g., freckles, hair), and it enables intuitive, scale-specific control of the synthesis”
