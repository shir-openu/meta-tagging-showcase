transferfrom organochlorine toxicology: the persistent-organic-pollutant framework, carried from chlorinated chemicals to fluorinated ones, including the expectation that concentrations rise with age - which fails here— “For PFOS, PFOA, and PFNA, however—unlike lipophilic POPs whose serum concentrations increase with age —concentrations were quite similar among the four age groups (Tables 2–5), a finding that agrees with previous data.”
mechanismproduction phase-out reaching human serum electrochemical fluorination of PFOS and related compounds stopped in 2002, and three of the four common PFCs show lower geometric means four years later— “The apparent reductions in concentrations of PFOS, PFOA, and PFHxS most likely are related to discontinuation in 2002 of industrial production by electrochemical fluorination of PFOS and related perfluorooctanesulfonyl fluoride compounds.”
mechanismdifferential elimination half-life PFHxS declines least because it clears slowest - 7.3 years against 4.8 for PFOS and 3.5 for PFOA - so the same phase-out shows up later in it— “These findings may be related to the lower concentrations of PFHxS than of PFOS or PFOA and to differences in the estimated geometric mean serum elimination half-life (PFHxS, 7.3 years; PFOA, 3.5 years; and PFOS, 4.8 years).”
mechanismseparate formation pathways PFOS and PFOA correlate more strongly with each other than either does with PFHxS, which the authors read as a shared source that PFHxS does not have— “Furthermore, the correlation between the serum concentrations of PFOS and PFOA was higher than correlations of PFHxS and either PFOA or PFOS, suggesting potential common exposure path-way(s) for PFOA and PFOS, but probably not for PFHxS”
mechanismchildhood carpet contact PFHxS is higher in adolescents than adults, and the proposed reason is contact with carpets treated with it - a route specific to one compound— “The higher concentrations of PFHxS in children and adolescents could be related to their increased contact with carpeted floors containing PFHxS, which is used for specific postmarket carpet-treatment applications.”
