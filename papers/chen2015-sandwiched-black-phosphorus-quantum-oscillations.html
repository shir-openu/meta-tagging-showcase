The period of SdHterm·dom oscillations 37 , 38 in 2D electron gas is 1/ B F and the longitudinal resistance is given by Δ R = R ( B , T )cos[2 π ( B F / B +1/2+ β )], where R ( B , T ) is the amplitude and β (0≤ β ≤1) is the Berry phaseterm·dom. β is known to be 0 in conventional 2D electron gas systems and 1/2 in grapheneterm 1 , 2 . To examine the 2D nature of hole gas in few-layer BPterm, we plot d R /d B as a function of 1/ B for different gate voltages. As shown in Fig. 3b , the oscillations at different gate voltages are periodic but exhibit different frequencies. A smaller period 1/ B F is observed at higher gate voltages where the carrier density n is larger, which is consistent with the theoretical oscillation period 37 expressed as 1/ B F =2/Φ 0 n , where Φ 0 =4.14 × 10 −15 Tm 2 is the flux quantum. For example, at V g =−50 V the oscillation period is ∼0.0138 T −1 , corresponding to a carrier density of ∼3.5 × 10 12 cm −2 , which is in excellent agreement with the value obtained from the gate capacitance model n = C g ( V g − V th )/ e ( Fig. 3f ). To further reveal the 2D nature of the charge carriers in few-layer BPterm, we carried out experimentsterm·mth to investigate the angular dependence of the SdHterm·dom oscillations. The linear dependence between the magnetic field B F and 1/cos θ confirms that the charge carriers in few-layer BPterm are in 2D nature, where θ is the tilting angle between the applied magnetic fields and the normal direction of the substrate plane (see Supplementary Note 7 and Supplementary Fig. 9 ). The Landau levelterm·dom (LLterm·dom) index N is plotted and the linear fit yields a Berry phaseterm·dom β =0 in our BPterm samples ( Fig. 3e ). A smaller LLterm·dom index would be achieved if magnetic fields could be further increased.

The cyclotron effective massterm·dom m c of hole carriers in few-layer BPterm can be obtained by fitting the temperature dependence of resistivity Δ R (see Fig. 3c ) using the Lifshitz–Kosevich formula A T ∝ T /sinh (2 π 2 k B Tm c / ℏ eB ), where k B is the Boltzmann constant. We fit Δ R at high magnetic fields to gain a better resolution as shown in Fig. 3d . The fitting results produce a cyclotron effective massterm·dom 0.27±0.02 m e at V g =−60 V. Owing to structural anisotropy, the carrier effective mass in few-layer BPterm is also highly anisotropic. Previous theoretical analyses 16 show that the effective masses along the X- and Y- directions are m x ∼0.14 m e and m y ∼0.89 m e respectively. The measured cyclotron effective massterm·dom in our BPterm samples is ∼0.27 m e , which is reasonable as compared with the theoretical value for few-layer BPterm 16 .

Finally, we designed a special sample (Sample C, 10 nm thick) to demonstrate the effects of transport anisotropy on the SdHterm·dom oscillations in BPterm. First, this sample was cut along the X-direction ( Fig. 4a ). The Hall mobilityterm·dom was ∼1,481 cm 2 V −1 s −1 at 1.7 K (∼716 cm 2 V −1 s −1 at room temperature). This experimental results are consistent with the conclusions in previous reports 15 , 33 . However, the magneto-resistivity in this reshaped sample (along the Y-direction) did not show any signature of SdHterm·dom oscillations with magnetic fields up to 8 T. A much stronger magnetic field is needed to observe SdHterm·dom oscillations in the sample.

Discussion In summary, we have demonstrated high-quality BN–BP–BN heterostructuresterm·def achieved by encapsulating atomically thin BPterm between BNterm layers followed by annealingterm·mth at high temperature. The BN–BP–BN heterostructureterm·def protects BPterm from property degradation and allows us to investigate the intrinsic properties of BPterm. The BNterm–BPterm–BNterm samples show excellent stability in the atmospheric environment with a high mobilityterm·dom ∼1,350 cm 2 V −1 s −1 and a large on–off ratio exceeding 10 5 . Quantum oscillationsterm·key and zero Berry phaseterm·dom are observed in BPterm hole gas at a magnetic field of 6 T at cryogenic temperatures. The ultra-clean interfaces realized by our fabrication process can effectively suppress charge trapterm·key states. They shed important light on how to improve the quality of atomically thin BPterm for practical applications in BP-based nanoelectronic devices.

Methods Sample preparation and transport measurements Bulk phosphorus and BNterm crystals (Polartherm grade PT110) were purchased from Smart-elements and Momentive, respectively.
