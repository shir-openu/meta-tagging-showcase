content-observations: finding · quantity · variable · definition · key-term — grounded in the full text
findingStone duality lifts to an equivalence between Stone spaces with closed relations and boolean algebras with subordination relations.— “Stone duality generalizes to an equivalence between the categories StoneR of Stone spaces and closed relations and BAS of boolean algebras and subordination relations.”
findingSplitting the equivalences (closed equivalence relations) in the allegory of Stone spaces and closed relations produces a category equivalent to compact Hausdorff spaces with closed relations.— “Splitting equivalences in StoneR yields a category that is equivalent to the category KHausR of compact Hausdorffspaces and closed relations.”
findingSplitting the equivalences on the algebraic side produces a category equivalent to de Vries algebras with compatible subordination relations.— “Similarly, splitting equivalences in BAS yields a category that is equivalent to the category DeVS of de Vries algebras and compatible subordination relations.”
findingCompact Hausdorff spaces with closed relations are equivalent to de Vries algebras with compatible subordinations, settling the open problem of finding a de Vries-style dual for KHausR.— “Applying the machinery of allegories then yields that KHausR is equivalent to DeVS, thus resolving a problem recently raised in the literature.”
findingThat equivalence cuts down to an equivalence between compact Hausdorff spaces with continuous maps and the wide subcategory DeVF of de Vries algebras whose morphisms obey two extra conditions, giving an alternative to de Vries duality.— “The equivalence between KHausR and DeVS further restricts to an equivalence between the category KHaus of compact Hausdorffspaces and continuous functions and the wide subcategory DeVF of DeVS whose morphisms satisfy additional conditions.”
findingThe gain over classical de Vries duality is that composition of morphisms becomes ordinary composition of relations rather than the ad hoc de Vries composition.— “One advantage of this approach is that composition of morphisms is usual relation composition.”
findingThe equivalence is realised concretely by sending a closed relation R to the subordination on regular opens defined by U S V exactly when R maps the closure of U inside V.— “The key is to associate with each closed relation R : X → Y the relation SR : RO(X) → RO(Y ) given by U SR V ⇐⇒ R[cl(U )] ⊆ V”
findingIsomorphisms in the intermediate categories are badly behaved: two S5-subordination spaces can be isomorphic in StoneER while their underlying Stone spaces differ in cardinality.— “Not every isomorphism in StoneER is a structure-preserving bijection; two objects (X1, E1) and (X2, E2) may be isomorphic even if X1 and X2 do not share the same cardinality.”
findingIn the de Vries algebra category DeVS, by contrast, every isomorphism is a structure-preserving bijection.— “Isomorphisms in DeVS are given by structure-preserving bijections.”
findingThe new category DeVF is dually isomorphic to the classical category DeV of de Vries algebras and de Vries morphisms, so classical de Vries duality is recovered from the new one.— “The contravariant functors described in Definition 6.13 establish a dual isomorphism between DeVF and DeV.”
findingThe construction yields, as a by-product, a new proof that the category of compact Hausdorff spaces is the exact completion of the category of Stone spaces.— “Thus, as a consequence we obtain an alternative proof of the fact that KHaus is the exact completion of Stone.”
