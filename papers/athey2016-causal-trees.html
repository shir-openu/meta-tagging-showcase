limitationrequires the unconfoundedness (conditional randomization) assumption — “we maintain the assumption of randomization conditional on the covariates”
limitationSample splitting for honest estimation costs precision, which is only partly offset by the removal of bias. — “Although there is a loss of precision due to sample splitting (which reduces sample size in each step of estimation), there is a benefit for fit in terms of eliminating bias that offsets at least part of the cost.”
limitationIn the simulations honest estimation is measurably worse than adaptive estimation on treatment-effect mean-squared error. — “The results show that there is a cost to honest estimation in terms of MSEτ , varying by design and estimator.”
limitationThe no-interference (SUTVA) assumption the methods rest on can fail when units are connected in a network. — “This assumption may be violated in settings where some units are connected through networks.”
limitationHybrid variants (adaptive tree construction combined with honest leaf estimation) are left unexamined. — “We do not consider those variations in this paper.”
limitationThe quantity the method estimates is never observed for any unit in the data. — “This creates complications for conventional methods because we do not observe unit level causal effects for any unit”
limitationThe paper's central adjective is a stipulated property of data use, not a claim about candour. — “We say that a model is “honest” if it does not use the same information for selecting the model structure (in our case, the partition of the covariate space) and for estimation given a model structure”
limitationThe improvement claimed for honesty is measured in coverage, and the paper names what it costs. — “we show that honest estimation can result in substantial improvements in coverage of confidence intervals, where our method attains nominal coverage rates, without much sacrifice in terms of fitting treatment effects”
limitationThe comparison class is conventional tree estimation under a name the paper assigns it. — “We contrast ‘’honest” estimation with “adaptive” estimation used in conventional CART, where the same data is used to build the partition and estimate leaf effects”

findingAthey & Imbens use recursive partitioning (regression trees) to discover subpopulations with heterogeneous treatment effects data-drivenly, without pre-committing to subgroups. — “We provide a data- driven approach to partition the data into subpopulations which differ in the magnitude of their treatment effects...
