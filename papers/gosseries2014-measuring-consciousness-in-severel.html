method — evidence— “The promise of such theoretically based markers is illustrated by recent findings that used a perturbational approach to assess the levels of consciousness.”
transferfrom information theory / algorithmic complexity: the normalized Lempel-Ziv complexity measure, originally defined for finite symbol sequences, is computed on TMS-evoked EEG spatiotemporal response maps to yield the perturbational complexity index— “PCI estimates both the information content and the integration of brain activations through the computation of the normalized Lempel-Ziv complexity (Lempel & Ziv 1976) of the significant EEG spatiotemporal responses to TMS.”
mechanismConsciousness arises from a system's capacity to integrate information, so that networks supporting it must strike an optimal balance between functional integration and differentiation— “In the case of the brain, the theory predicts that consciousness-supporting networks should present an optimal balance between functional integration and differentiation (Boly et al. 2009).”
mechanismLoss of consciousness occurs through a breakdown of cortical effective connectivity, so that a local perturbation can no longer propagate as a widespread differentiated causal interaction— “During non–rapid eye movement sleep (NREM), under general anesthesia (e.g., midazolam), and in VS/UWS patients, TMS typically triggers a stereotypical slow wave that stays local, which indicates a breakdown of effective connectivity (Ferrarelli et al. 2010, Massimini et al. 2005, Rosanova et al. 2012).”
mechanismPreserved cortical backward (top-down) connections generate the larger long-latency evoked components seen in MCS relative to VS/UWS— “The higher amplitude of long latency components in MCS patients as compared with VS/UWS patients could be linked to preserved function in cerebral backward connections (Figure 2f ) (Boly et al. 2011).”

sensebedside consciousness - WHAT THE PATIENT CAN SHOW, NOT WHAT THEY HAVE: the assessment is intrinsically gated by behavioral responsiveness, and the same reading is returned by a patient who is unconscious and by one who is conscious and unable to demonstrate it, which the review states cannot be treated as proof either way— “bedside assessment of consciousness is intrinsically gated by behavioral responsiveness. sensethe 40% misdiagnosis rate - CONDITIONAL ON THE INSTRUMENT: it applies to diagnosis based solely on clinical consensus without appropriate behavioral scales, so the number measures a practice rather than a difficulty, and the practice is nameable and fixable— “Misdiagnosis of unawareness is very frequent (up to 40%) when diagnosis is based solely on clinical consensus, without use of appropriate behavioral scales”
sensethe active-task paradigm - VALID ONLY IF A CONTROL HOLDS THAT HAS NOT BEEN RUN: its status as evidence of willful response depends on passive listening not producing a similar pattern, and the authors say validation studies should be performed to establish that— “Thus, validation studies should be performed to ensure that the passive listening of the instruction to perform a task cannot elicit a brain activity pattern similar to the one from a voluntary response”
controla separate experiment run on the same task — held — rules out reflexive activation as the source of the response, and so establishes the task's specificity to conscious responses— “In a separate experiment, this task was controlled for the absence of reflexive activation and, thus, for its specificity to detect only conscious responses (Naci et al. 2013).”
controlcomparing active against passive conditions, and runs attending to the patient's own name against runs attending to another name — held — rules out a nonreflexive response being mistaken for command-following, and a nonspecific response being mistaken for a targeted one— “The search for a difference between active and passive conditions as well as between runs with attention to the patient’s own name and runs with attention to another name offers a control for both the presence of nonreflexive responses and for specificity.”
controlinstructing subjects NOT to perform the task and recording what happens — held — rules out activation that would have occurred without any intention to comply - the response is absent when compliance is withheld— “Here again a control experiment shows no response when the subjects are instructed not to do the task.”
