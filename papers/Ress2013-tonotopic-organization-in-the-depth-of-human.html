mechanismBecause BOLD reports synaptic input and local-circuit activity more than output spiking, the measured map tracks the interdigitated tonotopy of cochlear-nucleus afferents rather than the smooth map of IC neurons, producing patches.— “Accordingly, our results may more closely reflect the patchy, somewhat interdigitated tonotopic organization of bottom-up neuronal inputs to IC from the cochlear nucleus, as reported by anterograde labeling studies (Oliver and Morest, 1984 ; Hofstetter and Ehret, 1992 ; Loftus et al., 2008 ).”
mechanismAt 3 T half the BOLD signal is intravascular, so tissue near large draining veins yields strong contrast and tissue away from them yields little, imposing a vascular patch pattern on the frequency map.— “If so, the observed patchy structure may reflect stronger contrast from regions of IC where there are larger concentrations of draining veins, while little contrast is observed in other regions.”
mechanismAttentive auditory tasks bias processing to the left hemisphere, and largely ipsilateral corticocollicular feedback carries that bias down to the colliculus, yielding stronger left-IC gradients.— “Auditory tasks that require attentive processing are known to show a left-hemispheric bias (Bryden et al., 1983 ) and can modulate IC activity via corticocollicular connections (Rinne et al., 2007 , 2008 ).”
mechanismEach voxel averages millions of neurons of mixed frequency preference, so within-voxel heterogeneity broadens the apparent tuning and compresses the measured gradient range.— “Each voxel encompasses a volume of ∼1.7 μL containing several million neurons, so some heterogeneity of response can be expected.”
mechanisma surface model of the IC allows virtual penetrations normal to the tissue, a hemodynamic analogue of an electrode track— “Thus, the notion of using tissue depth as a reference frame was proven to work fairly well, despite the patchy character of the data.”

findingHigh-resolution 3 T fMRI resolves a depth tonotopy in human inferior colliculus, with superficial tissue preferring low frequencies and deeper tissue high frequencies, in both ICs of every subject.
findingUnlike the smooth gradients reported at 4.7 T and 7 T, the frequency map measured here is spatially patchy rather than continuous.
findingRe-running the whole analysis on data blurred with a 2-mm kernel reproduced the same patchy layout, so the patchiness is signal rather than thermal noise.
