mechanismsynonymous mutations as a process signal a synonymous change cannot itself create a neoantigen, but it is produced by the same mutational process that produced the nonsynonymous ones elsewhere, so counting it raises the sample size of the rate estimate without changing what the rate is about— “Synonymous mutations are counted in order to reduce sampling noise. While synonymous mutations are not likely to be directly involved in creating immunogenicity, their presence is a signal of mutational processes that will also have resulted in nonsynonymous mutations and neoantigens elsewhere in the genome.”
mechanismgermline filtering without a matched normal with no normal tissue sequenced, inherited variants must be removed by matching against reference databases - so the filter's accuracy is the databases' coverage of the patient's ancestry— “We found that filtering out germline alterations and rare variants was important to obtaining accurate measurements of TMB, and this will especially be important in patients from ethnic backgrounds not well represented in sequencing datasets.”
