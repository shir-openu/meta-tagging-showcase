mechanismthe measurement is what absorption left behind 90 to 95 per cent of the SCFAs produced in the colonic lumen are taken up by the mucosa before anything is collected. A fecal SCFA concentration is therefore the small remainder, and a difference between patients and controls could be a difference in production, in absorption, or in transit - the number cannot distinguish them— “Quantification of human intestinal SCFAs only provides steady state levels and may not accurately reflect the level of bacterial production as most SCFAs produced in the colonic lumen (90–95%) are absorbed by the gut mucosa”
mechanisman intervention whose sign is a property of the model germ-free conditions make IL-10-deficient chronic colitis less severe and acute chemically-induced colitis worse. Any statement of the form 'the microbiota protects against colitis' is therefore incomplete without naming the model, and two papers reporting opposite results may both be right— “This may go either way, e.g., IL-10 deficient mice show less severe chronic bowel inflammation in germfree (GF) conditions ( 13, 14), while acute chemically-induced colitis is exacerbated in GF mice compared to mice with a normal microbiome”
mechanisma null result whose treatment lacked the active ingredient probiotics showed no significant effect in Crohn's disease - and the review notes in the next sentence that the probiotics evaluated in those studies were not butyrate-producing bacteria. The hypothesis being tested was about butyrate and the intervention did not contain any— “So far, probiotic treatments have not shown a significant effect in inducing or maintaining remission of active or quiescent CD, or in preventing relapse of CD after surgically-induced remission (146, 147). However, probiotics evaluated in these studies were not butyrate-producing bacteria.”
mechanisma division of labour that makes composition into chemistry Bacteroidetes make acetate and propionate; Firmicutes make butyrate. A shift in phylum ratio is therefore automatically a shift in which metabolite the colon receives, which is why a compositional 16S result can be read as a functional claim and why that reading is only as good as the division— “Bacteroidetes (gram-negative) and Firmicutes (gram-positi ve) are the most abundant phyla in the intestine, with members of the Bacteroidetes mainly producing acetate and propionate, while Firmicutes mostly produce butyrate in the human gut”
mechanismoxygen as the intermediate butyrate absorbed and oxidised by the epithelium consumes local oxygen. Deplete the anaerobes with antibiotics, butyrate falls, the mucosa stops consuming oxygen, and the colon becomes hospitable to aerobes such as Salmonella - a chain in which the metabolite acts by changing the physical environment rather than by signalling— “The susceptibility due to the depletion of anaerobic bacteria (induced by antibiotics ) is associated to a reduction in butyrate levels, thus promoting an aerobic environment and the expansion of aerobic bacteria suc h as Salmonella typhimurium”
