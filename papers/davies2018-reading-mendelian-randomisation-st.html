limitation**EVERY ASSUMPTION IS PER-VARIANT AND MOST STUDIES USE MANY VARIANTS**: `Generally, the three key assumptions MUST HOLD FOR EACH OF THE GENETIC VARIANTS.` A study with a hundred-variant allele score is making three hundred assumptions, of which the exclusion restriction is `NOT DIRECTLY TESTABLE` in the general case - the tools listed against it are biological knowledge, association tests against alternative pathways, and MR Egger
limitationAND THE FIX FOR PLEIOTROPY IS ITSELF ARBITRARY: `INCLUDING OR EXCLUDING A GENETIC VARIANT IN THIS WAY CAN BE ARBITRARY AND LIMITED BY AVAILABLE DATA; such pruning should be done with caution (IF AT ALL)`, and `EXCLUDING VARIANTS ON THE BASIS OF VERTICAL PLEIOTROPIC EFFECTS WILL BIAS FINDINGS`. **The remedy and the damage differ by which pleiotropy you think you are looking at**, and that judgement is not testable from the data
limitationAND THE ESTIMATE IS LIFELONG WHILE THE DECISION IS NOT: `the numerical effect estimates MAY NOT BE CLINICALLY MEANINGFUL. WILL INTERVENTIONS AT A SPECIFIC AGE HAVE THE SAME SIZED EFFECTS?` The vitamin D and multiple sclerosis case is given as the example - `such protection MAY BE LIMITED TO CHILDHOOD AND ADOLESCENCE AND NOT LATER ADULT LIFE`
limitationAND THE TWO-SAMPLE DESIGN BUYS POWER AND ADDS ASSUMPTIONS: `These advantages come with TWO ADDITIONAL ASSUMPTIONS: THE TWO SAMPLES ARE ASSUMED TO REPRESENT THE SAME UNDERLYING POPULATION, and OVERLAP IN PARTICIPANTS BETWEEN THE TWO SAMPLES CAN CAUSE BIAS towards the risk factor-outcome association.` **A design chosen because the data is cheaper requires two claims about populations nobody measured together**
limitationAND WIDE INTERVALS ARE NOT A NULL: `when the Mendelian randomisation estimate has VERY WIDE CONFIDENCE INTERVALS that overlap the observational estimate AND INCLUDE THE NULL ..., VERY LITTLE CAN BE INFERRED from the Mendelian randomisation results.` Read with the falsely-reassuring heterogeneity test, **both of the paper's warnings are about mistaking imprecision for agreement**
limitationAND THE METHODOLOGICAL SETTLEMENT IS OPEN: `THE CONVERSATION ON HOW TO CONDUCT, REPORT, AND INTERPRET MENDELIAN RANDOMISATION STUDIES IS STILL ONGOING.` A guide with 4,781 citations, saying that the thing it is a guide to is not settled
