limitationThe probe reports spiking only, so it complements rather than replaces intracellular and all-optical methods. — “Compared to these alternatives, the μLED probe approach is limited in that it only provides information about spiking (and not about intracellular calcium, Vm, or transmembrane currents) and should therefore be viewed as a complementary method.”
limitationThe indirect test of heating effects on spike waveforms is weakened by concurrent high-frequency oscillations at high power. — “However, high-power illumination was occasionally accompanied by high-frequency (2 kHz) oscillations, limiting the interpretational power of these observations.”
limitationphoton extraction is poor by construction: 33% internal efficiency, half the light lost into the substrate, most of the rest trapped by total internal reflection — “These photons generated at the active region are emitted in all directions. Approximately 50% of the photons are emitted toward the substrate and are absorbed at the GaN/silicon interface. Of the remaining photons, only 8.8% can escape from total internal reflection (TIR) at the GaN/brain interface, which has a high index of refraction contrast (n GaN = 2.45 and n brain = 1.36). Additionally, the Ni/Au current spreading layer has 75% measured transparency to blue light. The photon extraction efficiency is therefore only 3.33%, which is similar to results from a previous report”
limitationperiodic driving injects a low-frequency artefact into the recordings, present in opsin-free animals and removed only by offline filtering — “A limitation of the current version of μLED probes is the low-frequency artifacts induced during periodic activation. As a result, they did not hinder the analysis of spiking or LFP effects ( Figures 4 , 5 , and 6 ), but limited the range of stimulus waveforms that could be applied.”
limitationthere is no accepted safe-temperature standard for implanted devices, so the 1 degree threshold used here is the authors' own — “As there is no accepted threshold temperature for the safe operation of implantable neural devices ( Elwassif et al., 2006 ), we loosely define the “threshold” as 1”
limitationwhether a few milliseconds above 38 degrees harms the adjacent network is stated as unknown — “Beyond 3.85 V input, the peak temperature can transiently reach 38°C; however, whether a few milliseconds of heating beyond 38°C has adverse effect on the adjacent neural network is unknown.”
limitationthe probe reports spiking only, not calcium, membrane potential or currents, and is offered as complementary rather than superior — “Compared to these alternatives, the μLED probe approach is limited in that it only provides information about spiking (and not about intracellular calcium, Vm, or transmembrane currents) and should therefore be viewed as a complementary method.”
