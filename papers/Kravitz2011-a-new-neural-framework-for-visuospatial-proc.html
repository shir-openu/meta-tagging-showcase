mechanismThe three pathways acquire their different functions from their differing cortical targets rather than from their overlapping parietal sources — granular six-layered targets support perception while the agranular premotor target supports action — “To account for how these three pathways support such distinctly different visuospatial functions — conscious perception and nonconscious action — and yet emerge from overlapping sources within the posterior parietal cortex, we suggest that their differential cortical targets are largely responsible.”
mechanismRegional function is generated by the region's pattern of connectivity, so densely and reciprocally connected regions necessarily share functional properties and functions blend in gradients along a pathway — “A major premise in this perspective is that the function of a region emerges from its particular pattern of connectivity, and that densely and reciprocally interconnected regions must share at least some functional properties.”
mechanismThe PCC and RSC sit mid-pathway and perform the conversion of egocentric parietal space codes into the allocentric codes used by the medial temporal lobe — “Consistent with its position in the parieto – medial temporal pathway, the PCC has also been proposed as an area that contributes to the translation between the egocentric representations of space in the posterior parietal cortex and the allocentric representations in the MTL (for example, REF. 119 ).”
mechanism — “This stream is now known to consist of a set of projections from early visual cortical areas to posterior regions of the parietal cortex — the latter including medial portions of the superior parietal lobule (SPL) — together with interconnections among those parietal regions.”
mechanism — “The posterior parietal region seems to contain predominantly egocentric representations of space, whereas the MTL contains spatial representations of scenes that are mainly allocentric.”

limitationNo research directly compares the relative importance of the different functional properties within the same parietal region, so the proposed anatomical divisions are not yet functionally adjudicated. — “Unfortunately, there has been little research that directly compares the relative importance of these different functional properties (for example, peripersonal versus extrapersonal coding) within the same region.”
limitationEmpirical data on the intermediate relay areas (PCC, RSC) of the proposed parieto-medial temporal pathway are sparse. — “This new framework also highlights the paucity of empirical data regarding the intermediate areas in the parieto – medial temporal pathway.”
limitationThe trifurcation is not claimed to be exhaustive; further pathways may exist. — “In proposing a trifurcation in the extension of the dorsal stream, we do not mean to imply that there are three pathways only, for there may well be others.”
limitationScope is restricted to visuospatial processing, neglecting parietal contributions to spatial processing in other modalities. — “In limiting this Review to visuospatial processing, we recognize that we have largely neglected the role that the posterior parietal cortex plays in spatial processing in other modalities.”
limitationthe framework's central premise - that function follows connectivity - is stated as a premise rather than demonstrated — “A major premise in this perspective is that the function of a region emerges from its particular pattern of connectivity, and that densely and reciprocally interconnected regions must share at least some functional properties.”
limitationthe area-by-area exposition is described by the authors as purely didactic, with gradients assumed instead — “In this Review, we have examined the functional properties of each area along the parieto – medial temporal pathway separately, but this approach is purely didactic. In fact, we assume that there are gradients of function within and between these areas, including their posterior parietal and medial temporal endpoints.”
limitationhow the reference-frame transformations are actually performed remains unclear — “The multisynaptic projections pass through the PCC and RSC, each of which seems to contribute to the coordination of egocentric and allocentric frames of reference, although it is still unclear how these transformations between reference frames are brought about.”
limitationthere may be more than three pathways; the trifurcation is not claimed to be exhaustive — “In proposing a trifurcation in the extension of the dorsal stream, we do not mean to imply that there are three pathways only, for there may well be others.”
limitationthe granular versus agranular account is explicitly left for future research — “Whether or not these differences in the makeup of their cortical targets help to account for the striking differences in the functional characteristics — perception versus action — of the three pathways is a matter for future research.”
limitationnavigation evidence does not exclude other visuospatial functions for the pathway — “The multisynaptic projections pass through the PCC and RSC, each of which seems to contribute to the coordination of egocentric and allocentric frames of reference, although it is still unclear how these transformations between reference frames are brought about. This pathway’s functional properties — uncovered in neuroimaging and lesion studies — clearly indicate that it is crucial for navigation, although such evidence does not exclude the possibility that it may contribute to other forms of visuospatial function as well.”

findingThe dorsal stream is shown to trifurcate beyond parietal cortex into parieto-prefrontal, parieto-premotor and parieto-medial temporal pathways, the last running through posterior cingulate and retrosplenial cortex to the medial temporal lobe. — “Here, we identify three pathways emerging from the dorsal stream that consist of projections to the prefrontal and premotor cortices, and a major projection to the medial temporal lobe that courses both directly and indirectly through the posterior cingulate and retrosplenial cortices.”
findingBecause each of the three pathways serves a different class of visuospatial function, neither the 'Where' nor the 'How' characterisation of the dorsal stream is adequate. — “The existence of these multiple pathways, each mediating a different class of visuospatial functions, suggests that earlier characterizations of the dorsal stream as being either a perceptual Where or a motoric How pathway were insufficient to adequately capture the diversity of its visuospatial functions.”
findingThe site of damage along the parieto-medial temporal pathway predicts which specific form of topographic disorientation a patient shows. — “In addition, we show that damage to the parieto–medial temporal pathway in humans results in various forms of topographic disorientation 15 that differ predictably depending on where along the pathway the damage has occurred.”
findingHuman resting-state fMRI connectivity between IPL, PCC, RSC and parahippocampal cortex converges with the monkey tract-tracing evidence for the proposed pathway. — “Strong correlations were also observed between time courses of fMRI responses in regions that constitute the parieto–medial temporal pathway — that is, the IPL, PCC and RSC — and the parahippocampal cortex ( FIG. 3 ; see REF. 53 for similar results in monkey and see REF. 54 for related results in human connectivity).”
findingWithin the inferior parietal lobule the labour is divided: only the rostral part specialises in guiding action in the space around the body, while the caudal part feeds the navigation pathway. — “Within the IPL however, only the rIPL (also known as area 7b, and composed of areas PFG and PF) is specialized for guiding action in peripersonal space.”
findingThe dorsal stream is reconceived as a widespread visuospatial system reaching frontal, temporal and limbic cortex and supporting both conscious perception and non-conscious spatial processing. — “We conclude that the dorsal stream, which was originally defined as the pathway between the striate cortex and the posterior part of the inferior parietal lobule, actually comprises a widespread visuospatial processing system that contributes to both spatial perception and non-conscious spatial processing across numerous cortical areas in the frontal, temporal and limbic lobes.”
findingThe medial temporal lobe is proposed as the site where the ventral (object) and dorsal (spatial) streams converge into a unified percept. — “Our Review suggests, as one potential answer, that a major point of convergence and perceptual integration is within the MTL, to which both the ventral stream and the parieto – medial temporal pathway project 188 .”
findingthe dorsal stream trifurcates beyond parietal cortex into prefrontal, premotor and medial temporal pathways — “We review anatomical and functional evidence in primates indicating that the dorsal stream actually gives rise to three distinct, major pathways; a parieto–prefrontal pathway, a parieto–premotor pathway and a parieto–medial temporal pathway ( FIG. 1c ), which primarily support spatial working memory, visually guided action and spatial navigation, respectively.”
findinghuman resting-state connectivity reproduces the monkey anatomy of the parieto-medial temporal pathway — “Strong correlations were also observed between time courses of fMRI responses in regions that constitute the parieto–medial temporal pathway — that is, the IPL, PCC and RSC — and the parahippocampal cortex ( FIG. 3 ; see REF. 53 for similar results in monkey and see REF. 54 for related results in human connectivity).”
findingdamage at different points along the pathway yields predictably different topographic disorientations — “In addition, we show that damage to the parieto–medial temporal pathway in humans results in various forms of topographic disorientation 15 that differ predictably depending on where along the pathway the damage has occurred.”
findinglandmark agnosia leaves orientation and map-making intact but destroys landmark recognition — “Patients with this type of agnosia have no difficulty in orienting themselves within an environment or in producing maps, however, they cannot recognize prominent, navigationally relevant landmarks”
findinganterograde topographic disorientation leaves orienting intact but prevents learning new routes — “Furthermore, in keeping with its strong connections to the hippocampus, damage to the presumed human homologue of areas TF and TH leads to anterograde topographic disorientation 15 , 160 , 161 , in which patients can no longer form representations of new environments.”
findingthe parahippocampal place area responds equally to furnished and emptied rooms, tracking layout rather than content — “Finally, in line with the role of TF/TH in creating spatial representations of complex environments, the PPA responds strongly during topographic and spatial learning (for example, REFS 169 – 171 ) and is more sensitive to the spatial layout of complex scenes than to their semantic content 165 , 172 (see REFS 173 , 174 for opposing views): it shows equivalent activation in response to seeing furnished rooms containing many objects and the same rooms when emptied of all objects”
