findingNeurons expressing PV, SST or the 5HT3a receptor together account for nearly all GABAergic interneurons of primary somatosensory cortex, with the 5HT3aR group making up about 30%. — “This analysis revealed that 5HT3aR neurons account for ~30% of the total population of GABAergic neurons, and that three group of neurons, those expressing PV, SST, and 5HT3aR, account for nearly all, if not all, GABAergic interneurons in primary somatosensory cortex ( Fig. 1 ).”
findingPV and SST proteins mark two non-overlapping interneuron populations, even though some PV cells carry SST mRNA. — “Thus, the data support the view that PV and SST protein expression defines two independent groups of GABAergic interneurons.”
findingIn layers I-III the 5HT3aR interneurons outnumber PV interneurons two to one, overturning the assumption that PV cells are the largest interneuron class there. — “Within layers I–III, the number of 5HT3aR-expressing neurons actually exceeds by two fold the number of PV-positive neurons, traditionally believed to represent the largest interneuron class.”
findingEvery VIP-expressing interneuron in somatosensory cortex also expresses the 5HT3a receptor, placing the whole VIP class inside the 5HT3aR group. — “Lee et al. (2010) found that all VIP-expressing neurons in somatosensory cortex express 5HT3a receptors.”
findingThe 5HT3aR agonist mCPBG depolarized every 5HT3aR-positive subtype tested across all layers. — “Local somatic application of mCPBG, a 5HT3aR-selective agonist, to EGFP-positive neurons evoked fast and robust depolarization in more than 40 cells tested throughout all layers and including all the subtypes identified electrophysiologically.”
findingPV- and SST-expressing neurons showed no response to the same agonist, confirming the pharmacological separation of the three groups. — “In contrast, no response was observed after application of mCPBG to PV and SST-expressing neurons.”
findingPV and SST interneurons arise exclusively from the medial ganglionic eminence, while the caudal ganglionic eminence supplies about 30% of cortical interneurons including the entire VIP population and the reelin-positive/SST-negative neurogliaform cells. — “We now know that the CGE gives rise to ~30% of all cortical interneurons and that this group not only contains the bipolar calretinin cells but also the entire VIP as well as the reelin-positive/SST-negative population that includes neurogliaform cells.”
findingThe EGFP reporter line matched receptor expression essentially perfectly, with no EGFP-positive cell lacking 5HT3aR mRNA. — “There were no false positives, no neuron expressed EGFP that did not also contain 5HT3aR mRNA transcripts.”
findingIn olfactory cortex inhibition moves from dendrite to soma during a burst, the reverse of the soma-then-dendrite sequence in neocortex and hippocampus, even though the interneurons themselves look alike. — “Thus, in olfactory cortex, inhibition shifts from the dendrite to the soma, the opposite of what is observed in neocortex and hippocampus, although the properties of the interneurons seem similar in the three structures.”
findingThe marker proportions may not carry over to primates: 74% of GABAergic neurons in macaque V1 express PV, far more than in rodents. — “Also, it has been reported that 74% of GABAergic neurons in the primary visual cortex (V1) of macaques express PV, much higher than the values reported in rodents ( Van Brederode et al., 1990 ).”
findingthree marker-defined groups account for nearly all, if not all, GABAergic interneurons in primary somatosensory cortex — “This analysis revealed that 5HT3aR neurons account for ~30% of the total population of GABAergic neurons, and that three group of neurons, those expressing PV, SST, and 5HT3aR, account for nearly all, if not all, GABAergic interneurons in primary somatosensory cortex”
findingthese two groups of neurons account for 40 and 30% of GABAergic interneurons in somatosensory cortex — “These two groups of neurons account for 40 and 30% of GABAergic interneurons in somatosensory cortex”
findingAN EXHAUSTIVE PARTITION CONSTRAINS EVERY OTHER MARKER: VIP, NPY, CCK, calretinin and calbindin must either label small populations or fall inside the three groups — “The conclusion that PV-, SST-, and 5HT3aR-expressing neurons account for nearly if not all GABAergic interneurons in mouse somatosensory cortex implies that other interneuron markers such as the neuropeptides VIP, NPY, and cholecystokinin (CCK) and the Ca 2+ -binding proteins calretinin and calbindin either label small interneuron populations or are expressed in one or more of the three groups defined above.”
findingthe third group is the majority interneuron population in layers I to III and outnumbers parvalbumin cells there twofold — “5HT3aR interneurons primarily populate superficial neocortical layers I–III ( Fig. 1 ). In fact, these cells are the predominant population of interneurons (~60% of all interneurons) in these layers. Within layers I–III, the number of 5HT3aR-expressing neurons actually exceeds by two fold the number of PV-positive neurons, traditionally believed to represent the largest interneuron class.”
findingTHE REPORTER WAS VALIDATED IN BOTH DIRECTIONS: no cell carried EGFP without the transcript, and 99.8 percent of receptor-expressing cells carried EGFP — “Using the GENSAT line, Lee et al. (2010) found a nearly perfect overlap between EGFP and 5HT3a receptor mRNA expression in the neocortex. There were no false positives, no neuron expressed EGFP that did not also contain 5HT3aR mRNA transcripts. Conversely, there were virtually no false negatives because 99.8 ± 0.3% of neocortical cells expressing the receptor were EGFP positive.”
findingand the transcript was checked against function, with responses to a selective agonist in more than 40 reporter-positive cells — “Furthermore, it is likely that 5HT3aR mRNA expression reliably reports on the presence of functional receptors. Functional responses were detected to the specific 5HT3aR-agonist mCPBG in more than 40 EGFP-positive neurons recorded in the cortex of this transgenic line”

findingBUT BY mRNA THEY DO: under 10 percent of PV cells carry SST transcripts overall, 16.3 percent in layer VI, and a large percentage by RT-PCR of sorted cells — “We do find that using in situ hybridization (ISH) some PV-containing neurons (<10%) in somatosensory cortex contain SST mRNA. quantity99.8 ± 0.3% — receptor-expressing cells that were reporter-positive - the sensitivity of the tag — “Using the GENSAT line, Lee et al. Conversely, there were virtually no false negatives because 99.8 ± 0.3% of neocortical cells expressing the receptor were EGFP positive.”

quantity~60% of all interneurons — the 5HT3aR share within layers I to III — “5HT3aR interneurons primarily populate superficial neocortical layers I–III ( Fig. 1 ). Within layers I–III, the number of 5HT3aR-expressing neurons actually exceeds by two fold the number of PV-positive neurons, traditionally believed to represent the largest interneuron class.”

variableinput resistance — “X94 cells in S1 cortex were located in layer IV and V and had axonal projections that instead of reaching and targeting layer I, innervate profusely layer IV. The cells also differed from Martinotti cells in a number of electrophysiological properties. X94 cells had a much lower input resistance, approaching that of FS cells. They had spikes of shorter duration and often produced a stuttering firing pattern. They were capable of firing at higher frequencies than Martinotti cells but, in contrast to FS neurons, exhibited spike frequency adaptation”
variablefacilitating — “Excitatory inputs onto Martinotti cells are generally strongly facilitating, which is a key feature that distinguishes these interneurons from FS neurons”
definitionfast-spiking (FS) cells — “FS cells fire sustained high-frequency trains of brief APs with a large and fast AHP and with little spike frequency adaptation.”
definitionbasket cells and chandelier cells — “There are two types of PV neurons: basket cells, neurons that make synapses at the soma and proximal dendrite of target neurons and typically have multipolar morphology, and chandelier cells, which target the axon initial segment (AIS) of pyramidal cells ( Kawaguchi and Kubota, 1997 ; Ascoli et al., 2008 ).”
definitionMartinotti cells — “SST expression in neocortex has typically been associated with Martinotti cells (MCs), GABAergic interneurons with ascending axons that arborize in layer I spreading horizontally to neighboring columns and making synapses on the dendritic tufts of pyramidal neurons.”
definitionneurogliaform (spiderweb) cells — “These cells, also called spiderweb cells, originally described by Ramon y Cajal, have a characteristic morphology consisting of a small, round soma from which multiple dendrites spread radially in all directions and have a wider round axonal plexus composed of fine branches ( Kawaguchi and Kubota, 1997 ; Olah et al., 2007 ).”
definitiongenetic inducible fate mapping (GIFM) — “By linking this expression to the expression of the enzyme cre-recombinase and a cre-dependent reporter, cells can be permanently labeled during development and then followed during their entire lifespan ( Zinyk et al., 1998 ).”
definitionmultipolar bursting (MB) cells — “These neurons, which they called multipolar bursting (MB), had a firing pattern in response to depolarizing steps that differed significantly from that of FS cells and consisted of an initial burst of two or three APs followed by a pronounced afterhyperpolarizing gap and a subsequent train of tonic spiking at low frequency.”
definitionlate spiking (LS) firing pattern — “Neurogliaform cells typically have a late spiking (LS) firing pattern characterized by a slow ramp depolarization preceding firing at threshold.”
definitionfast-spiking — “PV expression has been associated with the fast-spiking firing pattern since the 1980s ( Kawaguchi et al., 1987 ). This correlation between fast spiking and PV expression has been confirmed by many laboratories ( Cauli et al., 1997 ; Kawaguchi and Kubota, 1997 ; Gibson et al., 1999 ; Ascoli et al., 2008 ; Xu and Callaway, 2009 ), including our own. We have recorded from several hundred PV-expressing neurons in several mouse lines expressing GFP in PV neurons and virtually all are fast-spiking.”
