limitationResidual systematic errors in the polarization spectra are not folded into the quoted error bars, because the authors judge any such characterisation would be incomplete and could not be given a proper probabilistic form. — “In this paper we therefore do not explicitly model an increase in error bars due to these residual systematic errors – any such characterization would inevitably be incomplete, and it would also be impossible to give the necessary probabilistic characterization required for meaningful quantitative error bars.”
limitationImperfect corrections for polarization efficiency and temperature-to-polarization leakage remain the dominant systematic error in the high-multipole polarization spectra. — “Inaccuracies in the corrections for effective polarization efficiencies and temperature-to-polarization leakage are the main contributors to systematic errors in the Planck polarization spectra at high multipoles.”
limitationThe origin of the disagreement between Planck and the Lyman-alpha BAO measurements is left unresolved. — “Further work is needed to assess whether the discrepancy between Planck and the Ly α BAO results is a statistical fluctuation, is caused by small systematic errors, or is a signature of new physics.”
limitationResidual modelling uncertainty in the small-scale polarization is estimated, not eliminated, and is stated to shift parameters at the half-sigma level. — “Improved modelling of the small-scale polarization leads to more robust constraints on many parameters, with residual modelling uncertainties estimated to affect them only at the 0.5σ level.”

limitationThe likelihood used for the 2018 analysis was built on a fiducial model taken from the previous data release. — “we need to adopt a fiducial model. For the 2018 analysis, we adopted the best-fit CamSpec base-ΛCDM model from PCP15 to construct a likelihood from the 2018 temperature maps.”
limitationCluster counts are excluded from the main parameter grid because their mass calibrations are uncertain. — “Given the uncertainties in these calibrations, we do not use cluster counts in our main parameter grid.”
