mechanismTHE MODEL EVERY NUMBER IN THE PAPER IS QUOTED AGAINST IS INTRODUCED AS A QUOTED LABEL, WITH A PARAMETER COUNT AND A SCOPE: we refer to this model, **WHICH CAN BE SPECIFIED BY SIX PARAMETERS**, as **“BASE” ΛCDM IN THIS PAPER**. The scare quotes and the words `in this paper` are both the collaboration's: the label is declared local to the document, and what it denotes is fixed by a count of parameters rather than by a description — “We refer to this model, which can be specified by six parameters, as “base” ΛCDM in this paper.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
mechanismWHICH PARAMETERS ARE PRIMITIVE IS STATED AS A CHOICE, NOT AS A FACT ABOUT THE UNIVERSE: the first six parameters are **THE ONES ON WHICH WE IMPOSE FLAT PRIORS AND USE AS SAMPLING PARAMETERS**; the remaining parameters **ARE DERIVED FROM THE FIRST SIX**. The same physics could be parameterised the other way round, and the paper says which way it chose — “The first six parameters here are the ones on which we impose flat priors and use as sampling parameters; the remaining parameters are derived from the first six.”
mechanismAN ENTIRE CLASS OF PARAMETERS IS NAMED “NUISANCE” IN SCARE QUOTES - AND THE QUOTES COME OFF IN THE NEXT SENTENCE, WHERE THEY ARE SAMPLED ON THE SAME FOOTING AS THE COSMOLOGICAL ONES: a physically motivated parametric model containing **“nuisance” parameters**; **these nuisance parameters ARE SAMPLED, ALONG WITH COSMOLOGICAL PARAMETERS**, during MCMC exploration of the likelihood. The hedge is dropped one sentence after it is made, and the objects it hedged about are treated as first-class — “a physically motivated parametric model containing “nuisance” parameters. These nuisance parameters are sampled, along with cosmological parameters, during Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) exploration of the likelihood.”
mechanismA CEILING ON THE PAPER'S OWN INTERPRETATION, STATED BY THE PAPER: a total systematic uncertainty of around half a sigma **MAY BE MORE REALISTIC**, and **VALUES SHOULD NOT BE OVERINTERPRETED BEYOND THIS LEVEL**. Not a limitation of the data but an instruction to the reader about how far the numbers may be read - issued by the people who produced them — “A total systematic uncertainty of around 0.5σ may be more realistic, and values should not be overinterpreted beyond this level.”
mechanismTWO WAYS OF DOING ONE CORRECTION ARE NAMED IN A SINGLE SENTENCE, BY CONTRAST WITH EACH OTHER: adopting the estimates from one spectrum for both (**we call this the “MAP-BASED” APPROACH**); or applying independent estimates from each (**the “SPECTRUM-BASED” APPROACH**). Neither name would mean anything without the other; the pair is defined by the difference between two procedures — “We have investigated ways of correcting for effective polarization efficiencies: adopting the estimates from EE (which are about a factor of 2 more precise than T E) for both the T E and EE spectra (we call this the “map-based” approach); or applying independent estimates from T E and EE (the “spectrum-based” approach).” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
mechanismTHE NULL EXPLANATION IS DECLARED THE BASELINE ASSUMPTION, IN THOSE WORDS: alternatively, it could just be a statistical fluctuation, **WHICH IS OUR BASELINE ASSUMPTION**. Which of the competing accounts gets the default is a choice, and it is named as one rather than argued for — “Alternatively, it could just be a statistical fluctuation, which is our baseline assumption.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]

mechanismA COINED ACRONYM IS MINTED FOR OBJECTS THE ANALYSIS ITSELF PRODUCES, AND THE RULE THAT DECIDES HOW MANY OF THEM COUNT IS LEFT UNNAMED: the rotation diagonalizing the Fisher inverse **DEFINES** the eigenmodes; truncation of the hierarchy, to determine the number used for parameter estimation, is performed **ACCORDING TO SOME SUITABLY CHOSEN SELECTION CRITERION** - **AND WE REFER TO THESE MODES AS “EXEMS”**. One sentence defines the objects, the next leaves the rule for counting them to be chosen suitably, and the third gives them a name — “The rotation diagonalizing the Fisher inverse defines the xe eigenmodes. mechanismA DISAGREEMENT IS LEFT OPEN WITH ITS THREE POSSIBLE MEANINGS ENUMERATED AND NONE CHOSEN: further work is needed to assess whether the discrepancy **IS A STATISTICAL FLUCTUATION, IS CAUSED BY SMALL SYSTEMATIC ERRORS, OR IS A SIGNATURE OF NEW PHYSICS**. Noise, error, and discovery are offered as the alternatives, in that order, and the paper declines to rank them — “Further work is needed to assess whether the discrepancy between Planck and the Ly α BAO results is a statistical fluctuation, is caused by small systematic errors, or is a signature of new physics.”
mechanismAN ASSUMPTION IS JUSTIFIED BY WHAT THE SIMPLEST MODELS PREDICT, WITH THE PRECISION CAVEAT IN A PARENTHESIS: the base model assumes that the spatial hypersurfaces are flat, **SUCH AS WOULD BE PREDICTED (TO WITHIN MEASURABLE PRECISION) BY THE SIMPLEST INFLATIONARY MODELS**. The warrant for the assumption is the simplicity of a theory, and the hedge is about what an instrument could see — “The base-ΛCDM model assumes that the spatial hypersurfaces are flat, such as would be predicted (to within measurable precision) by the simplest inflationary models.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
mechanismA FEATURE THAT PULLS PARAMETERS AWAY FROM THE MODEL IS REPORTED **AND REBUTTED IN THE SAME SENTENCE**, BY NAMING THE PROBES THAT DO NOT SEE IT: a feature which pulls some parameters that affect the lensing amplitude away from the model; **HOWEVER, THIS IS NOT SUPPORTED BY THE LENSING RECONSTRUCTION OR** (in models that also change the background geometry) **BAO DATA**.
