content observations: finding · quantity · definition · variable · statistic · key-term — each grounded in a verbatim quotation from the paper
findingThe project reversed its approach mid-way: reusing existing thesauri beat building its own. — “it became clear as the project progressed that a more promising approach would be to focus on reusing existing thesauri to make the data FAIR”
findingThat reversal cost coverage: nine modelling categories were cut to four. — “the nine categories initially identified for modeling the thesaurus (person, film work, occupation, place, technique, institution, film genre, theme, and publication) were reduced to four categories”
findingDespite metadata being central to visibility and reuse, the resource's own metadata had been very limited. — “WFPP's application of metadata has been very limited”
quantity9 → 4 — modelling categories before and after the shift to reusing existing thesauri — “were reduced to four categories relevant to the structured collection of filmographies”
quantitynine categories reduced to four — the concrete cost of switching from a bespoke thesaurus to reusable vocabularies
