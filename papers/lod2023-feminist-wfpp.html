limitationThe paper reports only preliminary results; it is a poster describing a methodological approach rather than a completed, evaluated system. — “This poster outlines the methodological approach of the project and presents preliminary results.”
limitationThe scope was narrowed mid-project from nine modelling categories to four, restricting coverage to filmographic data. — “First, the nine categories initially identified for modeling the thesaurus (person, film work, occupation, place, technique, institution, film genre, theme, and publication) were reduced to four categories relevant to the structured collection of filmographies: person, film work, institution, and place.”
limitationThe authors state as an open problem that LOD modelling forces categorisation, standardisation and a reduction of complexity that may not accommodate the plurality of feminist film historiography. — “How can the plurality of feminist film historiography be accounted for when modeling research data as LOD requires categorization and standardization, and thus a reduction of complexity?”
limitationModelling as LOD requires standardisation, which reduces exactly the complexity the field is trying to preserve — “How can the plurality of feminist film historiography be accounted for when modeling research data as LOD requires categorization and standardization, and thus a reduction of complexity?”
limitationClassification is taken to be never neutral, so the vocabularies being reused carry values that are not the project's own — “How can we incorporate feminist values into the design of categories if we assume that classification practices are never neutral but always entangled with political attitudes and values (Bowker / Star 1999; Drabinski 2013; D'Ignazio / Klein 2020)?”
limitationThe gaps left by lost sources and undocumented feminized labour have no obvious representation in a data model — “In the context of feminist film historiography, we also have to ask how the numerous blind spots and knowledge gaps that exist due to lost sources and the limited documentation of "feminized labor" (Hill 2016: 5) can be represented in data-based approaches (Dang 2020).”
limitationThe poster reports an approach and preliminary results rather than outcomes, so nothing here is yet evaluated — “This poster outlines the methodological approach of the project and presents preliminary results.”
