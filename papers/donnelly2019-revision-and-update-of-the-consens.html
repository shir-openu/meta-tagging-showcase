mechanism**THE LABELS DID NOT CHANGE AND THE CLASSES DID**: `There is NO CHANGE IN THE CLASSIFICATIONS of `proven,` `probable,` and `possible` IFD, ALTHOUGH THE DEFINITION OF `PROBABLE` HAS BEEN EXPANDED AND THE SCOPE OF THE CATEGORY `POSSIBLE` HAS BEEN DIMINISHED.` **A trial reporting `probable IFD` in 2010 and one reporting it in 2021 are not counting the same patients, and neither label says so.** The corpus holds definitions that moved and were renamed; this one moved and kept its name, which is the harder case to detect downstream— “There is no change in the classifications of “proven,” “probable,” and “possible” IFD, although the definition of “probable” has been expanded and the scope of the category “possible” has been diminished.”
mechanism**AND THE DEFINITION IS EXPLICITLY NOT FOR TREATING ANYONE**: `Importantly, these definitions are SPECIFICALLY INTENDED FOR THESE PURPOSES ONLY AND NOT TO DIRECT OR GUIDE PATIENT CARE.` Restated at the end: `we have emphasized the need to DIFFERENTIATE BETWEEN DEFINITIONS OF IFD REQUIRED FOR CLINICAL RESEARCH FROM THOSE THAT INFLUENCE CLINICAL PRACTICE. In clinical practice, MANY WOULD ADMINISTER AN ANTIFUNGAL AGENT ... WITHOUT THERE BEING SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE TO SATISFY THE CONSENSUS DEFINITIONS OF IFD.` **A definition bounded by PURPOSE, not by population or by evidence base** - and the authors say plainly that practice does not follow it and should not— “Importantly, these definitions are specifically intended for these purposes only and not to direct or guide patient care.”
mechanism**AND FOR ELEVEN YEARS THE THRESHOLD BELONGED TO THE ASSAY MANUFACTURER**: `there was NO AGREEMENT ABOUT APPROPRIATE THRESHOLDS, so THE MANUFACTURERS' ANALYTICAL THRESHOLDS WERE ADOPTED.` This revision takes it back - `REVISED THRESHOLDS FOR GM NOW REPLACE THOSE OF THE MANUFACTURER` - and the replacement still names a brand: `A single threshold (>80 pg/mL) using the FUNGITELL test ... is recommended; there is INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE TO INCLUDE ASSAYS PRODUCED BY OTHER MANUFACTURERS.` **The boundary of a disease category was, and partly still is, a commercial parameter**— “In the previous definitions [ 1 ], indirect tests for diagnosing IFD were only included if there was sufficient evidence that they had been standardized and validated. Moreover, commercial tests were included only if criteria for interpretation had been provided. Hence, while tests for GM and BDG were incorporated, tests for detecting fungal…
mechanismAND THE THRESHOLD WAS DELIBERATELY INDEXED TO THE SPECIMEN RATHER THAN THE PATIENT: `We adopted DIFFERENT THRESHOLDS FOR DIFFERENT SPECIMENS RATHER THAN FOR DIFFERENT HOST GROUPS.` Two ways to make one test mean the same thing across a population, and the choice is stated. **Compare prigerson2009, where items that behaved differently across host groups were DELETED rather than re-thresholded**— “We evaluated Aspergillus galactomannan for both adults and children and specific patient groups and its utility and validity for different clinical specimens. We adopted different thresholds for different specimens rather than for different host groups [ 33–35 ] ( Table 2 ).”
mechanism**AND A WORKING GROUP FAILED AND SAID SO**: `group 10 (IFD definitions in ICU patients) WAS UNABLE TO GENERATE RECOMMENDATIONS THAT PRESERVED A LEVEL OF CERTAINTY CONSISTENT WITH THE EXISTING DEFINITIONS except for proven IFD, and therefore undertook a separate initiative.` One of ten groups reports that its population cannot be brought inside the definition at the certainty the definition requires. **The honest outcome of an extension attempt, printed rather than papered over**— “However, group 10 (IFD definitions in ICU patients) was unable to generate recommendations that preserved a level of certainty consistent with the existing definitions except for proven IFD ( Table 1 ) and therefore undertook a separate initiative [ 8 ].”
mechanism**AND A TERM WAS STRUCK OUT FOR IGNORANCE**: `'COLONIZATION' with Cryptococcus spp. IS NO LONGER INCLUDED IN THE DEFINITIONS AS IT IS POORLY UNDERSTOOD and its natural history is unknown.` simonneau2018 KEEPS a whole class - group 5 - for precisely this reason, `the LACK OF ROBUST DATA allowing a definite recognition of predominant pathophysiological mechanisms`. **Same criterion, opposite operation: one field files the not-understood into a class of its own, the other deletes the term**— ““Colonization” with Cryptococcus spp. is no longer included in the definitions as it is poorly understood and its natural history is unknown.”
mechanismAND ANOTHER TERM WAS ABANDONED FOR A PARTITION: `The term `DISSEMINATED CRYPTOCOCCOSIS` as distinct from CNS cryptococcosis HAS BEEN ABANDONED IN FAVOR OF THE TERMS `PULMONARY,` `CNS` AND `OTHER EXTRAPULMONARY SITES.`` A term whose meaning was `everything beyond one site` replaced by naming the sites. **A negative definition traded for an enumeration**— “The term “disseminated cryptococcosis” as distinct from CNS cryptococcosis has been abandoned in favor of the terms “pulmonary,” “CNS” and “other extrapulmonary sites.””
mechanismAND A CLASS WAS EXTENDED TO PEOPLE WITH NO SYMPTOMS: `we also recognize cryptococcal infection among individuals in high-risk host groups WHO HAVE FEW, IF ANY, SYMPTOMS AND ONLY A POSITIVE SERUM CRYPTOCOCCAL ANTIGEN TEST (asymptomatic cryptococcal antigenemia). THIS CONDITION MAY BE MORE COMMON THAN SYMPTOMATIC DISEASE ... and so are NOW INCLUDED IN THESE DEFINITIONS.` The justification is prognostic - they `MAY DEVELOP CLINICAL CRYPTOCOCCAL DISEASE UNLESS TREATED` - which is simonneau2018's argument for the 21-24 mmHg band, in a different discipline the same year— “However, we also recognize cryptococcal infection among individuals in high-risk host groups who have few, if any, symptoms and only a positive serum cryptococcal antigen test (asymptomatic cryptococcal antigenemia). mechanismAND THE DIAGNOSTIC TESTS CARRY THEIR OWN SCOPE LIMITS INTO THE DEFINITION: BDG is refused as evidence for a specific disease - `the group DID NOT SUPPORT the use of serum BDG TO RULE IN patients for clinical trials or for defining IA or IC, AS BDG DETECTION IS NOT SPECIFIC FOR ANY ONE IFD`; tissue histology `SIGNIFIES PROVEN FUNGAL DISEASE BUT NOT THE IDENTITY OF THE FUNGUS INVOLVED`; and T2Candida's predictive value `INCREASES FROM 62% ... TO 92%` with the population. **Each test answers a different question, and the definition has to say which**— “The group did not support the use of serum BDG to rule in patients for clinical trials or for defining IA or IC, as BDG detection is not specific for any one IFD.”
mechanismAND A STANDARD WAS SET AS CRITERIA RATHER THAN AS A METHOD: `There are relatively few commercial PCR assays, and MOST INVESTIGATORS HAVE DEVELOPED METHODS IN-HOUSE. As such, the FPCRI was established TO DEVELOP CRITERIA FOR Aspergillus PCR RATHER THAN A STANDARDIZED METHOD PER SE.` **Standardising the output instead of the procedure** - the only move available when the procedures are already heterogeneous, and the reason PCR could be admitted in 2019 after exclusion in 2008— “There are relatively few commercial PCR assays, and most investigators have developed methods in-house. As such, the FPCRI was established to develop criteria for Aspergillus PCR rather than a standardized method per se.”

sense`proven` IFD - histology, culture or a sterile-site specimen. `Can apply to ANY patient, regardless of whether the patient is immunocompromised` - the only one of the three categories that is not restricted by host
sense`probable` IFD - a host factor AND a clinical feature AND mycological evidence, `proposed for immunocompromised patients only, except for endemic mycoses`. **Expanded in this revision without changing the word**
sense`possible` IFD - a host factor and a clinical feature with the mycology missing. The category that `attracted controversy` where the other two did not - **defined by an absent measurement**
sense`host factor` - `a characteristic of individuals CLEARLY PREDISPOSED TO, AND NOT SIMPLY AT RISK OF, an IFD`. Impaired gut wall integrity raises the risk of candidiasis and is refused as a host factor for not being specific enough
sense`exposure` (endemic mycoses) - `a history of residence in an endemic area, NO MATTER HOW REMOTE, travel to an endemic area, or contact with fomites such as soil or vegetation`. A criterion with no time limit at all
