mechanismSynchronous inhibition from fast-spiking cells opens a narrow window in which excitation is effective, which is what synchronises the ensemble— “Current theory predicts that gamma oscillations are generated by synchronous activity of fast-spiking inhibitory interneurons, with the resulting rhythmic inhibition producing neural ensemble synchrony by generating a narrow window for effective excitation.”
