transferfrom cat auditory neurophysiology: reversible cooling deactivation, and the dorsal/ventral dissociation it produced, imported as both method and expected result pattern— “For example, Lomber & Malhotra (2008) dissociated the function of auditory cortical regions when they showed that inactivation of the anterior auditory field, AAF, in cats impaired auditory pattern discrimination, while lesions of area DP, a hypothesized dorsal stream region, impaired sound localization ( Lomber and Malhotra 2008 ).”
mechanismCooling VLPFC silences a small anterolateral zone where auditory and multisensory cells are concentrated while visual cells, being spread across dorsal and ventral prefrontal cortex, keep working — producing an auditory/audiovisual deficit with visual memory spared.— “Consequently, inactivation of VLPFC affected a large proportion of auditory and multisensory cells while sparing visual neurons.”
mechanismHolding an acoustic stimulus already consumes VLPFC storage, so adding a face to remember exhausts the remaining capacity there and the extra item fails when VLPFC is inactivated.— “It is also possible that during audiovisual processing, when VLPFC is maintaining an acoustic stimulus, this burdens working memory so that adding an additional item to remember (a face) absolutely requires the remaining temporary storage in VLPFC.”
mechanismMatch enhancement appears earlier in prefrontal cortex than in temporal pole or primary auditory cortex, implying prefrontal top-down feedback drives the mnemonic modulation of the auditory circuit.— “The match enhancement effects observed in the PFC had an earlier latency during the matching sound presentation compared to the match enhancement seen in the dorsal temporal pole ( Ng et al., 2014 ) or in the primary auditory cortex ( Bigelow et al., 2014 ) suggesting top-down feedback is modulating the circuit during memory demand.”
