phenomenongradual accumulation of evidence in perceptual decision-making— “Gradual accumulation of evidence favoring one or another choice is considered a core component of many different types of decisions, and has been the subject of many neurophysiological studies in non-human primates.”
claim-typecritique— “Consequently, we consider the PPC to have the lowest likelihood of being an important center of the causally relevant circuit for gradual evidence accumulation.”
method — evidence— “Here we will first briefly describe studies with non-human primates into the neural basis of the evidence accumulator.”
object of study — evidence— “To study the macro- and micro-circuitry causally responsible for gradual accumulation, we must first know which brain regions are part of that circuitry.”
mechanismWhen evidence is weak the accumulator drifts only slowly away from the decision boundary, so it takes longer to establish which side it is on, which is why difficult decisions are slow.— “In this model, when the evidence is strong, the accumulator’s value quickly diverges away from the decision boundary, and it rapidly become easy to say on which side of the decision boundary it is.”
mechanismFOF acts downstream of accumulation, categorizing the accumulator's value into a choice, which is why its activity is required near the go cue rather than throughout the stimulus.— “the FOF’s main role in the task lies in categorizing the value of the accumulator, an operation that occurs after the graded evidence accumulation itself”
mechanismDiscrepant PPC inactivation results across rodent visual tasks may arise because the inactivation spreads beyond PPC into neighbouring small secondary visual areas that supply sensory input.— “the observed effects from inactivations targeting rodent PPC could perhaps be due to inactivation spillover into one of the many small V2 areas”
mechanismEncoding was inferred from ramping firing rates that rise faster on easy trials than hard ones, exactly as the accumulator model predicts— “Critically, when averaged across easy trials, firing rates ramped slowly; but when averaged across difficult trials, firing rates ramped much more sharply--precisely as expected of the evidence accumulator ([ 25 – 27 ]; Fig. 1b ).”
mechanismTuning curves separate two regions that look alike in averages: one reads out the graded value, the other a categorical side of the boundary— “Thus what is best read out from FOF firing is not the graded value of the accumulator, but rather which categorical decision report the subject should make if the trial ended at that point in time.”

mechanismMicrostimulation cannot localise its own effect, because it acts largely on axons passing through rather than on the cells at the site
mechanismThe proposed test for circuit membership is three conditions at once - inactivation matters, it matters during the accumulation window specifically, and the graded value is encoded there
