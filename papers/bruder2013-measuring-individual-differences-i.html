conceptconspiracy mentality — the general propensity to subscribe to theories blaming a conspiracy of ill-intending individuals or groups for important societal phenomena
conceptconspiracist ideation — Swami et al's name for the same thing - the paper marks the synonymy with `sometimes called`
conceptConspiracy Mentality Questionnaire — five items, no named conspiracies, one dimension, three languages
conceptGeneric Conspiracist Beliefs Scale — Brotherton et al's competing instrument - 15 items from an exploratory factor analysis of 75, resolving into FIVE components
conceptConspiracy Mentality Scale — Imhoff and Bruder's 12-item instrument, which names neither conspiracies nor the groups behind them
conceptBelief in Conspiracy Theories Inventory — Swami et al's 15 items about SPECIFIC theories - the approach this paper argues is bound to a time and place
conceptspecific conspiracy theories — the particular beliefs a generic scale is meant to predict without naming
conceptmeasurement equivalence — the property tested by multi-group confirmatory factor analysis, and the one the paper says the competing instruments had not established
conceptdiscriminant validity — the requirement that the new construct correlate with its neighbours BUT NOT SO HIGHLY that it stops being a separate thing - a bound specified in advance
conceptanomia — one of the neighbouring constructs conspiracy mentality is tested against, alongside low socio-political power
conceptbacktranslated — the translation check: translate, have a different bilingual render it back, then resolve every disagreement by discussion
conceptschizotypy — a personality measure used as a convergent comparison, alongside paranoid ideation and the Big Five
