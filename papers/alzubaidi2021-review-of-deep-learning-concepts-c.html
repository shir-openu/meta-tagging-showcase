mechanismvanishing gradient through repeated multiplication back-propagation multiplies each layer's derivative down the network, so with a squashing activation like the sigmoid, N small derivatives multiplied together make the gradient decline exponentially - and the first layers, which recognise the essential elements of the input, are the ones that stop updating— “However, this weight updating may not occur in some cases due to a vanishingly small gradient, which in the worst case means that no extra training is possible and the neural network will stop completely.”
