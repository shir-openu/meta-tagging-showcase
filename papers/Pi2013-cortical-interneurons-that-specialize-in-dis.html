These factors include but are not restricted to (i) celltype-specific availability of the epitope 36 , (ii) varying expression levels and subcellular patterns of the antigens 35 , 37 , (iii) details of the experimental procedure”
limitationthe exclusion thresholds were relaxed for VIP cells specifically, using light-evoked waveform correlation instead — “Some of the VIP neurons did not reach the threshold of isolation distance and L-ratio. For these neurons, we exploited the waveform information carried by light-evoked action potentials, which aided the isolation of these units. We calculated waveform correlation between spontaneous action potentials of VIP neurons and average light-evoked waveform. We restricted the clusters to the upper 5 percentile of the bootstrapped distribution of correlation coefficients.”
limitationanalysis windows differed between the two recorded areas, chosen from the observed response latencies — “For the mPFC, minimal and maximal firing was determined as the minimum and maximum of the SDF within 100 ms from the light pulses. For ACx longer windows were used according to the observed differences in response latencies (200 ms after light flashes for response and 400 ms before light pulses for baseline).”
limitationdatasets were pooled across layers and areas on the strength of null results rather than demonstrated equivalence — “There was no significant differences in IPSC amplitude and fraction of responded neurons in different cortical layers and the results were combined. Som and Pv showed similar short-term plasticity in ACx and mPFC. Hence, the two data sets were combined.”
limitationthe difference in VIP yield between the two areas is attributed to delivery method rather than biology — “The difference in the relative proportion of recorded VIP neurons in ACx and mPFC is likely due to technical reasons. First, ChR2 was delivered virally into mPFC, whereas we used a reporter line (Ai32) for ACx. Therefore, ChR2 expression in mPFC was spatially limited and the level of expression could vary. Second, superficial layers in mPFC were more difficult to target because they are located underneath midline blood vessels.”
limitationthe overlap between this circuit and the layer 1 disinhibitory circuit is left undetermined — “However, the extent to which these two disinhibitory circuits overlap remains to be determined, as only a fraction of layer 1 interneurons express VIP.”

findingVIP-expressing interneurons carry out disinhibitory control across several neocortical areas and are switched on by reinforcement signals. — “Here we show that a class of interneurons that express vasoactive intestinal polypeptide (VIP) mediates disinhibitory control in multiple areas of neocortex and is recruited by reinforcement signals.”
findingThe circuit module is VIP activation transiently suppressing somatostatin interneurons and a subset of parvalbumin interneurons, which respectively control principal-cell input and output. — “We identified a basic disinhibitory circuit module in which activation of VIP interneurons transiently suppresses primarily somatostatin- and a fraction of parvalbumin-expressing inhibitory interneurons that specialize in the control of the input and output of principal cells, respectively”
findingPyramidal neurons are only a minor direct target of VIP cells; the effect on them is indirect. — “In contrast to these inhibitory neurons only a small fraction of pyramidal neurons responded to VIP activation, suggesting that pyramidal neurons are a minor monosynaptic target of the VIP population”
findingVIP-evoked IPSCs in parvalbumin cells decay about three times faster than in somatostatin cells and depress more strongly with repeated stimulation. — “Inhibitory currents (IPSCs) recorded in Pv neurons decayed faster than those recorded from Som neurons (decay time constant; Som: 18±2ms, Pv: 6±1ms), and also displayed stronger short-term synaptic depression”
findingc-Fos labelling shows the delayed-activated cells are pyramidal (CaMKIIα-positive) rather than VIP neurons. — “Eight percent of c-Fos-immunopositive neurons expressed VIP, while the others expressed the pyramidal marker CaMKIIα”
findingThe disinhibited cells in auditory cortex are not random: they are preferentially the tone-responsive, frequency-tuned neurons. — “These results reveal that in auditory cortex, the VIP circuit disinhibits a functionally specific subset of neurons that tend to be tone-responsive and frequency-tuned.”
findingVIP stimulation changes tuning curves in two different ways: divisively scaling down the inhibited cells and additively raising the baseline of the delayed-activated cells. — “Thus, the effect of VIP stimulation on auditory tuning curves is explained by the divisive gain modulation of inhibited cells and the additive firing rate change of the delayed activated neurons.”
findingEvery recorded VIP neuron was phasically activated by punishment at short latency, and the same response followed both air puff and foot shock, indicating a general aversive-feedback signal. — “In all VIP neurons punishment (air puff, n=6 or foot shock, n=4) generated strong phasic activation at short latencies (peak at 50±12ms, p<0.01).”
findingVIP neurons respond to reinforcement uniformly as a class, unlike the heterogeneous responses of the surrounding cortical population. — “These results reveal that VIP interneurons respond in unison to reinforcement feedback, distinct from the more diverse responses observed in the rest of the cortical population.”
findingThe study maps a specific circuit operation, disinhibition, onto a genetically defined cell type. — “Genetic targeting and optical activation enabled us to map a particular circuit function, disinhibitory control, onto a molecularly defined cell-type.”
findingactivating 1-2% of cortical neurons changed firing in about 20% of them — “This excitation-inhibition-excitation sequence, observed in two functionally different cortical regions, is the signature of a disinhibition process: activated VIP interneurons inhibit other interneurons, releasing some pyramidal neurons from inhibitory control.”
