senseA MODERATOR THAT REVERSES THE SIGN RATHER THAN WEAKENING IT: `High-level construals reduce the resource focus and enhance a goal focus, which DIMINISHES AND EVEN REVERSES the depletion effect.` Not attenuation - inversion. **So `depletion reduces self-control` is true at one level of a moderator and false at the other**, and an edge carrying `effect_direction: reduces` would be wrong for half of this paper's own design. Fourth record today where the axis vocabulary has no single sign to record— “High-level construals reduce the resource focus and enhance a goal focus, which diminishes and even reverses the depletion effect”
senseDEPLETION WORKS THROUGH A SELF-ASSESSMENT, NOT THROUGH A RESOURCE: `At low-level construals, individuals rely on RESOURCE ACCESSIBILITY CUES (e.g., feelings of tiredness) to determine self-control. Hence, they exert less self-control ONLY WHEN THEY ASSESS THEMSELVES AS DEPLETED.` The person reads their own tiredness as evidence about how much control they have left, and acts on the reading. **That makes the depletion effect a judgement about oneself rather than a fact about oneself** - and it predicts that anything which changes what you consult will change the effect, which is exactly what the construal manipulation does— “At low-level construals, individuals rely on resource accessibility cues (e.g., feelings of tiredness) to determine self-control. Hence, they exert less self-control only when they assess themselves as depleted”
senseAND THE CONTROL THAT TURNS THAT FROM AN INTERPRETATION INTO A RESULT: `DESPITE EXPERIENCING SIMILAR LEVELS OF TIREDNESS as those at lower construal levels, the self-control exhibited by participants at higher levels of construal was NOT ADVERSELY AFFECTED by their feelings of tiredness.` **The resource state is held constant and the effect still disappears.** Same tiredness, different use of it. That is the sentence that locates the phenomenon in what the person consults rather than in what they have, and without it the construal result would be compatible with high-level construal simply making people less tired— “Despite experiencing similar levels of tiredness as those at lower construal levels, the self-control exhibited by participants at higher levels of construal was not adversely affected by their feelings of tiredness”
senseTWO CONSTRUAL LEVELS, TWO DIFFERENT MEDIATORS, ONE DESIGN: `A focus on resource limitations, specifically feelings of tiredness, MEDIATED the effect ... for individuals at LOWER construal levels`, while `At HIGHER construal levels, individuals' perceptions of the BENEFITS TO GOALS attained by performing the second task mediated the treatment effects.` **The moderator does not turn one mechanism up and down - it swaps which mechanism is running.** A record that stored only `construal level moderates depletion` would lose the fact that the two arms are not the same process at different strengths— “A focus on resource limitations, specifically feelings of tiredness, mediated the effect of initial resource depletion on subsequent self-control for individuals at lower construal levels.
