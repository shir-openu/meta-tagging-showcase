phenomenonresponse priming by luminance flankers— “Response priming by luminance flankers depends neither on the actual physical luminances of the flankers nor on their subjective brightness, as assessed in a psychophysical matching task; instead, it seems to depend exclusively on the local luminance contrast of the flankers to their immediate background.”
claim-typemechanism— “Indeed, the experiment clearly shows that priming effects reverse in exactly those conditions in which the flankers’ local contrast to the background reverses, indicating that it is, indeed, local flanker-to-background contrast that drove the priming effect.”
method — evidence— “They were instructed to keep their eyes on the fixation point and to respond as quickly and correctly as possible, pressing the key on the side where the brighter target was presented.”
mechanismThe feedforward sweeps triggered in turn by flankers and targets still carry only local contrast information, so motor activation is driven by raw local contrast while conscious brightness is settled later by recurrent interactions.— “this view suggests that the feedforward sweeps successively triggered by the flankers and targets are still dominated by local contrast signals.”
mechanismConscious lightness and brightness are computed later, by recurrent processing that trails the initial feedforward wave.— “The conscious perception of lightness and brightness will be determined later in processing, probably as the result of recurrent interactions trailing behind the initial processing wave”
mechanismResponse priming accesses a stimulus representation that precedes lightness constancy, i.e. one that has not yet discounted illumination.— “We adopt the less radical view that response priming reflects a stimulus representation that is earlier and less elaborate than the one underlying conscious vision and, consequently, deficient in lightness constancy.”
mechanismPlacing the high-luminance flanker on the lighted segment turns it into a local decrement (and the low-luminance flanker on the shadowed segment into a local increment), which is what inverts the priming sign.— “In the attenuation condition, however, the high-luminance flanker was a local decrement because it appeared on an even more luminant background”
builds-on Moore and Brown (2001), visual search under a dark transparent filter— “Our conclusions are in agreement with a study by Moore and Brown (2001), in which visual search for highluminance and low-luminance targets was performed under conditions in which parts of the display seemed to be watched through a dark transparent filter.”

sense**A QUALITATIVE DISSOCIATION, AND IT IS A REVERSAL RATHER THAN AN ATTENUATION:** `These findings indicate a QUALITATIVE DISSOCIATION between the subjective brightness of the stimuli and their propensity for priming a motor response: EVEN THOUGH ONE STIMULUS CAN APPEAR BRIGHTER THAN ANOTHER, IT CAN TRIGGER RESPONSES AS IF IT WERE DARKER.` Not a smaller effect in one condition - **the sign flips**, while the observer's own brightness match goes the other way. A quantitative dissociation can be argued about with a power analysis; a reversal cannot— “These findings indicate a qualitative dissociation between the subjective brightness of the stimuli and their propensity for priming a motor response: Even though one stimulus can appear brighter than another, it can trigger responses as if it were darker”
sense**AND THE CONTROL THAT MAKES THIS DIFFERENT IN KIND FROM EVERY OTHER DISSOCIATION IN THIS CORPUS:** `Note that this dissociation MAKES NO USE OF VISUAL MASKING OR BRIEF STIMULUS PRESENTATIONS OF ANY KIND: It is obtained even though the presented stimuli are ALL IN PLAIN VIEW, and it relies solely on characteristic differences in processing dynamics between the priming and matching tasks.` **dehaene1998 flashes a numeral for 43 ms between masks; marcel1983, holcomb2005 and Finkbeiner2011 all argue about whether the prime was seen. This paper lets the observer look straight at it.** So the gap is not between seen and unseen but between two live representations of one visible stimulus - which is a different claim, needing none of the machinery the awareness literature spends its credibility on— “Note that this dissociation makes no use of visual masking or brief stimulus presentations of any kind: It is obtained even though the presented stimuli are all in plain view, and it relies solely on characteristic differences in processing dynamics”
sense**THE CLAIM IS A TRIPLE NEGATIVE THAT NAMES ITS WINNER:** `Response priming by luminance flankers depends NEITHER on the actual physical luminances of the flankers NOR on their subjective brightness, as assessed in a psychophysical matching task; instead, it seems to depend EXCLUSIVELY on the LOCAL LUMINANCE CONTRAST of the flankers to their immediate background.` **The two excluded candidates are the physical variable and the perceptual variable** - between them, everything anyone would have guessed - and each was excluded by a measurement rather than by an argument. The third is named, and it is a property of the stimulus's relation to its own neighbourhood— “Response priming by luminance flankers depends neither on the actual physical luminances of the flankers nor on their subjective brightness, as assessed in a psychophysical matching task; instead, it seems to depend exclusively on the local luminance contrast of the flankers to their immediate background”
sense**AND THE INFERENCE IS CAREFULLY PARTIAL:** `If priming effects are in systematic disagreement with the flankers' perceived brightness values, this suggests that AT LEAST SOME ASPECTS of lightness constancy are ABSENT FROM THE RESPONSE ACTIVATION PROCESS.` **`at least some aspects` and `suggests` are both in the quote**, and they are the difference between this and a claim that the motor pathway is pre-constancy. The illusion used here IS a consequence of lightness constancy - the authors say so - so its failure to reach the response is evidence about one pathway, not a map of the visual system— “If priming effects are in systematic disagreement with the flankers’ perceived brightness values, this suggests that at least some aspects of lightness constancy are absent from the response activation process”
