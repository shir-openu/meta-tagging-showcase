mechanismearly endosomes mature into multivesicular bodies whose membrane invaginates to form intraluminal vesicles; those ILVs become exosomes when the MVB fuses with the plasma membrane rather than with a lysosome— “Exosomes are nanosized membrane vesicles released by fusion of an organelle of the endocytic pathway, the multivesicular body, with the plasma membrane.”
mechanismTHE FATE DECISION IS THE MECHANISM AND IT IS THE LEAST KNOWN PART: an MVB can go to the lysosome for degradation or to the plasma membrane for release, and `little is known about the molecular mechanisms and the cellular statues that regulate this balance`— “Multivesicular bodies can either be directed to lysosomes where their content is degraded or transported to the plasma membrane for exosome release. Little is known about the molecular mechanisms and the cellular statues that regulate this balance.”
mechanismAMPHISOMES AND LYSOSOMES PRODUCE THE SAME APPARENT OBJECT BY OTHER ROUTES: ILVs inside a fusing amphisome `will appear extracellularly as exosomes`, and a lysosome secreting before full degradation releases remaining ILVs `as exosomes`— “Furthermore, when amphisomes fuse with the plasma membrane, the ILVs included in the amphisome will appear extracellularly as exosomes. Also if a lysosome secretes its content before it is fully degraded, remaining ILVs can be released as exosomes.”
critiques the practice of calling the 100,000x g pellet `exosomes`, on the ground that it also holds small microvesicles and protein aggregates and its changes need not be about exosome release— “It is also important to mention that the term “exosomes” is often used to refer to the pellet obtained after 100,000× g ultracentrifugation. Although this pellet is probably enriched in exosomes, it might also contain small microvesicles as well as protein aggregates.”
critiques studies assigning a molecule to a specific step of exosome biogenesis, on the ground that the steps are not easy to distinguish experimentally— “Several molecules have been implicated in these processes, but due to methodological challenges, it is not easy to distinguish them experimentally, and in many studies it is not clear at which step the investigated molecule/factor operates”
supports Kowal et al.'s finding that the small-EV pellet contains distinct populations, which motivates the retreat from `exosome` to `sEV`— “In fact, it was recently suggested that this pellet should be referred to as small EVs (sEVs), since it was shown to contain different vesicle populations”

A DEFINITION BY ROUTE - the property is a history, not a feature of the object in hand— “Exosomes are nanosized membrane vesicles released by fusion of an organelle of the endocytic pathway, the multivesicular body, with the plasma membrane.”
senseexosome, AS USED IN THE LITERATURE THIS REVIEW SURVEYS: the pellet from a 100,000x g spin. senseTHE COINING, 1987, WITH ITS REASON: Rose Johnstone chose `exosome` because `the process seemed to be akin to reverse endocytosis`.
