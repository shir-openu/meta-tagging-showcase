content observations: finding · quantity · definition · variable · statistic · key-term — each grounded in a verbatim quotation from the paper
findingDistant reading's weakness is its dependence on one or two authoritative histories per national literature, which flattens that literature. — “One of the weaknesses of this approach lies in its exclusive reliance on canonical and authoritative historiographies, one or two for each national literature, something which is bound to over-simplify the complexities of national literatures.”
findingThe proposed alternative is to aggregate the readings of many readers, including non-professionals. — “we demonstrate how computer-based analysis of different readings carried out by many readers – not necessar- ily professionals – produces a relatively minute picture”
findingThe corpus was chosen for being large enough to need algorithms and small enough to still be thought about. — “it is a fairly convenient corpus: large enough for algorithmic reading, but not too large for human reflection”
findingThe authors commit in advance to reporting the limits of the approach, not only its benefits. — “we will reflect theoretically on the conceptual benefits, as well as the limits, of public distance reading”
