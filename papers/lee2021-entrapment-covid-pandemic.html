4. Discussion COVID-19term·key is on the rise worldwide and is an unprecedented problem. This study used the concept analysisterm·key developed by Walker and Avant (walker) [ 22 ] and the ACE star modelterm·mth [ 24 ] to analyze the concept of the feelings of entrapmentterm·dom during the COVID-19term·key pandemic. Out of control, feelings of no escape, feelings of trapped, feelings of robbed, and feelings of hopelessnessterm·dom were identified as the attributes of this analysis.

First, we discuss “out of control”. During the COVID-19term·key pandemic, people experienced increased mental distress and were also expected to exercise high levels of self-controlterm·dom concerning personal and social health behaviors [ 49 ]. Second, the feelings of no escape occur when we perceive no visible solution to escape the chronic stressful situation wherein the feeling of entrapmentterm·dom has progressed [ 70 ].
