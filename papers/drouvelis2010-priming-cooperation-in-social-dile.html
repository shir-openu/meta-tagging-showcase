findingSolving a word-search puzzle seeded with cooperation words raised voluntary contributions to a one-shot public good once subjects' gender was controlled for. — “Our findings suggest that priming cooperation increases contribution levels, controlling for subjects’ gender.”
findingThe raw treatment difference in mean contributions was not statistically significant, so the priming effect only emerges once composition is controlled for. — “levels are not significantly different, irrespective of the presence of priming (twosided test; p-value = 0.249).”
findingThe effect was concentrated in women: positively primed females contributed about 3.25 tokens more than neutrally primed females, whereas priming did not move male contributions. — “compared to males, i.e. positively primed females contribute roughly 3.25 tokens”
