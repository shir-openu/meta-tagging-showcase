mechanisman information bottleneck forces the network to keep only what is statistically salient - the compression is what makes the vector a representation rather than a copy— “This bottleneck—here the fixed-length continuous vector—induces the network to learn a compressed representation that captures the most statistically salient information in the data.”
mechanismthe variational penalty fills in the space: adding noise to the encoding forces the decoder to handle points between molecules, so the space has no dead areas— “The intuition is that adding noise to the encoded molecules forces the decoder to learn how to decode a wider variety of latent points and find more robust representations.”
