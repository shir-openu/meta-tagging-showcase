transferfrom evolutionary biology (theory of sexual reproduction): dropout as a neural-net regularizer inspired by the evolutionary role of sex - breaking co-adaptations, analogous to genetic recombination— “A motivation for dropout comes from a theory of the role of sex in evolution”
mechanismrandomly drop units during training so neurons cannot co-adapt; this approximates averaging an exponential ensemble of thinned networks, regularizing the model— “The key idea is to randomly drop units (along with their connections) from the neural network during training”
extends prior regularization methods for neural networks— “This significantly reduces overfitting and gives major improvements over other regularization methods.”
extends denoising autoencoders (Vincent et al., 2008, 2010), where noise is added to the input units— “Our work extends this idea by showing that dropout can be effectively applied in the hidden layers as well and that it can be interpreted as a form of model averaging.”
sensethe motivation for dropout - AN ANALOGY, FLAGGED AS ONE: a theory of the role of sex in evolution, in which combining half of each parent's genes beats copying a good genome directly; the paper presents this as a motivation and not as a derivation, and its only proof of an equivalent regularizer is for linear regression— “A motivation for dropout comes from a theory of the role of sex in evolution (Livnat et al., 2010). Sexual reproduction involves taking half the genes of one parent and half of the other, adding a very small amount of random mutation, and combining them to produce an offspring”
sensethe test-time network - A STAND-IN FOR AN ENSEMBLE THAT IS NEVER BUILT: a single unthinned network with scaled weights approximates an equally weighted geometric mean over an exponential number of thinned networks, so the rule everyone implements is a cheap approximation to a computation nobody performs— “We propose to do this by approximating an equally weighted geometric mean of the predictions of an exponential number of learned models that share parameters”
sensea thinned network - ONE SAMPLE FROM 2^n: applying dropout amounts to sampling a thinned network, and a net with n units is described as a collection of that many possible thinned networks all sharing weights, so 'a dropout network' names a distribution over architectures rather than one architecture— “Applying dropout to a neural network amounts to sampling a “thinned” network from it”
controlthe same architectures trained with and without dropout, differing only in whether a thinned network is sampled for each training case in a mini-batch — held — rules out an improvement owed to the architecture, the data or the training schedule rather than to dropout itself— “The same architectures trained with and without dropout have drastically different test errors as seen as by the two separate clusters of trajectories.”
edgedropout —operationalised-as→ thinned— “Applying dropout to a neural network amounts to sampling a “thinned” network from it”
