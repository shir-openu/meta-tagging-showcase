mechanismConfidence arises because evidence for each option accumulates stochastically during value comparison, so trial-to-trial noise makes the final gap between accumulators (and hence confidence, speed and accuracy) vary even at fixed initial value difference. — “This model predicts that subjective confidence reflects the stochastic accumulation of evidence during the value comparison process.”
mechanismInter-trial noise in the comparison process drives the joint pattern of confidence, reaction time and accuracy. — “The intuition is that, even within a particular level of initial DV, inter-trial noise in the value comparison process results in some trials having greater final DV’s (higher confidence) than others.”
mechanismConfidence computed in vmPFC becomes reportable by being read out by a hierarchically distinct second-order network in rRLPFC. — “One computationally plausible hypothesis is an hierarchical model where confidence in a comparison process is “read out” by an anatomically distinct second-order network”
mechanismNoise added at the read-out stage degrades the correspondence between reported confidence and actual accuracy, producing individual differences in metacognition. — “Intuitively, if reported confidence is a noisy facsimile of the confidence inherent in a decision process, the relationship between confidence and behavior will weaken, and metacognitive accuracy will decrease 26 .”

mechanism — “Finally, since the model predicts that confidence reflects the stochastic evolution of a value comparison process, it will only be weakly related to initial DV. mechanism — “An additional parameter (σ conf ) governs the noise in the read-out of Δe (i.e. decision confidence). If σ conf is zero the information about confidence (Δe) is uncorrupted, resulting in a pronounced shift in the choice accuracy between high confidence and low confidence trials (red arrow). As the level of metacognitive noise increases (more positive values of σ conf ) the shift between the two curves (low and high confidence) diminishes. Differences in σ conf account for the inter-subject variability in metacognitive reportability we observed behaviorally.”

one-sample t-test — “In a logistic regression model predicting subsequent reversal from both ∣DV∣ and initial confidence, initial confidence was a significant negative predictor of choice reversal (mean standardised regression coefficient −0.0083 ± 0.0034 SD; one-sample t-test t (19) = −10.9, P < 10 −9 ). These data support a hypothesis that low confidence may be associated with subsequent changes of mind.”
general linear model — “To test this hypothesis we constructed a general linear model (GLM) of our fMRI data in which each trial was modulated by two parametric regressors: ∣DV∣ and confidence orthogonalised with respect to ∣DV∣.”
