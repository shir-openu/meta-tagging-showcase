phenomenontonotopic organization of an inhibitory auditory brainstem nucleus
claim-typeexistence
transferfrom visual neuroscience: retinotopy as the template for what a sensory map is and why it matters — “Moreover, quantitative maps provide predictive tools to determine the optimal stimulus for a particular neuron based on its location in the tissue. Using this information investigators can 1) relate anatomical, biophysical, and pharmacological characteristics of a neuron to sensory processing based on stimulus selectivity ( Smith and Rubel, 1979 ; Kuba et al., 2005 ); and 2) compare the precision of processing across sensory areas or across species.”
mechanismSON response patterns are inherited from its nucleus angularis input, which is why sustained and onset types and diverse tuning shapes appear there — “Firing patterns of SON neurons to ipsilateral tones appear to be shaped primarily by the inputs from the NA, one of two main excitatory inputs to the SON.”
mechanismthe narrow hearing range of the chicken makes the frequency-to-place function linear rather than logarithmic — “This linear relationship, as opposed to a log relationship often observed in auditory centers, is probably a consequence of the limited frequency range of hearing in chickens”
mechanismsummation of individually subthreshold spike waveforms raises the apparent threshold of multiple-unit recordings — “This increase reflects multiple-unit recordings with neuronal waveforms that individually were not detected by using our threshold criterion, but summed to form detectable waveforms at higher sound intensities.”
mechanismthe broad, non-restricted SON to nucleus laminaris projection supplies the anatomical substrate by which inhibition laterally sharpens frequency tuning — “This projection pattern suggests an anatomical substrate for lateral sharpening of frequency tuning in NL neurons.”
mechanism — “Inhibitory components of many neuronal circuits comprise interneurons residing alongside principal neurons ( Huang et al., 2007 ). Thus, independent evaluation of the organizing features of these circuit components is difficult. However, in the auditory brainstem, inhibitory neurons often reside in nuclei that project to one or more targets, thus providing opportunities to study sensory mapping in inhibitory long-range projection nuclei”
mechanism — “To combine data from all 19 reconstructed SONs, locations of the recording sites were converted into normalized distances along three orthogonal axes in the SON (see Materials and Methods). These normalized coordinates allowed all recording sites to be mapped in a standardized SON.”
mechanism — “Because the presence and arrangement (linear or nonlinear, single or multiple gradients) of the tonotopic organization in SON were unknown, we performed principal component analysis (PCA) on the combined dataset to determine the appropriate dimension(s) to observe the possible tonotopic organization. The first principal component accounted for 72% of the variance in the data”
mechanism — “Inhibition from the SON expands the dynamic range and sharpens temporal coding of auditory neurons to enable sound localization”
