phenomenona hermeneutical circle in which visualizing the annotations sends the analyst back to re-assess the categories that produced them— “after visualizing the material in this way, it often turns out that the categories themselves need to be re-assessed. In other words, the categorization and visualization in themselves create a sort of hermeneutical circle in which both parts influence one another reciprocally.”

mechanisma tagset too large and too hierarchical to grasp in the annotation tool itself is given an interactive network-oriented representation in the separate visualisation tool— “Large, hierarchical tagsets are hard to grasp and visualize in CATMA, and ViS-À-ViS offers an interactive representation of the tagset in a network-oriented chart”
mechanismthe chart collapses and uncollapses level by level, and a level's size is drawn as the number of children it has, which is what makes the inner hierarchy of the tagset readable as shape— “Here a representation of the tagset is visible and the interactive chart enables one to collapse or uncollapse the various levels. The size of each level relates to the number of children it has, which once more offers a useful insight into the structure and inner hierarchy of the tagset.”

In other words, instead of providing an answer concerning the “correct” annotation, it raises a question about it and promotes once more literary speculation.”
