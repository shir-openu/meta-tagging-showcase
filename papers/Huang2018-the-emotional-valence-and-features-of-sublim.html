phenomenonsubliminal affective priming of facial expression perception— “The SAFFIMAP test assesses the effect of subliminally-presented affective priming images on the perception of a target-face expression (neutral or angry).”
claim-typeexistence— “Priming images containing a face compared to those not containing a face significantly impaired performance on neutral or angry targetface evaluation.”
method — evidence— “Subjects responded to the emotion portrayed in a pixelated target-face by indicating via key-press if the expression was angry or neutral.”
mechanismfaces in the prime are processed preferentially, and the perceived prime face then competes with and interferes with perception of the target face— “This suggest that processing of faces occurs preferentially to the processing of non-face containing images, and that the face perceived in the priming image interferes with the ability to perceive the target-face expression.”
mechanisma face in the prime sets up an expectation of an emotionally charged target, which speeds and biases the response toward 'angry'— “Taken together these results suggest that the face in the prime image prompts the viewer to expect an emotionally charged target-face, which quickens (see Supplement D) and biases the response towards the angry rather than neutral-target face.”
mechanisman egocentric vantage-point recruits action-preparation processes, which lower the priority given to affect processing and so degrade angry-face recognition— “From this result, we suggest that, because of the direct relevance of the egocentric vantage-point to the viewer, the egocentric prime image may engage a series of processes relevant to potential action preparation, which could weaken the priority of affective processing, and thus interfere with the ability to recognize the angry target-face…
extends Vaina, Rana, Cotos Cheng, Huang, & Podea (2014) preliminary study of affective subliminal priming in schizophrenic subjects— “Using the same experimental protocol as in our previous study (Vaina et al., 2014), here we investigate in young healthy subjects how the affective content of the priming image, the presence of a face or the vantage-point of the prime content (image centricity) impact the effect of affective subliminal priming on the identification of…
contradicts Andrews, Lipp, Malan, & Könjg (2011)— “This result is at odds with the findings of Andrews et al. (2011), who reported that there was no subliminal affective priming effect when priming with emotional facial expressions (Andrews, Lipp, Malan, & Könjg, 2011).”
builds-on Kemps, Erauw, and Vandierendock (1996)— “This result is in agreement with Kemps, Erauw, and Vandierendock (1996) finding that facial expressions influenced the liking rating of Korean ideographs at exposure duration of 30 ms and longer, while pictures without faces did not elicit an affective reaction until they were presented for at least 100 ms.”
senseA NAMED HYPOTHESIS GIVEN A BOUNDARY CONDITION RATHER THAN A VERDICT: `the AFFECTIVE PRIMACY HYPOTHESIS which predicts that affective information can be accessed automatically, preceding conscious cognition, HOLDS TRUE IN SUBLIMINAL PRIMING ONLY WHEN THE PRIMING IMAGE CONTAINS A FACE.` Not confirmed, not refuted - restricted. **And the condition is a property of the STIMULUS**, where Q-787's ambiguity condition is a property of the outcome situation and Holcomb2005's is a property of the observer. Three different places a priming effect can be conditional, and a corpus that records only `X primes Y` loses all three— “the affective primacy hypothesis which predicts that affective information can be accessed automatically, preceding conscious cognition, holds true in subliminal priming only when the priming image contains a face”
senseTHE PRIME MAKES THE TASK HARDER, WHICH IS THE CASE THE STANDARD DEFINITION EXCLUDES. `Subliminally priming with a face-containing image SIGNIFICANTLY IMPAIRS the subjects' ability to perceive the emotional expression of the target-face.` Minton2017 (Q-780) quotes McNamara defining priming as `an IMPROVEMENT in performance` and then strikes the word out, because `priming can also involve a change in performance that is neutral or results in POORER performance`. **This record is the counterexample that correction needed** - a real interference effect, in a different literature, from a paper that never mentions the definitional dispute
