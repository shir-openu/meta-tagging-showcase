mechanismTHE VOCABULARY IS A CROSSWALK, AND THE PAPER SHOWS ITS WORKING. In Gust’s study, they corresponded with the ‘immunisation advocates’ or ‘go along to get along’ groups who see the importance of childhood vaccination and are confident in its safety”
mechanismAND A SECOND CROSSWALK ONTO A THEORY OF BEHAVIOUR CHANGE: `The majority of parents are in the ACTION AND MAINTENANCE stage (cautious or unquestioning acceptors). limitationTHE CATEGORIES ARE NOT STABLE AND THE PAPER SAYS SO: `the spectrum described DOES SIMPLIFY what is often a complex process of decision making WHICH MAY INVOLVE PARENTS MOVING BETWEEN POSITIONS OVER TIME`. A discrete five-way classification of something the authors describe as continuous and time-varying - the same tension macdonald2015 states about hesitancy, arriving here from the practice end three years earlier
limitationTHE VOCABULARY RESTS ON THREE US STUDIES TOTALLING 647 PEOPLE, two of them n=30 and n=33: `While the parental positions were developed from THREE US STUDIES, we have applied our knowledge and experience from other countries to their modification`, and the proportions `WILL VARY OVER TIME, WITHIN REGIONS, AND BETWEEN PRACTICES`
