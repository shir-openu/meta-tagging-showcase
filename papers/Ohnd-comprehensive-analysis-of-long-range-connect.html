phenomenonlong-range connectivity of the mouse posterior parietal cortex— “Although many recent works have been performed with the mouse as a model system, a comprehensive understanding of long-range connectivity of the mouse PPC is scarce, preventing integrative interpretation of the rapidly accumulating functional data.”
claim-typeexistence— “In this study, we summarized the quantitative long-range connectivity from and to the PPC by analyzing the Allen Mouse Brain Connectivity Atlas data and confirmed key findings by both anterograde and retrograde neurotracers.”
method — evidence— “In this study, we conducted a detailed neuroanatomic and bioinformatic analysis of the Allen Mouse Brain Connectivity Atlas data to summarize afferent and efferent connections to/from the PPC.”
mechanismAbsence of a direct visual-to-motor cortical route forces visual decision signals through PPC, so inactivating PPC impairs visual but not whisker-based decisions— “There is essentially no direct connection between VIS and MO (Wang et al. 2012) and the main indirect connection from VIS to MO is through the PPC (Zingg et al. 2014).”
mechanismReciprocal PPC-thalamic loops sustain persistent choice-selective firing during a delay— “The current study showed that the PPC has a strong reciprocality with LP, PO, and LD, suggesting the possibility that the reciprocal connectivity with thalamic nuclei may be a general connectivity motif that supports the persistent activity.”
mechanismTracing and axon-volume quantification disagree because one reports where cell bodies sit and the other where synapses land— “For instance, when L5 neurons receive inputs on their distal dendrites, an experimental approach will show L5, where cell bodies are located, whereas bioinformatic analysis will show L1, where the synapses occur.”
builds-on Oh et al. 2014; Harris et al. 2019 (brain-wide quantitative projection analyses)— “The same method has been used in previous brain-wide quantitative analyses of projection patterns (Oh et al. 2014; Harris et al. 2019), and thus was used in this study for comparative analysis of inputs to/outputs from PPC.”
builds-on Zingg et al. 2017 (AAV1 transsynaptic anterograde tracing)— “Taking advantage of AAV1, which is a widely used anterograde tracer with transsynaptic potential (Zingg et al. 2017), in combination with reporter mouse line (Gt(ROSA)26Sortm9(CAG-tdTomato), Ai9), we selectively labeled the neuronal cell bodies that receive VISa inputs.”
extends the rat connectivity record in BAMS (Brain Architecture Management System)— “We also observed inputs from LGd, VPL, VPM and VAL in mice, which were not reported in rats (no reported data in BAMS).”
contradicts prior rat reports of a zona incerta (ZI) projection to VISrl— “It has been reported in rats that light projections from the ZI to the VISrl exist, however, we found no clear evidence of projections to the PPC (see Supplementary Figs 9 and 10k).”
sensethe PPC's borders - TWO DELINEATIONS THAT DISAGREE: there is a weak consensus on anatomical borders, and the paper reports that functional retinotopic mapping did not clearly define the boundaries or mismatched the architectonic definition, so the region has two methods of fixing it and they do not agree— “However, it has been reported that even with functional retinotopic mapping by intrinsic optical imaging, using voltage-sensitive dyes or Ca2+ responses, the boundaries of VISa and VISrl were not clearly defined or mismatched with the architectonic definition of the PPC”

findingThe mouse posterior parietal cortex sends its heaviest cortical output to secondary motor cortex, primary visual cortex and the barrel field of primary somatosensory cortex.— “The PPC predominantly projected to secondary motor, primary visual, and the barrel field of the primary somatosensory cortex (MOs, VISp, and SSp-bfd; Figs 2 and 3).”
findingPPC output to the thalamus is concentrated in three associative nuclei, LP, PO and LD.— “Strong PPC projections were found in the associative thalamic regions, LP, PO, and LD.”
findingThe heaviest inputs to the PPC come from ipsilateral secondary motor, auditory and visual cortex plus primary somatosensory cortex.— “The largest inputs arise from ipsilateral secondary cortical regions (motor, auditory, and visual) and the primary somatosensory region (Fig. 5).”
findingHypothalamic output of the PPC is essentially confined to the zona incerta, a connection not previously reported.— “Within the hypothalamus, moderate projections from the PPC appeared almost exclusively in the ZI, for both VISa and VISrl”
findingWhat separates PPC from neighbouring visual cortex is that it gets more multimodal spatial input and less primary visual input.— “In summary, the enhanced multimodal spatial input and decreased primary visual input distinguishes the PPC from adjacent regions of the visual cortex.”
findingThe strongest reciprocal cortical partners of PPC are MOs, SSp-bfd, VISal, VISam and VISpm.— “In the cortex, MOs, SSp-bfd, VISal, VISam, and VISpm showed the greatest mutual connection, for which pairs both VISa and VISrl showed the projection strength above the threshold (Fig. 11a,b).”
findingThe strongest reciprocal thalamic partners of PPC are LP, PO, LD and PF.— “In the thalamus, LP, PO, LD, and PF were found to have the strongest reciprocal connections (Fig. 11c,d).”
findingNeuromodulatory input to PPC is dominated by cholinergic fibres, with weaker serotonergic and histaminergic input.— “In summary, there was a distinct cholinergic input to the PPC, with much lighter serotonergic and histaminergic inputs to the PPC; patterns were similar in adjacent regions.”
findingThe posterior thalamic nucleus projects topographically, its dorsomedial neurons reaching VISa and its lateral neurons reaching VISrl.— “Dorsomedial PO neurons projected to VISa, whereas neurons in lateral portion of PO…
findingCompared with rats, mice have lighter LD and LP projections to PPC but clear ventrobasal (VPL, VPM) and LGd input that rats were not reported to have.
findingThe single largest mouse-rat discrepancy in PPC output is the infralimbic area, strongly innervated in rat and only trace-labelled in mouse.
findingTop-down cortical inputs from ILA and ACAd and higher-order thalamic input from VAL and PO terminate mainly in layer 1 of PPC.
findingOverall the mouse PPC connectome closely resembles the rat's, with a minority of clear species differences.
