mechanismand the audience-relativity is made concrete rather than left as a caveat: practitioners may be able to read a small Bayesian network where laymen are better served by a few weighted features— “Finally, we note that the notion of interpretability also depends on the target audience. Machine learning practitioners may be able to interpret small Bayesian networks, but laymen may be more comfortable with a small number of weighted features as an explanation.”
mechanismTHE IMPOSSIBILITY IS STATED BEFORE THE CRITERION IS WEAKENED: an explanation cannot be completely faithful unless it IS the complete description of the model, so the requirement imposed instead is that it be faithful in the vicinity of the instance being explained— “Although it is often impossible for an explanation to be completely faithful unless it is the complete description of the model itself, for an explanation to be meaningful it must at least be locally faithful, i.e. it must correspond to how the model behaves in the vicinity of the instance being predicted.”
mechanismAND THE IMPLICATION IS STATED TO RUN ONE WAY ONLY: local fidelity does not imply global fidelity - a feature that matters globally may not matter locally, and the reverse - while global fidelity would imply local, and globally faithful interpretable explanations are said to remain a challenge— “We note that local fidelity does not imply global fidelity: features that are globally important may not be important in the local context, and vice versa. While global fidelity would imply local fidelity, identifying globally faithful explanations that are interpretable remains a challenge for complex models.”

mechanismTHE TRADE-OFF IS PUT IN THE OBJECTIVE RATHER THAN LEFT TO JUDGEMENT: unfaithfulness in the neighbourhood is minimised while a separate measure of the explanation's own complexity is held low enough for a human to read, and the authors note the same formulation admits different explanation families, fidelity functions and complexity measures— “In order to ensure both interpretability and local fidelity, we must minimize L(f, g, πx) while having Ω(g) be low enough to be interpretable by humans. The explanation produced by LIME is obtained by the following: ξ(x) = argmin L(f, g, πx) + Ω(g) (1) g∈G This formulation can be used with different explanation families”
mechanismthe neighbourhood is explored BY SAMPLING rather than by assumption, precisely to keep the method model-agnostic: no assumption is made about the model, and the local loss is approximated by drawing samples weighted by proximity to the instance— “We want to minimize the locality-aware loss L(f, g, πx) without making any assumptions about f , since we want the explainer to be model-agnostic. Thus, in order to learn the local behavior of f as the interpretable inputs vary, we approximate L(f, g, πx) by drawing samples, weighted by πx.”
