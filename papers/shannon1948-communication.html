limitationThe continuous-case results are deliberately not developed with full generality or measure-theoretic rigour. — “We will not attempt, in the continuous case, to obtain our results with the greatest generality, or with the extreme rigor of pure mathematics, since this would involve a great deal of abstract measure theory and would obscure the main thread of the analysis.”
limitationThe existence proof of the noisy-channel coding theorem does not yield a usable construction. — “An attempt to obtain a good approximation to ideal coding by following the method of the proof is generally impractical.”
limitationFor non-white noise the capacity cannot be computed explicitly, only bounded. — “In the case of an arbitrary perturbing noise (not necessarily white thermal noise) it does not appear that the maximizing problem involved in determining the channel capacity C can be solved explicitly.”
limitationSource rates relative to a fidelity criterion have only been computed in a handful of simple cases. — “In fact, the actual calculation of rates has been carried out in only a few very simple cases.”
limitationShannon states that the uniqueness theorem for H, and the assumptions behind it, are in no way necessary to the theory; the justification is deferred to the implications of the definitions. — “This theorem, and the assumptions required for its proof, are in no way necessary for the present theory. It is given chiefly to lend a certain plausibility to some of our later definitions. The real justification of these definitions, however, will reside in their implications.”
limitationThe 50% redundancy figure for English is bounded to statistical structure within about eight letters. — “The redundancy of ordinary English, not considering statistical structure over greater distances than about eight letters, is roughly 50%.”
