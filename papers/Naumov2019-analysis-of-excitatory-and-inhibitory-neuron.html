phenomenonIh and HCN channel diversity across inferior colliculus neuron types— “The present study shows that GABAergic neurons have slowly activating hyperpolarizing-activated current (Ih) and an adaptive firing pattern whereas at least four types of glutamatergic neurons exist regarding their Ih properties and firing patterns.”
claim-typeexistence— “We conclude that HCN channel subtypes are differentially distributed in IC neuron types and correlate with neurotransmitter type and firing pattern.”
method — evidence— “Neuron types were classified upon electrophysiological measurements of the hyperpolarizing-activated current (Ih) in acute brain slices of young adult mice.”
mechanismDifferential distribution of HCN channel subtypes across neuron classes (or, alternatively, second-messenger modulation of Ih) produces the observed differences in Ih kinetics and voltage dependence— “The differences in Ih kinetics and voltage dependence indicate that either HCN channel subtypes are differentially distributed in the various neuron types or that modulation of Ih via second messengers might be responsible for the observed differences.”
mechanismGABAergic neurons carry a slow Ih because their current is conducted by channels assembled from HCN2 and HCN4 subunits— “We conclude from these experiments that GABAergic neurons in the IC exhibit Ih that is mainly transmitted via ion channels composed of HCN2 and HCN4 subunits.”
mechanismFast Ih in excitatory neurons is carried by HCN1/HCN2 channels while slow Ih is carried by HCN2 and sometimes HCN4, so subunit composition sets the kinetic class— “In contrast, different types of excitatory neurons exist, with either fast Ih transmitted via HCN1/HCN2, or slow Ih transmitted via HCN2 and in some cases HCN4.”
mechanismBecause HCN4 is restricted to GABAergic cells and is cAMP-sensitive, neuromodulators that raise second-messenger levels would selectively retune the integrative properties of the inhibitory population— “Since HCN4 channels are primarily expressed in GABAergic neurons, neuromodulatory inputs that increase intracellular second messenger concentration (e.g., serotonin) may predominantly target the integrative properties of the GABAergic population”
mechanismA large, fast-activating Ih depolarizes the membrane after release from hyperpolarization and thereby enables rebound spiking in specific excitatory subclasses— “This indicates that rebound firing is facilitated by large and fast-activating Ih and mainly found in specific subclasses of IC neurons.”
replicates Zhao et al. (2011)— “This confirms previous results (Zhao et al. 2011) and shows that almost all neurons in the CIC expressing EYFP and channelrhodopsin are also GAD67(cid:2) and thus GABAergic.”
contradicts Ono et al. (2005)— “The same study also identified a population of GABAergic neurons with build-up or/and pause response, a firing pattern that we did not observe in the CIC.”
extends Koch et al. (2004)— “As HCN1 has been shown to be tonotopically organized in the rat IC (Koch et al. 2004) we quantified whether a similar differential distribution of HCN1 was also present in the mouse IC (Fig. 4D).”
replicates Ono et al. (2017)— “This was also seen in a recent in vivo study where two distinct groups of GABAergic CIC neurons according to their firing pattern in response to acoustic stimulation were indistinguishable (Ono et al. 2017).”
