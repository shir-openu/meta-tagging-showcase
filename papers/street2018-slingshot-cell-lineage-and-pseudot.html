limitationEVERY METHOD IN THIS FIELD, INCLUDING THIS ONE, REQUIRES PART OF THE ANSWER AS INPUT: `all existing lineage inference methods explicitly or implicitly make the assumption that A STARTING STATE CAN BE IDENTIFIED BY THE USER`. Slingshot asks for the initial cluster; Wishbone and DPT for an initial cell; Monocle and Embeddr for the direction of a path. THE ARROW OF DEVELOPMENT IS NOT IN THE DATA
limitationthe upstream choices - normalisation, dimensionality reduction, clustering - have `a potentially large impact on the final result`, and the paper does not fix them. That is a deliberate design decision (`these choices should therefore NOT BE HARDCODED`) and it means A SLINGSHOT RESULT IS NOT REPRODUCIBLE FROM THE METHOD NAME ALONE
limitationK, THE NUMBER OF CLUSTERS, IS THE PARAMETER THAT MATTERS, AND IT FAILS AT BOTH ENDS: at K=3 `Slingshot is often unable to detect the branching event`, and at higher K it `begins to OVERFIT and identify more spurious branching events`. The paper reports both, which is more than the robustness-to-clustering result on its own would suggest
limitationand it says the failure is not its own: `these issues are COMMON TO ALL CLUSTER-BASED MST METHODS and NOT MITIGATED by the use of simultaneous principal curves` - the smoothing helps the pseudotimes, not the topology
limitationthe output is A POINT ESTIMATE WITH NO INTERVAL. The paper names two distinct uncertainties it does not quantify - STRUCTURAL (which lineage a cell belongs to) and TEMPORAL (where along it) - and offers a bootstrap as something a user could do, not as something the method does
limitationthe real-data demonstration was SUPERVISED WITH THE ANSWER IT IS JUDGED AGAINST: the HBC cluster was given as the origin and mSus as a terminal state, and correctness is then agreement with known biology. The paper is careful enough to report which supervision mattered - `ONLY THE FIRST HAD AN EFFECT on the eventual cluster-based MST` - which is what keeps this a caveat rather than a circularity

conceptpseudotime — a one-dimensional coordinate standing in for developmental progress, computed per lineage by projection onto a fitted curve
conceptlineage — an ordered set of cell clusters from a shared start to a unique terminal cluster
conceptSlingshot — the two-stage method introduced here: cluster MST for structure, simultaneous principal curves for pseudotime
conceptcluster-based MST — the first stage - a minimum spanning tree drawn over CLUSTERS, which is what fixes the number of lineages and where they branch
conceptminimum spanning tree — drawn between nodes that are CLUSTERS OF CELLS, not cells - the paper's first stage, and the step it shares with TSCAN and Waterfall
conceptcluster-based MST methods — the family Slingshot belongs to - TSCAN and Waterfall are the others - and the family whose failure modes at extreme K it shares
conceptKendall rank correlation coefficient — the agreement statistic between true and inferred pseudotime that defines `accuracy` in this benchmark
conceptaccuracy score — the per-dataset number: best-matching inferred lineage per true lineage, averaged
conceptsupervision — user-supplied initial and optional terminal clusters - the part of the answer every method in the field needs before it runs
conceptsimultaneous principal curves — the paper's novel step - principal curves fitted to all branches at once, which is what makes the pseudotimes robust to the clustering while leaving the topology to the MST
conceptstructural uncertainty — which lineage a cell is assigned to - named by the paper and not quantified by it
concepttemporal uncertainty — where along a lineage a cell sits - the second named, unquantified uncertainty
