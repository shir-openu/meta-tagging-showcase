phenomenonself-reported mathematics performance in undergraduates— “This study therefore explored the role of motivation, peer assisted learning, quality of teaching and student interest on student performance in mathematics.”
claim-typemechanism— “It was concluded that students learning interest in mathematics partially mediated the relationships between mathematics learning motivation and performance in mathematics; as well as between mathematics teaching quality and performance in mathematics.”
method — evidence— “The study design was a survey, with structured questionnaire being used for data collection.”
mechanismPeer assisted learning does not reach performance directly; it first raises students' interest in mathematics, and that interest is what raises performance— “Peer assisted learning influenced students’ mathematics learning interest, whiles interest further influenced performance in mathematics.”
mechanismInterest is a necessary link in the chain: peer learning that fails to build interest produces no performance gain at all— “That is, peer assisted learning which does not result in building students’ mathematics learning interest, will have no effect on performance in mathematics of students.”
contradicts prior peer-tutoring findings that peer assisted learning directly determines mathematics performance (Thurston et al., 2020; Rockinson-Szapkiw & Wendt, 2020; Alegre et al., 2019)— “This relationship is contrary to past findings.”
senseteaching quality - AS ANALYSED HERE: a single higher-order variable, with its first-order dimensions deliberately not examined— “This study however focused on the higher-order variables (teaching quality and learning interest), without specific attention on the firstorder variables.”
senseperformance in mathematics - as a latent variable measured by questionnaire items, whose number is decided after the fact by the factor loadings— “Originally, performance in mathematics had four measurement items (observed variables), students’ mathematics interest had seven measurement items”
sensemediation - used here in two senses that the paper distinguishes by result: PARTIAL, where the direct path survives (motivation, teaching quality), and FULL, where it does not (peer assisted learning)— “This present study in line with this, found that mathematics learning interest fully mediated the relationship between peer assisted learning and performance in mathematics”
