phenomenonsubliminal perception under visual masking
claim-typemechanism
mechanismA CHANGE OF OPERATIONAL DEFINITION RECLASSIFIES THE SAME UNCHANGED TASK FROM EVIDENCE OF UNAWARENESS TO EVIDENCE OF AWARENESS: according to this **NEW OPERATIONAL DEFINITION OF AWARENESS**, the discrimination tasks **PREVIOUSLY USED AS INDIRECT MEASURES** thought to prove non-conscious influences **NOW BECAME THE DIRECT MEASURE** thought to **INDEX AWARENESS** of the stimulus. Nothing about the experiment changed; the definition moved, and the evidence changed sides with it — “According to this new operational definition of awareness, the discrimination tasks previously used as indirect measures thought to prove non-conscious influences now became the direct measure thought to index awareness of the stimulus.”
mechanismAND THE REDEFINITION LEAVES A MEASUREMENT VACANCY, WHICH IS STATED AS A PROBLEM RATHER THAN A CONSEQUENCE: such a **NEW DEFINITION OF AWARENESS LEFT RESEARCHERS WITH THE DIFFICULT PROBLEM OF INVENTING NEW INDIRECT MEASURES** of subliminal processing. Redefining the term did not merely reinterpret the old results, it removed the instruments, and new ones had to be invented to fill the place the definition had emptied — “Of course, such a new definition of awareness left researchers with the difficult problem of inventing new indirect measures of subliminal processing.”
mechanismA THRESHOLD FOR A MENTAL STATE IS DEFINED AS A PERFORMANCE LEVEL ON A TASK, AND THE DEFINITION IS ARGUED FOR RATHER THAN ASSUMED: Eriksen further argued that the **OBJECTIVE THRESHOLD FOR CONSCIOUSNESS SHOULD BE DEFINED AS A SITUATION WHERE FORCED-CHOICE DISCRIMINATION IS AT CHANCE**. The word `objective` in the term names the kind of evidence the definition admits, and what it denotes is a number produced by a procedure — “Eriksen further argued that the objective threshold for consciousness should be defined as a situation where forced-choice discrimination is at chance.”
mechanismAND THAT DEFINITION IS THEN JUDGED TOO CONSERVATIVE BY THE PEOPLE WHO DECLINED IT, WITH THE REASON GIVEN AS CONTAMINATION OF THE MEASURE: their main argument against the use of objective measures was that they can lead to **ABOVE-CHANCE PERFORMANCE DUE TO NON-CONSCIOUS INFLUENCES**, thus making the definition of an objective threshold **AN EXTREMELY CONSERVATIVE MEASURE OF CONSCIOUS ACCESS**. The measure meant to detect awareness is said to respond to the very thing it is supposed to exclude — “Their main argument against the use of objective measures was that they can lead to above-chance performance due to non-conscious influences, thus making the definition of an objective threshold an extremely conservative measure of conscious access.”
mechanismA CRITERION FOR A SUBJECTIVE REPORT IS SHOWN TO DEPEND ON THE EXPERIMENTER RATHER THAN ON THE SUBJECT: this **CONFIDENCE CRITERION DEPENDS ON THE EXPECTATIONS THAT THE EXPERIMENTER IS IMPOSING ON THE SUBJECT**. What the measurement records is partly a property of the person taking it, which is stated flatly and in one sentence — “This confidence criterion depends on the expectations that the experimenter is imposing on the subject.”
mechanismA THRESHOLD ESTIMATE IN WIDE USE IS DECLARED STATISTICALLY UNRELIABLE, WITH THE SAMPLE SIZE NAMED: Merikle used signal detection theory to show that the **SMALL SAMPLE OF ITEMS FREQUENTLY USED TO EVALUATE THE THRESHOLD** for conscious perception - **NOT MORE THAN 20 TRIALS** - **IS NOT STATISTICALLY RELIABLE**. The number that defines the term is shown to be unmeasurable at the sample size the field was using — “Second, Merikle (1982) used signal detection theory to show that the small sample of items frequently used to evaluate the threshold for conscious perception (not more than 20 trials) is not statistically reliable.”
mechanismA MEASUREMENT PROCEDURE'S OMISSION IS SHOWN TO BIAS THE TERM IT DEFINES, AND THE DIRECTION OF THE BIAS IS GIVEN: since semantically related target stimuli can **INCREASE, RETROSPECTIVELY, THE IDENTIFICATION OF MASKED PRIMES**, the **ABSENCE OF ANY TARGET STIMULI DURING THRESHOLD DEFINITION PROCEDURES** in the earlier experiments **PROBABLY UNDERESTIMATED PRIME VISIBILITY**. The calibration omitted stimuli that are known to change the quantity being calibrated — “Since semantically related target stimuli can increase, retrospectively, the identification of masked primes ( Bernstein et al . 1989 ), the absence of any target stimuli during threshold definition procedures in the experiments of Marcel and others probably underestimated prime visibility.”
mechanismAN INFERENCE PATTERN THE FIELD RELIED ON IS REFUTED IN GENERAL, NOT CASE BY CASE: the problem with this logic is that **QUALITATIVE DISSOCIATIONS NEED NOT REFLECT A DISSOCIATION BETWEEN CONSCIOUS AND SUBLIMINAL PROCESSING**. What is denied is not a result but the licence to read results of that shape in that way — “However, as outlined by Holender (1986) , the problem with this logic is that qualitative dissociations need not reflect a dissociation between conscious and subliminal processing.”
mechanismAND AN ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATION OF THE SAME DATA IS OFFERED IN TERMS OF RESOURCES RATHER THAN CONSCIOUSNESS: rather than being due to visibility, these qualitative dissociations **COULD BE DUE TO DIFFERENCES IN THE TIME OR RESOURCES NEEDED TO STRATEGICALLY PROCESS THE PRIME**. The contrast that was taken to mark a boundary between two kinds of mind is re-described as a difference of degree — “Thus, rather than being due to visibility, these qualitative dissociations could be due to differences in the time or resources needed to strategically process the prime.”
mechanismA TERM IS GIVEN A NUMERIC CRITERION, WITH THE WORD `GENUINELY` DOING THE DEFINITIONAL WORK: Kouider and Dupoux assessed prime awareness across several prime durations and showed that a prime **CAN BE CONSIDERED AS GENUINELY INVISIBLE ONLY IF ITS DURATION IS BELOW 50 MS**. Membership in the category is decided by a measured quantity crossing a stated value — “Kouider & Dupoux (2001) assessed prime awareness in this method across several prime durations and showed that a prime can be considered as genuinely invisible only if its duration is below 50 ms.”
mechanismA QUANTITY IS ESTIMATED AT A POINT THAT WAS NEVER OBSERVED, BY EXTRAPOLATION, AND THE PROCEDURE IS DESCRIBED PLAINLY: instead of demonstrating chance performance for all subjects, they proposed a **REGRESSION METHOD THAT USES THE VISIBILITY MEASURE AS A PREDICTOR OF THE PRIMING EFFECT** - and **THE INTERCEPT OF THE REGRESSION WAS THEN USED TO EVALUATE THE AMOUNT OF PRIMING FOR NULL PERFORMANCE ON PRIME VISIBILITY**. The effect at zero visibility is computed rather than measured, because zero visibility was never achieved — “Instead of demonstrating chance performance on objective measures of awareness for all subjects, they proposed to use a regression method that uses the visibility measure as a predictor of the priming effect. The intercept of the regression was then used to evaluate the amount of priming for null performance on prime visibility”
mechanismTHE PARADIGM'S WHOLE LOGIC RESTS ON A NULL RESULT, AND THE REVIEW SAYS SO IN ITS OWN TERMS: a **DIRECT MEASURE** such as identification, discrimination or detection **IS USED TO SHOW NULL SENSITIVITY** on the masked stimulus. The evidence that the stimulus was not seen is the failure of a test, which is what makes every later dispute about the test's power rather than about the phenomenon — “A direct measure such as identification, discrimination or detection is used to show null sensitivity on the masked stimulus.”
mechanismTWO FAMILIES OF MEASURE FOR ONE CONSTRUCT ARE NAMED, AND A PREFERENCE BETWEEN THEM IS RECORDED AS A HISTORICAL FACT: while all the above studies used **OBJECTIVE MEASURES OF AWARENESS**, in accord with Eriksen's criticism, Cheesman and Merikle **PRIVILEGED SUBJECTIVE MEASURES**, as in the earliest research on subliminal perception. The same word is measured two incompatible ways, and which way is used is a school rather than a finding — “While all the above studies used objective measures of awareness, in accord with Eriksen's (1960) criticism, Cheesman & Merikle (1986) privileged subjective measures, as in the earliest research on subliminal perception.”
mechanismAN ASSUMPTION IS NAMED AND TRACED TO THE HYPOTHESIS THAT LICENSES IT: this assumption is based on the **DIRECT MOTOR SPECIFICATION HYPOTHESIS**, according to which an adequate response is **UNCONSCIOUSLY ASSOCIATED WITH A STIMULUS WITHOUT HAVING TO BE MEDIATED BY THE SEMANTIC LEVEL**. The assumption is not defended here; it is given a name and a citation, which is how a premise becomes inheritable — “This assumption is based on the direct motor specification hypothesis according to which an adequate response is unconsciously associated with a stimulus without having to be mediated by the semantic level”
mechanismAND THE FIELD'S PRESENT STATE IS SPLIT INTO A SETTLED PART AND A DISPUTED PART, IN ONE SENTENCE: although the **EXISTENCE OF SUBLIMINAL PERCEPTION IS LARGELY ACKNOWLEDGED TODAY**, some researchers **STILL DEBATE THE SEMANTIC INTERPRETATION** of these experiments. What is agreed is that something happens; what is disputed is what to call the level at which it happens — “although the existence of subliminal perception is largely acknowledged today, some researchers still debate the semantic interpretation of these experiments.”
relation — “In this concluding section, we would like to argue that those results fit with the tripartite distinction of subliminal, preconscious and conscious processing that one of us has recently proposed ( Dehaene et al . 2006 ).”
relation — “In the global neuronal workspace framework ( Dehaene & Naccache 2001 ; Dehaene & Changeux 2004 ), which extends Baars' (1988) cognitive theory of consciousness, the human brain is viewed as a collection of specialized processors that mostly operate non-consciously, but whose content can be consciously accessed whenever they are linked to a global, metastable assembly involving distant prefrontal and parietal neurons with long-range axons.”
differs insubliminal → preconscious — WHICH OF THE TWO REQUIREMENTS FAILED - subliminal is a failure of bottom-up strength and cannot be rescued by attention; preconscious is a failure of top-down attention on information that already carries enough activation. The states can produce the same behavioural report and differ in what attention WOULD have done — “two types of non-conscious processes, which we call subliminal and preconscious”
effect directionmasking → subliminal — increases — “made subliminal by the joint use of brief presentations and masking”
replicationcontested
limitationThe review's primary scope is masking paradigms; inattention paradigms are treated only by comparison. — “While this review focuses on subliminal masking paradigms, where the evidence for semantic processing is limited to specific experimental conditions, it is also interesting to consider other paradigms where non-conscious perception is induced by inattention.”
limitationThe review cannot resolve why ERP studies disagree over whether the N400 is modulated by genuinely subliminal semantic priming. — “The reason for this discrepancy remains an open question for future research.”
limitationWhether attention modulates subliminal priming at the lowest sensory levels is left unresolved. — “Nonetheless, it remains an open question for further research whether attention modulates subliminal priming even at the lowest levels of sensory processing.”
limitationThe workspace model's central prediction about which processes are exclusive to consciousness remains untested. — “While highly suggestive evidence already exists (e.g. Kunde 2003 ), the testing of this prediction remains a key issue for further research.”
