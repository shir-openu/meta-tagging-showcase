phenomenonchronic clinically significant depression and anxiety in patients facing a life-threatening cancer diagnosis— “Cancer patients often develop a chronic, clinically significant syndrome of psychosocial distress having depressed mood, anxiety, and reduced quality of life as core features, with up to 40% of cancer patients meeting criteria for a mood disorder”
claim-typeexistence— “High-dose psilocybin produced large decreases in clinician- and self-rated measures of depressed mood and anxiety, along with increases in quality of life, life meaning, and optimism, and decreases in death anxiety.”
method — evidence— “This randomized, double-blind, cross-over trial investigated the effects of a very low (placebo-like) dose (1 or 3 mg/70 kg) vs. a high dose (22 or 30 mg/70 kg) of psilocybin administered in counterbalanced sequence with 5 weeks between sessions and a 6-month follow-up.”
object of study — evidence— “The effects of psilocybin were studied in 51 cancer patients with life-threatening diagnoses and symptoms of depression and/or anxiety.”
research question — evidence— “This randomized, double-blind, cross-over trial investigated the effects of a very low (placebo-like) dose (1 or 3 mg/70 kg) vs. a high dose (22 or 30 mg/70 kg) of psilocybin administered in counterbalanced sequence with 5 weeks between sessions and a 6-month follow-up.”
contribution — evidence— “High-dose psilocybin produced large decreases in clinician- and self-rated measures of depressed mood and anxiety, along with increases in quality of life, life meaning, and optimism, and decreases in death anxiety.”
mechanismTHE MYSTICAL-TYPE EXPERIENCE ON SESSION DAY MEDIATES THE DOSE EFFECT: the dose does not reach the therapeutic outcome directly, it reaches it through what the participant experienced during the session— “Mystical-type psilocybin experience on session day mediated the effect of psilocybin dose on therapeutic outcomes.”
mechanismPSILOCIN IS A 5-HT2A AGONIST that alters cortical and subcortical activity and brain network dynamics - offered as the pharmacological route and explicitly labelled speculative for the therapeutic effect— “As a 5-HT2A agonist, the psilocybin metabolite psilocin directly and indirectly affects various brain cortical and subcortical areas and alters brain network dynamics”
mechanismTELLING EVERYONE THAT PSILOCYBIN WOULD BE GIVEN EACH SESSION IS WHAT MAKES THE LOW DOSE WORK AS A COMPARATOR: it removes the participant's ability to infer condition from the fact of administration, which is how expectancy was minimised without an inert placebo— “Instructions to participants and staff minimized expectancy effects.”
critiques antidepressants and benzodiazepines for depressed mood and anxiety in cancer patients, where the efficacy evidence is called limited and conflicting and benzodiazepines are recommended only short-term— “Antidepressants and, less frequently, benzodiazepines are used to treat depressed mood and anxiety in cancer patients, although evidence suggesting efficacy is limited and conflicting, and benzodiazepines are generally only recommended for short-term use because of side effects and withdrawal”
critiques psychological approaches to emotional distress in cancer, which have shown only small to medium effects with low quality of reporting in many trials— “Although psychological approaches have shown only small to medium effects in treating emotional distress and quality of life, with low quality of reporting in many trials”
replicates the authors' own previous psilocybin work in healthy volunteers, whose adverse-effect and persisting-effect patterns this trial reproduces in patients— “As in previous research, the study documented enduring increases in positive changes in attitudes and mood on both the participant-rated Persisting Effects Questionnaire and on the Community Observer Questionnaire ( Griffiths et al., 2006, 2011). However, neither of these measures has been independently validated.”
senseblinding - IN THIS TRIAL A MATTER OF DEGREE THAT THE AUTHORS MEASURED RATHER THAN ASSERTED. Every participant was told psilocybin would be given on each session, so the comparison is high dose against a very low dose, not against an inert placebo. The paper's own verdict is `SOME protection`, not blinding— “Overall, we conclude that the blinding procedures provided some protection against a priori monitor expectancy strongly determining outcomes of the psilocybin dose manipulation.”
sensevery low dose - A PLACEBO-LIKE DOSE AND NOT A PLACEBO, and the authors say the difference matters: 1 mg/70 kg was assumed largely inactive and they state that some pharmacological activity cannot be ruled out, and that a far lower dose would have been preferable— “Although it was assumed that 1 mg/70 kg would be largely pharmacologically inactive, some pharmacological activity of this dose cannot be ruled out entirely.”
sensemystical-type experience - USED HERE AS A MEASURED MEDIATOR ON SESSION DAY, not as a description of how the drug felt. It is the variable through which dose reaches therapeutic outcome, which makes it part of the causal claim rather than a side observation— “Mystical-type psilocybin experience on session day mediated the effect of psilocybin dose on therapeutic outcomes.”

evidential statusmechanism — The neuropsychopharmacological mechanisms of psilocybin therapeutic effects remain speculative
limitation1 mg/70 kg was ASSUMED largely pharmacologically inactive and the authors state that some activity cannot be ruled out entirely
limitationthe authors say a far lower dose - around 0.01 mg/70 kg - would have been preferable, to assure inactivity while keeping the instruction that psilocybin would be given each session
limitationthe cross-over after 5 weeks precluded a double-blind across-group assessment of high-dose efficacy at that point - the design bought within-subject data at the cost of the between-group comparison
limitationneither the Persisting Effects Questionnaire nor the Community Observer Questionnaire has been independently validated
limitationclinician ratings were not blinded, and the authors say blinded clinician ratings would strengthen the study
limitationn = 51, highly educated and predominantly White, which the authors say limits the generality of the conclusions
limitationthe blinding design depends on not disclosing the specific arms, which the authors note may be impossible in settings that require full disclosure
limitationthe approach is bounded by extensive exclusion criteria and by the significant time and cost of professional support before, during and after the session
limitationpatients may be reluctant to take part because high doses have sometimes produced transient psychological distress or anxiety
limitationthe neuropsychopharmacological mechanism of the therapeutic effect is stated to remain speculative, and how the enduring effects appear in cortical networks or neuroplastic change remains to be established
limitationa multisite study in a larger and more diverse population is called for to establish generality and safety
