Since then, therefore, the researchers mainly focused on increasing PCEterm·dom of the PSCsterm·dom by developing efficient photovoltaic materials (donor 6 – 8 and acceptor 9 , 10 ) and efficient electrode buffer layer materials 11 – 16 , and optimizing active layerterm·dom morphology 17 – 19 , etc. For the PSCsterm·dom with fullerene derivatives (especially PC 71 BM) as acceptors, PCEterm·dom has gradually increased to over 10% recently by designing low-bandgap polymer donorsterm·key such as PTB7-Th 20 , PNTz4T 21 , and PffBT4T-C 9 C 13 (ref. 22 ), etc. In recent 2 years, low-bandgap nonfullerene n -OS acceptors, such as IDTBR 23 , m -ITIC 24 , IDICterm 25 , etc., have attracted great attention owing to their advantages of broad and strong absorption, easy tuning electronic energy levels, and high morphology stability in comparison with the fullerene derivative acceptors 26 . PCEterm·dom of the PSCsterm·dom with n -OS as acceptors has rapidly increased to over 11% by using medium bandgap polymer donorsterm·key such as J71 (ref. 27 ), PBDB-T 28 , PB3T 29 , etc.

Now, the PCEterm·dom has reached the threshold for application. Next step, we should consider the stability and cost issues for commercial application of the PSCsterm·dom. However, very few efforts have been made on reducing the costs of the photovoltaic materials, and costs of the efficient polymer donorsterm·key reported so far were too high to meet commercial application of the PSCsterm·dom due to their complicated molecular structures, verbose multi-steps synthesis, and multiple purifications 30 . Actually, poly(3-hexylthiophene)term (P3HTterm) is still the main donor material for the fabrication of large area PSCsterm·dom 31 , because it can be synthesized in large scale with relatively low costterm·key. Hence, developing low-cost and efficient polymer donorsterm·key becomes one of the greatest challenges for the application of PSCsterm·dom.

Herein, we design and synthesize a low-cost polymer donorterm·key poly [(thiophene)-alt-(6,7-difluoro-2-(2-hexyldecyloxy)quinoxaline)] (PTQ10term) (Fig. 1a ). The results indicate that PTQ10term is a promising polymer donorterm·key for commercial products, and it will make the application of PSCsterm·dom highly promising.

Charge carrier recombination To understand the effect of TAterm·mth or TAterm·mth + SAterm·mth treatments on the enhanced photovoltaic performance, the charge carrier recombination behavior in the traditional structured PSCsterm·dom was studied by measuring the dependence of J sc and V oc on light intensity ( P light ). The relationship of J sc and P light can be depicted by the formula J sc ∝( P light ) α , where the value of α indicates the degree of bimolecular recombinationterm·dom. The value of α should be 1 when bimolecular recombinationterm·dom do not occur in donor/acceptor blend films, and there is some bimolecular recombinationterm·dom if α value is smaller than 1 (ref. 38 ). Figure 2c displays the plots of log J sc versus log P light , and α values are 0.95, 0.96, and 1.00 for the devices without (as-cast), with TAterm·mth, and with TAterm·mth + SAterm·mth treatments, respectively. The gradually increased values of α indicate the reduced bimolecular recombinationterm·dom when the blend films are processed with TAterm·mth and TAterm·mth + SAterm·mth compared to the as-cast devices. Especially, α value of 1 for the PSCsterm·dom with TAterm·mth + SAterm·mth treatment indicates that there is no bimolecular recombinationterm·dom in the TAterm·mth + SAterm·mth treated devices. For the as-cast devices and TA-treated devices, another plausible reason for the deviation of the α values from unity, can be understood in term of the build-up of space-charge in the device due to the unbalanced electron-hole mobility as indicated by Blom’s work 39 , 40 . Figure 2d shows the plots of V oc versus ln ( P light ) of the PSCsterm·dom. If bimolecular recombinationterm·dom is the exclusive recombination form, the slope of the fitting straight line of V oc versus ln ( P light ) should be kT / e (where e is the elementary charge, k is the Boltzmann constant, and T is the Kelvin temperature) 41 . The slopes of the fitting lines for the as-cast, TA-treated, and TAterm·mth + SA-treated devices are 0.920 kT/e , 0.924 kT/e , and 0.988 kT/e , respectively. The slope very close to kT/e for the TAterm·mth + SA-treated PSCsterm·dom indicates that almost no other recombination occurs in the devices with TAterm·mth + SAterm·mth treatment. The results of J sc and V oc dependence on P light indicate that there are very little charge carrier recombination in the optimized PSCsterm·dom treated with TAterm·mth + SAterm·mth, which consequently results in the best PCEterm·dom of 12.70% for the PSCsterm·dom with TAterm·mth + SAterm·mth treatment.
