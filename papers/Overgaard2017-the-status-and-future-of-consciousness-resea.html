content-observations: finding · quantity · variable · definition · key-term — grounded in the full text
findingThe field's two dominant problems are not independent: neural correlates cannot be identified without first committing to a measure of consciousness, so the measurement problem is prior to the correlate problem.
findingObjective (indirect) measures cannot escape the weaknesses of introspective report, because calibrating any objective measure requires pairing a behaviour with a subjective report in the first place.
findingSo-called no-report paradigms introduce nothing that escapes introspection either, since the link between the objective marker and conscious experience can only be established by direct introspective report.
findingWhich measurement method is used determines which answer is obtained about the neural correlates of consciousness: some methods yield prefrontal correlates of visual consciousness, others yield occipital/parietal correlates without prefrontal involvement.
findingRecent reviews favour the view that consciousness is associated with early occipital/parietal activation rather than late prefrontal activation, with late activity treated as a confound of metacognition and report.
findingEmpirical consciousness data currently do not discriminate between metaphysical positions, because existing findings can be accommodated by every theory of the mind-brain relation.
findingThe next major challenge the author identifies is measuring consciousness "from the outside" - in non-responsive coma and vegetative-state patients, who have a neural system but no report, and in artificial systems, which may report but have no biological neural system.
findingDespite these unsolved foundations, consciousness research already works as a discipline producing meaningful and reproducible results.
findingdifferent methods of measuring consciousness yield different conclusions about which brain regions are its correlates
findingrecent reviews favour the occipital/parietal reading over the prefrontal one
findingthe same early activations are read as the correlate of consciousness by one camp and as preconscious by the other
findingthe existing evidence is compatible with every metaphysical position, so it settles none of them
