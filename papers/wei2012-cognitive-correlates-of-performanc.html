phenomenoncognitive correlates of advanced mathematics learning— “The goal of the current study was to investigate cognitive correlates of learning advanced mathematics.”
claim-typeexistence— “The results showed that spatial abilities were significantly correlated with performance in advanced mathematics after controlling for other factors.”
method — evidence— “The current study investigated the associations between students’ performance on a test of advanced mathematics and a battery of 17 cognitive tasks on basic numerical processing, complex numerical processing, spatial abilities, language abilities, and general cognitive processing.”
mechanismAdvanced mathematical notation must be laid out and held in a mental spatial arrangement, so spatial ability constrains how well the symbols, numerals and letters of a formula can be represented and manipulated— “For advanced mathematics, it is crucial to have accurate mental spatial layout of mathematical symbols.”
mechanismLearning advanced mathematics requires comprehending unfamiliar technical terminology, the same semantic-comprehension process tapped by the word semantics task, which is why word semantics predicts advanced mathematics— “Subjects in the current study needed to understand the 18 mathematical terminologies, just as they would with words included in the word semantics task.”
mechanismBasic number sense drops out as a predictor because advanced mathematics operates far away from numerosity and numbers, unlike elementary mathematics which directly involves them— “One explanation for this is that elementary mathematics involves numerosity and numbers, but advanced mathematics is too far removed from basic numerical processing.”
builds-on Shepard & Metzler (1971) mental rotation task— “The three-dimensional mental rotation task was based on Shepard’s mental rotation task (Shepard & Metzler, 1971).”

limitationThe headline construct was assessed over a narrow range, which the authors name first among their limitations.— “First, a relatively small range of high-level mathematical abilities were assessed in the current study. That is, we only investigated the ability to…
limitationThe sample excludes both ends of the ability range the conclusions are about.
limitationTHE NULL WAS PREDICTED, NOT DISCOVERED, and the authors say so before reporting it: 'we EXPECTED TO DISCONFIRM this number sense hypothesis because advanced mathematics is far removed from basic numerical processing', adding that in a college sample the variation in basic number processing should be limited. So the finding is confirmatory of the authors' expectation and partly explained in advance by their sampling, which is a different epistemic object from a surprising null.
