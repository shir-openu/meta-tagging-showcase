transferfrom communication science / social psychology (communication accommodation theory): the theory of how humans accommodate their speech to an interlocutor, re-applied to design a chatbot that accommodates its style to the user— “Our study will further contribute to research by adapting communication accommodation theory (Giles et al., 2010) to the context of human-AI interaction, thereby showing that adapting the CA’s communication style to users has the potential to positively influence their user experience.”
mechanismAccommodating one's communication style to the interlocutor signals an attitude toward them and thereby steers how the speaker and the interaction are evaluated— “The theory suggests that humans adapt their communication style to show their attitude toward the interlocutor and steer the evaluation of the speaker and the interaction (Giles et al., 2010).”
mechanismAn interaction partner that accommodates the user's needs signals attention and caring, which raises perceived social presence, which in turn produces enjoyment— “When a (human or technology) interaction partner considers the needs and preferences of the interlocutor during the interaction, this signals attention and caring (Bitner et al., 1990; Giles et al., 2010), thereby enhancing the perceived social presence (Hess et al., 2009; Van Pinxteren et al., 2020).”
mechanismMatching the chatbot's communication style to the user's cognitive abilities lowers the cognitive effort the interaction demands and raises its efficiency, which drives re-use intention— “For example, if the CA’s communication style matches the user's cognitive abilities, this will likely decrease the required cognitive effort and improve interaction efficiency (Giles et al., 2010; Goodhue and Thompson, 1995).”
mechanismThe unequal split between rational and intuitive participants left that group underpowered, producing the absence of significant differences— “A possible explanation could be the imbalance of the number of participants that were classified as rational or intuitive (10 compared to 4)—which will be addressed in the main study.”
