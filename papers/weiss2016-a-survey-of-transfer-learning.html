mechanismWHY TRANSFER LEARNING EXISTS AT ALL: ordinary machine learning assumes train and test come from one domain with one distribution, and the method is what you do when that assumption is false and the target data is expensive to collect— “An assumption of traditional machine learning methodologies is the training data and testing data are taken from the same domain, such that the input feature space and data distribution characteristics are the same.”
mechanismNEGATIVE TRANSFER IS DEFINED BY COMPARISON, NOT BY DAMAGE: a learner trained on the target alone is compared against one trained with the source added, and transfer is negative when the plain learner wins— “negative transfer occurs when the performance of fT1(·) is greater than the performance of fT2(·). The topic of negative transfer addresses the need to quantify the amount of relatedness between the source domain and the target domain and whether an attempt to transfer knowledge from the source domain should be made.”
mechanismPSEUDO LABELS CORRECT A DISTRIBUTION USING GUESSES AT THE THING BEING CORRECTED FOR: with no labeled target data, labels are estimated and then used to fix the conditional distribution - which the paper itself calls problematic— “To address the lack of labeled target data, some solutions estimate the labels for the target data (called pseudo labels), which are then used to correct the conditional distribution differences. This method is problematic because the conditional distribution corrections are being made with the aid of pseudo labels.”
mechanisminstance transfer reweights source examples to correct MARGINAL distribution differences, and works best precisely when the CONDITIONAL distribution is already the same— “A common method used in this case is for instances from the source domain to be reweighted in an attempt to correct for marginal distribution differences.”
mechanismthe two feature approaches differ in direction: one bends the source onto the target, the other maps both into a shared latent space— “Feature-based transfer learning approaches are categorized in two ways.”
mechanismparameter transfer shares model parameters or combines multiple reweighted source learners into an ensemble for the target— “The third transfer category is to transfer knowledge through shared parameters of source and target domain learner models or by creating multiple source learner models and optimally combining the reweighted learners (ensemble learners) to form an improved target learner (examples in Gao [37], Bonilla [8], and Evgeniou [33]).”
mechanismsource classifiers are weighted by how close their conditional distribution is to the target, summed, and used to manufacture pseudo labels for unlabelled target data— “This is accomplished by first building a classifier for each separate source domain.”

limitationTHE LITERATURE'S VOCABULARY IS INCONSISTENT AND THE PAPER SAYS SO: transfer learning and domain adaptation are used for similar processes, and the supervised / semi-supervised / unsupervised labels mean different things to different authors— “Throughout the literature on transfer learning, there are a number of terminology inconsistencies. Phrases such as transfer learning and domain adaptation are used to refer to similar processes. The following definitions will be used in this paper.”
limitationSome surveyed solutions are tied to their application and are stated not to generalise to others— “In some cases, the proposed transfer learning solutions are very specific to the underlying application and cannot be generically used for other applications.”
limitationCorrecting the conditional distribution rests on pseudo labels, which the paper names as problematic because the correction uses estimates of the very labels it lacks— “To address the lack of labeled target data, some solutions estimate the labels for the target data (called pseudo labels), which are then used to correct the conditional distribution differences. This method is problematic because the conditional distribution corrections are being made with the aid of pseudo labels.”
limitationNo single repository exists for the surveyed solutions, so the software behind the literature is scattered - the paper asks for one— “A single open-source software repository for published transfer learning solutions would be a great asset to the research community.”
limitationheterogeneous solutions mostly assume the domain distributions are the SAME, and need further adaptation steps when they are not— “The majority of the heterogeneous transfer learning solutions are focused on aligning the input spaces of the source and target domains with the assumption that the domain distributions are the same.”
limitationtransferring on a defined relationship between domains is named as the least used of the four categories— “The last transfer category (and the least used approach) is to transfer knowledge based on some defined relationship between the source and target domains (examples in Mihalkova [74] and Li [62]).”
