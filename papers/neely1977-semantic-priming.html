sense**THE PAPER'S MOVE IS TO MAKE THE CONFOUND INTO THE DESIGN.** Earlier work could not say how much facilitation came from attention and how much from automatic spreading, because relatedness and expectancy travel together: a prime that is related to the target is also a prime that makes it expected. Neely breaks them apart - `the presence or absence of AUTOMATIC SPREADING ACTIVATION was controlled by whether the prime and the target were semantically related`, while conscious attention `was controlled by variations in the PROBABILITY that the priming cue itself would appear`. Two knobs where the field had one— “First, the presence or absence of automatic spreading activation was controlled by whether the prime and the target were semantically related or semantically unrelated”
sense**AND THE CELL THAT PAYS FOR IT IS BODY-heart: A SIGN REVERSAL PRODUCED BY NOTHING BUT DELAY.** The prime is semantically related to the target, but the subject expects a different category - so automatic activation says facilitate and conscious attention says inhibit, in the same trial. `there was an INHIBITION effect at the 2,000 msec SOA, which decreased as the SOA decreased until, at the 250-msec SOA, IT BECAME A FACILITATION EFFECT.` Not a difference in size between conditions - **the same condition changing sign** as the slow mechanism is given less time to act. That is as close to a crossing double dissociation as a priming experiment gets— “On BODY-heart type trials, there was an inhibition effect at the 2,000 msec SOA, which decreased as the SOA decreased until, at the 250-msec SOA, it became a facilitation effect”
sense**THE ASYMMETRY THAT MAKES THE INFERENCE WORK, STATED AS A PROPERTY RATHER THAN A RESULT:** `Automatic spreading activation produces only facilitatory effects and no inhibitory effects.` Both mechanisms facilitate; only one inhibits. So facilitation is evidence of nothing in particular and **inhibition is evidence of attention** - which is why the whole design is built to produce inhibition and why the 250-msec SOA, where inhibition vanishes, is the control rather than a null result— “Automatic spreading activation produces only facilitatory effects and no inhibitory effects”
sense**AND IT IS THE FIFTH RECORD RUNNING WHERE THE INSTRUMENT IS PART OF THE PHENOMENON - BUT THE FIRST WHERE THE AUTHOR KNEW.** Finkbeiner2011: task difficulty decides what counts as subliminal. erb2019: associative overlap decides whether a CSE is control. eckstein2007: which awareness test decides whether the prime was seen. lukatela2007: within- vs between-subjects decides whether the lexicons look separate. Here SOA decides WHICH OF TWO MECHANISMS you are measuring - and Neely makes that the independent variable instead of discovering it afterwards. **The corpus now holds the 1977 paper that turned the artefact into the design, and the 2007 paper that found the same class of artefact still undetected in its own neighbouring literature.**— “The stimulus onset asynchrony (SOA) between the prime and the target letter string varied between 250 and 2,000 msec”
sense**AND THE MECHANISM CLAIM IS SHARPER THAN `ATTENTION SLOWS THINGS DOWN`:** `the conscious-attention mechanism DOES NOT INHIBIT THE BUILDUP OF ACTIVATION in unattended logogens but rather INHIBITS THE READOUT of information from unattended logogens.` The activation still accumulates; what attention withholds is access to it. **That is a falsifiable division of labour** - it predicts an unattended item can be primed without being reportable, which is the exact question Finkbeiner2011, eckstein2007 and hesselmann2016 are still arguing about thirty to forty years later, and none of the three cites this sentence for it— “the conscious-attention mechanism does not inhibit the buildup of activation in unattended logogens but rather inhibits the readout of information from unattended logogens”

Concepts

conceptspreading activation— “Automatic spreading activation has an effect only when the prime and the target are semantically related.”
conceptlexical decision— “The target word to which the subject was to make a lexical decision was preceded by a neutral warning signal on some trials and by either a semantically related word prime or a semantically unrelated word prime on other trials.”
conceptautomatic spreading activation — the fast component: strategy-free, needs no capacity, and **fires on semantic relatedness alone**. Its diagnostic property is what it CANNOT do - `produces only facilitatory effects and no inhibitory effects` - which is what makes inhibition a clean signature of the other mechanism— “First, the presence or absence of automatic spreading activation was controlled by whether the prime and the target were semantically related or semantically unrelated”
conceptconscious attention — the slow component: limited-capacity, expectancy-driven, and **the only source of inhibition in the theory**. Neely controls it by the PROBABILITY that the target comes from the primed category - a property of what the subject has learned to expect, not of the words— “The subject's conscious attention was controlled by variations in the probability that the priming cue itself would appear as an element in the target array”
conceptinhibition — responses SLOWER than the neutral baseline. **The theory's load-bearing asymmetry**: automatic activation cannot produce it, so its presence licenses an inference about attention that facilitation never could— “On BODY-heart type trials, there was an inhibition effect at the 2,000 msec SOA, which decreased as the SOA decreased until, at the 250-msec SOA, it became a facilitation effect”
conceptfacilitation — responses FASTER than the neutral baseline. **Both mechanisms produce it**, which is exactly why facilitation alone cannot tell you which one you are looking at - the reason the design needs inhibition and needs SOA. Its own signature is being EARLY: `the facilitation occurred sooner following the presentation of the priming cue than did the inhibition`, because the fast mechanism has no competitor at short delays— “Also, as predicted, the facilitation occurred sooner following the presentation of the priming cue than did the inhibition”
conceptlogogen — the lexical unit activation spreads to and attention is focused on. Inherited from Morton, and the level at which both mechanisms are said to act - which is what lets one theory hold a fast process and a slow one without them being about different things— “the conscious-attention mechanism produces inhibition in the retrieval of information from unattended logogens.”
conceptexpectancy — what conscious attention BUILDS from the prime - a set of semantic features the subject is prepared for. **It is the causal middle term the two-process theory needs**: attention does not act on the target directly, it generates an expectancy, and the target is then fast or slow depending on whether it matches. That is also what makes the Shift conditions possible at all— “the subject has a tendency to respond on the basis of the similarity between the semantic features of the expectancy generated, via conscious attention, to the word prime and the semantic features activated by the target letter string. If the semantic features”
