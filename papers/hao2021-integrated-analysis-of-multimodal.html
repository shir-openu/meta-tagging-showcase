phenomenoncell states that one measurement type cannot separate and another can - CD4 and CD8 T cells blend in the transcriptome and separate in protein; dendritic cells do the reverse— “Although established technologies like single-cell RNA-seq (scRNA-seq) are capable of discovering new cell types and states in heterogeneous tissues, transcriptomics alone is often incapable of separating molecularly similar, but functionally distinct, categories of immune cells.”
claim-typemethod— “Our approach consists of four broad steps, as explained in detail below: (1) Constructing independent _k_-nearest neighbor (KNN) graphs for both modalities. (2) Performing within and across-modality prediction (3) Calculating cell-specific modality weights. (4) Calculating a WNN graph.”
method — evidence— “We provide a detailed mathematical description for each component of the WNN workflow in the STAR Methods, highlighting three key steps: (1) obtaining within modality and cross-modality predictions, (2) converting these to prediction affinities, based on a cell-specific bandwidth kernel, and (3) calculating modality weights using a softmax…
object of study — evidence— “Even within the same dataset, the relative utility of each modality to define cell states may vary across individual cells.”
research question — evidence— “Here, we introduce “weighted-nearest neighbor” analysis, an unsupervised framework to learn the relative utility of each data type in each cell, enabling an integrative analysis of multiple modalities.”
contribution — evidence— “Our approach represents a broadly applicable strategy to analyze single-cell multimodal datasets and to look beyond the transcriptome toward a unified and multimodal definition of cellular identity.”
mechanismprediction accuracy as evidence of informativeness the core move: if a cell's neighbours in one modality predict its measured profile well, that modality is capturing its state; if they predict badly, it is not. The weight is the ratio of those two accuracies— “Next, we independently averaged the molecular profiles of protein neighbors and RNA neighbors (i.e., predicted the molecular contents of a cell from its neighbors), and compared the averages to their original measured values.”
mechanismadaptive kernel bandwidth why rare cells are not penalised: a fixed neighbour count for the kernel bandwidth would hold common and rare states to the same scale, so the bandwidth is set from the 20 cells with the LOWEST non-zero Jaccard similarity - similar enough to matter, unlikely to be the same state— “For each cell , we therefore aim to identify the 20 cells in the dataset with the _lowest_ non-zero Jaccard similarity. We expect that these represent cells that exhibit some similarity with cell , but are unlikely to reside in the same molecular state.”
mechanismshared-neighbour filtering how a spurious edge is told from a real one - the Jaccard metric counts shared nearest neighbours, so local density separates well-supported links from coincidental similarity— “However, it is possible to identify these spurious edges through the use of the Jaccard metric. This identifies the number of shared nearest neighbors between two cells, thereby exploiting the local density of each data point to separate well-supported from spurious edges.”
mechanismcompensation between modalities what the weighting buys: one data type is allowed to make up for another's weakness, cell by cell, so the combined analysis is not limited by whichever measurement happens to be poor for that cell— “We confirmed that our results were robust to a range of values for _k_ ( Figure S2), and the incorporation of protein information in the WNN graph does not come at the expense of identifying transcriptomically congruent neighborhoods”
distinguishes-from totalVI and MOFA+, two recently introduced multimodal integration methods based on deep neural networks with a hierarchical Bayesian model and on factor analysis respectively— “We next benchmarked WNN analysis against two recently introduced methods for multimodal integration: multi-omics factor analysis v2 (MOFA+) ( Argelaguet et al., 2020), which uses a statistical framework based on factor analysis, and totalVI ( Gayoso et al., 2019), which combines deep neural networks with a hierarchical Bayesian model.”
distinguishes-from scArches, a method for mapping single-cell RNA-seq queries onto reference datasets— “We benchmarked our method against scArches, a recently developed method for mapping scRNA-seq queries to reference datasets ( Lotfollahi et al., 2020) and observed that our approach yielded substantial improvements in accuracy and performance”
critiques the practice of defining cell states from the transcriptome alone, which cannot separate functionally distinct T cell populations however sensitive the technology— “Although established technologies like single-cell RNA-seq (scRNA-seq) are capable of discovering new cell types and states in heterogeneous tissues, transcriptomics alone is often incapable of separating molecularly similar, but functionally distinct, categories of immune cells.”
extends the unsupervised clustering analysis of Wilk et al.'s COVID-19 PBMC dataset, which found six T cell clusters where reference mapping resolved twelve— “Notably, populations of developing and differentiated neutrophils, which were identified by the original manuscript as being uniquely present in COVID-19 samples but were absent from our reference, could not be successfully mapped”
builds-on Barshan et al.'s supervised principal component analysis, applied here with the WNN graph as the supervising structure— “Instead of seeking to identify a low-dimensional projection that maximizes total variance as in PCA, sPCA identifies a projection of the transcriptome dataset that maximally captures the structure defined in the WNN graph.”
sensemodality weight - A PER-CELL NUMBER, NOT A PER-DATASET ONE, AND THE PAPER SAYS IT HAS TWO POSSIBLE MEANINGS. It is a proxy for the TECHNICAL QUALITY of a measurement in that cell, and it `may also reflect the BIOLOGICAL IMPORTANCE of each modality in determining cellular identity` - progenitors and differentiated cells came out with divergent weights. The same figure can mean `this instrument worked here` or `this axis is what this cell is`, and nothing in the number distinguishes them— “Last, we note that the modality weights learned in our procedure serve not only as a proxy for the technical quality of a measurement type, but may also reflect the biological importance of each modality in determining cellular identity.”
senseweight, as computed here - EARNED BY A PREDICTION TEST, not assigned. Each cell is predicted from its neighbours within each modality and across them, the predictions are compared with the cell's actual measurements, and the RATIO of those accuracies becomes the weight after a softmax. Non-negative, unique to each cell, summing to 1— “The RNA and protein modality weights are non-negative, unique to each cell, and sum to 1.”
senseunsupervised, of this procedure - MEANS UNAWARE OF CELL-TYPE LABELS, AND THAT IS WHAT MAKES THE AGREEMENT EVIDENCE. CD8 T cells got high protein weights and dendritic cells high RNA weights, `despite the fact that the calculation of modality weights was unsupervised and unaware of cell-type labels`. The recapitulation of a known biology is the check, and it only counts because the labels were withheld— “We observed that cells classified as CD8+ T cells were assigned higher protein modality weights, whereas DCs were assigned higher RNA modality weights, recapitulating our biological expectations despite the fact that the calculation of modality weights was unsupervised and unaware of cell-type labels”
sensespurious edge - A LINK BETWEEN CELLS THAT RESEMBLE EACH OTHER WITHOUT BEING IN THE SAME STATE, and the paper counts them rather than describing them: 944 wrong CD8/CD4 edges in the RNA graph, 12 in the protein graph, 20 in the combined one. A quality claim with an integer behind it— “Recent work has clearly demonstrated that KNN-graphs are prone to the formation of spurious edges, which represent links between cells that share some similarity molecular profiles, but are not in a matched molecular state”
senseannotation, in this atlas - THREE NESTED TIERS AND A CELL CARRIES ALL THREE. Level 1 has 8 categories, level 2 has 30, level 3 has 57. A result reported at one tier is not comparable with a result reported at another, and the granularity is a choice the reader has to make rather than one the data settles— “To assist in the interpretation of our clusters, we assign each cell three annotations with increasing granularity (level 1, 8 categories; level 2, 30 categories; level 3, 57 categories).”
sensesupervised, in `supervised principal component analysis` - SUPERVISED BY THE WNN GRAPH, NOT BY A HUMAN. sPCA finds the transcriptomic projection that best captures the structure the multimodal analysis already found, so the supervision comes from the other modality rather than from labels. `Supervised` and `unsupervised` are both used in this paper about the same pipeline, meaning different things— “Instead of seeking to identify a low-dimensional projection that maximizes total variance as in PCA, sPCA identifies a projection of the transcriptome dataset that maximally captures the structure defined in the WNN graph.”

no type for thismodality weights → cell states — A NUMBER WHOSE MEANING IS UNDECIDED, AND THE PAPER SAYS SO. The weight is a proxy for the technical quality of a measurement AND may reflect the biological importance of that modality to the cell's identity. Those are different claims about different things and the same float carries both. There is no relation type for `this measure has two candidate interpretations and we cannot separate them` - the nearest available, `operationalised-as`, would pick one and hide the other
no type for thismodalities → cell states — AN ASSUMPTION OF NON-CONFLICT, WHICH IS THE METHOD'S PREMISE AND ITS SECOND STATED LIMITATION. `WNN assumes that modalities do not define conflicting sets of cell states`, and the authors flag that the assumption may fail for morphological and functional data. Every one of the eight relation types asserts something HOLDS between two concepts; none can say `these two are assumed not to disagree, and the method has no behaviour if they do`
