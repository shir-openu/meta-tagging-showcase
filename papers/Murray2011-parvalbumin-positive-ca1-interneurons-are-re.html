The dissociation the paper reports is between memory types, but the population it silenced is defined by markers whose mapping to sub-types the supplement never asserts outright. — “Cells 1 and 2 were both PV- and SOM-positive and expressed TeLC. limitationA residual signal that would count against the manipulation's completeness is explained rather than measured: leftover VAMP2 immunoreactivity in TeLC-positive terminals 'was probably due to antibody recognition of cleavage products with reduced affinity'.
