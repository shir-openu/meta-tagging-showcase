limitationThe authors do not claim their frame is the subtlest possible racial cue; many respondents openly equate the inner city with black Americans. — “We cannot, of course, claim that the inner-city frame is the most subtle of all possible racial frames, as evidenced by our finding (see footnote 4) that many clearly equate the inner city with blacks.”
limitationThe design tests policy preferences only; extension to candidate preferences is asserted but untested. — “While our research has focused on policy preferences, we see no reason why we would not obtain the same results if we focused on candidate preferences-a topic for future analysis.”
limitationThe analysis is restricted to white respondents; the African-American subsample was dropped for lack of variance on the outcome. — “The study also included a sample (N = 579) of African-American respondents who were not used in this analysis, in part because there was virtually no variation in responses to the dependent measure among African-Americans, who overwhelmingly rejected the prison option regardless of experimental condition.”
