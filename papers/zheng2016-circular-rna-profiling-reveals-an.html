conceptcircRNAs — covalently closed RNAs from exonic back-splicing; mostly low-abundance and possibly by-products, with a minority present at levels suggesting they are made on purpose
conceptcircHIPK3 — the abundant circRNA formed from Exon2 of HIPK3, named in this paper; 1,880 supported back-spliced reads against 30, 7, 6 and 2 for the other four isoforms of that locus
conceptciRS-7 — a circRNA with more than 70 miR-7 binding sites, identified as a miRNA inhibitor; also called CDR1as
conceptCDR1as — the second name for ciRS-7, and in this paper's discussion the atypical case - not derived from pre-mRNA, containing only repeat elements
conceptSRPBM — spliced reads per billion mapping - back-spliced junction reads normalised by mapped reads in billions and by read length, so that two libraries can be compared
conceptCR — circular ratio - circular junction reads over the sum of circular and linear junction reads at one end of an exon
conceptspecificity score — 1 minus the Jensen-Shannon distance between a gene's expression profile across tissues and perfectly specific expression in one tissue
conceptTopHat2 — the aligner used for the initial mapping, chosen over Bowtie2 on an unquantified false-positive concern
conceptBowtie2 — the aligner not used, on the stated ground that it `may result in false positive rate`
conceptHIPK3 gene — the protein-coding locus whose Exon2 circularises; its linear mRNA and its circle have different half-lives and only the circle affects growth
conceptcircRNA abundance — the quantity the whole catalogue is built on - counted, not estimated, as junction reads
concepttotal number of reads spanning back-spliced junctions — the operational stand-in for how much of a circRNA is present; `an ABSOLUTE measure` in the paper's words, though it is a count of one read class
concept1−JSD — one minus the Jensen-Shannon distance between a gene's cross-tissue profile and perfectly specific expression - the formula the specificity score IS
conceptcell proliferation — the outcome circHIPK3 modulates - assayed by CCK-8 and EdU incorporation across HuH-7, HCT-116, HeLa and HEK-293T
conceptHIPK3 mRNA — the linear transcript of the same gene - half-life under 4 h against circHIPK3's over 24 h, and knocking it down does not affect cell growth
