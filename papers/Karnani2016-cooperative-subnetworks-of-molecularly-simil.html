findingIn awake mice, VIP interneurons were more strongly correlated with other VIPs than with non-VIP cells, showing within-population co-activity during ongoing activity. — “During both epochs and across 11 movies from 5 animals (5–19 VIPs and 41–137 Non-VIPs per movie), VIP pairs had higher pairwise correlation coefficients than non-VIP pairs or VIP/non-VIP pairs ( Figure 1C,D , statistics in figure legends).”
findingNearby pyramidal cells contact VIPs and SOMs at similar rates but with opposite short-term dynamics, depressing onto VIPs and facilitating onto SOMs. — “PCs formed excitatory synapses onto VIPs and SOMs at similar rates, but synapses onto VIPs were depressing, while those onto SOMs were facilitating ( Figure 2A ).”
findingTwo-photon input mapping found essentially no shared presynaptic excitatory cells between a VIP and a SOM, whereas PV pairs shared about a fifth of their inputs. — “This proportion was 22.6 ± 5.6 % for PV-PV pairs and −0.8 ± 4.9 % for VIP-SOM pairs (P = 0.015, Figure 3H ).”
findingMembrane-potential cross-correlations were high within an interneuron population and lowest between VIPs and SOMs, supporting separate local excitatory drive. — “However VIP-SOM pairs had significantly lower correlations than all other pairs (0.014 ± 0.006, n = 18, P < 0.05, distance 61.8 ± 10.1 µm), supporting the notion that VIPs and SOMs are innervated by distinct sets of PCs.”
findingForcing three SOM interneurons to fire significantly increased the spontaneous firing of a fourth, uninduced SOM, demonstrating cooperative within-population recruitment. — “Driving three SOM cells to fire was sufficient to significantly increase firing in the fourth SOM (68 ± 22 % increase in spontaneous firing, n = 13, Figure 5B ), demonstrating within-population recruitment.”
findingBlocking GABA-A and GABA-B receptors sharply reduced the SOM cooperative recruitment effect, identifying disinhibition as a main driver. — “This increase was significantly reduced to 16 ± 7 % during GABA receptor blockade (n = 7, paired t-test P = 0.007, Figure 5D,E ), indicating that disinhibition of SOMs by SOMs is an important mechanism for their cooperative recruitment.”
findingVIP cooperativity was insensitive to GABA blockade but reduced by nicotinic blockade, implicating acetylcholine released by VIPs in recruiting other VIPs. — “In contrast to GABAergic blockade, this cholinergic blockade significantly reduced the firing rate increase ( Figure 5I ) suggesting that ACh released from VIPs helps recruit other VIPs during sustained activity.”
findingSOMs never inhibited other SOMs and VIP-to-VIP inhibition was rare, removing the main obstacle to within-population co-activity. — “The lowest inhibitory connectivity rates were within SOMs (0%, in agreement with Hu et al., 2011 , Ma et al., 2012 ), within VIPs and from VIPs to PVs ( Figure S4 ).”

We demonstrate a variety of synergistic mechanisms, involving population-specific local excitation, GABAergic disinhibition and excitation through electrical coupling, which likely underlie IN population co-activity. Firing of a few SOM or VIP INs recruits additional members within the cell type via GABAergic and cholinergic mechanisms, thereby amplifying the output of the population as a whole.”
findingand the conclusion is that these populations work as cooperative units, generating an amplifying nonlinearity in circuit output — “Our data suggest that IN populations work as cooperative units, thus generating an amplifying nonlinearity in their circuit output.”
