phenomenoncardinality bounds for Hausdorff and regular spaces— “Many bounds on the cardinality of a topological space X involve the pseudocharacter cardinal function, ψ(X), or its variant, the closed pseudocharacter ψc(X).”
claim-typeexistence— “Using weaker versions of the cardinal function ψc(X), we derive a series of new bounds for the cardinality of Hausdorff spaces and regular spaces that do not involve ψc(X) nor its variants at all.”
method — evidence— “As their proofs rely on sophisticated closing-off arguments, these results are deemed “difficult” in the sense of Hodel [15], section 4.”
mechanismbounding |X| by the family of regular open sets and then bounding that family by a closing-off argument on density, so the pseudocharacter can be weakened without loss— “This is achieved by establishing that |X| ≤ |RO(X)|wψc(X), where RO(X) is the collection of regular open subsets of X, and, using a modified version of Sun’s closing-off argument, that d(X) ≤ πχ(X)c(X)dψc(X) for any Hausdorff space X.”
mechanismconstructing an injection of the space into a family of small subsets indexed by weak closed pseudobases, which transfers existing bounds from ψc(X) to wψc(X)— “Using one-to-one map arguments, we can replace ψc(X) with wψc(X) in other cardinality bounds.”
extends the cardinality bounds of Šapirovskiĭ [22] and Sun [23]— “We improve the results of ˇSapirovski˘ı and Sun by showing |X| ≤ πχ(X)c(X)wψc(X) if X is regular, and |X| ≤ πχ(X)c(X)dψc(X)wψc(X) if X is Hausdorff.”
extends Gotchev, Tkachenko and Tkachuk's bound πw(X)ot(X)ψc(X) [13]— “We show in the next result that ψc(X) can be replaced with wψc(X).”
extends the Bella-Cammaroto bound d(X)t(X)ψc(X) [1] as improved by the author in [6]— “We show in Theorem 4.17 that in fact ψc(X) can further be replaced with wψc(X).”

findingFor a quasiregular Hausdorff space the cardinality is bounded by πχ(X)^(c(X)wψc(X)), which strengthens Šapirovskiĭ's classical bound by replacing the pseudocharacter with the weak closed pseudocharacter.— “Theorem 3.9. If X is quasiregular and Hausdorff then |X| ≤ πχ(X)c(X)wψc(X).”
findingFor any Hausdorff space the cardinality is bounded by πχ(X)^(c(X)dψc(X)wψc(X)), improving Sun's bound by splitting the closed pseudocharacter into its dense and weak parts.— “Theorem 3.10. If X is Hausdorff then |X| ≤ πχ(X)c(X)dψc(X)wψc(X).”
findingEvery Hausdorff space satisfies |X| ≤ 2^(d(X)wψc(X)), a bound the author reports as new even in the stronger form with the ordinary closed pseudocharacter.— “Theorem 4.9. If X is Hausdorff then |X| ≤ 2d(X)wψc(X).”
findingThe density of an arbitrary Hausdorff space is bounded by πχ(X)^(c(X)dψc(X)), extending to Hausdorff spaces a density bound previously known only for regular or quasiregular spaces.— “Theorem 3.2. If X is Hausdorff then d(X) ≤ πχ(X)c(X)dψc(X).”
findingThe cardinality of a Hausdorff space is bounded by the number of its regular open sets raised to the weak closed pseudocharacter, which is the lemma underpinning most of the paper's new bounds.— “Proposition 3.8. If X is Hausdorff, then |X| ≤ |RO(X)|wψc(X).”
findingCompact examples with countable dψc(X)wψc(X) but arbitrarily large cardinality show the product dψc(X)wψc(X) cannot substitute for the pseudocharacter in the classical bound 2^ψ(X) for compact Hausdorff spaces.— “Compact examples show that ψ(X) cannot be replaced with dψc(X)wψc(X) in the bound 2ψ(X) for the cardinality of a compact Hausdorff space X.”
findingA quasiregular Hausdorff space whose cellularity is at most the continuum and whose π-character is countable has at most 2^c points.— “It follows from Corollary 4.6(3) that a quasiregular Hausdorff space with cellularity at most c and countable π-character has cardinality at most 2c.”
findingThe Bella–Cammaroto-type bound d(X)^(t(X)ψc(X)) can be sharpened for Hausdorff spaces to d(X)^(wt(X)wψc(X)), weakening both tightness and closed pseudocharacter simultaneously.— “Theorem 4.17. If X is Hausdorff then |X| ≤ d(X)wt(X)wψc(X).”
findingIn a homogeneous Hausdorff space the new dense closed pseudocharacter collapses to the ordinary closed pseudocharacter, so it yields no improvement there.— “Thus if X is homogeneous and Hausdorff we have dψc(X) = ψc(X).”
findingσ-compact homogeneous spaces of arbitrarily large cardinality with wψc(X) ≤ c show that 2^wψc(X) fails as a cardinality bound for that class.— “Therefore, 2wψc(X) is not a bound for the cardinality of all σ- compact homogeneous spaces.”
