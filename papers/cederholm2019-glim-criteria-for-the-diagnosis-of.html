sense`malnutrition` (GLIM) - at least ONE phenotypic criterion (weight loss, low BMI, reduced muscle mass) AND at least ONE etiologic criterion (reduced intake or assimilation, inflammation or disease burden)
sense`phenotypic criterion` - what the body shows; the only half used for severity grading into Stage 1 (moderate) and Stage 2 (severe)
sense`etiologic criterion` - why it happened; required for diagnosis, excluded from grading, and kept `to guide appropriate intervention and anticipated outcomes`
sense`at risk` - the output of step one, established `BY THE USE OF ANY VALIDATED SCREENING TOOL`; the definition deliberately does not name which
sensethe four etiology-based categories - chronic disease WITH inflammation, chronic disease with MINIMAL OR NO perceived inflammation, ACUTE disease or injury with severe inflammation, and STARVATION including hunger and food shortage
controlrequiring one criterion from EACH half rather than any N of five — held — rules out a diagnosis made on phenotype alone, which would not distinguish malnutrition from constitutional thinness, or on cause alone with no manifestation
controlexcluding the etiologic criteria from severity grading — held — rules out a severity score that moves with the diagnosis behind the malnutrition rather than with the patient's state
controlcarrying a separate BMI threshold for Asian populations instead of one global cut — partial — rules out a criterion that misclassifies whole regions because it was set elsewhere
controlkeeping cachexia, sarcopenia and frailty as separate nomenclature to be used in parallel — held — rules out a broad new category absorbing three established ones and losing what each was for
