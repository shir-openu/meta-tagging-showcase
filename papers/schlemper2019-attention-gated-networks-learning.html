mechanisma gate initialised to do nothing the gating parameters start so that every feature vector passes at every location - the network begins as its own baseline and has to learn to suppress, which is why no multi-stage training schedule is needed and why any difference is attributable to what the gate learned rather than to where it started— “Gating parameters are initialised so that attention gates pass through feature vectors at all spatial locations. Moreover, we do not require multiple training stages as in hard-attention based approaches therefore simplifying the training procedure.”
mechanismcoarse context deciding fine attention the gating signal is taken from a coarser grid, where the receptive field is large enough to know where the organ is, and applied to a finer map, where the resolution is enough to draw its boundary - which is the same division of labour a cascade makes between its two networks, done inside one— “We commonly observe that AGs initially have a uniform distribution and pass features at all spatial locations.
