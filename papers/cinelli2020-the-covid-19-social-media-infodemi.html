mechanismvolume and reaction come apart Gab posts about 70% as much questionable as reliable content and reacts to it about three times more. Counting posts and counting reactions therefore rank the platforms differently, and the amplification factor exists precisely to separate them— “Among less regulated social media, a peculiar effect is observed in Gab: while the volume of posts from questionable sources is just the ∼ 70% of the volume of posts from reliable ones, the volume of reactions for the former ones is ∼ 3 times bigger than the volume for the latter ones.”
