phenomenoncortical visual pathways for perception and action
claim-typemechanism
mechanismDifferent output requirements (perceptual identification vs online sensorimotor transformation) demand different visual computations, and this drives the anatomical segregation of the two streams. — “These differences are largely a reflection of the specific transformations of input required by perception and action.”
mechanismDorsal-stream processing proceeds without reaching consciousness, which keeps moment-to-moment viewer-centred information from disrupting the perceptual constancies computed in the ventral stream. — “It may be that information can be processed in the dorsal system without reaching consciousness, and that this prevents interference with the perceptual constancies intrinsic to many operations within the ventral system that do result in awareness.”
mechanismSeparate processing modules evolved because vision is put to functionally different uses. — “It seems plausible from a functional standpoint that separate processing modules would have evolved to mediate the different uses to which vision can be put.”
relation — “In this article, it is proposed that this distinction ('what' versus 'how') - rather than the distinction between object vision and spatial vision ('what' versus 'where') - captures more appropriately the functional dichotomy between the ventral and dorsal projections.”
replicationuntested
limitationThe proposed division of labour between the two streams is not claimed to be absolute. — “We do not, however, wish to claim that the division of labour we are proposing is an absolute one.”
limitationCorrelating human neuropsychological findings with monkey neurophysiology requires caution. — “While any correlations between human neuropsychology and monkey neurophysiology should only be made with caution, it is likely that humans share many features of visual processing with our primate relatives - particularly with the Old World monkeys in which most of the electrophysiology has been carried out.”
limitationThe evidence base lacks detailed analyses of how task-specific most visuospatial disorders are. — “Unfortunately, we lack detailed analyses of the possible specificity of most such disorders: in many, the deficit may be restricted to particular behavioural tasks.”
limitationThe paper replaces the principle by which the same two pathways are divided. — “proposed that this distinction ('what' versus 'how') - rather than the distinction between object vision and spatial vision ('what' versus 'where') - captures more appropriately the functional dichotomy between the ventral and dorsal projections”
limitationAn earlier term is criticised by name for grouping behaviours it could not separate. — “However, the notion of 'localization' failed to distinguish between the many different patterns of behaviour that vary with the spatial location of visual stimuli, only some of which turn out to rely on tectal mechanisms”

content-observations: finding · quantity · variable · definition · key-term — grounded in the full text
findingThe visual-form agnosic patient DF scaled her grip aperture to object width normally when reaching to grasp, despite being unable to perceive that width. — “However, when she was asked simply to reach out and pick up the block, the aperture between her index finger and thumb changed systematically with the width of the object, just as in normal subjects (Fig. 2B).”
findingThe same patient's manual estimates of object width bore no relation to actual object size and were highly variable trial to trial. — “When she was asked to indicate the width of a single block by means of her index finger and thumb, her matches bore no relationship to the dimensions of the object and showed considerable trial to trial variability (Fig. 2A).”
findingThe dorsal/ventral division cannot be reduced to the magnocellular/parvocellular retinal division, because both streams receive input from both pathways. — “In short, it now appears that the dorsal and the ventral streams each receive inputs from both the magno and the parvo pathways.”
findingSpatial attention is not the exclusive property of the dorsal stream; attentional modulation occurs in ventral-stream areas as well. — “We conclude that spatial attention is physiologically non-unitary 55, and may be as much associated with the ventral system as with the dorsal.”
findingThe authors conclude that cortical visual modularity is organised by output requirements all the way from input to motor output, not only by input categories. — “Functional modularity in cortical visual systems, we believe, extends from input right through to output.”
findingParietal damage produces a broad visuomotor disorder (grasp shaping and hand orientation), not merely a disorder of spatial localization. — “Despite her profound inability to recognize the size, shape and orientation of visual objects, DF showed strikingly accurate guidance of hand and finger movements directed at the very same objects”
