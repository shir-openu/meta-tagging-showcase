The context: Dynamic, multidimensional, real-life healthcare settings Student nursesterm·key need clinical experiences from various healthcare settings that offer opportunities for successful learning. Such a learning environment needs to be stimulating, self-motivating and supportive with a diverse range of healthcare professionals, real-life patients and a complex psychosocial setting rich in learning resources (Van Rossem et al. 2019 ).
