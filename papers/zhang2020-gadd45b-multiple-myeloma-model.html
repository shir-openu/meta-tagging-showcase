Discussion The proposed model in this paper aims to investigate how the interaction between NF-κBterm·dom and JNKterm·dom signalling via the GADD45βterm·dom|MKK7term·dom complex promotes the development of MMterm·dom. The model can not only simulate the temporal variation of bone cells, the bone volumeterm·dom and biochemical factors involved in the NF-κBterm·dom and JNKterm·dom pathways, but it can also mimic the underlying mechanism in which the coupling between the two pathways inhibits apoptosisterm·key in MMterm·dom cells. Additionally, the efficacies of GADD45β- and NF-κB-targeted therapies were compared based on the model simulation. This work only demonstrates the temporal variation in the GADD45βterm·dom concentration after the activation of the NF-κBterm·dom pathway. The variations in other biochemical factors related to NF-κBterm·dom signalling were not repeated here as they have been analysed in [ 25 ] but are included in electronic supplementary material, Appendix C.

As mentioned above, JNKterm·dom_PP plays an essential role in the apoptosisterm·key of MMterm·dom cells, and JNKterm·dom_PP is able to inhibit the growth of MMterm·dom cells. According to figure 8 , the decrease in MMterm·dom cells can be explained by the rising amount of JNKterm·dom_PP after the inhibition of GADD45βterm·dom binding to MKK7term·dom. NF-κB-targeted therapies are most often performed by inhibiting IKKterm·dom, which is essential for NF-κBterm·dom activation [ 8 ]. Based on model simulations ( figure 10 ), the GADD45β-targeted therapy is suggested to be more sensitive than the NF-κB-targeted therapy as it can produce a greater suppression of MMterm·dom cells than IKKterm·dom inhibition when each are suppressed to the same degree. In addition to the higher sensitivity, GADD45β-targeted therapy has a high MMterm·dom cell specificity. This approach can not only kill MMterm·dom cells effectively, but it also has no effect on NF-κBterm·dom levels. Therefore, it can be predicted that the side effects seen with NF-κB-targeted treatment would be avoided. However, these in silico hypotheses generated from the mathematical model described in this paper will ultimately have to be validated by further experimental data to confirm the clinical potential of GADD45β-targeting therapies. It should also be noted that the proposed simplified model does not take into account the interactions with several other proteins and other cell structures, which represents a limitation of this study. Specifically, although the differentiation of progenitors into active osteoclasts and osteoblasts contains several intermediate stages, the model only considered four osteoblastic and three osteoclastic lineages, and contained three state variables: osteoblast precursors, active osteoblasts and active osteoclasts. Additionally, IκBs, including IκBα, IκBβ, IκBγ and IκBε, can also bind to NF-κBterm·dom and form a complex but we only considered IκBα.

Conclusion This paper describes a mathematical model of MMterm·dom that was used to investigate how NF-κBterm·dom signalling acts to include the interplay between NF-κBterm·dom and JNKterm·dom signalling that was not included in the previous models. The model not only reconstructs how the invasion of MMterm·dom cells disturbs the steady state of the bone microenvironment and triggers the variation in bone cells, but it also mimics the changes in biochemical factors involved in the NF-κBterm·dom and JNKterm·dom pathways. In addition, the model also investigates the efficacies of GADD45β- and NF-κB-targeted treatments, suggesting that GADD45β-targeted therapy is more effective but has no apparent side effects. The simulation results match the experimental observations. This model helps to illuminate the essential function of the crosstalk between the NF-κBterm·dom and JNKterm·dom pathways during MMterm·dom development. It is anticipated that this model could be employed as a useful tool to initially investigate and even explore potential therapies involving the NF-κBterm·dom and JNKterm·dom pathways. More work is required to improve the built model to take into account many others parameters such as the interactions with other protein partners, with membranes and other cell structures.
