transferfrom communications and radar detection theory: signal detection theory, carried into psychology to quantify discrimination between two stimulus types - and it brought its vocabulary with it, so psychological stimuli are still called `signal` and `noise`— “Psychologists first applied the theory in studies of perception, where subjects discrimi nated between signals (stimuli) and noise (no stimuli).”
mechanismSignal detection theory is underused because textbooks describe the theory but omit the computational methods needed to implement it.— “One possible reason for this apparent underutilization of SOT is that relevant textbooks rarely describe the methods needed to implement the theory.”
mechanismMoving the criterion shifts hit and false-alarm rates together, so a liberal criterion inflates both; only reducing the overlap of the signal and noise distributions can raise hits while lowering false alarms.— “The only way to increase the hit rate while reducing the false-alarm rate is to reduce the overlap between the signal and the noise distributions.”
mechanismOrdinary least squares yields biased ROC slope and intercept estimates because it assumes only one variable carries error, while both rate z scores are measured with sampling error.— “In rating tasks, the empirically determined hit and false-alarm rates both contain sampling error, so OLS provides biased estimates of the slope and intercept.”
