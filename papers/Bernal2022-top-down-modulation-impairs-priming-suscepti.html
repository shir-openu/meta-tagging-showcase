phenomenonpriming of complex decision-making— “The aim of the present research is to evaluate the priming susceptibility on decisions.”
claim-typemechanism— “Our results suggest complex decision-making is susceptible to priming, depending on top-down modulation.”
method — evidence— “To address these issues from a political perspective, online experiments were conducted.”
transferfrom natural language processing / computational linguistics: target-level sentiment analysis (assigning a polarity label to a text-target pair), plus a purpose-built annotated Spanish headline dataset, imported into a cognitive/social neuroscience study to quantify the emotional valence attached to each candidate in the press— “This problem is usually called target-level sentiment analysis, in which a polarity label is assigned to a pair (text, target), where the target is present on the text.”
mechanismInstructing that the choice matters recruits deliberate Type-2 processing in place of the automatic Type-1 processing that priming acts on, so susceptibility to priming is lost— “From the DPT perspective, the DM process involved in the UST could be well explained by Type-1 or automatic processes, while in IMT, the instructions could induce Type-2 processes 19 .”
mechanismSaturation: with 2000 ms exposures every face passes the priming threshold before testing, so repetition no longer differentiates the options— “so that in this condition all four categories’ faces reach the threshold equally before the testing phase (saturation hypothesis).”
mechanismOverlapping: with a long acquisition phase the choice process begins during acquisition, so reflective processing overlaps the priming window— “A second hypothesis is that, since the acquisition phase is so long, choice processes are already taking place in this phase.”
mechanismAt the social scale, repeated media exposure builds familiarity while positively valenced coverage builds trust, and both then raise the probability of voting for a candidate— “We hypothesised that greater exposure to the image or information about the candidate favours greater familiarity, just as greater association with positive emotional valence would favour greater trust.”
mechanismEmotion-triggered neuromodulation during memory consolidation makes emotional priming stronger and longer-lasting than repetition priming— “During consolidation, neuromodulatory systems (mediated by hormones and neuropeptides), triggered by emotional experiences, can modulate the strength of the memory; that is, how well the memory would be evoked 79 .”
builds-on Dual Process Theories of reasoning and decision-making— “In order to analyse the behavioural differences in both experimental groups, Dual Process Theories (DPT) provide a relevant theoretical framework.”
builds-on Endogenous Modulation of Memory Consolidation Theory— “Although our experimental design does not allow us to assess whether priming enters into consolidation, the Endogenous Modulation of Memory Consolidation Theory provides a theoretical framework for interpreting our results.”
senseTHE TITLE SAYS `IMPAIRS` AND THE RESULT IS A REVERSAL. `Participants in the UST group chose the faces that were shown 12 times significantly more, WHILE THOSE IN THE IMT GROUP WERE MORE LIKELY TO CHOOSE FACES PRESENTED ONCE.` Told the choice is unspecified, people pick what they have seen most; told it matters, they pick what they have seen LEAST. An instruction that inverts a preference is a stronger finding than one that removes it, and the abstract's verb understates the result— “Participants in the UST group chose the faces that were shown 12 times significantly more, while those in the IMT group were more likely to choose faces presented once (Fig. 1 C). Response Time (RT), analysed by Generalised Linear Models, in the experimental trials was significantly lower in the UST group”
senseONE INSTRUCTION, TWO PRIMING TYPES, ONLY ONE MOVES. The design crosses repetition priming (mere frequency of exposure) with emotional priming (valenced phrases attached to faces), and the importance instruction reverses the repetition effect while `positive faces were equally chosen by both groups`. So top-down control does not turn priming down as a whole - it reaches the frequency-based effect and not the affective one— “Our working hypothesis is that mere repetition of an option (repetition priming) or its emotional valence (emotional priming) would prime the choice preference, but this susceptibility to priming could be impaired by the task for which it is chosen”
sense`priming susceptibility` as a construct in its own right - the paper measures not the effect but the OPENNESS to it, which is what allows an instruction to be the independent variable and priming to be the dependent one. Most records in this corpus put priming on the causal side; here it is the thing being caused to happen or not— “The aim of the present research is to evaluate the priming susceptibility on decisions. Our working hypothesis is that mere repetition of an option (repetition priming) or its emotional valence (emotional priming) would prime the choice preference, but this susceptibility to priming could be impaired by the task for which it is chosen…
senseTHE SECOND STUDY IS A NATIONAL ELECTION. 3,673 surveys during the 2019 Argentine presidential campaign plus a count of news mentions: familiarity and trust explain voting probability and correlate with how often each candidate appeared in the press and with the actual result. The laboratory's repetition variable becomes MEDIA FREQUENCY, and the choice becomes a vote - an ecological check that most priming records in this corpus do not attempt— “The familiarity and trust to each candidate explained the voting-probability for most of them, as well as correlated with their frequency of mentions in the news, their positive associations, and election results”
controlthe order of Experiment #1 and Experiment #2, and the position of the Control Trial within each, randomised separately for every participant — held — rules out an order effect - that the priming result depends on where in the session the control trial happened to fall— “The order in which the Experiment #1 and Experiment #2 appear, as well as the Control Trial position (Exp#1) and the Control Trial #1 or #2 (Exp#2), were all randomised for each participant.”

content-observations: finding · quantity · variable · definition · key-term — grounded in the full text
findingRepetition priming shifted choice only when the task was unspecified: the unspecified-task group picked the most-repeated face, while the important-task group tended to pick the once-shown faces.— “Participants in the UST group chose the faces that were shown 12 times significantly more, while those in the IMT group were more likely to choose faces presented once”
findingThe repetition-priming group difference survived even when faces were presented subliminally (20 ms).
findingWith long (2000 ms) presentations the group difference in choice preference vanished, although the response-time difference remained.
findingEmotional semantic priming was not blocked by the task instruction: both groups preferred the face paired with positive phrases.
findingThe authors read this as emotional valence priming the decision independently of the nature of the task.
findingUnspecified-task participants also decided faster than important-task participants, in both experimental and control trials.
findingTwenty-four hours later the repetition-priming effect had disappeared, with no group or frequency differences remaining.
findingEmotional priming still produced a significant preference on day 2, though flattened relative to day 1.
findingIn the electoral survey study, trust and familiarity were the variables that best accounted for stated voting probability per candidate.
findingActual vote share correlated significantly with stated voting probability (and with familiarity and campaign expenses).

conceptrepetition priming — here it is MERE FREQUENCY of exposure to a face, and it is the effect that the importance instruction reverses - the only one of the two priming types that moved at all
conceptemotional priming — the other arm - faces paired with phrases of different emotional valence - and the one that did NOT move: positive faces were chosen equally by both groups, so the instruction damps one kind of priming and not the other
conceptpriming susceptibility — the paper's own noun for the thing being modulated - not the effect but the OPENNESS to it, which is what lets an instruction be the independent variable
concepttop-down — and here it is remarkably cheap to induce: not attention, not training, but being told the choice is for an IMPORTANT task rather than an unspecified one
conceptfamiliarity — the field version of the laboratory's repetition variable - measured for real candidates in a real election, and found to explain voting probability and to track how often each candidate was mentioned in the news
conceptvoting-probability — the field study's outcome, self-reported across two survey periods during a live campaign - and the quantity that familiarity and trust are found to explain
conceptcomplex decision-making — the paper's frame, and it is chosen to make the laboratory task and the election commensurable - a choice with social consequences rather than a preference judgment
