findingFigurative language resists computational analysis because of how figurative expressions are structured, not because of the size of the text. — “The analysis of figurative language, rather than literal language, poses certaindifficulties due to the very structure of figurative expressions.”
findingThe method combines two techniques rather than one: topic classification to label the figures, network analysis to relate them. — “In this article I suggesta computational approach for the analysis of figurative language, which employs two main methods: topic classification and network analysis.”
findingFigurative analysis is not confined to literary criticism; it matters wherever the object of study is text. — “The analysis of figurative language plays a major role in literary criticism and, to varying degrees, in otherfields of the humanities and social sciences whose orientation is textual.”
