mechanismVision acts as a teaching signal that corrects the auditory spatial map because visual location estimates are far more reliable (lower response variance) than auditory ones, producing self-supervised learning without external feedback— “It seems that vision provided a teaching signal for auditory recalibration because the visual estimates were much more reliable (much lower variance in responses) ( Burge et al., 2010 ).”
mechanismPerceived unity of the two signals plus the size of the discrepancy jointly gate recalibration, so a sizable map shift happens only when the conflict is likely to reflect systematic error rather than noise, which keeps the perceptual system stable— “Therefore, the dependency of recalibration on the perceived unity of the signals as well as its dependence on the degree of discrepancy together suggests that a sizable shift in the auditory map does not occur all the time and is selective to situations wherein the discrepancy likely reflects a systematic error in one of the modalities.”
