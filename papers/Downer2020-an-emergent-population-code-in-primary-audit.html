phenomenonfeature-selective auditory attention— “In order to illuminate how A1 supports flexible perception in rich acoustic environments, we recorded from A1 neurons while rhesus macaques performed a feature-selective attention task.”
claim-typemechanism— “Rather, these results suggest that sound-encoding in A1 relies on synergies among neurons, and these synergies support the neural code for selective listening.”
method — evidence— “We recorded from 92 A1 neurons of two adult rhesus macaques, one female (W, 8 kg) and one male (U, 12 kg).”
transferfrom psychophysics: per-subject psychophysical detection thresholds measured beforehand and used to set the stimulus values for single-unit recording, so neural responses are read against each animal's own perceptual scale— “The precise AM and ∆BW values used during recording sessions were tailored to each subject’s psychophysical thresholds for detection of each sound feature (AM and ∆BW) in isolation, determined prior to recording.”
mechanismBecause feature tuning co-varies within single neurons, a gain applied to individual cells cannot separate the two features; only re-weighting neurons at the population level recovers a feature-specific signal.— “In other words, A1 neurons rarely uniquely encode one sound feature or the other because feature tuning co-varies at the single-neuron level (similar to Fig. 3e-h). This prevents single-neuron tuning from providing a robust basis for attentional perceptual enhancement.”
mechanismMixed selectivity cancels the average effect of feature-specific gain, which is why single-neuron measures show nothing while the population does.— “Thus, when single neurons exhibit mixed selectivity in their tuning to different sensory features, FSG as a mechanism of attention exhibits no average attentional enhancement (Fig. 3g,h).”
mechanismThe attentional benefit is carried by higher-order correlations among neurons, i.e. it is an emergent property of the ensemble rather than a sum of single-cell effects.— “This suggests that our observed population enhancement of sound feature encoding constitutes an emergent effect, relying on higher-order correlations among A1 neurons.”
mechanismPopulation coding unfolds in stages over the stimulus: a fast feature-general change detection followed by finer feature discrimination.— “These temporal dynamics suggest that early population responses function to detect a rapid change, irrespective of feature, whereas sustained responses elicit finer-grained feature selectivity.”
contradicts Niwa et al. (2012b), Schwartz and David (2017) and von Trapp et al. (2016), which reported attention-related changes in A1 single-neuron tuning— “Therefore, when comparing feature tuning across the more rigorous demands of different feature attention conditions, we do not observe tuning changes that have been found in several prior studies comparing A1 tuning between passive vs. active conditions and between attend-toward vs. attend-away conditions (Niwa et al., 2012b; Schwartz and…
builds-on Mante et al. (2013) and Rigotti et al. (2013) population-level analyses of prefrontal cortex— “This prompted us to conduct a population-level analysis, inspired by studies performed in PFC (Mante et al., 2013; Rigotti et al., 2013), to make sense of the heterogeneous single-neuron representations.”
sensesensory representation - AT THE SINGLE-NEURON LEVEL: not enhanced by attention in these data, which is the paper's own negative result— “attention does not enhance the single-neuron representation of attended features in our data.”
sensesensory representation - AT THE POPULATION LEVEL: enhanced, tracking behaviour, and described as an emergent code NOT MEASURABLE at the single-neuron level— “These results suggest that an emergent population code not measurable at the single-neuron level might constitute the functional unit of sensory representation in PSC.”

for which uselimitation — the unit count in this study: 58 single units held for only two blocks are included, and the authors report that removing them does not qualitatively change the results— “Removing SUs with only 2 experimental blocks of isolation (n = 58) from analysis does not qualitatively affect our results nor main conclusions and interpretations.”
replicationuntested
limitationThe analysis uses only firing rate, not spike timing.— “An important consideration of the present report is our exclusive use of firing rate, as opposed to spike-timing measures of neural activity.”
limitationBecause many bandwidth targets were also amplitude-modulated, phase locking could signal a target in both attention conditions, so the task may not have been demanding enough to reveal attentional effects on spike timing.— “It is important to note that the design of our task is such that, even when attending to the spectral feature, many target sounds are temporally modulated as well, and therefore phase-locking can signal the presence of a target in both attention conditions.”
limitationNeurons were pooled across sessions and trials resampled, so real correlated and intrinsic variability had to be reintroduced by modelling rather than measured simultaneously.— “An important consideration with this approach is that, by combining neurons across experimental sessions, and by randomly re-sampling trials, we fail to approximate correlated and intrinsic variability between and within neurons, respectively.”
limitationThe task may be too easy to force attention-related changes in A1 temporal dynamics.— “Thus, the present task may not sufficiently challenge the auditory system to drive attention-related changes in the temporal dynamics of A1 neurons’ spiking.”
limitationThe two levels of analysis disagree on the same data: attention does not enhance the single-neuron representation, while the population analysis shows enhanced encoding.— “attention does not enhance the single-neuron representation of attended features in our data. However, a population-level analysis reveals that ensembles of neurons exhibit enhanced encoding of attended sound features, and this population code tracks subjects’ performance.”
limitationThe physical stimulus differs between animals, because the values were tailored to each subject's own psychophysical thresholds rather than fixed.— “The precise AM and ∆BW values used during recording sessions were tailored to each subject’s psychophysical thresholds for detection of each sound feature (AM and ∆BW) in isolation, determined prior to recording.”
limitationThe condition labels are defined by a sensitivity criterion, not by a physical quantity: 'near threshold' means the level at which d-prime equals 1.— “Three values of each feature were used in recording sessions: one near psychophysical threshold (defined as the modulation level at which the subject’s sensitivity, measured using d’ (Wickens, 2002) was equal to 1), one slightly above threshold (d’ ~ 1.2), and one well above threshold (d’> 1.5).”
limitationAn inclusion decision was made and checked, and the check is reported rather than the criterion being justified in advance.— “Removing SUs with only 2 experimental blocks of isolation (n = 58) from analysis does not qualitatively affect our results nor main conclusions and interpretations.”

findingIndividual A1 neurons carried mixed selectivity, encoding both the spectral and temporal sound features and the attention condition.— “We find that single neurons tend to be highdimensional, in that they exhibit substantial mixed selectivity for both sound features, as well as task context.”

findingSingle-neuron feature sensitivity measured by ROC area was statistically identical across the two attention conditions.— “Though we do observe some scatter around the unity line, neurons increase or decrease their AM ROC area with equal likelihood, (sign rank test, p = 0.74).”
