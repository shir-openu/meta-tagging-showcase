mechanismTHE FIELD'S PROBLEMS ARE DIVIDED BY THE METHOD THAT ADDRESSES THEM, NOT BY SUBJECT MATTER: the easy problems are DEFINED as those directly susceptible to the standard methods of cognitive science - explanation in terms of computational or neural mechanisms - and the hard ones as those that seem to RESIST those methods. The dividing principle is tractability by an instrument— “The easy problems of consciousness are those that seem directly susceptible to the standard methods of cognitive science, whereby a phenomenon is explained in terms of computational or neural mechanisms. The hard problems are those that seem to resist those methods.”

Each of these phenomena needs to be explained, but some are easier to explain than others.”

mechanismTHE METHODS ARE SAID TO BE FITTED TO A CLASS OF PROBLEM BY DESIGN, WHICH IS WHY A RESIDUE REMAINS: the usual methods were developed PRECISELY to explain the performance of cognitive functions and do a good job of it - and as they stand they are ONLY equipped for that, so on the remaining problem the standard approach HAS NOTHING TO SAY. The gap is attributed to the instrument's design, not to insufficient effort— “These methods have been developed precisely to explain the performance of cognitive functions, and they do a good job of it. But as these methods stand, they are only equipped to explain the performance of functions. When it comes to the hard problem, the standard approach has nothing to say.”

mechanismELIMINATION BY REDEFINITION IS NAMED AS A MOVE: some deny the phenomenon outright, and OTHERS ACHIEVE THE SAME EFFECT by allowing that experience exists ONLY IF the word is equated with something like the capacity to discriminate and report. The denial is performed by the definition rather than by an argument— “Some explicitly deny the phenomenon, holding for example that what is not externally verifiable cannot be real. Others achieve the same effect by allowing that experience exists, but only if we equate “experience” with something like the capacity to discriminate and report.”
mechanismA CRITERION IS GIVEN FOR WHEN A TERM MAY LEGITIMATELY BE RETIRED, AND IT TURNS ON THE TERM'S ROLE RATHER THAN ITS TRUTH: the vital spirit was put forward AS AN EXPLANATORY POSIT, to explain certain functions, AND COULD THEREFORE BE DISCARDED once those functions were explained without it - whereas an EXPLANANDUM IN ITS OWN RIGHT is not a candidate for that kind of elimination— “The vital spirit was put forward as an explanatory posit, in order to explain the relevant functions, and could therefore be discarded when those functions were explained without it. Experience is not an explanatory posit but an explanandum in its own right, and so is not a candidate for this sort of elimination.”
mechanismTHE OBVIOUS PRECEDENT IS RAISED AND THE DISANALOGY IS LOCATED PRECISELY IN WHAT WAS EVER IN DOUBT: vitalist scepticism was doubt about whether physical mechanisms COULD PERFORM THE FUNCTIONS - while the conceptual claim that explanation of functions IS WHAT IS NEEDED was IMPLICITLY ACCEPTED by both sides. The historical parallel is disarmed by separating the empirical bet from the conceptual premise it never touched— “What drove vitalist skepticism was doubt about whether physical mechanisms could perform the many remarkable functions associated with life, such as complex adaptive behavior and reproduction. The conceptual claim that explanation of functions is what is needed was implicitly accepted”
mechanismA FALLACY ABOUT HOW TWO TERMS COME TO BE LINKED IS NAMED AND GIVEN A LAW: the attractiveness of quantum theories of consciousness is attributed to a LAW OF MINIMIZATION OF MYSTERY - consciousness is mysterious and quantum mechanics is mysterious, SO MAYBE THE TWO MYSTERIES HAVE A COMMON SOURCE. The proposed connection is between the two terms' obscurity, not between their contents— “The attractiveness of quantum theories of consciousness may stem from a Law of Minimization of Mystery: consciousness is mysterious and quantum mechanics is mysterious, so maybe the two mysteries have a common source.”
mechanismA CLASS OF THEORY IS SHOWN TO RUN THE EXPLANATION BACKWARDS: these theories ASSUME THE EXISTENCE of consciousness and USE IT IN THE EXPLANATION of quantum processes. The term appears in the explanans where it was wanted in the explanandum— “Rather, these theories assume the existence of consciousness, and use it in the explanation of quantum processes.”
