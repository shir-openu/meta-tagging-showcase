phenomenonmotivational goal-priming of voluntary force exertion— “Here, we tested whether motivational priming can increase motor output even if the priming is fully visible (supraliminal), and whether the priming effect occurs through increased cortical excitability.”
claim-typeexistence— “Analysis showed that for both the supraliminal and subliminal conditions, reaction time decreased and total force, MEP magnitude, and pupil dilation increased.”
method — evidence— “The priming effect was measured behaviourally by handgrip force and reaction time to the grip cue after the priming was complete.”
mechanisma motor-goal representation becomes associated with positive affect, and that association acts as a reward signal that induces unconscious motivation for the goal whether or not the goal is consciously seen— “Therefore, based on the proposed mechanism for unconscious goal pursuit 24 , an association of a motor-goal representation with positive affect can similarly arise in the background of consciousness and act as a reward signal, inducing unconscious motivation for the motor goal, regardless of whether the goal is consciously perceived or…
mechanismpotentiated dopaminergic activity drives the pupil-linked noradrenergic system, which raises the gain of cortical neuronal interactions and thereby facilitates goal pursuit outside awareness— “Therefore, potentiated dopaminergic activity might enhance activity within the pupil-linked noradrenergic system, which in turn might boost the gain of neuronal interactions in the cortex”
mechanismtwo coactivated processes produce unconscious goal pursuit: ideomotor preparation of the action and subcortical detection of positive reward signals that recruits effort— “In the first, action is prepared at the perceptual, sensory, and motor levels, based on an ideomotor principle. In the second, positive reward signals are detected and processed in subcortical regions such as the basal ganglia in the limbic system, allowing effort for action to be recruited”
mechanismin the visible-prime group the goal representation is activated without an act of conscious will, so the performance gain comes from unconscious goal activation rather than from consciously adopting the goal— “the goal-priming effect on task performance might have resulted from unconscious activation of the goal to perform well rather than the conscious perception of the goal”
extends the authors' earlier study using the same affective-motivational priming paradigm (reference 23)— “The current study substantially follows the earlier study with the same paradigm of the affective-motivational priming and conceptual analysis as those in the present study, in which physical exerting verbs have been shown to acts as motor goals”
replicates the authors' previous study linking the affective motivational effect to the dopaminergic and pupil-linked neuromodulatory systems (reference 5)— “This is consistent with our previous study, which showed how the affective motivational effect on the motor system is related to both the reward-associated dopaminergic system and the pupil-linked neuromodulatory system”
sensePRIMING MEASURED IN FORCE. The dependent variable is how hard somebody squeezes a handgrip, with pupil dilation and motor-evoked potentials alongside it. Nearly every other record in this corpus measures priming as speed, accuracy, choice or judgement; this one measures a physical output and a physiological state, which means the effect has to survive translation into the motor system rather than staying in a decision— “The priming effect was measured behaviourally by handgrip force and reaction time to the grip cue after the priming was complete. Physiologically, the effects were measured by pupil dilation and motor-evoked potentials”
senseBOTH CONDITIONS WORK - AND THE PAPER REFUSES THE EQUIVALENCE READING, WHICH IS THE ROW THAT MATTERS. `Therefore, it is clear that both subliminal and supraliminal goal-priming with positive words INCREASED THE VARIABLES, while WE SHOULD BE CAUTIOUS ABOUT THE INTERPRETATION THAT THE DEGREE OF INCREASE WAS IDENTICAL between the two conditions.` The effect survives visibility; the SAMENESS of the effect is explicitly not claimed, and the discussion offers the opposite possibility too - that supraliminal presentation `might cause greater enhancement`. A paper declining an equivalence its own abstract could have been read as asserting— “Therefore, it is clear that both subliminal and supraliminal goal-priming with positive words increased the variables, while we should be cautious about the interpretation that the degree of increase was identical between the two conditions”
senseTHE AWARENESS CHECK IS THE OBJECTIVE KIND, AND IT CARRIES ITS NUMBER: `Response accuracy was 50.15% +/- 6.23%, indicating that their judgements did not differ from chance, and that they could not see the priming words.` This is the zero-sensitivity criterion that Schmidt2011 argues should be retired because it presupposes an exhaustive awareness measure (Q-756), and the design Van-den-Bussche2013 calls confounded because masking changes strength as well as visibility (Q-753). The corpus now holds the criterion, its critique and a study using it— “Response accuracy was 50.15% ± 6.23%, indicating that their judgements did not differ from chance, and that they could not see the priming words”
senseAND ONE ASYMMETRY SURVIVES THE EQUIVALENCE: the supraliminal participants `did not experience any SUBJECTIVE DIFFERENCE IN EFFORT immediately after the motor action AS PARTICIPANTS IN THE SUBLIMINAL GROUP DID`. Force, reaction time, MEPs and pupil dilation all move alike across the awareness boundary - and the felt effort does not. findingNeither the behavioural nor the physiological priming effects differed between the visible-prime and invisible-prime groups, so conscious perception of the goal appears unnecessary for the motivational effect.
findingPriming with exertion words paired with reward words raised grip force and MEP amplitude and shortened reaction time in both groups.
findingMotor-evoked potentials showed that the exertion-word priming excited the corticospinal pathway in both the supraliminal and the subliminal group.
findingPupil size increased during priming in the vertical and in the combined horizontal-plus-vertical measures in both groups.
findingSkin potential level was unchanged, so the effects cannot be attributed to a rise in general arousal.
findingA separate detection experiment showed the 33 ms primes were at chance-level visibility, confirming the subliminal manipulation.
findingBayesian model comparison favoured models without a Condition-by-Group interaction, but only at the anecdotal level of evidence, so equality of the two priming routes should not be overclaimed.
findingSupraliminal participants noticed the exertion words but none grasped the affective-shaping link between primes and reward words or its connection to their later performance.
findingThe authors conclude that awareness of the primed behavioural goal is not a necessary condition for goal pursuit to recruit physical resources.

Concepts
terms this paper carries, each with the sentence it was cut from — the layer that joins one paper to another. Terms with no quote yet are on CONCEPTS.html.
conceptgoal-priming — the manipulation - words related to effort, paired with positively reinforcing words - and the paper's question is whether it needs to be invisible to work
conceptsupraliminal — fully visible priming, and the condition this study adds - the previous literature had shown the effect only when the prime could not be seen
conceptforce — the dependent variable, and it is a PHYSICAL QUANTITY - how hard the participant squeezes, rather than how fast they judge or which option they pick. Almost nothing else in this corpus measures priming this way. The prior result it builds on is quantified: a 7% increase in maximum voluntary hand-grip contraction
conceptmotor output — the general term the mechanism question is posed in - what the priming is supposed to increase, before it is pinned to a particular grip or potential
conceptpupil dilation — the physiological index, used as a proxy for the noradrenergic system - and the paper is explicit that it is INDIRECT: dilation has been used extensively as an indirect measure of locus coeruleus activity
conceptcortical excitability — the proposed route from prime to force, measured by motor-evoked potentials to transcranial magnetic stimulation - so the mechanism is not inferred from behaviour but recorded alongside it
conceptmotor-evoked potentials — the excitability measure itself - the response of the motor system to a magnetic pulse, recorded during the priming task rather than after it
