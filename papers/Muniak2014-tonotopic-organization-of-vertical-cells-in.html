transferfrom cochlear anatomy: a frequency model built from AN innervation by cochlear origin, imported as an external criterion against which cell positions are predicted— “This model is based on the distribution of AN innervation in the CN with respect to cochlear origin, and makes no assumptions about nor imposes directionality to frequency organization throughout the volume of the nucleus.”
transferfrom psychoacoustics of the outer ear: the pinna transfer function and the frequency range of spectral notches, used to explain an anatomical cell-count gradient— “With the mouse ear, spectral notches are largely observed in the >20 kHz range ( Lauer et al., 2011 ), which is where our vertical cell counts are the lowest. Furthermore, the transfer function of the mouse ear shows overall gains in the 10 – 20 kHz range ( Lauer et al., 2011 ), where our cell counts are higher.”
mechanismAuditory-nerve input pattern sets each cochlear nucleus neuron's best frequency, so the cochleotopic auditory-nerve projection imposes tonotopy on vertical cells— “Given evidence that the pattern of AN input largely determines the BF of neurons in the CN ( Bourk et al., 1981 ; Fekete et al., 1984 ; Spirou et al., 1993 ; Ryugo and May, 1993 ), it is reasonable to conclude that the arrangement of vertical cell BFs would follow suit.”
mechanismVertical cell axons ramify locally inside an isofrequency slab, which is what makes their inhibition of pyramidal (type IV) cells fall at or near best frequency— “Thus, the near-BF inhibition of type IV units (pyramidal cells) by type II units is a result of the local ramification of vertical cell axons within an isofrequency slab.”
mechanismFrequency-specific inhibitory demand in the cochlear nucleus, set by the gains and spectral notches of the mouse ear transfer function, shapes how many vertical cells are found at each frequency— “Hence, the distribution of vertical cell numbers might relate to the overall inhibitory requirements in the CN with respect to frequency.”
mechanismretrograde labelling from a physiologically characterised AVCN site gives every labelled DCN cell an inherited BF, since the injection site's BF is measured and the cell's is not— “Furthermore, comparisons to a frequency model of the CN ( Muniak et al., 2013 ) demonstrated that the BF of each cell, inferred from the AVCN recording site, closely matched that of AN input.”
mechanismnormalising every case into one 3D template lets cells from separate animals be merged into a single dataset— “Cases were normalized by independently aligning each surface to a template CN surface ( Muniak et al., 2013 ) using an iterative routine in Amira that produced an affine transformation matrix.”
mechanismdendrites and local axons stay within an isofrequency sheet defined by the incoming auditory nerve fibres, so inhibition lands on matched frequencies— “For each cell, the collective dendritic arborization formed a “spatial envelope” that conformed to an isofrequency sheet defined by the incoming auditory nerve fibers.”
