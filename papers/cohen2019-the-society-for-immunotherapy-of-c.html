limitationONE BIOMARKER, TWO SCORING SYSTEMS, DIFFERENT CUT POINTS BY JURISDICTION: the European approval requires `PD-L1 with a >= 50% TUMOR PROPORTION SCORE` while the later FDA approval uses `a PD-L1 COMBINED POSITIVE SCORE >= 1`. Same protein, different denominators - TPS counts tumour cells, CPS counts tumour and immune cells - and the regulators chose differently

limitationTHE PAPER STATES ITS OWN RECOMMENDED DEFINITION TWICE, IN DIFFERENT WORDS. The recommendation table reads `compared to the expected OR PRIOR growth rate`; the body text carrying the 70.6% vote reads `compared to the expected growth rate`. `prior` is a measurable quantity for this patient and `expected` is a modelled one, so the two versions are not interchangeable at the bedside - and the table is the surface a clinician reads.
