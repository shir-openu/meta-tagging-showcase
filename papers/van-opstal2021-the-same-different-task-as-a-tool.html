phenomenonunconscious semantic processing and information integration— “The function of consciousness has often been investigated by looking at the cognitive processes that can (not) be performed without conscious awareness.”
claim-typemethod— “Here, I will argue that a subliminal priming version of the same-different task is a perfect tool to investigate these processes because it (i) allows a clear separation between the stimuli that are consciously processed and those that remain unconscious, and (ii) requires the unconsciously presented stimuli to be integrated.”
method — evidence— “An overview of the work that has used the subliminal samedifferent task to target these processes is presented, and some suggestions for how the task could be used in future work are made.”
mechanismA generic same-different algorithm is applied unconsciously to the prime pair whatever its format, and the response it evokes then facilitates or interferes with the response demanded by the conscious target pair.— “the response evoked by the unconscious same-different judgment on the primes facilitates or interferes with the response required for the same-different judgment on the targets.”
mechanismIf 'same' judgments are holistic and parallel while 'different' judgments are serial, then global-workspace theory predicts that only the parallel 'same' route can run without conscious access, so priming should come from same primes.— “According to the global neuronal workspace theory of consciousness, parallel processes can operate non-consciously, but serial processing requires conscious access [22,50–52].”
mechanismUnconscious integration succeeds in this task because the stimuli fall within a sufficiently small spatial (and temporal) window and are of limited complexity.— “It could be that also the spatial window should be sufficiently small to make unconscious integration possible [25] or that the complexity of the information should be limited.”
extends the dual process model of same-different judgements and the global neuronal workspace theory of consciousness— “If the dual process model of same-different judgements is correct, it could thus be hypothesized that priming in the subliminal SD task would work for primes that are the same, but less so for primes that are different.”
sensesubliminal priming - an operational definition with a NUMBER in it: the prime is shown for 50 ms or less and immediately followed by masks, and awareness is treated as absent because of the timing. Set beside Hoewe2020, where unawareness is established by asking the participant afterwards, this is the same criterion secured by apparatus instead of by report— “When the prime stimulus is presented for 50 ms or less and immediately followed by mask stimuli, participants have no awareness of the presence of this prime stimulus”
sensegenuine unconscious processing - a term defined by what it must be distinguished FROM. The two criteria exist so that an effect cannot be attributed to the conscious preparation of response links, and to rule out visual similarity. The definition is a list of alternative explanations that have to be closed off— “It has therefore been suggested that for genuine unconscious processing to take place, some criteria need to be met: First, primes should come from large categories or from a novel set outside of the task set so that subliminal priming effects cannot be attributed to the conscious preparation of response links. Second, primes and targets…
sensethe same-different task, as a tool rather than a topic - what recommends it is a STRUCTURAL property: the judgement concerns the relation between two targets, so the primes can be drawn from an entirely different set and still bear on the response— “Instead of centrally presenting a single prime and a single target on every trial, in the subliminal SD task two masked primes and two targets are presented side by side (Figure 1c & d) and participants are required to judge if the two target stimuli are the same or different”
differs inprimes —distinct-from→ targets — THE FORMAT THEY ARE PRESENTED IN, required to differ so that an effect cannot be explained by visual similarity between the two— “Second, primes and targets should be presented in different formats to avoid that any priming effect is caused by visual similarities”— “presented in different formats”
noteprimes —distinct-from→ targets — A methodological REQUIREMENT stated as a distinction - the two must be kept apart by design, which is why this literature ends up inventing tasks rather than analyses.— “Second, primes and targets should be presented in different formats to avoid that any priming effect is caused by visual similarities”

notecriteria —part-of→ subliminal priming — The first criterion, and its justification is the whole content: primes must come from a large or novel set SO THAT the effect cannot be attributed to consciously prepared response links. The rule is shaped by the alternative it excludes.
