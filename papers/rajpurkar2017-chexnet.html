phenomenonpneumonia detection on chest radiographs
claim-typemethod
transferfrom artificial intelligence (deep learning / computer vision): a deep convolutional network (DenseNet) applied to detect disease from chest X-rays at radiologist level — “a 121-layer convolutional neural network trained on ChestX-ray14”
mechanismthe binary cross entropy is weighted by the prevalence of the OPPOSITE class - w+ is the negative share and w- the positive share - so the rarer label carries the larger weight — “For a single example in the training set, we optimize the weighted binary cross entropy loss L(X, y) = −w+ · y log p(Y = 1|X) −w− · (1 − y) log p(Y = 0|X), where p(Y = i|X) is the probability that the network assigns to the label i, w+ = |N |/(|P |+|N |), and w− = |P |/(|P |+|N |) with |P | and |N | the number of positive cases and negative cases of pneumonia in the training set respectively.”
mechanismdense connections improve the flow of information and gradients through the network, which is what the paper credits for making the optimization of a very deep network tractable — “DenseNets improve flow of information and gradients through the network, making the optimization of very deep networks tractable.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
mechanismthe network is not trained from scratch: the final layer is replaced and the rest starts from ImageNet weights — “We replace the final fully connected layer with one that has a single output, after which we apply a sigmoid nonlinearity. The weights of the network are initialized with weights from a model pretrained on ImageNet”
mechanismthe learning rate is cut only when the validation loss stops improving, and the returned model is the best validation checkpoint rather than the last — “We use an initial learning rate of 0.001 that is decayed by a factor of 10 each time the validation loss plateaus after an epoch, and pick the model with the lowest validation loss.”
mechanismeach radiologist and the model are scored against the OTHER FOUR radiologists' labels, taken as ground truth, and the reported figure is the mean of those four scores — “We compute the F1 score for each individual radiologist and for CheXNet against each of the other 4 labels as ground truth. We report the mean of the 4 resulting F1 scores for each radiologist and for CheXNet, along with the average F1 across the radiologists.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
mechanismsignificance is taken from the DIFFERENCE between the two averages computed on the same bootstrap samples, and read off whether the 95% CI on that difference contains zero — “To determine whether CheXNet’s performance is statistically significantly higher than radiologist performance, we also calculate the difference between the average F1 score of CheXNet and the average F1 score of the radiologists on the same bootstrap samples. If the 95% CI on the difference does not include zero, we conclude there was a significant difference between the F1 score of CheXNet and the F1 score of the radiologists. We find that the difference in F1 scores — 0.051 (95% CI 0.005, 0.084) — does not contain 0, and therefore conclude that the performance of CheXNet is statistically significantly higher than radiologist performance.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
mechanismthe heatmap is the weighted sum of the final convolutional feature maps, weighted by the final classification layer's own weights for that pathology, then upscaled and overlaid — “To generate the CAMs, we feed an image into the fully trained network and extract the feature maps that are output by the final convolutional layer. Let fk be the kth feature map and let wc,k be the weight in the final classification layer for feature map k leading to pathology c. We obtain a map Mc of the most salient features used in classifying the image as having pathology c by taking the weighted sum of the feature maps using their associated weights. Formally, Mc = (cid:88) k wc,kfk. We identify the most important features used by the model in its prediction of the pathology c by upscaling the map Mc to the dimensions of the image and overlaying the image.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]

limitationthe paper identifies three limitations of its radiologist comparison — “We identify three limitations of this comparison.”
limitationThe paper announces three limitations of the radiologist comparison but enumerates only two (a 'First' and a 'Third'); the second is missing from the text. — “We identify three limitations of this comparison.”
limitationOnly frontal radiographs were shown to both model and radiologists, although a share of accurate diagnoses requires the lateral view. — “First, only frontal radiographs were presented to the radiologists and model during diagnosis, but it has been shown that up to 15% of accurate diagnoses require the lateral view (Raoof et al., 2012); we thus expect that this setup provides a conservative estimate of performance.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
limitationNeither model nor radiologists could use patient history, which is known to change radiologist diagnostic performance. — “Third, neither the model nor the radiologists were not permitted to use patient history, which has been shown to decrease radiologist diagnostic performance in interpreting chest radiographs (Berbaum et al., 1985; Potchen et al., 1979);” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
limitationThe standard the model is measured against is the other radiologists' labels, not the presence of disease. — “We compute the F1 score for each individual radiologist and for CheXNet against each of the other 4 labels as ground truth”
limitationThe training labels were produced by text extraction from reports rather than by anyone looking at the images. — “annotate each image with up to 14 different thoracic pathology labels using automatic extraction methods on radiology reports”
limitationEvery image the extractor did not mark becomes a negative example. — “We label images that have pneumonia as one of the annotated pathologies as positive examples and label all other images as negative examples” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
limitationThe model reports fourteen findings while the headline number describes one of them. — “instead of outputting one binary label, ChexNet outputs a vector t of binary labels indicating the absence or presence of each of the following 14 pathology classes” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
