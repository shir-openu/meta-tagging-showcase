mechanismpoles of a continuation, not solutions of the problem the quasi-normal frequencies are the zeros of a Wronskian in the analytic continuation of the Green function to Re(s) < 0. The corresponding functions grow exponentially at both ends and are not physical states; what is physical is that they control the late-time decay in any bounded region— “First of all we should note that because of Re(s) < 0 the function f+ grows exponentially for small and large x! The corresponding spacetime solution is therefore not a physically relevant solution, unlike the normal modes.”
