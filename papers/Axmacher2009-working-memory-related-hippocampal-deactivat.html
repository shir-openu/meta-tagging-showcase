mechanismEarly list items processed by single-item, hippocampus-independent working memory leave the hippocampus deactivated, so no encoding into long-term memory takes place and the item is forgotten— “Our present findings demonstrate that hippocampus-independent WM at early list-positions is not beneficial for LTM and results in subsequent forgetting.”
mechanismWhen multi-item, hippocampus-dependent working memory is recruited instead at early list positions, the same hippocampal engagement supports long-term encoding— “Recruitment of hippocampus-dependent WM at early list positions thus actually supports LTM formation.”
mechanismItems early in the list are rehearsed more, and later items interfere with earlier ones, which is why the primacy advantage arises and why hippocampal engagement stops predicting memory later in the list— “Based on the model of Atkinson and Shiffrin (1968) , we suggest that items later in the list are not rehearsed as much as the items earlier in the list (explaining the primacy effect) and that later items interfere with previous words.”
