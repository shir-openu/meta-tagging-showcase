mechanismthe boundary against policy criticism is enforced by a worked NEGATIVE example rather than by a rule— “Importantly, critique or negative attitudes towards policy issues is not in itself a sufficient condition for PDD, even if phrased in harsh terms. For example, the sentence “This [budget] is an example of negligence and promiscuous conduct, endangering both the economy and society in Israel.” is not annotated as PDD.”
mechanismevery proportion is computed by aggregating at the SPEAKER level first, so that prolific politicians cannot dominate the rate— “For all analyses, the proportions were computed by first aggregating at the speaker level to avoid over-weighting more active politicians.”
mechanismdistinctive targets per political bloc are identified with a weighted log-odds method rather than by raw frequency— “Using the weighted log odds method proposed by Monroe et al. (2008), we identify the PDD target tokens most strongly associated with each political bloc.”
builds-on Rivlin-Angert (2025), on delegitimization of political identities— “The objective of PDD is to establish political dominance and suppress alternative narratives by narrowing the boundaries of acceptable discourse and excluding specific ideas and ideologies from being considered legitimate (Rivlin-Angert, 2025).”
builds-on Mor-Lan et al. (2024), IsraParlTweet dataset of Knesset speeches— “We utilize the Isra- ParlTweet dataset (Mor-Lan et al., 2024), a full archive of Knesset floor speeches from 1992-2023.”
