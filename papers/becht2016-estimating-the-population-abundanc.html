Concepts
terms this paper carries, each with the sentence it was cut from — the layer that joins one paper to another. Terms with no quote yet are on CONCEPTS.html.
conceptMCP-counter — the method introduced here - a per-population score computed on each sample independently, designed for comparison across samples rather than between cell types
concepttranscriptomic markers — gene features expressed in one and only one cell population with little within-population variation - a definition restrictive enough that the paper says such markers may not exist for a given population at all
conceptcell population — the unit of the estimate; which populations are estimable was decided by which had purified positive samples and appropriate negative controls in public repositories
conceptnegative controls — samples known not to contain the population - 742 tumour cell lines from 21 anatomical origins here, and the availability of these is what decides which populations can be measured
conceptarbitrary units — the scale of the output - proportional to abundance, with no absolute meaning, and dependent on the gene expression platform, so cross-platform comparison is excluded
conceptCIBERSORT — the deconvolution method that estimates within-leukocyte proportions; correct at its own question and, by construction, blind to changes in total leukocyte content
conceptflow cytometry — the technique whose logic CIBERSORT-style methods inherit - proportions within one sample - and which MCP-counter is deliberately not modelled on
conceptlimit of detection — the smallest RNA fraction at which a population is distinguishable from non-hematopoietic controls - below 2% for every population assayed, and from 1/950 to 1/50 depending on which
conceptimmunohistochemical — the ex vivo cell-density measurement used as an independent check, and the technique MCP-counter's estimates are said to be conceptually closest to
