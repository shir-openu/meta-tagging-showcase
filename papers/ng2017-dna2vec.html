limitationThe standard encoding asserts that every pair of sequences is equally unlike every other, which is false of the sequences themselves. — “Worse yet, the distance between any arbitrary pair of one-hot vectors is equidistant, even though ATGGC should be closer to ATGGG than CACGA”
limitationEvery similarity result in the paper is measured against one stipulated alignment scoring, including a gap penalty of zero. — “All of the Needleman-Wunsch similarity score in this paper were computed using Biopython’s align.globalxx function, which used a match score of 1, mismatch of 0 and gap penalty of 0”
limitationThe method's justification is a hypothesis about words, carried over to nucleotides, and the paper marks the inspiration as loose. — “This reconstruction of context of words is loosely inspired by the linguistic concept of distributional hypothesis, which states that words that appear in the same context have similar meaning”

findingdna2vec adapts word2vec to learn distributed vector representations of variable-length DNA k-mers, avoiding the curse of dimensionality and equidistance of one-hot encodings. — “we propose a novel method to train distributed representations of variable-length k-mers. Our method is based on the popular word embedding model word2vec”
findingSumming dna2vec vectors approximates nucleotide concatenation, and cosine similarity of the vectors correlates with Needleman-Wunsch alignment similarity. — “the summing of dna2vec vectors is akin to nucleotides concatenation. We also demonstrate that there is correlation between Needleman-Wunsch similarity score and cosine similarity of dna2vec vectors.”
