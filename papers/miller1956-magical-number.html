limitationthe recurrence of 'seven' across channel capacity and memory span may be merely coincidental — “And yet when we examine the matter more closely, there seems to be a reasonable suspicion that it is nothing more than a coincidence.”
limitationThe suggested span of perceptual dimensionality (about ten) is stated to have no supporting objective evidence. — “I suspect that there is also a span of perceptual dimensionality and that this span is somewhere in the neighborhood of ten, but I must add at once that there is no objective evidence to support this suspicion.”
limitationSome reported channel-capacity values are probably underestimates because data were pooled across subjects before computing transmitted information. — “I should add, however, that these values are apt to be slightly too low because the data from all subjects were pooled before the transmitted information was computed.”
limitationThe central theoretical unit, the chunk, is not precisely defined. — “The contrast of the terms bit and chunk also serves to highlight the fact that we are not very definite about what constitutes a chunk of information.”
limitationThe subitizing comparison rests on a guess, since the number-estimation data were never analysed in informational terms. — “The data on number estimates have not been analyzed in informational terms; but on the basis of the published data I would guess that the subjects transmitted something more than four bits of information about the number of dots.”
limitationThe paper's own closing position is that the recurring seven is probably coincidence, not one underlying limit — “Perhaps there is something deep and profound behind all these sevens, something just calling out for us to discover it. But I suspect that it is only a pernicious, Pythagorean coincidence.”
limitationThe unit that the memory limit is counted in is admitted to be undefined — “The contrast of the terms bit and chunk also serves to highlight the fact that we are not very definite about what constitutes a chunk of information.”
limitationThe chunk independence holds only over the range tested so far, which the author states rather than assumes — “The span of immediate memory seems to be almost independent of the number of bits per chunk, at least over the range that has been examined to date.”
limitationThe span of seven is claimed for unidimensional judgments specifically, not judgments in general — “I would propose to call this limit the span of absolute judgment , and I maintain that for unidimensional judgments this span is usually somewhere in the neighborhood of seven.”
limitationThe information-theoretic machinery is deliberately paraphrased away, so the argument as presented cannot be checked against the formalism it rests on — “In the interests of communication I have suppressed the technical details of information measurement and have tried to express the ideas in more familiar terms; I hope this paraphrase will not lead you to think they are not useful in research.”
