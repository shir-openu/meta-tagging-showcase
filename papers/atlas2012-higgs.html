phenomenonStandard Model Higgs boson search
claim-typeexistence
transferfrom statistics (hypothesis testing, p-values): statistical significance testing (p-values, sigma levels) used to establish the discovery of a new particle against background fluctuations — “the p-values corresponding to significances of 1 to 6”
mechanismthe whole search is reduced to ONE parameter of interest - a signal strength that scales the predicted signal - and it is defined so that zero is the background-only hypothesis and one is the Standard Model Higgs signal, so the hypothesis under test is a value of that one factor — “The parameter of interest is the global signal strength factor µ, which acts as a scale factor on the total number of events predicted by the Standard Model for the Higgs boson signal. This factor is defined such that µ = 0 corresponds to the background-only hypothesis and µ = 1 corresponds to the SM Higgs boson signal in addition to the background.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
mechanismhypothesised values of that strength are tested with a profile likelihood ratio, which extracts the signal strength from a full likelihood fit whose function INCLUDES all the parameters describing the systematic uncertainties and their correlations — “Hypothesised values of µ are tested with a statistic λ(µ) based on the profile likelihood ratio [122]. This test statistic extracts the information on the signal strength from a full likelihood fit to the data. The likelihood function includes all the parameters that describe the systematic uncertainties and their correlations.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
mechanismexclusion is decided by a stated numerical rule rather than by judgement: a signal strength is excluded at 95% confidence when its CLs value falls below 5%, and a Standard Model Higgs of a given mass counts as excluded when the value one is excluded at that mass — “Exclusion limits are based on the CLs prescription [123]; a value of µ is regarded as excluded at 95% CL when CLs is less than 5%. A SM Higgs boson with mass mH is considered excluded at 95% confidence level (CL) when µ = 1 is excluded at that mass.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
mechanismthe size of an excess is first expressed as the probability that background alone could produce a fluctuation at least that large, and only then converted into the equivalent number of standard deviations - the local significance — “The significance of an excess in the data is first quantified with the local p0, the probability that the background can produce a fluctuation greater than or equal to the excess observed in data. The equivalent formulation in terms of number of standard deviations, Zl, is referred to as the local significance.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
mechanismthat local number is then corrected for having searched a whole mass range rather than one point: the ratio of global to local probability is the trials factor, and the paper states what it depends on - the range of hypotheses considered, the mass resolutions of the channels combined, and the size of the excess itself — “The global probability for the most significant excess to be observed anywhere in a given search region is estimated with the method described in Ref. [124]. The ratio of the global to the local probabilities, the trials factor used to correct for the ”look elsewhere” effect, increases with the range of Higgs boson mass hypotheses considered, the mass resolutions of the channels involved in the combination, and the significance of the excess.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
mechanismthe tests are run in steps across the hypothesised mass rather than at one chosen point, and the asymptotic approximation the results rest on is not assumed to hold - it is validated by a separately described method — “The statistical tests are performed in steps of values of the hypothesised Higgs boson mass mH. The asymptotic approximation [122] upon which the results are based has been validated with the method described in Ref. [17].” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
mechanismthe combination across channels is built on the same single signal strength together with an explicit identification of which nuisance parameters correspond to the CORRELATED sources of systematic uncertainty — “The combination of individual search sub-channels for a specific Higgs boson decay, and the full combination of all search channels, are based on the global signal strength factor µ and on the identification of the nuisance parameters that correspond to the correlated sources of systematic uncertainty described in Section 8.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
mechanismthe jet energy scale and resolution are handled by a deliberately simplified scheme - independent nuisance parameters attached to final states with materially different selections and process sensitivity - and the simplification is defended by measurement: the results were found insensitive to the assumptions made about how those sources correlate — “A simplified scheme is used in which independent JES and JER nuisance parameters are associated with final states with significantly different kinematic selections and sensitivity to scattering processes with different kinematic distributions or flavour composition. This scheme includes a specific treatment for b-jets. The sensitivity of the results to various assumptions about the correlation between these sources of uncertainty has been found to be negligible.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
relation — “compatible with the production and decay of the Standard Model”
relation — “The analysis is improved in several aspects with respect to Ref. [83] to enhance the sensitivity to a low-mass Higgs boson.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]

limitationThe data establish a new neutral boson but are insufficient to determine whether it is in fact the Standard Model Higgs boson. — “Although these results are compatible with the hypothesis that the new particle is the Standard Model Higgs boson, more data are needed to assess its nature in detail.”
limitationIncreased pile-up degraded missing-transverse-momentum resolution so badly that the same-flavour WW final states had to be dropped from the 8 TeV analysis. — “Since the eµ channel provides most of the sensitivity of the search, only this final state is used in the analysis of the 8 TeV data.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
limitationThe same excess carries three significances: 5.9 sigma locally, about 5.1 sigma globally over 110-600 GeV, and 5.3 sigma over a narrower range whose bounds are lost to figure text in this staged copy. — “The presence of these uncertainties, evaluated as described in Ref. [138], reduces the local significance to 5.9 σ. The global significance of a local 5.9 σ excess anywhere in the mass range 110–600 GeV is estimated to be approximately 5.1 σ, increasing to 5.3” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
limitationThe correction from local to global significance is stated to depend on the mass range searched, the channels' mass resolutions, and the size of the excess. — “The ratio of the global to the local probabilities, the trials factor used to correct for the ”look elsewhere” effect, increases with the range of Higgs boson mass hypotheses considered, the mass resolutions of the channels involved in the combination, and the significance of the excess.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
limitationCompatibility with the Standard Model expectation is quantified at the 1.5 sigma level rather than asserted. — “Theoretical uncertainties are included so that the consistency with the SM expectation can be quantified. The data are compatible with the SM expectation at the 1.5 σ level.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
limitationOnly the spin-1 hypothesis is addressed, and only by the diphoton observation. — “The observation in the diphoton channel disfavours the spin-1 hypothesis” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
