mechanismthe prime biases how the subsequent sponsored content is processed, so the same content yields different judgments— “The manipulation by priming is assumed to cause biased information processing.”
mechanismbeing invited to participate lets viewers process the content more carefully, so they either confirm or reject the prime instead of simply absorbing it— “Therefore, participation with the video channel or outlet allows more careful information processing, leading to either confirmation or rejection of the priming (i.e., the persuasion attempt).”
mechanismdisclosing a persuasion attempt first activates cognitive persuasion knowledge, which then activates attitudinal knowledge (a stepwise persuasion process)— “It is suggested that the disclosure of a persuasion attempt first triggers cognitive persuasion knowledge that leads to activation of attitudinal knowledge.”
builds-on persuasion knowledge model (Friestad & Wright, 1994) and priming theory (Carroll & Einwiller, 2014; Celse & Chang, 2017)— “Building on the persuasion knowledge model (Friestad & Wright, 1994) and the theory of priming (Carroll & Einwiller, 2014; Celse & Chang, 2017), mere awareness of an intent to persuade may provoke in individuals various coping mechanisms and resistance to communication (Petty & Cacioppo, 1977; van Reijmersdal et al.…

sensepriming, in strategic communication - an ACT OF SELECTION on the audience's memory: certain patterns are activated and others are left out. The exclusion is written into the definition, which is what makes a positive and a negative primer symmetrical operations here— “Priming refers to providing contextual cues to a non-conscious information process that activates certain patterns in memory and leaves out others”
sensepriming as against framing, THIS FIELD'S VERSION - the two are granted the same function (activating awareness of an issue) and separated by what framing does in addition: attribute meanings. Compare the economics version, where the criterion is re-description of the decision problem, and the media-studies version, where it is awareness— “A tool of strategic communication, priming is like framing, in that it activates audience members’ awareness of an issue. However, framing directs audience members’ judgments by attributing certain meanings to an issue”
senseperceived authenticity - a construct whose positive definition is a list and whose working definition is its complement. The paper says so outright: it is OFTEN ALSO DEFINED BY NEGATION, with inauthenticity as falsity, externally motivated behaviour, and reciting a script rather than expressing one's own words— “It is often also defined by negation. Inauthenticity has been understood as falsity (Enli, 2015; Gilmore & Pine, 2007; Kernis & Goldman, 2006), externally motivated behavior and inconsistency (Moulard et al., 2015), and the act of “reciting a script,” rather than expressing one’s own words”

no type for thispriming versus framing, as this paper draws it: both activate awareness of an issue and framing additionally attributes MEANINGS to it → the same pair as drawn in drouvelis2010 / drouvelis2015 (framing RE-DESCRIBES a logically equivalent decision problem) and as reported unresolved in Hoewe2020 (where Ottati holds that framing IS a form of priming) — THREE PAPERS IN THIS CORPUS SEPARATE `priming` FROM `framing`, IN THREE FIELDS, ON THREE DIFFERENT AXES - AND NONE OF THEM CITES THE OTHERS. Economics (drouvelis, both papers) makes the criterion THE DECISION PROBLEM: framing re-describes a logically equivalent one, priming does not. Media studies (Hoewe2020) makes it AWARENESS, and reports its own field unable to settle whether the two are even distinct, quoting Ottati that `media framing constitutes a specific form of priming`. Strategic communication (this paper) makes it MEANING: the two share the activation of awareness and framing adds the attribution of meanings. **Not a disagreement about a fact - three literatures independently fixing one boundary and not noticing each other.** That is the negative space this project was built to make visible, and the schema has no relation type for `these papers partition the same pair differently`., and for the missing types.
