phenomenondisorders of consciousness after severe brain injury— “In this cross-sectional study, we recruited a consecutive sample of 143 patients who survived severe brain injury and developed a disorder of consciousness.”
claim-typemechanism— “At a circuit level, the implication of our findings is that, at least in the context of DOC, different aspects of consciousness, including willful behavior and arousal, might be mediated by thalamic and extrathalamic mechanisms, respectively.”
method — evidence— “Structural T1-weighted magnetic resonance images were collected, in a continuous sample of 143 severely brain-injured patients with DOC (and 96 volunteers), across 2 tertiary expert centers.”
mechanismWillful behaviour and arousal are supported by separate circuits, so thalamic damage removes organised willful behaviour while extrathalamic (basal ganglia) damage removes behavioural and electrocortical arousal— “At a circuit level, the implication of our findings is that, at least in the context of DOC, different aspects of consciousness, including willful behavior and arousal, might be mediated by thalamic and extrathalamic mechanisms, respectively.”
mechanismLoss of corticopetal thalamo-prefrontal projections (the mesocircuit) removes the drive that sustains organised behaviour and integrates information across cortex— “On the one hand, the negative association between motor/communication scores and atrophy along the anterior and dorsomedial regions of thalamus is consistent with the mesocircuit theory according to which corticopetal projections from thalamus to prefrontal cortex are crucial for sustaining organized behavior26 and integrating information…
mechanismPartial basal forebrain atrophy spares the coma-inducing threshold but impairs the fast cortical disinhibition that transiently amplifies sensory processing— “comalike state seen in animal models upon complete lesion of this region,18 but might nonetheless have been sufficient to affect the mechanisms of fast cortical disinhibition.”
builds-on the mesocircuit theory of disorders of consciousness— “On the one hand, the negative association between motor/communication scores and atrophy along the anterior and dorsomedial regions of thalamus is consistent with the mesocircuit theory according to which corticopetal projections from thalamus to prefrontal cortex are crucial for sustaining organized behavior26 and integrating information…
replicates postmortem examinations of thalamic damage after nontraumatic brain injury— “Third, paralleling postmortem examinations,50 we have also shown in vivo that patients suffering from nontraumatic brain injury exhibit a more widespread and left-lateralized degree of thalamic atrophy, consistent with the well-known poorer prognosis associated with nontraumatic etiology.1”
