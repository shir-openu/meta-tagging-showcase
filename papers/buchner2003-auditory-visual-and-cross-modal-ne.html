phenomenonnegative priming— “Negative priming refers to the phenomenon that a reaction to a stimulus can be slowed down or more likely to be in error if the stimulus has recently been ignored.”
claim-typeexistence— “To our knowledge, the present experiment is the first to demonstrate negative priming, using the same experimental task, with visual and auditory stimuli and also across these two modalities.”
method — evidence— “Auditory, visual, and cross-modal negative priming was investigated using a task in which participants judged whether stimuli were animals or musical instruments.”
mechanismpresenting a stimulus in one modality automatically activates its features in the other modality, so an ignored sound can prime (and slow) a later picture— “Second, the presentation of a stimulus in one modality may automatically activate features in other modalities.”
mechanismdistractor inhibition is applied only to distractors that interfere: nonconflict distractors need no inhibition, hence no later cost— “It could be assumed that nonconflict prime distractors do not interfere with the prime reaction, so that they need not be inhibited.”
mechanismthe probe target retrieves the earlier prime episode, which carries a 'no response was made' tag that conflicts with the current requirement to respond— “Within the episodic retrieval account, negative priming may be caused by the probe target’s cuing the retrieval of the perceptually similar prime display in which the distractor representation contains the information that no response was (to be) made to that stimulus.”
mechanismacoustic traces of ignored sounds persist in a long auditory store better than visual distractor traces persist in visual short-term memory, producing facilitation that offsets or reverses the negative-priming cost— “It seems possible that the acoustic aspects of auditory distractor representations are maintained more efficiently in a “long auditory store” (see Cowan, 1984) than the perceptual aspects of the representations of visual distractors are maintained in visual short-term memory.”
replicates Driver and Baylis (1993)— “This demonstration is consonant with the finding of auditory-to-visual negative priming by Driver and Baylis (1993).”
extends Driver and Baylis (1993, Experiment 1)— “To our knowledge, only Driver and Baylis (1993, Experiment 1) have investigated cross-modal negative priming, but only the auditory-tovisual side of it.”
senseA PRIME THAT HURTS BECAUSE IT WAS IGNORED, WHICH IS A RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN PRIME AND TARGET THAT NOTHING ELSE IN THIS CORPUS HAS. `A reaction to a stimulus can be SLOWED DOWN or more likely to be in error IF THE STIMULUS HAS RECENTLY BEEN IGNORED.` The manipulation is an INSTRUCTION TO DISREGARD, so what carries over is the act of suppression. **And it is a cost, not a benefit** - the second counterexample tonight to the standard `improvement` definition Minton2017 strikes out (Q-780), after Huang2018's interference effect— “Negative priming refers to the phenomenon that a reaction to a stimulus can be slowed down or more likely to be in error if the stimulus has recently been ignored”
senseAND IGNORING IS NOT ENOUGH - IT HAD TO HAVE BEEN COMPETING: `Negative priming was observed ONLY AFTER CONFLICT, but not after nonconflict, primes.` The ignored item must have called for a DIFFERENT response from the attended one. So the phenomenon is not about suppression in general but about suppression under competition, and a design that ignores an irrelevant stimulus with no competing response should find nothing. **Another `only when`, and the condition is a property of the RELATION between the two primes rather than of either one**— “Negative priming was observed only after conflict, but not after nonconflict, primes. Note, however, that prime and probe responses necessarily changed in the former case”
senseTHE AUTHORS MARK EXACTLY HOW MUCH OF THEIR CONCLUSION RESTS ON SOMETHING THEY CANNOT TEST: `ALTHOUGH THIS UNAVOIDABLE CONFOUND renders the interpretation of the present finding AMBIGUOUS IN PRINCIPLE, WE BELIEVE that the conflict/nonconflict nature of the primes, and not the prime-probe response alternation/repetition, determined the observed pattern.` Then: `IF THIS POSITION IS ACCEPTED, the observed data pattern is most easily explained within...` **Four markers in three sentences - unavoidable, ambiguous in principle, we believe, if this position is accepted - and everything downstream is explicitly conditional on the belief.** The confound is structural: conflict primes necessarily change the response, so the two cannot be separated by any version of this design— “Although this unavoidable confound renders the interpretation of the present finding ambiguous in principle, we believe that the conflict/nonconflict nature of the primes, and not the prime–probe response alternation/repetition, determined the observed pattern of performance”
senseAN UNEXPECTED RESULT ADMITTED AS UNEXPLAINED: `The positive priming on nonconflict prime trials in the auditory-auditory condition WAS UNEXPECTED. WE CAN ONLY SPECULATE about the underlying mechanisms.` The effect ran the WRONG WAY in one cell - facilitation where the paper predicts none - and it gets a paragraph of candidate explanations, each marked as speculation, rather than a footnote. **A record that stored only the headline `negative priming after conflict primes` would lose a cell that contradicts the framework**— “The positive priming on nonconflict prime trials in the auditory–auditory condition was unexpected. We can only speculate about the underlying mechanisms”
senseTHREE ACCOUNTS ARE TESTED AGAINST THE SAME PATTERN AND ONE OF THEM HAS TO BE PATCHED: distractor inhibition and episodic retrieval both accommodate the conflict result, but for the temporal discrimination account `IN ORDER TO EXPLAIN THE LACK OF NEGATIVE PRIMING ON NONCONFLICT TRIALS, THE TEMPORAL DISCRIMINATION ACCOUNT MUST BE EXTENDED.` Two alternative extensions are then offered, both hypothetical. **The paper reports which theory needs extra machinery to survive its data**, which is a more useful thing to record than which theory it prefers— “In order to explain the lack of negative priming on nonconflict trials, the…
controla MATCHED CONTROL TRIAL was built for every ignored-repetition trial by replacing the ignored prime with the stimulus that did not occur — held — rules out that the effect is a property of the probe pair rather than of the ignored prime - the probe is identical across the pair, so only the prime differs
controlthe response CATEGORY of the ignored prime was held constant across an ignored-repetition trial and its control — held — rules out that the difference is a response-category effect rather than an identity effect
