phenomenonhippocampal cell-assembly coding of auditory pitch and duration
claim-typemechanism
transferfrom philosophy: Bergson's philosophical account of time perception is imported as the interpretive model for why duration is never coded on its own but always alongside another stimulus attribute — “These non-independent processes for the coding of time in the working brain support Henri Bergson’s theory about the perception of time (Bergson, 1889).”
mechanismIndividual neurons belong to more than one cell assembly, so an assembly can distinguish two tasks that no single member neuron can distinguish - which is how the rat behaves differently while single-cell activity does not differentiate — “A solution to this contradiction between the single-neuronal activity and the animal’s behavior is the theory of cell-assembly coding”
mechanismCoding a task consists in the neurons of the corresponding assembly becoming correlated in activity while that task is being performed — “If the model outlined in Fig. 9 is correct, activity correlations among the neurons within each of the cell assemblies are expected when the information, i.e., tasks, are being coded by the cell assemblies.”
relation — “The present results suggest such overlapping of neurons among cell assemblies, and indicate that each type of task is coded not by single neurons but by cell assemblies comprised of the task-related neurons, as was found in a previous study (Sakurai, 1996a).”
relation — “The present model is largely consistent with the hypothesis of dual coding proposed in several recent studies (Arieli et al., 1996; Riehle et al., 1997; de Oliveira et al., 1997), which suggested that single neurons encode types of incoming stimuli and cell assemblies encode types of tasks, situations, contexts and/or inner cognitive functions, to process the incoming stimuli.”
relation — “These non-independent processes for the coding of time in the working brain support Henri Bergson’s theory about the perception of time (Bergson, 1889).”
differs incell assemblies → single neurons — what each level encodes: the stimulus attributes themselves, or the type of task in which they are processed — “The single neurons encode both auditory pitches and their temporal lengths and the cell assemblies encode types of tasks”
replicationuntested
limitationThe pitch and duration information may in fact be coded first in sensory cortex and only reflected in hippocampus. — “However, the sensory information of inputs (duration and pitch) and/or the tasks with them might be coded and processed ¢rst in sensory areas and then passed onto and re£ected in the hippocampal formation, because some of the sensory neocortical regions have actually been shown to have high degrees of neuronal dynamics underlying cell-assembly coding (e.g., Nicolelis et al., 1998; Ghazanfar and Nicolelis, 1999; Jancke et al., 1999).”
limitationRecording within one region cannot capture assemblies that are distributed across regions. — “Besides these investigations within regions of interest, investigation of a global cell assembly among plural regions is necessary, because ensemble coding by cell assemblies can be di¡use (Sakurai, 1996b, 1999a; Singer, 1999; Singer et al., 1997).”
limitationWhether the dual-coding scheme generalises beyond hippocampus is untested here. — “Other brain regions should also be studied, as dual coding with cell assembly might be common across many di¡erent brain regions.”
limitationNeuron pairs with fewer than 2000 combined discharges were excluded from the cross-correlation analysis. — “Because constructing cross-correlograms requires large enough numbers of neuronal discharges, neuron pairs whose combined total numbers of discharges were less than 2000 during a task were not included in the samples, as in the previous study (Sakurai, 1996a).”
limitationThe cell-assembly model is described as supported by the correlation analysis, not established by it. — “Cross-correlation analysis among activities of simultaneously recorded multiple neurons supported the suggested cell-assembly model”
limitationThe interpretation of temporal coding rests on an imported philosophical argument. — “His philosophical argument suggests that, though there must be real independent time in this world, we have a strong tendency to perceive or recognize time not as independent information but as information concomitant with other physical attributes of stimuli around us”

findingEvery hippocampal neuron that discriminated long from short tones in the duration task was also task-related in the pitch task, without exception. — “However, without exception, neurons which showed task-related di¡erential activity between the long and short tones in the duration-discrimination task were always task-related neurons in the pitchdiscrimination task.”
findingNeurons related only to the pitch task were the most numerous class, and not a single neuron was related to the duration task alone. — “The number of neurons related to only the pitch-discrimination task (P) was largest, followed by the many neurons related to both the durationand pitch-discrimination tasks (DP), but there were no neurons related to the duration-discrimination task alone (D).”
findingThe author infers that temporal information cannot be coded independently by dedicated neurons, whereas pitch information can. — “These results suggest that temporal information (long or short), in contrast to pitch information (high or low), cannot be coded independently by speci¢c neurons.”
findingThe cell assembly encoding the duration task is not separate but sits entirely inside the assembly encoding the pitch task. — “The cell assembly encoding the duration-discrimination task was completely contained within the cell assembly encoding the pitch-discrimination task.”
findingCross-correlation results matched the predictions of the nested cell-assembly model for 37 of the 48 neuron pairs against 11 that did not, a significant excess. — “The total number of pairs supporting the hypothesis is 37, whereas that of the other pairs is 11.”
findingThe study concludes for dual coding: single neurons carry both the pitch and the duration of the tones, while cell assemblies carry which task (context) the animal is performing. — “The single neurons encode both auditory pitches and their temporal lengths and the cell assemblies encode types of tasks (contexts or situations) in which the pitch and the temporal information are processed.”
