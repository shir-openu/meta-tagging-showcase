phenomenonhippocampal cell-assembly coding of auditory pitch and duration— “This study reports how hippocampal individual cells and cell assemblies cooperate for neural coding of pitch and temporal information in memory processes for auditory stimuli.”
claim-typemechanism— “Considering those results, this study concludes that dual coding by hippocampal single neurons and cell assemblies is working in memory processing of pitch and temporal information of auditory stimuli.”
method — evidence— “Multiple neurons were recorded from the rat pyramidal-cell layers of CA1 and CA3 during the performance of the two tasks.”
transferfrom philosophy: Bergson's philosophical account of time perception is imported as the interpretive model for why duration is never coded on its own but always alongside another stimulus attribute— “These non-independent processes for the coding of time in the working brain support Henri Bergson’s theory about the perception of time (Bergson, 1889).”
mechanismIndividual neurons belong to more than one cell assembly, so an assembly can distinguish two tasks that no single member neuron can distinguish - which is how the rat behaves differently while single-cell activity does not differentiate— “A solution to this contradiction between the single-neuronal activity and the animal’s behavior is the theory of cell-assembly coding”
mechanismCoding a task consists in the neurons of the corresponding assembly becoming correlated in activity while that task is being performed— “If the model outlined in Fig. 9 is correct, activity correlations among the neurons within each of the cell assemblies are expected when the information, i.e., tasks, are being coded by the cell assemblies.”
extends Sakurai (1996a) cell-assembly coding model of stimulus-discrimination tasks— “The present results suggest such overlapping of neurons among cell assemblies, and indicate that each type of task is coded not by single neurons but by cell assemblies comprised of the task-related neurons, as was found in a previous study (Sakurai, 1996a).”
builds-on dual-coding hypothesis (Arieli et al., 1996; Riehle et al., 1997; de Oliveira et al., 1997)— “The present model is largely consistent with the hypothesis of dual coding proposed in several recent studies (Arieli et al., 1996; Riehle et al., 1997; de Oliveira et al., 1997), which suggested that single neurons encode types of incoming stimuli and cell assemblies encode types of tasks, situations, contexts and/or inner cognitive…
builds-on Henri Bergson's theory of the perception of time (Bergson, 1889)— “These non-independent processes for the coding of time in the working brain support Henri Bergson’s theory about the perception of time (Bergson, 1889).”
sensethe division of labour - TWO LEVELS, TWO OBJECTS, IN ONE SENTENCE: single neurons encode the auditory pitches and temporal lengths themselves, while cell assemblies encode the TYPE OF TASK - the context in which that information is processed - so 'the hippocampus codes pitch' is true at one level and beside the point at the other— “The single neurons encode both auditory pitches and their temporal lengths and the cell assemblies encode types of tasks (contexts or situations) in which the pitch and the temporal information are processed”
sensedual coding - A BORROWED NAME, ATTRIBUTED: the paper states the phenomenon HAS BEEN CALLED dual coding and cites the source, then reports it demonstrated in several brain regions, so the term arrives with a history rather than being coined here; the citation itself is cut from the span because the staged text mangles the author name— “This phenomenon has been called ‘dual coding’”
sensethe cell-assembly model's standing - SUPPORTED BY A CORRELATION: the verification is that cross-correlation analysis among simultaneously recorded neurons supported the suggested model, which is a consistency check rather than an independent measurement of an assembly— “Cross-correlation analysis among activities of simultaneously recorded multiple neurons supported the suggested cell-assembly model”
