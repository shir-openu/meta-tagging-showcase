mechanismrepeated cannula and injector insertion destroys tissue in Layers I-III of the mPFC on both sides of the midline, removing projection neurons that normally regulate the nucleus accumbens and basolateral amygdala, and the loss of that top-down regulation is what blocks extinction learning— “Therefore, it is conceivable that the damage caused by the microinjection procedure resulted in altered mPFC regulation of the NAc and BLC, thereby impairing extinction of EtOH-CPP.”
mechanismcumulative mechanical damage from implantation plus repeated infusions amounts to a partial lesion of a region already shown to be necessary for extinction, so the injection itself reproduces the lesion effect regardless of what is infused— “The most parsimonious explanation for these findings is that the microinjection procedure, regardless of the compound being injected, caused tissue damage to an area of the brain shown to be necessary for extinction of EtOH-CPP [ 1 ].”
mechanismThe vehicle group could not serve as the control, because whatever impaired extinction affected vehicle and drug alike— “Because of the incomplete extinction in the Vehicle group, it remained unknown if inactivation of the mPFC with bupivacaine was able to impair extinction.”
mechanismOnly a group that never had the procedure could reveal it, which is why the no-surgery control was added in the second experiment— “Finally, an additional control group that did not receive surgery was included (No Sx group) in order to assess the effects of the handling portion of the microinjection procedure on extinction learning.”
mechanismThe proposed cause: the repeated procedure damages the very region it is delivering into, amounting to a partial lesion— “The most parsimonious explanation for these findings is that the microinjection procedure, regardless of the compound being injected, caused tissue damage to an area of the brain shown to be necessary for extinction of EtOH-CPP [ 1 ].”
mechanismBecause the cannula sat on the midline, the damage was bilateral rather than confined to one hemisphere— “Importantly, because these cannulae were located on the sagital midline, tissue damage to these layers of the cortex affected both hemispheres.”
mechanismThe damaged layers contain projection neurons to accumbens and amygdala whose activity changes with drug cues, which is a route from local damage to the behavioural failure— “Retrograde tracing experiments have shown that Layers II/III of the mPFC contain dense populations of projection neurons that terminate in both the nucleus accumbens (NAc) and basolateral amygdala complex…
mechanismSurgery and anaesthesia were ruled out by comparison with their own earlier study matched on strain, frame, coordinates, anaesthesia and surgeon
