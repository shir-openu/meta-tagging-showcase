findingThe mouse neocortex resolves into eight subnetworks: four somatic sensorimotor, two medial and two lateral, each with its own topology, communicating through selected cortical areas. — “The connectivity matrices and cortical map revealed that the entire cortex is organized into four somatic sensorimotor, two medial, and two lateral subnetworks that display unique topologies and can interact through select cortical areas.”
findingAnterograde and retrograde tracing agreed on 89% of matrix entries, cross-validating the connectivity dataset. — “Iterating through each matrix entry, 89% of connections exhibited mutual labeling by both anterograde and retrograde labeling methods, suggesting that the datasets were significantly similar.”
findingThe claustrum's cortical connectivity is asymmetric: it receives bilateral cortical input but sends output almost exclusively to the same hemisphere. — “The intracortical connectivity of the CLA displays a unique asymmetric pattern: cortical inputs to the CLA are bilateral, but outputs from CLA are almost exclusively ipsilateral”
findingThe temporal association, ectorhinal and perirhinal areas are back-labelled from layers 2 and 5a of the entire neocortex except ACAv, RSP and VISp, meaning this subnetwork collects input from nearly the whole cortex. — “A retrograde tracer injection in all three structures back labeled neurons in layers 2 and 5a of the entire neocortex with the exception of the ACAv, RSP, and VISp”
findingBecause every main node of the four somatic sensorimotor subnetworks is reciprocally connected, sensory and motor areas can interact directly without routing through higher-order association cortex. — “This organization allows direct interactions between sensory and motor areas in the absence of higher order association areas.”
findingThe medial subnetwork contains direct reciprocal connections between visual, auditory and primary somatosensory areas, which argues against mammalian primary sensory cortices being strictly unisensory. — “This supports the concept of cross-modal modulation, which challenges the idea that mammalian primary sensory cortices are strictly unisensory”
findingThe second medial subnetwork is a serial chain running from dorsal subiculum to RSPv to ACAv and then to the infralimbic, prelimbic and medial orbitofrontal areas. — “The second medial subnetwork is topologically distinct from the first in that it successively transmits information from the SUBd to the RSPv to the ACAv and then to the ILA, PL, and ORBm.”
findingVentral retrosplenial cortex is the sole neocortical area receiving dense dorsal subicular input, making it the entry point for dorsal hippocampal information into neocortex. — “The RSPv is the only neocortical recipient of dense inputs from the SUBd, through which information processed in the dorsal hippocampus can reach the neocortex”
findingThe dorsolateral prefrontal cortex is where all four somatic sensorimotor subnetworks converge. — “The PFCdl receives a confluence of information from all four somatic sensorimotor subnetworks.”
findingThe ventromedial prefrontal cortex is where the medial and lateral subnetworks converge, letting external sensory and internal visceral/gustatory information be integrated. — “The PFCvm receives convergent inputs from the medial and lateral subnetworks and provides an interface for integrating or communicating information regarding external stimuli (such as visual, auditory, somatic sensory) and internal stimuli (such as visceral and gustatory information).”
finding89% of connections were labelled by both the anterograde and retrograde methods, so the two datasets largely agree — “Iterating through each matrix entry, 89% of connections exhibited mutual labeling by both anterograde and retrograde labeling methods, suggesting that the datasets were significantly similar.”
findingclustering placed every node into a small number of distinct cortico-cortical modules — “The clustering demonstrated that all nodes fall into a few relatively distinct cortico-cortical subnetwork modules”
findingthe four somatic sensorimotor modules each formed a separate subnetwork — “Each of the four somatic sensorimotor modules ( orofaciopharyngeal, upper limb, lower limb/trunk, and whisker ) formed distinct subnetworks.”
findingthe claustrum and lateral entorhinal area have broad unique connections consistent with a hub role — “The insular and temporal areas along the lateral aspect of the cortex formed two distinct clusters and the broad and unique intracortical connections of the claustrum and lateral entorhinal area suggest they may serve as hubs, or regions of high network interaction ( Sporns, 2010 ).”
findingclaustral connectivity is asymmetric: inputs bilateral, outputs almost exclusively ipsilateral — “The intracortical connectivity of the CLA displays a unique asymmetric pattern: cortical inputs to the CLA are bilateral, but outputs from CLA are almost exclusively ipsilateral”
findingthe temporal subnetwork receives input from and projects back to nearly the entire neocortex — “A retrograde tracer injection in all three structures back labeled neurons in layers 2 and 5a of the entire neocortex with the exception of the ACAv, RSP, and VISp ( Figure 5C-D ) suggesting that this subnetwork receives input from nearly the entire neocortex.”
findingwithin the somatic sensorimotor subnetworks, sensory and motor areas interact directly without higher-order association areas — “Within the somatic sensorimotor network, all main somatic nodes within the four subnetworks are heavily and reciprocally connected. This organization allows direct interactions between sensory and motor areas in the absence of higher order association areas.”
findingthe lateral subnetworks are points of massive convergence, each connected to a large number of cortical areas — “The lateral subnetworks represent a point of massive convergence in the cortex. Interactions are centered on two major components: the AI in the anterolateral insula subnetwork, and the TEa/PERI/ECT complex in the posterolateral temporal subnetwork. Each receives input from, and projects back to, an extensive number of cortical areas.”
findingeach subnetwork converges onto a discrete territory of prefrontal cortex — “Projections from each of the identified subnetworks topographically converge onto discrete regions of the prefrontal cortex.”
findingneighbouring prefrontal zones are largely segregated from one another despite adjacency — “The relative segregation of these prefrontal zones combined with their specific cortical inputs and subcortical targets may help define their unique processing role and contribution to behavior.”
