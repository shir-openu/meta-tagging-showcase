mechanismTHE FIELD'S GOAL IS POSED AS A QUESTION AND THEN REPORTED TO HAVE NO AGREED FORM: will a program ever convert English text into a data structure that UNAMBIGUOUSLY AND COMPLETELY DESCRIBES THE MEANING - and AMONG NUMEROUS PROBLEMS, NO CONSENSUS HAS EMERGED ABOUT THE FORM OF SUCH A DATA STRUCTURE. The obstacle named first is not difficulty but the absence of agreement on what the answer would look like— “Will a computer program ever be able to convert a piece of English text into a data structure that unambiguously and completely describes the meaning of the natural language text? Among numerous problems, no consensus has emerged about the form of such a data structure.”
mechanismTHE FIELD'S TASKS ARE NAMED AS REDUCED OBJECTIVES ADOPTED BECAUSE THE REAL QUESTION IS UNRESOLVED: UNTIL SUCH FUNDAMENTAL PROBLEMS ARE RESOLVED, computer scientists MUST SETTLE FOR REDUCED OBJECTIVES - extracting SIMPLER REPRESENTATIONS describing RESTRICTED ASPECTS of the textual information. **What the field measures itself on is described as a substitute, in the paper's second sentence**— “Until such fundamental Artificial Intelligence problems are resolved, computer scientists must settle for reduced objectives: extracting simpler representations describing restricted aspects of the textual information.”
mechanismTHE SAME REPRESENTATIONS ARE GIVEN TWO DIFFERENT WARRANTS, AND ONE OF THEM IS A BELIEF: they are OFTEN MOTIVATED BY SPECIFIC APPLICATIONS, for instance bag-of-words for information retrieval - and they CAN ALSO BE MOTIVATED BY OUR BELIEF THAT THEY CAPTURE SOMETHING MORE GENERAL about natural language. An instrumental justification and an epistemic one, offered for the same objects— “These simpler representations are often motivated by specific applications, for instance, bag-of-words variants for information retrieval. These representations can also be motivated by our belief that they capture something more general about natural language.”
mechanismTHE ANNOTATIONS EXIST IN ORDER TO MAKE SYSTEMS COMPARABLE, AND THE BENCHMARKS ARE CREDITED WITH DRIVING THE RESEARCH: text corpora HAVE BEEN MANUALLY ANNOTATED with such data structures IN ORDER TO COMPARE THE PERFORMANCE of various systems, and THE AVAILABILITY OF STANDARD BENCHMARKS HAS STIMULATED RESEARCH. The ground truth's purpose is comparison, and the comparison is what made the field move— “Text corpora have been manually annotated with such data structures in order to compare the performance of various systems. The availability of standard benchmarks has stimulated research in Natural Language Processing (NLP)”
mechanismTHE METHOD IS DEFINED BY WHAT IT REFUSES TO USE, AND THE COST IS STATED IN THE SAME SENTENCE: the versatility is achieved BY TRYING TO AVOID TASK-SPECIFIC ENGINEERING AND THEREFORE DISREGARDING A LOT OF PRIOR KNOWLEDGE - instead of exploiting MAN-MADE INPUT FEATURES carefully optimized for each task. What is given up is named as the price of what is gained— “This versatility is achieved by trying to avoid task-specific engineering and therefore disregarding a lot of prior knowledge. Instead of exploiting man-made input features carefully optimized for each task, our system learns internal representations on the basis of vast amounts of mostly unlabeled training data.”
mechanismTHE GROUND TRUTH ITSELF COMES IN TWO VOCABULARIES, AND THE FIX IS TO PICK ONE: the ground truth for three of the tasks IS PROVIDED USING TWO DIFFERENT TAGGING SCHEMES, and IN ORDER TO ELIMINATE THIS ADDITIONAL SOURCE OF VARIATIONS the authors DECIDED TO USE THE MOST EXPRESSIVE scheme FOR ALL TASKS. **Two naming systems for the same annotations, normalised by choosing the more expressive - not the more common**— “The ground truth for the NER, CHUNK, and SRL tasks is provided using two different tagging schemes. In order to eliminate this additional source of variations, we have decided to use the most expressive IOBES tagging scheme for all tasks.”
mechanismTHE CHOICE OF HYPER-PARAMETERS IS CONCEDED TO BE ONE OF MANY EQUALLY GOOD ONES, WITH THE UNEXPLORED SPACE NAMED: although they ended up with A PARTICULAR CHOICE, MANY OTHER CHOICES ARE ALMOST EQUALLY AS GOOD - ALTHOUGH PERHAPS THERE ARE OTHERS THAT ARE BETTER, AS WE COULD NOT DO A FULL GRID SEARCH. The reported configuration is presented as a sample rather than an optimum— “However, we should say we believe that although we ended up with a particular choice of parameters, many other choices are almost equally as good, although perhaps there are others that are better as we could not do a full grid search.”
mechanismTHE SCALE OF THE EXPERIMENT IS ATTRIBUTED TO THE HARDWARE RATHER THAN TO THE SCIENCE, WRYLY: very long training times MAKE SUCH STRATEGIES NECESSARY FOR THE FORESEEABLE FUTURE - and IF WE HAD BEEN GIVEN COMPUTERS TEN TIMES FASTER, WE PROBABLY WOULD HAVE FOUND USES FOR DATASETS TEN TIMES BIGGER. The design is described as a function of the machines available— “Very long training times make such strategies necessary for the foreseeable future: if we had been given computers ten times faster, we probably would have found uses for datasets ten times bigger.”

Failures are gathered as evidence about a practice rather than about the groups that attempted it— “On the other hand, Sutton and McCallum (2005a) obtain results somewhat below the state-of-the-art using joint decoding for SRL and syntactic parsing. Musillo and Merlo (2006) also describe a negative result at the same joint task.”
