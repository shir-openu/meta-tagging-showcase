phenomenonbidirectional optogenetic control of individual inhibitory neurons in freely-moving mice— “Using these devices, the same cortical PV cell could be activated and silenced.”
claim-typemethod— “We developed a lens-free approach for constructing dual-color headmounted, fiber-based optical units: any two wavelengths can be combined.”
method — evidence— “Here, we present a novel design for a dual-color, headmounted, fiber-optic based device combined with recording electrodes.”
transferfrom laser optics / photonics: The simple astigmatic Gaussian beam model, a solution of the paraxial Helmholtz equation from laser-beam physics, is imported to predict coupling efficiency and set the optical pathway lengths of the implantable neural device.— “Like the Gaussian beam model, the SAG model is a solution of the paraxial Helmholtz equation.”
mechanismChoosing an inhibitory (PV) opsin-expressing cell reverses the sign of the expected synaptic side-effect, so any blue-light-driven rate increase in the tagged cell must come from direct illumination rather than from trans-synaptic drive— “when the opsin-expressing cell is inhibitory, synaptic effects are reversed and any rate increase following blue light illumination stems from direct illumination (with the caveat of disynaptic dis-inhibition).”
mechanismOptically driven PV cells inhibit their postsynaptic pyramidal partners, so activating the PV cell lowers pyramidal firing and silencing it raises pyramidal firing— “These observations are consistent with inhibitory synaptic connections between the optically manipulated PV cells and the putative PYR”
mechanisma recorded neuron can inherit a rate change it never received light for With excitatory-cell expression, a non-illuminated opsin-free neuron may inherit a rate increase or decrease through glutamatergic connections from directly illuminated pre-synaptic neurons - which is why the inhibitory-cell design reverses the sign and makes the inference possible.— “a nonilluminated/opsin-free recorded neuron may inherit a rate increase/decrease via glutamatergic connections from directlyilluminated, opsin-expressing pre-synaptic neurons”
extends the authors' earlier GRIN-lens waveguide dual-color device (references [16], [17])— “We have recently reported such an advance [16], [17], but that approach was tailored to specific wavelengths (405 and 638 nm), required custom-made parts (e.g. GRIN lenses), utilized a full nanofabrication facility, and was applied only to head-fixed anesthetized animals.”
extends previous demonstrations of activating and silencing the same neuron in brain slices, a head-fixed mouse and a freely-moving rat (references [8], [26], [16], [15])— “This is not the first time that the same neuron is both activated and silenced optically: previous demonstrations were in brain slices [8], [26], in a head-fixed mouse [16], and in a freely-moving rat [15].”
