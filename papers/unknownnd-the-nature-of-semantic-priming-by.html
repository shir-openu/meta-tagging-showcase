findingWhen the task made the response mapping non-spatial so that only embodied priming could operate, no priming appeared at all — neither facilitation nor inhibition. — “no priming effect (neither facilitatory nor inhibitory; E5).”
findingIn the identical paradigm with gender primes and gender symbols, where embodiment was impossible but evidence accumulation was possible, facilitatory priming reappeared. — “effect could arise, priming does emerge, and it is facilitatory (E6).”
findingThe pattern gives a case where unconscious words are processed semantically yet activate no sensorimotor representation, so motor activation is not an automatic or necessary part of semantic processing. — “(lack of awareness) where we see semantic processing, but no sensorimotor activation.”
