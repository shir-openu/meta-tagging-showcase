phenomenoncontext-specific improvements in interference resolution (priming of control)— “We used fMRI to test the priming of control hypothesis, the proposition that implicit contextual cues can trigger bottom-up retrieval of specific top-down control settings.”
claim-typemechanism— “These results suggest that top-down control states can be bound into episodic event representations and can subsequently be primed by other features of those representations.”
method — evidence— “Here, we harnessed functional magnetic resonance imaging in humans to test whether these behavioral findings truly reflect online, bottom-up priming of top-down attentional control settings.”
mechanismTop-down control settings are bound into episodic event-file representations, so that later presentation of another feature of that representation (the contextual cue) implicitly retrieves and reinstates the control setting.— “we propose that top-down control settings associated with specific contextual information can be bound into episodic “event file” representations, and that, subsequently, their retrieval can be implicitly primed by other features of that representation”
mechanismExperienced conflict triggers an arousal/noradrenergic response which, via Hebbian associative learning, binds the current bottom-up context representation to the top-down task/biasing representation.— “According to the model, the experience of conflict elicits an arousal response (accompanied by noradrenalin release) that in turn promotes the binding of current bottom-up stimulus/context representations and top-down task representations/biasing processes via Hebbian (associative) learning”
extends Vandenberghe et al., 2001; Yantis et al., 2002; Serences et al., 2004; Shulman et al., 2009— “These findings extend the established role of the mSPL in mediating explicitly cued shifts of attention ( Vandenberghe et al., 2001 ; Yantis et al., 2002 ; Serences et al., 2004 ; Shulman et al., 2009 ) to include the retrieval and execution of implicitly primed control settings.”
replicates Crump et al., 2006— “Furthermore, contrary to the suspicion that that this phenomenon merely reflects aggregate congruency sequence effects, context-specific improvements in interference resolution were dissociable from such trial-by-trial “conflict adaptation” effects in the current study, verifying previous reports ( Crump et al., 2006 ).”
senseA FALSE DICHOTOMY, NAMED AS SUCH: `Recent behavioral findings, however, suggest that this juxtaposition of automatic and controlled processing may in fact represent A FALSE DICHOTOMY.` The distinction between bottom-up association and deliberate top-down control is the oldest working partition in this literature, and the paper's contribution is to show a case that sits on both sides of it - an ASSOCIATIVE trigger for a CONTROL state— “Recent behavioral findings, however, suggest that this juxtaposition of automatic and controlled processing may in fact represent a false dichotomy”
sensePRIMING APPLIED TO A CONTROL SETTING - a fifth kind of thing for the word in this corpus, beside a concept, a behaviour, a goal and a sentence structure. `Top-down control states can be BOUND INTO EPISODIC EVENT REPRESENTATIONS and can subsequently be PRIMED by other features of those representations.` The claim is that a control configuration is storable and retrievable like any other feature of an episode, so the machinery already built for priming concepts applies to it unchanged— “These results suggest that top-down control states can be bound into episodic event representations and can subsequently be primed by other features of those representations”
senseTHE MIRROR OF Kiefer2012, tagged earlier tonight. Kiefer argued that top-down configuration GATES unconscious processing - control reaching down into the automatic. This paper argues the other direction: an automatic associative cue TRIGGERS a control state. Between them the boundary is crossed both ways, and neither needs the other to be wrong. What is left of `automatic` after both is the pair of criteria Kiefer kept - not conscious, not deliberately initiated - and nothing about autonomy— “However, recent behavioral studies have cast doubt on the validity of this dichotomy, showing that implicit contextual cues can modulate performance in a way suggestive of an associative triggering of specific top-down control states”
senseAWARENESS CHECKED, NOT ASSUMED, AND THE CONCLUSION IS DEFINITIONAL: the effect occurred `despite the participant's lack of awareness of systematic variation in conflict frequency, illustrating the implicit nature of the phenomenon and delivering further evidence QUESTIONING THE NOTION THAT CONTROLLED PROCESSING IS INHERENTLY VOLITIONAL`. A survey after scanning quantified what participants noticed. The target is not a finding but a property the word `controlled` is usually taken to carry— “Crucially, this “pure” expression of contextual conflict-control occurred despite the participant's lack of awareness of systematic variation in conflict frequency, illustrating the implicit nature of the phenomenon and delivering further evidence questioning the notion that controlled processing is inherently volitional”

controlthe first trial of each run and all responses faster than 150 ms or slower than 2000 ms were discarded - 1.6 per cent of trials — held — rules out that the mean is pulled by anticipations and lapses rather than by the manipulation— “Before analyzing the performance data, we excluded the first trial of each run and all trials with excessively fast/slow responses (>150 ms/<2000 ms; 1.6% of all trials).”
