mechanismTHE CLASS IS A SIZE RANGE AND NOTHING ELSE: microplastics are `synthetic solid particles ... WITH SIZE RANGING FROM 1 um TO 5 mm ... which are insoluble in water`. Composition, origin and shape are all left open - `regular or irregular shape`, `either primary or secondary manufacturing origin`. ONLY THE TWO NUMBERS ARE BINDING— “Microplastics (MPs) are defined by [ 17 ] as “synthetic solid particles or polymeric matrices, with regular or irregular shape and with size ranging from 1 μm to 5 mm, of either primary or secondary manufacturing origin, which are insoluble in water.”
mechanismand the class below it ABUTS IT EXACTLY: nanoplastics are `particles that are UNINTENTIONALLY PRODUCED within the size range FROM 1 TO 1000 nm`. 1000 nm IS 1 um. The boundary between the two classes is one number with nothing behind it - AND THE NANOPLASTIC DEFINITION SMUGGLES IN A CRITERION THAT IS NOT PHYSICAL AT ALL: a 500 nm particle made on purpose is not a nanoplastic under it, and an identical one made by accident is— “The authors of [ 111 ] observed the Pb absorption capacity on nanoplastics (particles that are unintentionally produced within the size range from 1 to 1000 nm [ 113 ]) that were produced from microplastics that were collected on a beach exposed to the North Atlantic Gyre.”
mechanismthe additives are NOT PART OF THE PLASTIC: `the additives, in almost all cases, ARE NOT CHEMICALLY BOUND to the plastic polymer; only some flame retardants are polymerized with plastic molecules, becoming part of the polymeric chain`. That is why a particle can act as a vector - what it carries can leave it— “The additives, in almost all cases, are not chemically bound to the plastic polymer; only some flame retardants are polymerized with plastic molecules, becoming part of the polymeric chain”
mechanismand EVERY BARRIER HAS ITS OWN NUMBER: <150 um crosses the gastrointestinal epithelium, <2.5 um enters by endocytosis through M cells, <100 nm penetrates the corneous layer of skin, ~20 um penetrates organs and ~10 um reaches all of them. THE BIOLOGY IS A LADDER OF SIZE THRESHOLDS, and the class boundary at 1 um sits in the middle of it rather than at any of them— “Once ingested, particles smaller than 2.5 μm can enter the gastrointestinal tract ( Figure 6 ) through endocytosis by M cells (specialized epithelial cells of the mucosa-associated lymphoid tissues) of Peyer”
