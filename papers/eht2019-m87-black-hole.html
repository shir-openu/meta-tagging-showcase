mechanismthe first images are made by four teams working independently and without interaction, in the authors' words to minimize shared bias - and each team nonetheless recovered a ring of similar diameter brightened to the south— “In the first stage, four teams worked independently to reconstruct * the first EHT images of M87 using an early engineering data release. mechanisma parameter combination is admitted to the Top-Set only if it both fits the observed data AND reconstructs four known geometries - ring, crescent, filled disk, asymmetric double - from synthetic data, so the imaging parameters are validated against ground truth and not only against the object under study— “We determined a “Top- Set” of parameter combinations that both produced images of M87* that were consistent with the observed data and that reconstructed accurate images from synthetic data sets corresponding to four known geometric models (ring, crescent, filled disk, and asymmetric double source).”
mechanismbecause the reconstructed resolutions differ between pipelines, each image is blurred with a circular Gaussian to one common and deliberately conservative resolution before use— “Because the angular resolutions of the reconstructed images vary among the pipelines, we blurred each image with a circular Gaussian to a common, conservative angular resolution of 20 μas.”
