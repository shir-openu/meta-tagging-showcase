phenomenondescent for sheaves on compact Hausdorff spaces— “The first goal of these notes is to explain some descent results for ∞-categories of sheaves on locally compact Hausdorff spaces.”
claim-typeexistence— “Specifically, given a compactly assembled ∞-category ℰ, we show that the functor sending a locally compact Hausdorff space 𝑋 to the ∞-category Shpost(𝑋; ℰ) of Postnikov complete ℰ-valued sheaves on 𝑋 satisfies descent for proper surjections.”
method — evidence— “Section 2 proves Theorem 0.5 and derives some consequences in shape theory.”
mechanismDescent fails because every compact Hausdorff space is covered by a profinite set, and sheaves on profinite sets are already Postnikov complete while sheaves on a general compact Hausdorff space are not, so the target of the limit is forced to satisfy a completeness the source lacks.— “The reason for this failure of descent is that every compact Hausdorff space admits a surjection from a profinite set, and the ∞-category of sheaves on a profinite set satisfies a strong completeness property which the ∞-category of (hyper)sheaves on a general compact Hausdorff space does not satisfy.”
extends Dyckhoff [7, Theorem 3.11; 8] and Clausen–Scholze [23, Theorem 3.2] comparison of sheaf and condensed cohomology— “We extend this result in two directions: to locally compact Hausdorff spaces and to very general coefficients.”
builds-on Clausen–Scholze's condensed/pyknotic mathematics, Barwick–Haine, and Lurie's work on ultracategories— “Our motivation comes from condensed/pyknotic mathematics developed by Clausen–Scholze [20; 21; 22; 23], in our joint work with Barwick [2, Chapter 13; 3], and in Lurie’s work on ultracategories [17; 18, Chapter 4].”

findingFor a compactly assembled coefficient ∞-category, the assignment of the ∞-category of Postnikov complete sheaves to a locally compact Hausdorff space satisfies descent along proper surjections.— “Specifically, given a compactly assembled ∞-category ℰ, we show that the functor sending a locally compact Hausdorff space 𝑋 to the ∞-category Shpost(𝑋; ℰ) of Postnikov complete ℰ-valued sheaves on 𝑋 satisfies descent for proper surjections.”
findingThe same result gives proper descent for left complete derived ∞-categories and shows that Postnikov complete sheaves form a sheaf on compact Hausdorff spaces for the topology of finite jointly surjective families.— “This implies proper descent for left complete derived ∞-categories and that the functor Shpost(−; ℰ) is a sheaf on the category of compact Hausdorff spaces equipped with the topology of finite jointly surjective families.”
findingNeither ordinary sheaves nor hypersheaves define a sheaf of ∞-topoi on the site of compact Hausdorff spaces, so the descent statement genuinely fails without Postnikov completion.— “The presheaves Sh, Shhyp ∶ Comp op → LTop∞ are not sheaves.”
findingPostnikov complete sheaves on a locally compact Hausdorff space embed fully faithfully into condensed objects over that space.— “Then the pullback functor 𝑐∗,post ∶ Shpost(𝑋; ℰ) → Shpost(Comp∕𝑋; ℰ) is fully faithful.”
findingConsequently the sheaf cohomology and the condensed cohomology of a locally compact Hausdorff space coincide.— “This implies that the condensed and sheaf cohomologies of a locally compact Hausdorff space agree.”
findingFor a space carrying a locally finite CW structure, condensed, singular and sheaf cohomology all coincide.— “As a consequence, the condensed, singular, and sheaf cohomologies of a topological space admitting a locally finite CW structure all agree”
findingSheaves on a profinite set are Postnikov complete, whereas sheaves on the Hilbert cube are not, so Postnikov completeness is not automatic for compact Hausdorff spaces.— “The ∞-topos of sheaves on a profinite set is Postnikov complete. On the other hand, the ∞-topos of sheaves on the Hilbert cube [0, 1] is not Postnikov complete.”
findingThe protruncated shape functor on compact Hausdorff spaces fails to preserve pullbacks, even though it preserves finite coproducts, cofiltered limits and Čech-nerve realizations.— “The protruncated shape does not preserve pullbacks of compact Hausdorff spaces.”
