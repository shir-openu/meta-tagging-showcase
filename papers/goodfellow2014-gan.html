phenomenonadversarial training of generative models— “In the proposed adversarial nets framework, the generative model is pitted against an adversary: a discriminative model that learns to determine whether a sample is from the model distribution or the data distribution.”
claim-typemethod— “We propose a new framework for estimating generative models via an adversarial process, in which we simultaneously train two models: a generative model G that captures the data distribution, and a discriminative model D that estimates the probability that a sample came from the training data rather than G.”
method — evidence— “We propose a new generative model estimation procedure that sidesteps these difficulties.”
transferfrom game theory: a minimax two-player game between a generator and a discriminator, adapting game-theoretic adversarial competition to train generative models— “This framework corresponds to a minimax two-player game”
mechanismadversarial training: a generator and discriminator compete in a minimax game - the generator learns to produce samples that fool the discriminator (which learns real vs fake), driving the generator toward the data distribution— “The generative model can be thought of as analogous to a team of counterfeiters, trying to produce fake currency and use it without detection, while the discriminative model is analogous to the police, trying to detect the counterfeit currency”
builds-on game theory (minimax two-player game)— “This framework corresponds to a minimax two-player game”
builds-on SML/PCD training [31, 29]— “This strategy is analogous to the way that SML/PCD [31, 29] training maintains samples from a Markov chain from one learning step to the next in order to avoid burning in a Markov chain as part of the inner loop of learning.”
builds-on the Parzen-window likelihood evaluation procedure of Breuleux et al. [8]— “This procedure was introduced in Breuleux et al. [8] and used for various generative models for which the exact likelihood is not tractable [25, 3, 5].”
extends the generative stochastic network (GSN) framework [5]— “Compared to GSNs, the adversarial nets framework does not require a Markov chain for sampling.”
extends noise-contrastive estimation (NCE) [13]— “However, rather than fitting a separate discriminative model, the generative model itself is used to discriminate generated data from samples a fixed noise distribution.”
sensethe training objective - AS ANALYSED vs AS TRAINED: the minimax value function of Eq. 1 is what the theorems are about; the implementation maximises a different function with the same fixed point, because the analysed one saturates early in learning— “This objective function results in the same fixed point of the dynamics of G and D but provides much stronger gradients early in learning”
senselog-likelihood - AS A PARZEN-WINDOW ESTIMATE: not computed from the model, but from a Gaussian fitted to samples drawn from it, because the model has no tractable density— “We estimate probability of the test set data under pg by fitting a Gaussian Parzen window to the samples generated with G and reporting the log-likelihood under this distribution”
senseadversarial nets - THE PAPER'S OWN NAME, and it names the special case, not the framework: the framework is general and 'adversarial nets' is what the authors call the multilayer-perceptron instance of it— “We refer to this special case as adversarial nets”
