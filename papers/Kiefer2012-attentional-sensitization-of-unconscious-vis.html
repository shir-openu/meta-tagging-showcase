phenomenonsubliminal masked priming
claim-typemechanism
transferfrom computational neuroscience / neural network modelling and single-cell neurophysiology: The gain parameter of neural network models, together with attentional gain modulation observed in primate single-cell recordings, is imported as the computational mechanism of the psychological attentional sensitization model — “Gain is a parameter in neural network modeling, which influences the probability that a neuron fires at a given activation level ( Hamker, 2005 ).”
mechanismTask representations raise the neuronal gain of task-relevant processing pathways and lower it in task-irrelevant ones, so unconscious stimuli are amplified or attenuated according to their congruence with the current task set — “Processing in task-relevant pathways is enhanced by increasing the gain of the neurons in the corresponding areas, whereas processing in task-irrelevant pathways is attenuated by a decrease of the gain ( Reynolds, Pasternak, & Desimone, 2000 ).”
mechanismPrefrontal top-down signals adjust the sensitivity of posterior processing pathways to incoming sensory input, whether or not that input reaches awareness — “unconscious cognition is assumed to be controlled by top-down signals from prefrontal cortex ( Haynes et al., 2007 ) that increase or decrease the sensitivity of processing pathways for incoming sensory input”
mechanismBackward inhibition suppresses the just-abandoned task set while the next task is prepared, which reverses the direction of the sensitization effect at long intervals — “This result pattern is compatible with the notion of a backward inhibition mechanism that suppresses irrelevant task sets in preparation of the next task”
mechanismAttentional sensitization applies to unconscious and conscious stimuli alike, being blind to whether the stimulus is consciously perceived — “We thus assume that an attentional sensitizing mechanism gradually enhances and attenuates stimulus processing irrespective of whether the stimulus is consciously perceived or not”
relation — “Hence, in contrast to classical theories of automaticity ( Posner & Snyder, 1975 ; Schneider & Shiffrin, 1977 ), automatic processes elicited by unconscious visual stimuli are under attentional control to some extent.”
relation — “We have therefore recently developed the attentional sensitization model of unconscious cognition ( Kiefer & Martens, 2010 ) that aims at explaining the various influences of top-down attention on different forms of unconscious automatic processing.”
differs inattentional sensitization → autonomous — WHETHER AN UNCONSCIOUS PROCESS CAN BE REACHED FROM ABOVE - the classical account makes autonomy definitional, this one makes prior attentional configuration a PRECONDITION for the process running at all. Not a weaker version of the same claim: the direction of dependence is reversed — “autonomous and independent of higher-level cognitive influences”
effect directiontop-down control → subliminal priming — increases — “reliably modulate subliminal priming effects”
effect directiontask sets → subliminal priming — increases — “is modulated by task sets”
effect directionattentional resources → subliminal priming — increases — “depends on attentional resources”
replicationuntested
limitationThe model's central neural assumption, a prefrontal top-down signal modulating posterior pathway sensitivity, has not yet been tested in detail. — “The assumption of the attentional sensitization model that control of unconscious processes is exerted by a prefrontal top-down signal, which in turn influences the sensitivity of processing pathways in posterior brain areas, should be tested in more detail by means of fMRI and electrophysiological recording techniques.”
limitationThe theory is not yet formally specified, so its predictions about the dynamics of attentional control remain imprecise. — “Finally, formal computational modeling of the proposed attentional sensitization mechanism (see e.g., Trapp, Schroll, & Hamker, 2012 ) is desirable to render our theory more precise and to derive further empirically testable predictions regarding the dynamics of attentional control of conscious and unconscious visual processing in various task domains.”
Content tags
content-observations: finding · quantity · variable · definition · key-term — grounded in the full text
findingA semantic induction task performed shortly before a masked prime enhanced subsequent subliminal semantic priming in both reaction time and N400, whereas a perceptual induction task attenuated it. — “At the short RPI, semantic priming effects on RT and N400 ERP component were obtained when a semantic task set was induced immediately before subliminal prime presentation, whereas a previously induced perceptual task set attenuated priming.”
findingThe pattern reversed at the long response-prime interval, with priming present after the perceptual but not the semantic induction task. — “At the long RPI, significant priming was found after the perceptual induction task, but not after the semantic task.”
findingSemantic and visuo-motor priming were doubly dissociated by induction task type, with visuo-motor priming appearing only after the perceptual task. — “Visuo-motor priming, depending on access to visual shape information, was only observed after the perceptual but not after the semantic induction task.”
findingAttentional sensitization operates even within the perceptual domain: only a shape induction task, not a colour one, produced occipito-parietal ERP priming. — “We found occipito-parietal ERP priming effects only subsequent to the shape induction task.”
findingA phonological letter-classification induction task, which discourages word reading, abolished subsequent subliminal semantic priming. — “In the latter experiment, semantic priming effects were only observed following the semantic classification, but not following the phonological letter classification induction task.”
quantity200 or 800 ms — The two response-prime intervals between the induction-task response and masked prime onset, manipulated to track how the task set decays — “We varied the time interval (either 200 or 800 ms) between the response to the induction task and the onset of the prime (RPI) in order to obtain information on how the influence of the induction task on masked priming unfolds over time.”
quantityabout 600 ms — Duration a task representation is claimed (from the task-switching literature) to remain active after task completion, motivating the choice of the two RPIs — “because the task switching literature suggests that a task representation is active for about 600 ms after task completion”
quantity40 targets — Large target set size in the cited Kiesel et al. expectancy manipulation, contrasted with a small four-target set — “In one condition, target set size was large (40 targets) so that a variety of words from different semantic categories was expected.”
variabletype of induction task (semantic vs perceptual, phonological word vs phonological letter, shape vs colour) (independent) — “Across experiments, we systematically varied the type of induction task to specify the attentional mechanisms which enhance or attenuate unconscious processing.”
variableresponse-prime interval (RPI) of 200 or 800 ms (independent) — “We varied the time interval (either 200 or 800 ms) between the response to the induction task and the onset of the prime (RPI) in order to obtain information on how the influence of the induction task on masked priming unfolds over time.”
variablesubliminal priming effect, measured as reaction time difference and N400 amplitude difference between related and unrelated pairs (dependent) — “In all experiments, subliminal priming effects were assessed with behavioral (shorter reaction time [RT] to semantically related than to unrelated prime target pairs) and ERP measures (larger amplitude of the N400 ERP component, an electrophysiological index of semantic processing, for semantically unrelated than for related prime-target pairs).”
variabledifficulty and verbal/non-verbal nature of the induction tasks (control) — “Across experiments, the difficulty of the semantic and perceptual induction tasks as well as their verbal or non-verbal nature was systematically varied.”
definitionattentional sensitization model of unconscious cognition — “We have therefore recently developed the attentional sensitization model of unconscious cognition ( Kiefer & Martens, 2010 ) that aims at explaining the various influences of top-down attention on different forms of unconscious automatic processing.”
definitiongain — “Gain is a parameter in neural network modeling, which influences the probability that a neuron fires at a given activation level ( Hamker, 2005 ).”
definitionpreemptive control — “In preemptive control , top-down influences are initiated in advance of stimulus pre-sentation.”
definitionreactive control — “Preemptive control can be exerted for both conscious and unconscious stimulus presentation, whereas only consciously perceived stimuli are susceptible to reactive control in response to ongoing or completed stimulus processing.”
definitiontask set — “we define task sets as an adaptive configuration of the cognitive system which is necessary to efficiently perform a given task”
definitionconditional automaticity — “Given this dependency on the precise configuration of the cognitive system, one might as well speak of conditional automaticity ( Bargh, 1989 ; Logan, 1989 ).”
definitionmasked visuo-motor response priming — “Masked visuo-motor response priming denotes faster responses to visual shape targets, when the masked prime (also a visual shape) indicates the same rather than a different response”
definitionmasked semantic priming — “It denotes facilitation of a response to a target word, when it is preceded by a semantically related masked prime word (e.g., chair-table ).”
definitionN400 ERP component — “Semantic priming modulates the N400 ERP component, a negative deflection peaking at about 400 ms with a centro-parietal topography ( Kutas & Hillyard, 1980 ).”
definitioninduction task paradigm — “In our induction task paradigm, prior to the masked priming procedure, participants were engaged in different induction tasks (e.g., semantic classification vs. perceptual classification) designed to induce a specific task set (e.g., semantic or perceptual task set).”

conceptautomaticity — “Given that attention and task demands are frequently found to modulate behavioral and neurophysiological effects, it is difficult to identify processes that actually meet the classical criteria for automaticity. The apparent lack of processes that fully meet the criteria of automaticity renders the classical view of automaticity unsatisfactory”
conceptautonomous — “Classical theories of automaticity assume that automatic processes elicited by unconscious stimuli are autonomous and independent of higher-level cognitive influences. In contrast to these classical conceptions, we argue that automatic processing depends on attentional amplification of task-congruent processing pathways”
conceptattentional sensitization — “we argue that automatic processing depends on attentional amplification of task-congruent processing pathways and propose an attentional sensitization model of unconscious visual processing: According to this model, unconscious visual processing is automatic in the sense that it is initiated without deliberate intention”
concepttop-down control — “However, automatic processing is susceptible to attentional top-down control and is only elicited if the cognitive system is configured accordingly. Thus, unconscious automatic processing and the notion of attentional control is not a contradiction as it has been previously thought”

These data suggest that attention enhances or attenuates unconscious visual processes”
