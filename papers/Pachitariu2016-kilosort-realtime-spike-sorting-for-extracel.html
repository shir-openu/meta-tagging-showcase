phenomenonspike sorting of high-channel-count extracellular recordings— “When multiple neurons close to the electrode fire action potentials, their spikes must be identified and assigned to the correct neuron based on the features of the recorded waveforms, a process known as spike sorting 1–15.”
claim-typemethod— “Here we introduce Kilosort, a spike sorting framework that meets these criteria, and show that it allows rapid and accurate sorting of large-scale in vivo data.”
method — evidence— “Here we overcome these limitations and present Kilosort, a new algorithm which takes advantage of a novel mathematical approach that greatly reduces the amount of calculation required, together with the computing capabilities of low-cost commercially available graphics processing units (GPUs).”
mechanismReplacing the full matrix convolution by a rank-3 low-rank template decomposition cuts the number of filtering operations by the ratio of channel count to template rank, which is what makes near-realtime sorting of hundreds of channels possible— “However, the low-rank decomposition of templates allows us to reduce the number of operations by a factor of Nchan/Nrank, where Nchan is the number of channels (typically > 100) and Nrank is the rank of the decomposed template (typically 3).”
mechanismOver-split clusters arise because the fitting algorithm splits a high-variance cluster across several templates in order to reduce total reconstruction error, which is why the resulting cluster pairs form one continuous density and can be merged automatically— “Instead, these clusters arise as a consequence of the algorithm partitioning clusters with large variance into multiple templates, so as to better explain their total variance.”
extends a previous template-matching method (numbered reference 6)— “This approach is related to a previous method 6, but that method requires a generic convex optimization that is slow for recordings with large numbers of channels.”
extends masked KlustaKwik (numbered reference 18)— “As we demonstrate with constructed ground-truth datasets, our system is more accurate than a current widely-used method 18.”
builds-on the hybrid ground truth benchmarking technique (numbered reference 18)— “We compared Kilosort and Klustakwik on 32 and 128 channel recordings, using a technique known as “hybrid ground truth” 18 (Fig. 3a-c).”
senseground truth, here - MANUFACTURED, NOT OBSERVED: the paper's own term is 'hybrid ground truth', and the data set is made by taking human-curated clusters from a previous analysis, denoising their waveforms and re-inserting them elsewhere in a real recording, so the target the sorters must recover is a cleaned copy of an earlier judgement— “To create a hybrid ground truth data set, we first selected all the clusters from a recording that had been previously analyzed with KlustaKwik, and curated by a human expert”
sensethe reference standard's provenance - THE COMPETITOR'S OUTPUT: the clusters come from a recording previously analysed with KlustaKwik, which is the algorithm Kilosort is being compared against; re-insertion at new locations makes detection a fresh problem, but the standard is still derived from one of the things measured against it— “To create a hybrid ground truth data set, we first selected all the clusters from a recording that had been previously analyzed with KlustaKwik, and curated by a human expert”
sensethe reported score - GIVEN TWICE, ONCE AFTER AN ORACLE MERGE: the same panels are repeated after greedy best possible merges, with the number of merges needed to reach that best score shown separately, so one of the two numbers is an upper bound rather than an outcome— “Same as (def) after greedy best possible merges. j, Number of merges required to reach best score”
controlthe denoised waveform SUBTRACTED from its original location after being added elsewhere — held — rules out an artificial increase in spike density at any point on the probe - a hybrid-data test that adds spikes without removing them changes the very statistic the sorter is being judged on— “To avoid increasing the spike density at any location on the probe, we also subtracted off the denoised waveform from its original location.”
