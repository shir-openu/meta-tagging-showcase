limitationThe survey deliberately stays at the level of conceptual ideas and omits the technical detail needed to make its statements precise. — “Throughout, my aim will be to highlight the main conceptual ideas of different approaches and to avoid embroiling myself in a thorough discussion of the myriad of technical details required to make all of the statements crisply precise.”
limitationThe scope is restricted to episodic RL and excludes the harder adaptive-control setting. — “This survey has focused on “episodic” reinforcement learning and has steered clear of a much harder problem: adaptive control.”
limitationThe theoretical comparison between value-function estimation and model estimation rests on worst-case upper bounds, which the author concedes is not a valid comparison. — “While comparing worst-case upper bounds is certainly not valid, it is suggestive that, as mentioned above, temporal differencing methods use only one defining equation per time step whereas model estimation uses d equations per time step.”
limitationFunction approximation, and deep RL specifically, is set aside rather than analysed. — “Note that in this section I have spent no time discussing deep reinforcement learning.”
limitationThe empirical evidence rests on a small set of simulation benchmarks that invite overfitting. — “Benchmarking is difficult, and having only a few simulation benchmarks encourages overfitting to these benchmarks.”
limitationThe label model-free does not mean the method is free of modelling; the author says modelling is heavily built into its assumptions. — “modeling is not heavily built into the assumptions of model-free RL algorithms”
limitationA discounting device had to be introduced to obtain a tractable equation, and the author names that as troubling. — “Also troubling is the fact that we had to introduce the discount factor in order to get a simple Bellman equation”
limitationThe scope claimed for the field is stated to be precisely the scope of a different, older field. — “A control engineer might be puzzled by such a definition and interject that this is precisely the scope of control theory”
