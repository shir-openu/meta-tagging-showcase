transferfrom control theory (optimal control): framing and analyzing reinforcement learning with the tools of optimal control, using the Linear Quadratic Regulator as the baseline — “the Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR) with unknown dynamics, perhaps the simplest and best-studied problem in optimal control”
mechanismA DEFINITION IS OFFERED AND THE OBJECTION TO IT IS VOICED IN THE SAME BREATH, IN ANOTHER FIELD'S VOICE: a control engineer MIGHT BE PUZZLED by the definition and interject that this is PRECISELY THE SCOPE OF CONTROL THEORY - and the consequence named is that the two communities remaining PRACTICALLY DISJOINT has led to the CO-DEVELOPMENT OF VASTLY DIFFERENT APPROACHES TO THE SAME PROBLEMS — “Reinforcement learning (RL) is the subfield of machine learning that studies how to use past data to enhance the future manipulation of a dynamical system. A control engineer might be puzzled by such a definition and interject that this is precisely the scope of control theory. That the RL and control communities remain practically disjoint has led to the co-development of vastly different approaches to the same problems.”
mechanismTHE SURVEY'S STATED PURPOSE IS TO SUPPLY A LANGUAGE, NOT A RESULT: it aims to provide A LANGUAGE for the two communities TO BEGIN COMMUNICATING, highlighting what each can learn from the other. The deliverable is a shared vocabulary between fields that already work on the same problems — “This survey aims to provide a language for the control and reinforcement learning communities to begin communicating, highlighting what each can learn from the other.”
mechanismEACH FIELD IS CHARACTERISED IN A SINGLE CLAUSE AND THE CONTRAST IS PUT IN THE MODELS: controls is the theory of designing complex actions FROM WELL-SPECIFIED MODELS, while reinforcement learning often makes intricate MODEL-FREE predictions FROM DATA ALONE - and the sentence immediately after says that YET BOTH aim at systems doing the same three things — “Controls is the theory of designing complex actions from well-specified models, while reinforcement learning often makes intricate, model-free predictions from data alone. Yet both RL and control aim to design systems that use richly structured perception, perform planning and control that adequately adapt to environmental changes, and exploit safeguards when surprised by a new scenario.”
mechanismONE FIELD IS DEFINED AS ANOTHER FIELD'S PROBLEM WITH A SINGLE CONDITION CHANGED: the main view of the survey is of reinforcement learning AS OPTIMAL CONTROL WHEN THE DYNAMICS ARE UNKNOWN - so the whole of one discipline is recast as a special case of another, and the difference between them is reduced to what is known about the system — “Reinforcement learning is the study of how to use past data to enhance the future manipulation of a dynamical system. How does this differ from ordinary machine learning? The main view of this survey is of reinforcement learning as optimal control when the dynamics are unknown.”
mechanismTHE EVALUATION WORD IS DECLARED NOT CLEARLY DEFINED AND THE COMPETING OPERATIONALISATIONS ARE SPELLED OUT: is an algorithm BEST if it crosses some reward threshold in the fewest samples, or if it achieves the highest reward given a fixed budget of samples, OR MAYBE THERE IS A MIDDLE GROUND. The word by which methods are ranked is shown to have at least three readings that would rank them differently — “Here “best” is also not clearly defined. Do we decide an algorithm is best if it crosses some reward threshold in the fewest number of samples? Or is it best if it achieves the highest reward given a fixed budget of samples? Or maybe there’s a middle ground?”
mechanismTHE SAME OPERATION HAS A DIFFERENT NAME IN THE OTHER FIELD, AND THE DIFFERENCES THAT SURVIVE THE TRANSLATION ARE NAMED: estimation of dynamical systems IS CALLED SYSTEM IDENTIFICATION IN THE CONTROL COMMUNITY - and it DIFFERS from conventional estimation because the inputs must be chosen to excite various degrees of freedom and because dynamical outputs are correlated. The translation is given AND its residue is stated — “Estimation of dynamical systems is called system identification in the control community [48]. System Identification differs from conventional estimation because one needs to carefully choose the right inputs to excite various degrees of freedom and because dynamical outputs are correlated over time with the parameters we hope to estimate, the inputs we feed to the system, and the stochastic disturbances.”
mechanismA SHORT NAME IS CHOSEN OVER THE COMMUNITY'S OWN, AND THE COMMUNITY'S IS CALLED VERBOSE: the estimated model is called THE NOMINAL MODEL and designing control as if it were true is called NOMINAL CONTROL - which is COMMONLY VERBOSELY REFERRED TO AS control under the principle of certainty equivalence. The author renames an existing concept for economy and says that is what he is doing — “The estimated model is called the nominal model, and I will refer to control design assuming the estimated model is true as nominal control. Nominal control, commonly verbosely referred to as “control under the principle of certainty equivalence,” serves as a useful baseline algorithm.”
mechanismA FIELD'S OWN CATEGORY TERM IS PUT IN SCARE QUOTES WHILE ITS CONTENTS ARE TESTED: the SO-CALLED MODEL-FREE methods popular in deep reinforcement learning are found CONSIDERABLY LESS EFFECTIVE in both theory and practice than simple model-based schemes on the baseline problem - so the label is held at arm's length in the same sentence that reports the result — “In particular, we will see that the so-called “model-free” methods popular in deep reinforcement learning are considerably less effective in both theory and practice than simple model-based schemes when applied to LQR.”
mechanismA FIELD'S CENTRAL DICHOTOMY IS ANNOUNCED AS SOMETHING TO BE DISSOLVED RATHER THAN ADJUDICATED: having laid out the split between model-free and model-based methodologies, the author states that he will argue the two perspectives CAN BE UNIFIED, COMBINING THEIR RELATIVE MERITS - the contribution is to remove the opposition, not to win it — “I then argue that model-free and model-based perspectives can be unified, combining their relative merits.”
mechanismTHE COMPARISON IS MADE POSSIBLE BY FORCING BOTH FIELDS ONTO ONE SHARED PROBLEM: the two sets of techniques are put ON THE SAME FOOTING through a case study of the linear quadratic regulator with unknown dynamics, and that baseline is what is said to ILLUMINATE THE VARIOUS TRADE-OFFS. A common benchmark is the instrument that makes two vocabularies commensurable — “Next, I try to put RL and control techniques on the same footing through a case study of the linear quadratic regulator (LQR) with unknown dynamics. This baseline will illuminate the various trade-offs associated with techniques from RL and control.” [checked against the archived source, not this rendering]
mechanismTHE ORACLE MODEL IS NAMED, AND ITS COMPLEXITY RELATIVE TO THE NEIGHBOURING FIELD IS STATED: the query protocol IS CALLED EPISODIC REINFORCEMENT LEARNING, and it is described as CONSIDERABLY MORE COMPLICATED than the oracle models typically considered in optimization, because each episode returns a complex feedback signal of states and rewards — “This is our oracle model and is called episodic reinforcement learning (See, for example Chapter 3 of Sutton and Barto [76], Chapter 2 of Puterman [58], or Dann and Brunskill [24]). We want the expected reward to be high for our derived policy, but we also need the number of oracle queries to be small. This oracle model is considerably more complicated than those typically considered in oracle models for optimization [53]. Each episode returns a complex feedback signal of states and rewards.”
mechanismA WHOLE FIELD'S COLLOQUIAL MEANING IS NAMED AND THEN SET ASIDE: most of the time when the term MACHINE LEARNING is used colloquially it refers to PREDICTION, and from that perspective even niche topics are prediction tasks - after which the paper states what makes its own subject different, that there are TWO SPECIAL VARIABLES — “Most of the time when the term machine learning is used colloquially, it refers to this sort of prediction. From this perspective, niche topics like semi-supervised learning [91] and matrix completion [34] are prediction tasks as well. By contrast, there are two special variables in reinforcement learning, u and r. The goal now is to analyze the features x and then subsequently choose a policy that emits u so that r is large.”

limitationThe survey deliberately stays at the level of conceptual ideas and omits the technical detail needed to make its statements precise. — “Throughout, my aim will be to highlight the main conceptual ideas of different approaches and to avoid embroiling myself in a thorough discussion of the myriad of technical details required to make all of the statements crisply precise.”
limitationThe scope is restricted to episodic RL and excludes the harder adaptive-control setting. — “This survey has focused on “episodic” reinforcement learning and has steered clear of a much harder problem: adaptive control.”
limitationThe theoretical comparison between value-function estimation and model estimation rests on worst-case upper bounds, which the author concedes is not a valid comparison. — “While comparing worst-case upper bounds is certainly not valid, it is suggestive that, as mentioned above, temporal differencing methods use only one defining equation per time step whereas model estimation uses d equations per time step.”
limitationFunction approximation, and deep RL specifically, is set aside rather than analysed. — “Note that in this section I have spent no time discussing deep reinforcement learning.”
limitationThe empirical evidence rests on a small set of simulation benchmarks that invite overfitting. — “Benchmarking is difficult, and having only a few simulation benchmarks encourages overfitting to these benchmarks.”
limitationThe label model-free does not mean the method is free of modelling; the author says modelling is heavily built into its assumptions. — “modeling is not heavily built into the assumptions of model-free RL algorithms”
limitationA discounting device had to be introduced to obtain a tractable equation, and the author names that as troubling. — “Also troubling is the fact that we had to introduce the discount factor in order to get a simple Bellman equation”
limitationThe scope claimed for the field is stated to be precisely the scope of a different, older field. — “A control engineer might be puzzled by such a definition and interject that this is precisely the scope of control theory”
