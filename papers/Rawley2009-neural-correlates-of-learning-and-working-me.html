transferfrom statistical physics: attractor dynamics, used to describe how a recurrent network holds a state — “Reverberatory networks, when left unperturbed, will settle into an attractor state defined by a stereotyped pattern of activity within the network ( Hopfield, 1982 ). Attractor models may undergo spontaneous oscillations, although network instantiations have also been shown to be capable of mediating sustained responses without oscillatory discharges”
transferfrom clinical neurology: the neglect syndrome, the signature deficit after parietal damage and the original evidence for parietal function — “Perhaps the signature effect of PPC damage is the neglect syndrome, typically occurring after a lesion in the right hemisphere. Disturbances include the inability to detect an object in the contralateral (left) visual hemifield (egocentric neglect) or to perceive the left side of objects, regardless of where they appear within the visual field (allocentric neglect).”
mechanismDelay-period activity is sustained because reciprocally connected pyramidal neurons re-excite each other, so discharges keep reverberating around the recurrent circuit after the stimulus is gone. — “Reverberating activity within recurrent circuits is a characteristic of this type of architecture: pyramidal neurons form reciprocal, excitatory connections between them and transmit action potentials to each other so that, even when the original stimulus is no longer present, discharges continue to reverberate in the network.”
mechanismSlow postsynaptic receptors (NMDA) keep the postsynaptic cell excited long enough for sparse presynaptic inputs to accumulate, which is what allows low-firing-rate cortical neurons to sustain reverberation. — “Given the fairly low firing rate of cortical pyramidal neurons, postsynaptic receptors with fairly slow time constants are essential for this function.”
mechanismDamage to spatial working memory nodes destroys the record of which locations have already been searched, and that failure of book-keeping in the good hemifield compounds the failure to attend to the contralesional one. — “These studies support the idea that when important nodes in the spatial working memory network are compromised or deafferented, an inability to keep track of previously searched locations in the ipsilateral hemifield may contribute to the failure to attend to the contralateral one ( Driver, 2002 ; Malhotra, 2005 ).”

findingParietal delay activity is overwritten by intervening distractors and codes whichever stimulus appeared most recently, while prefrontal neurons keep coding the behaviourally relevant one. — “Memory-related activity in the parietal cortex differs from the memory trace held in frontal cortical neurons in that it is disrupted by intervening, distracting stimuli; the activity of parietal neurons represents the most recent stimulus to appear, whether it was behaviorally relevant or not ( Constantinidis & Steinmetz, 1996 ).”
findingThe proportion of neurons showing working-memory activity is essentially the same in parietal areas 7a and LIP as in prefrontal areas 46 and FEF. — “Virtually identical percentages of neurons exhibiting working memory responses were observed in parietal areas 7a and LIP and prefrontal areas 46 and FEF ( Chafee & Goldman-Rakic, 1998 ).”
findingArea 7a neurons hold the remembered location of a visual stimulus even when no motor response to it is planned, so the signal is mnemonic rather than motor-preparatory. — “Later studies in area 7a demonstrated that posterior parietal neurons represent the remembered locations of visual stimuli, independent of a planned motor response ( Constantinidis & Steinmetz, 1996 ).”
findingLearning leaves a spectral signature in parietal spike trains: attending to and holding a stimulus in working memory lowers spectral power in the 5-10 Hz band. — “The results demonstrated that active attention and maintenance of a stimulus in working memory was characterized by distinct firing patterns, most notably a decrease in spectral power in the 5–10 Hz frequency range.”
findingColour selectivity in posterior parietal neurons, normally low, increases once colour becomes the cue that signifies which eye movement to make. — “However when animals execute a behavioral task for which color is important because it signifies eye movements, a larger percentage of PPC neurons exhibit selectivity for the color of the stimuli ( Toth & Assad, 2002 ).”
findingTMS during the delay period slows responses whether applied over prefrontal or parietal cortex, but TMS during the decision period impairs performance only over prefrontal cortex. — “Similarly, right Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) applied over both prefrontal and posterior parietal cortex during the delay phase of a spatial working memory task increased reaction times whereas TMS applied during the decision phase of the task disrupted performance only when applied over the prefrontal, but not PPC ( Koch, Oliveri, Torriero, Carlesimo, Turriziani, & Caltagirone, 2005 ).”
findingMonkeys trained on a salience-detection task signal the target faster and with more discriminable activity than untrained monkeys, although the overall response pattern is unchanged. — “These studies revealed that although the overall pattern of responses was similar, neuronal activity in monkeys trained to detect a salient stimulus in a visual display reflected the target faster, and produced a more discriminable level of activation.”
findingImaging meta-analysis places area 7 in every executive component of working memory tested - ordering, updating and manipulating items - as well as in spatial content. — “A meta-analysis of imaging studies involving working memory tasks revealed that area 7 was reliably activated by all aspects of executive function examined (ordering, updating, and manipulating items held in working memory), as well as the spatial content of working memory ( Wager & Smith, 2003 ).”
findingparietal and prefrontal cortex show virtually identical proportions of neurons with working memory responses — “The similarity in patterns of activity between prefrontal and parietal neurons was also confirmed by neurophysiological studies in both areas. Virtually identical percentages of neurons exhibiting working memory responses were observed in parietal areas 7a and LIP and prefrontal areas 46 and FEF”
findingparietal delay activity is overwritten by an intervening distractor; prefrontal activity is not — “Memory-related activity in the parietal cortex differs from the memory trace held in frontal cortical neurons in that it is disrupted by intervening, distracting stimuli; the activity of parietal neurons represents the most recent stimulus to appear, whether it was behaviorally relevant or not ( Constantinidis & Steinmetz, 1996 ). In contrast, frontal neurons continue to discharge even after the presentation of intervening stimuli, representing the behaviorally relevant stimulus held in memory”
findingTMS over prefrontal cortex disrupted the decision phase of a memory task while TMS over parietal cortex did not — “Similarly, right Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) applied over both prefrontal and posterior parietal cortex during the delay phase of a spatial working memory task increased reaction times whereas TMS applied during the decision phase of the task disrupted performance only when applied over the prefrontal, but not PPC”
findingcooling either region changes the output of the other, so the two are not independent modules — “The importance in the interaction between the two areas was revealed by deactivation of either PPC or PFC by cortical cooling which was shown to produce a profound effect on the output of neurons in the other area”
findingparietal neurons acquire colour selectivity when colour becomes task-relevant, having had little before — “PPC neurons typically have low selectivity for color; only a small percentage of area 7a neurons demonstrate significantly different responses to green and red stimuli of equal luminance ( Constantinidis & Steinmetz, 2001 ). However when animals execute a behavioral task for which color is important because it signifies eye movements, a larger percentage of PPC neurons exhibit selectivity for the color of the stimuli”
findingtrained animals' parietal neurons signalled the target faster and more discriminably than naive animals' — “A more fundamental effect of learning was revealed by comparing responses of PPC neurons in animals trained to perform a behavioral task ( Constantinidis & Steinmetz, 2001 ) with responses in animals naïve to behavioral training ( Constantinidis & Steinmetz, 2005 ). These studies revealed that although the overall pattern of responses was similar, neuronal activity in monkeys trained to detect a salient stimulus in a visual display reflected the target faster, and produced a more discriminable level of activation.”
findingattention and memory maintenance are separable by a fall in 5-10 Hz spectral power — “The results demonstrated that active attention and maintenance of a stimulus in working memory was characterized by distinct firing patterns, most notably a decrease in spectral power in the 5–10 Hz frequency range.”
findingtwo patients with frontal lesions and no neglect still failed spatial working memory, dissociating the two deficits — “Interestingly, poor performance was also observed in two patients with right lateral frontal lesions who showed no neglect ( Malhotra, 2005 ), confirming that a dissociation can exist between spatial working memory and neglect in some cases.”
findingthe Corsi task was modified to put all items on the vertical midline, removing the attentional bias from the memory measure — “In the latter study, modified versions of the Corsi blocks task were used to dissociate the working memory aspect of the pathology from neglect; viewers had to remember the order of a series of highlighted discs whose locations were restricted to the vertical midline, thus eliminating the attentional bias towards the ipsilesional visual field.”
findingMAPPING DECLINED: the parietal-prefrontal specialisation does not correspond to Baddeley's subsystems, since both regions do visual-spatial and executive work — “It should be noted however that this specialization does not correspond to distinct components or subsystems of the Baddeley model, as both visual-spatial and executive functions have been described in both parietal and prefrontal areas.”
findingMAPPING OFFERED, HEDGED: the conclusion nonetheless proposes parietal activity as a possible correlate of the visual-spatial sketchpad — “Posterior parietal activity could provide a physiological correlate to the “visual-spatial sketchpad” in Allan Baddeley’s model of working memory by allowing for the manipulation of items held in working memory into larger organized structures.”

It has also been posited that instead of being maintained in dedicated modality-specific buffers, information held in working memory is represented by the activity of those processes within the current focus of attention ( Cowan, 1988 ). Along similar lines, the idea has evolved that working memory is an emergent property of cognition and the network of rain regions that mediate it”
