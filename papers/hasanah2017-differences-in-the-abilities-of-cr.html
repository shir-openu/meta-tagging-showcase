phenomenoncreative thinking and problem-solving ability in mathematics learning
claim-typeexistence
mechanismCooperative group work makes students interact and exchange and inspect each other's ideas, which is what raises creative thinking — “This is in accordance with what was proposed by Slavin that cooperative learning encourages students to interact actively and positively in groups.”
mechanismIn STAD every problem is worked through in group discussion before the individual quiz, so students get repeated rehearsal at solving problems — “So all the problems to be solved are discussed in discussion to find the solution before each student undergoes the individual quiz.”
mechanismCreative thinking entails generating alternative solutions, so exercising it automatically exercises problem solving — which is why the two abilities interact with the learning model — “A person who has the ability to think creatively is not only able to solve problems that are non-routine, but also able to see various alternative problem solving.”
relation — “This is in accordance with what was proposed by Slavin that cooperative learning encourages students to interact actively and positively in groups.”
relation — “This is consistent with research conducted by Iin Septi Jannah Siregar.”

findingConsidered on its own, mathematical creative thinking was better in the STAD group than in the problem-solving-learning group. — “The second hypothesis finding resulted in the conclusion that the ability of creative thinking of mathematics in students who were taught by cooperative learning of STAD type was better than students who were taught by learning of problem solving.”
