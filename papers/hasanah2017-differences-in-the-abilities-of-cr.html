phenomenoncreative thinking and problem-solving ability in mathematics learning— “This study aims to determine the differences in the abilities of creative thinking and problem solving of mathematics in students who are taught by cooperative learning with the type of STAD and students who were taught with problem solving in class VII of Madrasah Tsanawiyah of Madinatussalam in Sei Rotan Learning Year 2015/2016.”
claim-typeexistence— “(4) There is a significant interaction between the learning model used with the ability to think creatively and the problem solving ability of mathematics in the students.”
method — evidence— “Therefore, this study is an experimental study with a quasi experiment design, because the class used has been formed before.”
mechanismCooperative group work makes students interact and exchange and inspect each other's ideas, which is what raises creative thinking— “This is in accordance with what was proposed by Slavin that cooperative learning encourages students to interact actively and positively in groups.”
mechanismIn STAD every problem is worked through in group discussion before the individual quiz, so students get repeated rehearsal at solving problems— “So all the problems to be solved are discussed in discussion to find the solution before each student undergoes the individual quiz.”
mechanismCreative thinking entails generating alternative solutions, so exercising it automatically exercises problem solving — which is why the two abilities interact with the learning model— “A person who has the ability to think creatively is not only able to solve problems that are non-routine, but also able to see various alternative problem solving.”
builds-on Slavin's account of cooperative learning— “This is in accordance with what was proposed by Slavin that cooperative learning encourages students to interact actively and positively in groups.”
builds-on Iin Septi Jannah Siregar's study of creative thinking and problem solving under problem-based vs conventional learning— “This is consistent with research conducted by Iin Septi Jannah Siregar.”

senseeffect of STAD cooperative learning - AS THE COMPONENT RESULTS: creative thinking is reported as BETTER, and problem solving is reported as BETTER, against the same comparison— “(2) The ability of creative thinking in students who are taught by cooperative learning of STAD type is better than students who are taught with learning of problem solving in the subject matter of circle.”
