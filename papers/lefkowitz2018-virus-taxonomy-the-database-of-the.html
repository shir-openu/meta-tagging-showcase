mechanismand virology is unusual in giving one body BOTH powers: `UNLIKE for other groups of biological organisms, the ICTV charge extends NOT ONLY to developing the guidelines for naming of taxa, BUT to establishing guidelines for TAXONOMIC CLASSIFICATION of viruses, AND APPROVING THE PROPOSED TAXONOMY AND NAMES BEFORE THEY BECOME OFFICIAL`. Zoology and bacteriology separate the naming rules from the classification decision; virology does not

limitationTHE DEMARCATION CRITERIA ARE WRITTEN BY THE PEOPLE WHO APPLY THEM, BY DESIGN: `Each subcommittee contains a series of study groups, GENERALLY ONE PER VIRUS FAMILY, THAT DEVELOP THE DEMARCATION CRITERIA USED TO DEFINE EACH NEW TAXON, AND EVALUATE PROPOSALS USING THOSE CRITERIA`. One committee per family writes the rule and judges the application - `the definer never fixes their own terms`, institutionalised
limitationAND ONE OF THOSE CRITERIA IS A BARE PERCENTAGE: `members of the genus Enterovirus share MORE THAN 42% AMINO ACID IDENTITY across the length of their polyprotein`. No derivation is offered for 42, and the sentence stating it carries no definitional language at all - it is a fact about members, doing the work of a criterion
limitationAND THE CHANGE TYPE STORED CAN MISREPRESENT THE CHANGE: `the most recent change ... was the CREATION of the species Alphacoronavirus 1 in 2009. BUT IN FACT, THIS WAS NOT A NEW SPECIES, but was A MERGING OF THREE EXISTING SPECIES ... INTO ONE, NEWLY RENAMED SPECIES ... In addition, the species were MOVED FROM THE NOW DEFUNCT GENUS coronavirus`. THREE OF THE SIX OPERATIONS AT ONCE, RECORDED AS ONE, and the paper has to say `but in fact` to correct its own database
limitationand the hierarchy is expected to move: `As new data are obtained either on the organisms previously studied, or on related organisms, THE CLASSIFICATION HIERARCHY MAY CHANGE`. A vocabulary that is correct at a release number rather than simply correct
