findingConsciousness research was rated less rigorous than other fields by 34% versus only 8% rating it more rigorous, and experts held this view as much as non-experts.
findingPerceived rigour does not track perceived scarcity: consciousness science was rated more rigorous than social neuroscience (33% vs 10%) yet still harder to fund and to get hired in.
