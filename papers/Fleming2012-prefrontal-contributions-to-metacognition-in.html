mechanismrostrolateral PFC re-represents object-level decision uncertainty at the meta level, which is what makes an explicit confidence report possible— “We discuss these findings in a theoretical framework where rlPFC re-represents object-level decision uncertainty to facilitate metacognitive report.”
mechanismobject-level uncertainty signals computed within the decision circuitry are re-coded for use in report, rather than confidence being read off the decision itself— “Instead, we suggest that these studies reveal neural representations of object-level task uncertainty that may be then re-represented for use in metacognitive report.”
mechanismmetacognitive accuracy arises from trial-by-trial access to internal decision uncertainty, not from monitoring one's own reaction time— “Instead, our behavioral data support an account where above-chance metacognitive ability is supported by trial-to-trial access to decision uncertainty.”
mechanisma staircase equates objective performance across individuals, so metacognitive ability is not confounded by task performance— “The aim of the staircase procedure was to equate objective performance across individuals, thus rendering metacognitive accuracy unconfounded by variation in task performance.”
mechanismthe Follow condition is yoked to the Report condition, matching the motor act of moving the cursor without any confidence judgment— “The Follow condition was yoked to the Report condition such that half of the previous 10 Report ratings were randomly selected to specify the current block of Follow target positions.”
mechanismthe starting cursor position is jittered to prevent motor preparation from carrying the confidence signal— “The initial cursor position on each trial was randomly jittered around the midpoint of the scale (±12% of scale length) to discourage advance motor preparation of the confidence response.”
mechanismno feedback is given, so confidence cannot be updated from knowledge of accuracy— “No feedback was given as to whether the perceptual decision was correct or incorrect.”
mechanismthe proposal: object-level uncertainty represented elsewhere is re-represented in rlPFC in a form suitable for explicit report— “Instead, we suggest that these studies reveal neural representations of object-level task uncertainty that may be then re-represented for use in metacognitive report. The mechanism underlying this process, particularly across different species, remains an open question.”
