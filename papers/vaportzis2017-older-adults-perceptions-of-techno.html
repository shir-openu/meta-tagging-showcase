mechanismBENEFIT OUTWEIGHS COST RATHER THAN COST DETERRING: technology acceptance in older adults tracks the perceived benefit more than the perceived price, which the paper reads through diffusion of innovations - adoption follows a clear benefit being visible— “This finding is consistent with previous studies suggesting the perception of potential benefits was more indicative of technology acceptance than perception of cost”
mechanismEAGERNESS AND LOW CONFIDENCE COEXIST: most participants wanted to adopt the technology and the majority simultaneously doubted their own ability to use it, so the obstacle reported is instruction rather than motivation— “Our findings suggest that most of our participants were eager to adopt new technology and willing to learn using a tablet. However, they voiced apprehension about lack of, or lack of clarity in, instructions and support.”
mechanismA CATEGORY CONFUSION UNDERNEATH THE BARRIERS: participants were unaware of the differences between device types, so 'technology' is being evaluated as one undifferentiated thing— “questions that they were unaware of the similarities and differences between different types of technology (e.g., laptop vs. tablet).”
