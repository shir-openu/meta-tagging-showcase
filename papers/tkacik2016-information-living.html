limitationThe review deliberately favours theoretical unity over biological detail. — “Throughout, we follow the physics tradition of emphasizing the unity of theoretical ideas at the expense of some of the biological complexity.”
limitationEntropy and mutual information are hard to estimate from finite samples, which limits the whole programme. — “Against this generality is the difficulty of actually estimating the entropy of a probability distribution from a limited set of samples.”
limitationInformation-theoretic quantities may not capture everything that matters biologically, since not all bits are equally useful to an organism. — “The universality of information-theoretic quantities is their strength, but it also leaves one with an uncomfortable feeling that there is more to the story of biological signal processing than bits.”
limitationThe maximum-entropy approach to protein folding rests on data sufficiency and approximations that remain open questions. — “This new approach to the protein folding problem has generated considerable excitement, but there are many open questions about the sufficiency of the available data and the approximations that have been used to solve the inverse problem.”
limitationThe connection between learning, statistical mechanics and field theory is explicitly out of scope. — “There are many interesting links between the learning problem, statistical mechanics, and field theory, which are beyond the scope of this review.”
limitationThe under-appreciation of the theory's limits is attributed to the title of the paper that founded it. — “Perhaps because Shannon called his work a mathematical theory of communication, it is not as widely appreciated”
limitationThe method is chosen precisely because it does not require knowing what matters. — “In complex systems, we may be unsure which features of the system dynamics are the most relevant. Even if we can identify the correct features, we might not know the biologically meaningful metric that measures the similarity or difference along these relevant dimensions. Information theory gives us a way out of these difficulties, because it singles out measures that are completely general”
limitationThe strongest semantic claim in the review is made in a form that declines to assert it. — “The resulting clusters exhibit a striking degree of semantic (rather than just syntactic) coherence, and it hard to resist saying that this purely information theoretic approach is extracting meaning”
