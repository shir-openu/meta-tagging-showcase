phenomenonsocial priming of prosocial helping in infants— “In the study described here, we tested whether connections between affiliation to the group and prosocial behaviors are so fundamental that they are seen even in infants.”
claim-typeexistence— “We show that the mere hint of affiliation dramatically increases prosocial behavior in infants.”
method — evidence— “In the current study, we primed 18-month-old infants with photographs evoking affiliation (two small dolls standing next to each other in the background of photographs of other objects) and measured the influence of the primes on infants’ tendency to be helpful.”
mechanismThe affiliation photographs activate a general prosocial orientation, which is then expressed in an unrelated helping act, rather than being copied from what was depicted— “Infants did not directly reproduce the situations depicted in the photographs; instead, the photographs triggered a general prosocial orientation that manifested itself in increased helping.”
mechanismAn implicit, non-conscious link between affiliation to the group and helping is engaged by the prime— “Thus, infants made the subconscious connection between affiliation and helping behavior.”
builds-on Warneken & Tomasello (2006)— “We know from previous studies that, if an adult is unable to reach an object he wants, 18-month-old infants will often pick up the object themselves and offer it to the adult (Warneken & Tomasello, 2006).”
senseprime - a BACKGROUND element of a photograph whose foreground is an ordinary household object, and the assistant naming the photograph aloud never refers to it. The construct is delivered by what is deliberately not mentioned— “For each photograph, the assistant named the foreground object and then commented on its color and finally its function, completely ignoring the prime.”
sensespontaneous helping - the main dependent measure, and it is defined by a CLOCK rather than by a judgement: within the first 10 s, before the experimenter has spoken to the infant or reached for the sticks herself— “The main dependent measure was whether infants helped the experimenter spontaneously, that is, whether they picked up at least one stick and offered it to her within the first 10 s of the test phase (before she spoke to the infants or reached for the sticks herself).”
senseaffiliation - as delivered here, an arrangement of two dolls in the background of a photograph, not a word list or a scenario. The whole construct rests on which way two small wooden figures are turned— “In the current study, we primed 18-month-old infants with photographs evoking affiliation (two small dolls standing next to each other in the background of photographs of other objects)…
sensethe back-to-back condition - the question it exists to answer, in the authors' own words: whether the effect belongs to the DOLLS or to their RELATION
sensebaseline - a condition included not to test the hypothesis but to DISAMBIGUATE THE DIRECTION of the effect: did the affiliation primes raise helping, or the individuality primes lower it
