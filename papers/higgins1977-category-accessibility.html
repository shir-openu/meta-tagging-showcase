phenomenoncategory accessibility in impression formation— “The present study examined the immediate and delayed effects of unobtrusive exposure to personality trait terms (e.g., "reckless," "persistent") on subjects' subsequent judgments and recollection of information about another person.”
claim-typemechanism— “The absence of a significant reproduction effect in the present study suggests that the characterization and evaluation effects found were due mainly to a direct effect of categorization on judgment.”
method — evidence— “As part of a "reading comprehension" study, 60 Princeton University undergraduates read a paragraph ambiguously describing a stimulus person.”
builds-on Warren (1972)— “Indirect evidence for this was obtained by Warren (1972).”
builds-on Bartlett (1932)— “Bartlett (1932) also suggests that the delayed influence of the categorization on reproduction and judgment may be greater than the immediate influence, as the stored details of the input information are more rapidly forgotten than the categorization.”
builds-on Spiro (1975)— “Spiro (1975) has found that reconstructive errors in reproduction do not occur when subjects are instructed to memorize the input information, but will occur otherwise.”
senseTHE APPLICABILITY NULL IS THE CONTROL THAT RULES OUT A VALENCE ACCOUNT, AND IT IS THE HARDEST WORKING RESULT IN THE PAPER: `the ABSENCE of any effects for NONAPPLICABLE trait terms suggested that exposure to trait terms with positive or negative associations WAS NOT IN ITSELF SUFFICIENT to determine attributions and evaluations.` Positive and negative words that did not FIT the target did nothing. So the effect is not mood, not evaluative contagion and not `nice words make you nicer` - it needs a CATEGORY the target can be sorted into. **Necessary-but-not-sufficient, stated in 1977**, and the same logical shape unknown2009 states for object recognition thirty-two years later— “However, the absence of any effects for nonapplicable trait terms suggested that exposure to trait terms with positive or negative associations was not in itself sufficient to determine attributions and evaluations”
senseTHE DELAYED EFFECT IS BIGGER THAN THE IMMEDIATE ONE, AND THE EXPLANATION IS CORRECTION RATHER THAN ACTIVATION: `It may be that an AWARENESS that one's categorizations are BIASED accounts of the stimulus information can function as a `DISCOUNTING CUE` ... that ATTENUATES the direct impact of the categorizations.` The activation does not grow; the correction fades. **So awareness here REDUCES the measured effect, and an immediate test UNDERSTATES it** - the opposite sign to Holcomb2005, where prime visibility PREDICTED the size of semantic priming. Two literatures with awareness pushing the measurement in opposite directions, which is a reason to record the direction and not only the presence of an awareness check— “It may be that an awareness that one's categorizations are biased accounts of the stimulus information can function as a "discounting cue" (Hovland, Lumsdaine, & Sheffield, 1949) that attenuates the direct impact of the categorizations”
senseA SECOND NULL SEPARATES TWO ROUTES: a category could bias judgement INDIRECTLY, by distorting what is remembered, or DIRECTLY, through its own implications. Reproduction of the description was unaffected, so `the characterization and evaluation effects found were due mainly to a DIRECT effect of categorization on judgment.` **The prime changed what people concluded without changing what they recalled** - which is what makes this a claim about judgement rather than about memory, and it is settled by an absence— “The absence of a significant reproduction effect in the present study suggests that the characterization and evaluation effects found were due mainly to a direct effect of categorization on judgment”
senseAND THEY UNDERCUT THAT NULL THEMSELVES, IN THE NEXT PARAGRAPH. Subjects `probably felt they should MEMORIZE the stimulus information because it was part of a `reading comprehension` test`, and Spiro (1975) found that reconstructive errors do NOT occur under memorise instructions - so `Reproductive errors consistent with the activated categories MIGHT HAVE OCCURRED in the present study if subjects had been given different instructions.` The absence that carries their direct-effect conclusion is attributed to their own design rather than to the world, in the same discussion that uses it— “Reproductive errors consistent with the activated categories might have occurred in the present study if subjects had been given different instructions for the stimulus information”
senseTHE AMBIGUITY CONDITION IS HERE TOO, IN 1977, AND THAT MAKES THREE PAPERS ACROSS 38 YEARS. `AT LEAST IN AMBIGUOUS SITUATIONS, category accessibility can also determine how a stimulus person is characterized.` North1999 says the effect holds `only when there is an element of UNCERTAINTY in product choice or when customers are in a state of LOW INVOLVEMENT`. money2013 says `a situation that does not leave much room for interpretation is unlikely to show a consistent or large priming effect`. **Social psychology 1977, marketing 1999, social psychology 2015 - the founding paper, a field study and a reply to a failed replication - all naming the same condition: a prime needs somewhere to act.** Higgins states it first and almost in passing, as a qualifier on a sentence about something else— “At least in ambiguous situations, category accessibility can also determine how a stimulus person is characterized”
