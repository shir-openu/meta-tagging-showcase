object of study — evidence— “First, single-cell expression has repeatedly been shown to exhibit a characteristic bimodal expression pattern, wherein the expression of otherwise abundant genes is either strongly positive or undetected within individual cells.”
research question — evidence— “We and other groups [ 4 – 7 ] have shown that the proportion of cells with detectable expression reflects both technical factors and biological differences between samples.”
contribution — evidence— “We propose a two-part, generalized linear model for such bimodal data that parameterizes both of these features.”
mechanismthe zero has two causes that produce the same observation - RNA below the threshold of detection, and genuine biological absence - and the paper says the second is CONFLATED WITH the first in the literature's term `extrinsic noise`— “This is due in part to low starting quantities of RNA such that many genes will be below the threshold of detection, but there is also a biological component to this variation (termed extrinsic noise in the literature) that is conflated with the technical variability”
mechanismthe cellular detection rate is the fraction of genes detected in a cell, and it REFLECTS BOTH technical factors and biological differences between samples - so the covariate proposed as nuisance is partly the thing being measured— “We and other groups [ 4 – 7 ] have shown that the proportion of cells with detectable expression reflects both technical factors and biological differences between samples.”
mechanisma physical route by which the confounding could arise is named rather than left abstract: TREATED CELLS COULD BECOME LARGER IN VOLUME, which would move the detection rate for the same reason the treatment moves expression— “It is possible that the CDR and treatment effects could be partially confounded, for example, treated cells could become larger in volume.”
critiques the model of independent expression between genes - `That CDR predicts expression levels CONTRADICTS` it, since an average across many genes could not affect any single gene were expression independent— “That CDR predicts expression levels contradicts the model of independent expression between genes, because the level of expression (averaged across many genes) would not affect the level in any given gene were expression independent.”
critiques co-expression analyses that do not adjust for the detection rate, in which `the apparent correlation between genes is greatly inflated`— “It is especially important to adjust for CDR when testing for co-expression between genes, or the apparent correlation between genes is greatly inflated”
extends Hicks et al.'s point that confounding between technical and biological effects is best handled by experimental design - conceded here as the optimal approach— “the optimal approach to handle confounding between technical and biological effects on the CDR is to design experiments with biological replicates across multiple batches.”
sensecellular detection rate - AS USED HERE, IN TWO INCOMPATIBLE ROLES IN ONE PAPER. In the abstract it is `a source of NUISANCE variation` to be adjusted away. In the results it `reflects BOTH technical factors AND biological differences`, its deviance rivals the treatment's, and it may be confounded with treatment because treated cells grow. THE SAME NUMBER IS THE ARTEFACT AND THE SIGNAL— “We and other groups [ 4 – 7 ] have shown that the proportion of cells with detectable expression reflects both technical factors and biological differences between samples.”
senseextrinsic noise - the literature's name for the biological half of the variation, and the paper's point is that the NAME DOES NOT SEPARATE IT: it arrives conflated with the technical variability in the same measurement— “This is due in part to low starting quantities of RNA such that many genes will be below the threshold of detection, but there is also a biological component to this variation (termed extrinsic noise in the literature) that is conflated with the technical variability”
sensebimodality - AS DEFINED BY THE OBSERVATION, not the cause: expression is `either strongly positive or undetected`. The model parameterises the two modes precisely because it cannot attribute any single zero to one cause or the other— “First, single-cell expression has repeatedly been shown to exhibit a characteristic bimodal expression pattern, wherein the expression of otherwise abundant genes is either strongly positive or undetected within individual cells.”
