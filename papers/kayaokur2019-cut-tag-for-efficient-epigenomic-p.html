mechanismTHE LOW BACKGROUND IS STRUCTURAL RATHER THAN STATISTICAL: the enzyme is tethered first and the excess washed away, and only THEN is it activated by Mg++. Nothing is cut until the antibody has already chosen the site— “We then activated the transposome by addition of Mg ++ , integrating adapters spanning sites of H3K27me3-containing nucleosomes”
mechanismand ChIP works the other way round - fragment first, then select - which is what the paper blames for its noise: `the VAGARIES OF CROSSLINKING AND FRAGMENTING chromatin have limited chromatin profiling by ChIP-seq to AN ARTISAN TECHNIQUE`— “While ChIP-based methods have been extensively used in model cell line systems, the vagaries of crosslinking and fragmenting chromatin have limited chromatin profiling by ChIP-seq to an artisan technique where each experiment requires optimization.”
mechanismand the residual background has a NAMED CAUSE with a testable signature: `some UN-TETHERED pA-Tn5 binding to exposed DNA in situ`. That is why accessible sites appear at all in a factor profile, and why read depth separates the two classes— “This probably results from some un-tethered pA-Tn5 binding to exposed DNA in situ, but it is straightforward to distinguish antibody-tethered sites from accessible sites by the vast difference in read coverage”
extends CUT&RUN - the same tethering strategy with the nuclease replaced by a transposase, which removes the separate library-preparation step— “This shows that CUT&Tag protects a “footprint” spanning 80 bp directly over the CTCF motif (Fig. 5c ). While the segment protected from Tn5 integration is larger than the ~45 bp protected from MNase in CUT&RUN 9 , this indicates that the tethered transposase produces high resolution maps of factor binding sites.”
contradicts ChIP-seq as a general-purpose chromatin profiling method - `the vagaries of crosslinking and fragmenting chromatin have limited chromatin profiling by ChIP-seq to AN ARTISAN TECHNIQUE where each experiment requires optimization`— “While ChIP-based methods have been extensively used in model cell line systems, the vagaries of crosslinking and fragmenting chromatin have limited chromatin profiling by ChIP-seq to an artisan technique where each experiment requires optimization.”

sense`true binding sites` - AS OPERATIONALISED HERE, THE PEAK CALLS OF A DIFFERENT METHOD. CTCF truth is CUT&RUN's peak list; accessibility truth is ATAC-seq's. THE WORD `TRUE` IS DOING WORK THAT NO EXPERIMENT IN THIS PAPER DOES, and the paper is explicit about which method supplied each list— “To determine if true CTCF binding sites could be distinguished from accessible features by read depth, we compared the CUT&Tag read count at high-confidence CTCF sites (defined by peak-calling on CUT&RUN data 18 ) to the CUT&Tag read count at accessible sites (defined by peak-calling on ATAC-seq data”
senseTHE ONE PLACE `TRUE` IS EARNED, AND THE PAPER SAYS WHY IT CHOSE IT: PRO-seq, picked `to validate RNAPII CUT&Tag WITHOUT RELYING UPON ANNOTATIONS` because it `provides direct mapping of RNAPII USING A METHOD THAT IS UNRELATED TO CHROMATIN PROFILING`. The independence is the argument, and it is stated as the reason for the choice— “To validate RNAPII CUT&Tag without relying upon annotations, which are typically based on mapping of processed transcripts, we chose transcriptional run-on data obtained with the base-pair-resolution PRO-seq technique, which provides direct mapping of RNAPII using a method that is unrelated to chromatin profiling”
sensethe NPAT experiment is a genuine POSITIVE CONTROL - a target chosen because its answer is already known. `NPAT binds only ~80 accessible sites in the histone clusters ... THUS WE CAN COMPARE TRUE BINDING SITES WITH ACCESSIBLE SITES`. Picking the target for the sake of the control is the rare move— “We used an antibody to the NPAT nuclear factor, a transcriptional coactivator of the replication-dependent histone genes, in CUT&Tag reactions. NPAT binds only ~80 accessible sites in the histone clusters on chromosome 1 and chromosome 6 24 , thus we can compare true binding sites with accessible sites.”
sense`signal-to-noise` - here it is THE FRACTION OF READS FALLING INSIDE CALLED PEAKS, at a fixed read depth. `Only CUT&Tag reaches a fraction of 0.6 within peaks` is therefore a statement about the method AND about whatever called the peaks— “We found that both CUT&RUN and CUT&Tag populate peaks more deeply than ChIP-seq or ATAC-seq, demonstrating that they have exceptionally low signal-to-noise (Fig. 3b ). senseand ATAC-seq is the counter-case that motivates the whole design: it maps accessible and factor-bound sites but `THE SPECIFIC CHROMATIN PROTEINS BOUND AT THESE SITES MUST BE INFERRED`. A method that sees WHERE but not WHO— “Although methods like ATAC-seq map accessible and factor-bound sites 17 , the specific chromatin proteins bound at these sites must be inferred from motif or chromatin profiling data.”
