4. Discussion The objective of this study was to analyze descriptively the main definitions and competences of the concept of social media literacyterm·dom. The results yielded 15 studies ( Table A1 in Appendix A ) that address social media literacyterm·dom by either conceptualizing it, or by referring to the competences of which it consists.
