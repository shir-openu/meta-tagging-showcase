transferfrom tabular and vector data mining: SMOTE and Borderline-SMOTE were built to interpolate new points for imbalanced TABULAR data and are carried over to images— “SMOTE and the extension of Borderline-SMOTE [30] create new instances by interpolating new points from existing instances via k-Nearest Neighbors.”

mechanismAUGMENTATION IS AN ASSUMPTION, AND THE PAPER STATES IT: that more information can be extracted from the dataset already held. Everything else follows from whether that is true of a given dataset— “This is done under the assumption that more information can be extracted from the original dataset through augmentations.”
mechanismWHAT AUGMENTATION IS ACTUALLY TEACHING: the invariances a recogniser must survive - viewpoint, lighting, occlusion, background, scale - are baked into the DATA rather than into the architecture— “The task of Data Augmentation is to bake these translational invariances into the dataset such that the resulting models will perform well despite these challenges.”
mechanismSIMULATING BIG DATA RATHER THAN OBTAINING IT: overfitting is not much of a problem when data is abundant, so augmentation gives a limited dataset the characteristics of an abundant one— “Overfitting is generally not as much of an issue with access to big data. Data Augmentation prevents overfitting by modifying limited datasets to possess the characteristics of big data.”
mechanisman unsafe transformation could be made useful rather than harmful by softening the label, so the model learns uncertainty instead of a falsehood - but building those labels is too costly— “A non-label preserving transformation could potentially strengthen the model’s ability to output a response indicating that it is not confident about its prediction.”
mechanismoversampling works by re-balancing the class distribution so the model is not biased toward answering with the majority class— “Oversampling is a technique used to re-sample imbalanced class distributions such that the model is not overly biased towards labeling instances as the majority class type.”
mechanismmedical imaging is short of data FOR STRUCTURAL REASONS, and the paper names rarity first: `It is especially difficult to build big medical image datasets DUE TO THE RARITY OF DISEASES`. The remaining three reasons it lists - patient privacy, the need for expert labellers, and the cost of the scans - are separated from this clause by a journal running header in the staged text and are not inside this span.— “It is especially difficult to build big medical image datasets due to the rarity of diseases”
mechanismrotation safety is set by one parameter: small rotations help digit recognition and large ones destroy the label, so the same transform is safe and unsafe at different magnitudes— “The safety of rotation augmentations is heavily determined by the rotation degree parameter.”
mechanismcropping and translation differ in what they preserve - cropping shrinks the input, translation keeps the spatial dimensions and pads the vacated space— “The contrast between random cropping and translations is that cropping will reduce the size of the input such as (256,256) → (224, 224), whereas translations preserve the spatial dimensions of the image.”
mechanismcolour transformations trade colour bias for spatial characteristics, which is a gain only where colour is not the distinguishing feature— “In effect, color space transformations will eliminate color biases present in the dataset in favor of spatial characteristics.”
mechanisma kernel filter can be rebuilt as a convolution layer with the right padding and activation, so the augmentation duplicates what the network already does— “For example, something like PatchShuffle Regularization could be implemented with a convolution layer.”

edgeTransfer Learning —distinct-from→ Pretraining— “This differs from Transfer Learning because in Transfer Learning, the network architecture such as VGG-16 [2] or ResNet [3] must be transferred as well as the weights.”
edgeDropout —is-a→ regularization technique— “Dropout [7] is a regularization technique that zeros out the activation values of randomly chosen neurons during training. This constraint forces the network to learn more robust features rather than relying on the predictive capability of a small subset of neurons in the network.”
edgeBatch normalization —is-a→ regularization technique— “Batch normalization [9] is another regularization technique that normalizes the set of activations in a layer. Normalization works by subtracting the batch mean from each activation and dividing by the batch standard deviation.”
relationData Augmentation —causes→ overfitting — augmentation gives a limited dataset the characteristics of big data, and overfitting is not much of an issue when data is abundant— “Overfitting is generally not as much of an issue with access to big data. Data Augmentation prevents overfitting by modifying limited datasets to possess the characteristics of big data.”
limitationAUGMENTATION CANNOT CREATE A CLASS THAT IS ABSENT: no method surveyed will produce a golden retriever from a dataset of bulldogs. It can lessen biases of lighting, occlusion, scale and background, and it cannot supply missing content— “no augmentation method discussed, from SamplePairing to AutoAugment to GANs, will create a golden retriever.”
limitationThe stated bias limit in the paper's own words, which bounds every technique it surveys— “Data Augmentation cannot overcome all biases present in a small dataset.”
limitationThe survey covers IMAGE data only, with transfer to other data domains asserted rather than shown— “This survey focuses on applications for image data, although many of these techniques and concepts can be expanded to other data domains.”
limitationThe taxonomy the survey is organised by does not partition its own subject - warping and oversampling are stated not to be mutually exclusive— “Oversampling and Data Warping augmentations do not form a mutually exclusive dichotomy. For example, GAN samples can be stacked with random cropping to further inflate the dataset.”
limitationsafety is domain-dependent, which is precisely what makes a generalisable augmentation policy hard— “This is somewhat domain dependent, providing a challenge for developing generalizable augmentation policies, (see Auto- Augment [38] for further exploration into finding generalizable augmentations).”
limitationthere is NO image processing function that cannot flip a label at some distortion magnitude - the strongest form of the claim, stated by the authors— “There is no image processing function that cannot result in a label changing transformation at some distortion magnitude.”
limitationconstructing refined labels for every unsafe augmentation is computationally expensive, which is why the cheap option is to avoid unsafe transforms— “However, constructing refined labels for every non-safe Data Augmentation is a computationally expensive process.”
limitationmany experiments deliberately restrict themselves to a subset of a large dataset in order to SIMULATE a limited-data problem rather than face a real one— “Many experiments constrain themselves to a subset of the dataset to simulate limited data problems.”
limitationkernel filters are very close to the internal mechanism of a CNN, which is the argument for making them a layer rather than a dataset operation— “A disadvantage of this technique is that it is very similar to the internal mechanisms of CNNs.”
limitationin medical imaging the gap between training and test data is more complex than position and translation, so geometric transformations have limited scope there— “Finally, in many of the application domains covered such as medical image analysis, the biases distancing the training data from the testing data are more complex than positional and translational variances.”
limitationcolour transformations can discard the information the task depends on - darkening may hide the object entirely— “Additionally, color transformations may discard important color information and thus are not always a label-preserving transformation.”
limitationtransformations that may alter the label, such as translation or random cropping, must be checked by hand— “Some geometric transformations such as translation or random cropping must be manually observed to make sure they have not altered the label of the image.”

findingAlexNet used augmentation to expand its training set by three orders of magnitude and the authors credit it with more than a percentage point of error reduction— “Data Augmentation is used in their experiments to increase the dataset size by a magnitude of 2048.”
findingGAN-based augmentation beat classic augmentation on liver lesion classification on both sensitivity and specificity— “This improved classification performance from 78.6% sensitivity and 88.4% specificity using classic augmentations to 85.7% sensitivity and 92.4% specificity using GAN-based Data Augmentation.”
findinghorizontal flipping is far commoner than vertical, and is not label-preserving on text recognition datasets— “Horizontal axis flipping is much more common than flipping the vertical axis.”
