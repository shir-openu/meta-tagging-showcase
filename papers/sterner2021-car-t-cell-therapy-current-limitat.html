mechanismthe CAR binds a surface antigen INDEPENDENTLY OF MHC, which is what makes the activation vigorous and also what removes the presentation step that normally restricts a T cell response— “CAR binding to target antigens expressed on the cell surface is independent from the MHC receptor resulting in vigorous T cell activation and powerful anti-tumor responses”
mechanismthe hinge sets the distance between the binding domain and the membrane, and therefore which epitopes are reachable: long spacers for membrane-proximal epitopes, short ones for membrane-distal— “In principle, the “optimal” spacer length is dependent on the position of the target epitope and the level of steric hindrance on the target cell in which long spacers provide added flexibility and allow more effective access to membrane-proximal epitopes or complex glycosylated antigens, while short hinges are more successful at binding…
mechanismIgG-derived spacers interact with Fc-gamma receptors and cause CAR-T cell depletion, so a choice made for geometry has an immunological cost the geometry does not predict— “IgG-derived spacers, however, can cause CAR-T cell depletion and thus, decreased persistence in vivo as they can interact with Fcγ”
mechanismthe two approved co-stimulatory domains produce different T cell fates and different metabolisms - CD28 gives effector memory and aerobic glycolysis, 4-1BB gives central memory and oxidative metabolism— “The co-stimulatory domains differ in their functional and metabolic profiles in which CARs with CD28 domains differentiate into effector memory T cells and primarily use aerobic glycolysis while CARs possessing the 4-1BB domain differentiate into central memory T cells and display increased mitochondrial biogenesis and oxidative metabolism 32…
mechanismantigen escape: the tumour survives by losing the one thing the CAR was built to see— “Although initially single antigen targeting CAR-T cells can deliver high response rates, the malignant cells of a significant portion of patients treated with these CAR-T cells display either partial or complete loss of target antigen expression.”
mechanismCRS is believed to be mediated primarily by IL-6, which is why blocking the IL-6 receptor is the treatment— “Pathophysiologically, CRS is believed to be primarily mediated by IL-6 and therefore, management relies on the use of IL-6 receptor blockade with tocilizumab and corticosteroids 3 – 5 .”
mechanismlowering the binding domain's affinity raises the antigen density needed to trigger the cell, so normal tissue expressing a little antigen is spared— “Decreasing the affinity of the antigen-binding domain would be expected to result in an increased requirement for higher antigen density on tumor cells in order for high levels of activation to be achieved.”
mechanismengineering the T cells to express heparanase lets them digest the proteoglycan barrier and enter the tumour— “CAR-T cells that have been engineered to express heparanase, an enzyme that degrades HSPG, show enhanced tumor infiltration and antitumor activity 68 .”
mechanismmatching chemokine receptors on the CAR-T cell to the chemokines the tumour actually emits improves trafficking— “One recently developed strategy that appears to significantly improve CAR-T cell trafficking involves expressing chemokine receptors on CAR-T cells that match and respond to tumor-derived chemokines 64 .”
mechanismneutralising GM-CSF lowers neurotoxicity and CRS while INCREASING CAR-T cell activity - the toxicity and the effect come apart— “Recent preclinical studies have demonstrated that neurotoxicity and CRS are decreased and CAR-T cell activity is increased after inhibition of macrophage activating and monocyte activating cytokine GM-CSF with lenzilumab 87 , 98 , 99 .”
mechanismlocal delivery removes the trafficking problem and the off-tumour problem at once, because the cells never circulate
