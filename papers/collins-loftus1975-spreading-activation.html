sense**AND THE MODEL PAYS FOR ITS OWN GENEROSITY, WHICH IS WHAT MAKES IT TESTABLE:** `Assumptions 2 and 3 impose A LIMITATION ON THE AMOUNT OF ACTIVATION that can be allocated in priming more than one concept, because THE MORE CONCEPTS THAT ARE PRIMED, THE LESS EACH WILL BE PRIMED.` A conservation constraint, derived from two numbered assumptions rather than added to fit a result. **It predicts that priming cannot scale freely** - a cost an unconstrained associative account does not carry, and a prediction later work can collect— “Assumptions 2 and 3 impose a limitation on the amount of activation that can be allocated in priming more than one concept, because the more concepts that are primed, the less each will be primed”
