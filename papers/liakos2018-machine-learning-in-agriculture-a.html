mechanismTHE LEARNING SIGNAL IS WHAT DIVIDES THE TWO FAMILIES: supervised learning has inputs paired with outputs and builds a mapping; unsupervised has no train/test distinction at all and looks for hidden structure. The difference is in what the data carries, not in the algorithm chosen— “In unsupervised learning, however, there is no distinction between training and test sets with data being unlabeled. The learner processes input data with the goal of discovering hidden patterns.”
mechanismDIMENSIONALITY REDUCTION RUNS BEFORE THE MODEL, IN BOTH FAMILIES, to give a lower-dimensional representation preserving as much information as possible - and it is applied specifically to avoid the effects of dimensionality rather than as a modelling choice— “It is usually performed prior to applying a classification or regression model in order to avoid the effects of dimensionality.”
mechanismCROP APPLICATIONS DOMINATE BECAUSE THEIR DATA NEEDS NO FUSION: ML predictions in crop management can be extracted from images alone, whereas livestock, water and soil work requires combining recorded data sources - so the distribution reflects DATA ASSEMBLY COST rather than the difficulty of the ML— “In contrast, when data recordings are involved, occasionally at the level of big data, the implementations of ML are less in number, mainly because of the increased efforts required for the data analysis task and not for the ML models per se.”
mechanismTHE APPROACHES ARE NOT WIRED INTO DECISIONS: every reviewed application is an individual solution not adequately connected with the decision-making process, which is the paper's own account of why the field has not consolidated— “At the moment, all of the approaches regard individual approaches and solutions and are not adequately connected with the decision-making process, as seen in other application domains.”
distinguishes-from ML applications to climate prediction, deliberately excluded from this review on the ground that it is a complete area by itself— “Climate prediction, although very important for agricultural production, has not been included in the presented review, considering the fact that ML applications for climate prediction is a complete area by itself.”
sensefeature - THREE NAMES FOR ONE THING, SAID IN ONE SENTENCE: `a set of attributes, ALSO KNOWN AS features or variables`. The corpus holds `variables` as a content_tags layer and `features` as a concept on other records, and this is the paper that says they are the same object seen from statistics, from machine learning and from experimental design— “Usually, an individual example is described by a set of attributes, also known as features or variables.”
sensesupervised / unsupervised - THE DIVIDING LINE IS THE LEARNING SIGNAL, NOT THE ALGORITHM. Supervised means inputs arrive with their outputs and the goal is a rule mapping one to the other; unsupervised means there is NO DISTINCTION BETWEEN TRAINING AND TEST SETS at all, which is a stronger statement than `unlabelled data` and is what makes the two families incomparable on accuracy— “In unsupervised learning, however, there is no distinction between training and test sets with data being unlabeled. The learner processes input data with the goal of discovering hidden patterns.”
sensereinforcement learning - PLACED HERE AS A PARTIAL CASE OF SUPERVISION rather than a third family: inputs partially available, some target outputs missing or given only as feedback to actions in a dynamic environment. Other papers in this corpus treat it as a peer of the other two, and the disagreement is about what the taxonomy is FOR— “In some cases, inputs can be only partially available with some of the target outputs missing or given only as feedback to the actions in a dynamic environment (reinforcement learning).”
