mechanismTHREE SOURCES FOR A MODEL'S ASSUMPTIONS ARE NAMED, AND WHAT THEY ASSERT IS DECLARED UNOBSERVABLE IN THE SAME CLAUSE: we have assumptions to make - FROM SCIENCE, INTUITION, OR OTHER DATA ANALYSES - that involve STRUCTURES WE BELIEVE EXIST IN THE DATA BUT THAT WE CANNOT DIRECTLY OBSERVE. The provenance of the assumptions and the inaccessibility of their objects are stated together— “Our data are complex and high-dimensional; we have assumptions to make—from science, intuition, or other data analyses—that involve structures we believe exist in the data but that we cannot directly observe; and finally our data sets are large, possibly even arriving in a never-ending stream.”
mechanismA FORMALISM IS DESCRIBED AS A LANGUAGE, AND THE INFERENCE PROCEDURE AS ITS READING: probabilistic graphical models GIVE US A VISUAL LANGUAGE FOR EXPRESSING ASSUMPTIONS about data and its HIDDEN STRUCTURE, and the corresponding algorithms LET US ANALYZE DATA UNDER THOSE ASSUMPTIONS, INFERRING THE HIDDEN STRUCTURE THAT BEST EXPLAINS OUR OBSERVATIONS. The notation is cast as a vocabulary and the computation as its interpretation— “In particular, probabilistic graphical models give us a visual language for expressing assumptions about data and its hidden structure. The corresponding posterior inference algorithms let us analyze data under those assumptions, inferring the hidden structure that best explains our observations.”
mechanismTHE PAPER'S OWN TASKS ARE SPLIT INTO TWO KINDS WITH DIFFERENT WARRANTS, AND THE UNOBSERVED PART IS PUT IN SCARE QUOTES: in DESCRIPTIVE tasks the models help EXPLORE the data with the hidden structure probabilistically FILLED IN; in PREDICTIVE tasks they FORM PREDICTIONS ABOUT NEW OBSERVATIONS. The same machinery serves two purposes that would be judged by different evidence— “In descriptive tasks, like problems #1 and #4 above, graphical models help us explore the data— the organization of books or the connections between genes and traits—with the hidden structure probabilistically “filled in.” In predictive tasks, like problems #2 and #3, we use models to form predictions about new observations. mechanismA MODEL CLASS IS DEFINED BY WHAT IT COSTS RATHER THAN BY WHAT IT CONTAINS: NONCONJUGATE MODELS are MODELS WHERE A RICHER PRIOR IS USED AT THE EXPENSE OF MATHEMATICAL CONVENIENCE - and they HAVE EXPANDED THE SUITE of tools available. The category's definition is a trade-off, with the thing traded away named explicitly— “This class of models is expressive, but nonconjugate models—models where a richer prior is used at the expense of mathematical convenience—have expanded the suite of probabilistic tools at our disposal.”
mechanismONE ALGORITHM IS RE-DESCRIBED AS ANOTHER, AND THE EQUIVALENCE IS STATED AS AN IDENTITY OF STEPS: the classical coordinate ascent algorithm CAN THUS BE INTERPRETED AS a projected natural gradient algorithm - and updating a parameter by taking a natural gradient step OF LENGTH ONE IS EQUIVALENT TO PERFORMING A COORDINATE UPDATE. Two names for one procedure, with the exchange rate given— “The classical coordinate ascent algorithm can thus be interpreted as a projected natural gradient algorithm (Sato, 2001). Updating a parameter by taking a natural gradient step of length one is equivalent to performing a coordinate update.”
mechanismA FORMAL FACT IS GIVEN AN INTUITIVE ROUTE ALONGSIDE THE FORMAL ONE, AND BOTH ARE LABELLED: FORMALLY this is most easily seen via other perspectives, THOUGH IT CAN BE SEEN INTUITIVELY WITH the stick-breaking construction - and then THE INTUITION IS THAT more of the stick is absorbed in the first break locations. The paper marks which of its two explanations is which— “Formally, this is most easily seen via other perspectives on the DP (Ferguson, 1973; Blackwell and MacQueen, 1973; Pitman, 2002), though it can be seen intuitively with the stick-breaking construction. The intuition is that as α gets smaller more of the stick is absorbed in the first break locations”
mechanismTHE EVALUATION CRITERION IS OPERATIONALISED AS A PROBABILITY ASSIGNED TO WITHHELD WORDS: under the predictive distribution, A BETTER MODEL WILL ASSIGN HIGHER PROBABILITY TO THE HELD-OUT WORDS - and the procedure divides each document into observed and held-out words, KEEPING THE SETS OF UNIQUE WORDS DISJOINT. What BETTER means is fixed by a number and a partition, and the partition rule is stated— “Under this predictive distribution, a better model will assign higher probability to the held-out words. In more detail, we divide each test document’s words w into a set of observed words wobs and held-out words who, keeping the sets of unique words in wobs and who disjoint.”
mechanismA PROCEDURE IS NAMED BY COMPOSING TWO EXISTING NAMES: interleaving the coordinate ascent updates with an update that increases the bound IS CALLED VARIATIONAL EXPECTATION-MAXIMIZATION - and the stochastic version is then described by what it does DIFFERENTLY, updating the two simultaneously. The name records its parentage and the variant is defined against it— “In the non-stochastic setting, α can be optimized by interleaving the coordinate ascent updates in Figure 3 with an update for α that increases the ELBO. This is called variational expectation-maximization.”

sensea topic - A WEIGHTED DISTRIBUTION OVER THE VOCABULARY: not a subject, a theme or an aboutness, but a probability vector over words, shown to a reader by printing its most frequent members - which is why a multi-word name has no way to stay together inside one— “Each topic is a weighted distribution over the vocabulary and each topic’s plot illustrates its most frequent words”
sensetopic proportions - A DISTRIBUTION OVER TOPICS, so a distribution over distributions: the same word does duty at two levels in one sentence, and a document's 'topics' are weights on objects that are themselves weights on words— “Each document in the collection is associated with a vector of topic proportions θd, which is a distribution over topics”
