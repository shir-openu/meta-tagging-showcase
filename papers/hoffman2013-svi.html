limitationThe general algorithm is derived only for conjugate exponential-family models and does not cover nonconjugate models such as correlated or dynamic topic models. — “We developed our algorithm with conjugate exponential family models.”
limitationConvergence is only to a local optimum, and very small minibatches can lead to a poor one. — “Stochastic variational inference is guaranteed to converge to a local optimum but taking large steps on the basis of very few data points may lead to a poor one.”
limitationThe quality of the solution and speed of convergence depend on how the forgetting rate, delay and minibatch size are set. — “Although stochastic variational inference algorithm converges to a stationary point for any valid κ, τ , and S, the quality of this stationary point and the speed of convergence may depend on how these parameters are set.”
limitationWhat is optimised is a bound standing in for a quantity the method cannot compute. — “Since we cannot compute p(x | α) exactly, an approximate approach is to maximize the fitted variational lower bound”
limitationA prior is made exchangeable for simplicity, and the paper notes the conditional then is not. — “but for simplicity we assume exchangeable Dirichlet priors; that is, we require that all of these parameters are set to the same value”
limitationThe evaluation is built from a split of each test document's words, constructed to be disjoint. — “we divide each test document’s words w into a set of observed words wobs and held-out words who, keeping the sets of unique words in wobs and who disjoint”
limitationHow many topics a collection needs is answered by the model's own posterior. — “Given a collection of documents, the posterior distribution of the hidden structure determines how many topics are needed to describe them”
