sense`integration by congruence` - species limits at the INTERSECTION of two or more independent characters. Stringent; risks beta error, false negatives, and a bias toward old species
sense`integration by cumulation` - species limits from divergence in ONE OR MORE not necessarily overlapping characters. Permissive; risks alpha error, false positives, and taxonomic inflation
sense`unconfirmed candidate species (UCS)` / `deep conspecific lineage (DCL)` / `confirmed candidate species (CCS)` - three states of an undescribed lineage, defined by what the evidence currently supports rather than by the organism
sense`candidate species` - `a set of organisms identified as a putative new species`, in the paper's own glossary; named as `Hirudo medicinalis [Ca3 Siddall et al. 2007]`
sense`subspecies` - `infraspecific Linnaean category sometimes used to classify allopatric or parapatric populations showing SOME DEGREE OF DIVERGENCE ... NOT CONSIDERED LARGE ENOUGH FOR THE SPECIES RANK`. A rank defined by a quantity nobody specifies
