content-observations: finding · quantity · variable · definition · key-term — grounded in the full text
findingIn a corpus of 3,003 naturally occurring prepositional-object / double-object pairs, both members used the same construction in nearly two-thirds of cases, and the tendency was significantly stronger when prime and target shared a verb - the lexical boost observed outside the laboratory, in speech nobody arranged. — “In nearly two-thirds of instances, the first and second member of the pair involved the same construction – that is, priming was observed. Interestingly, this tendency to repeat structure was significantly stronger when the prime and target used the same verb than when they did not”
findingAbstract syntactic form - parts of speech and the phrasal constituents built from them - has a large influence on structural priming, which the review takes as the method's central evidential yield for autonomous syntax while explicitly denying that it is the only thing that primes. — “The production of a sentence critically depends upon an abstract syntactic form that is defined in terms of part-of-speech forms (e.g., nouns, verbs, prepositions) and phrasal constituents organized from those (noun phrases, verb phrases, prepositional phrases), and this abstract syntactic form has a large influence upon structural priming. This runs against more functionalist approaches to syntactic structure (e.g., Bates & MacWhinney, 1982 ), as well as other views that eschew traditional representations of syntactic knowledge (e.g., McClelland, St. John, & Taraban, 1989 ). However, this conclusion should not be taken to mean that only abstract syntactic forms cause priming. We have already suggested that most or all levels of representation may prime. In short, abstract syntax is a central but apparently not exclusive type of knowledge that causes structural priming”
findingRepeating the verb between prime and target enhances priming, and in at least one study the effect was not significant at all without verb repetition - which is what makes the lexical boost a candidate for a second mechanism rather than a mere amplifier. — “When the verb was not repeated, this tendency was not significant, and priming was significantly stronger when the verb was repeated than when it was not”
quantity3003 — prepositional-object / double-object sentence pairs Gries identified in a corpus of British English — “Gries (2005) identified 3003 pairs of prepositional-object and double-object constructions (which were not necessarily adjacent) in a corpus of British English”
quantitytwo-thirds — the share of those corpus pairs whose two members used the same construction - priming visible in unarranged speech — “In nearly two-thirds of instances, the first and second member of the pair involved the same construction – that is, priming was observed”
variableverb repetition between prime and target (independent) — “Priming was significantly enhanced by verb repetition. Other studies have replicated these results”

The recent explosion of research in structural priming has made it the dominant means of investigating the processes involved in the production (and increasingly, comprehension) of complex expressions such as sentences”
