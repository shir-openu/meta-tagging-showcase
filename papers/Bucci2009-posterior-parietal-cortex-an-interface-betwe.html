mechanismPPC output travels through postrhinal, entorhinal and perirhinal cortex to the hippocampus, giving an anatomical route by which parietal processing shapes learning— “The anatomical connections between PPC and corticohippocampal pathways in the medial temporal lobe provide a clear pathway through which information processing in PPC may influence learning.”
