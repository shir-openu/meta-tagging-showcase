mechanismrelevance conservation between layers the relevance held by a unit is redistributed onto the units that fed it, and the total is preserved from one layer to the next, so the prediction survives intact all the way down to the pixels— “The underlying Formula (2) can be interpreted as a conservation law for the relevance R in between layers of the feature processing.”
mechanismgreedy layer-at-a-time propagation relevance is computed one layer at a time rather than globally, which is cheap but can strand relevance in a lower layer where it is then distributed randomly— “A first aspect that needs to be investigated is the greediness of the layer-wise relevance propagation procedure, in the sense that it is computed one layer at a time, and its potential impact on the quality of heatmaps: While computationally advantageous, some of the backpropagated relevance might encounter a dead-end in the lower layers and…
mechanismthe gradient at the prediction point points toward the nearest local optimum, which may carry the SAME SIGN as the prediction - so it does not point at the boundary the explanation is relative to— “The differential Df(x) at the prediction point does not necessarily point to a root which is close under the Euclidean norm.”
mechanisma root is found by taking any candidate with the opposite prediction sign and searching the line between them - the segment must cross zero, so interval intersection locates it— “It is clear that the line l(a) = ax + (1 − a)x′ must contain a root of f which can be found by interval intersection.”
mechanismthe root point is specified by CONSTRAINTS rather than by a formula - it must be a root and must lie near the data point, with the rest deferred— “In particular, the root point x 0 at which the Taylor decomposition is computed is constrained to satisfy f(x 0) = 0 and to lie not too far (e.g. within a fixed radius) from the actual data point x.”
mechanismthe two approaches differ in what they conserve: the earlier work rectifies activations to keep feature maps non-negative, this one uses SIGNED activations to conserve the relevance quantity across layers— “uses rectified linear units to project information from the unfolded maps towards the inputs with the aim to ensure the feature maps to be non-negative.”
distinguishes-from the standard task of optimising a classifier for accuracy and generalisation— “Making, say, a nonlinear classification decision for one particular novel data point transparent to the user is essentially orthogonal to the standard task of optimizing for an excellent and well generalizing classifier.”
critiques the claim that non-linear models' success justifies their opacity— “While highly successful in e.g. hard classification, regression or ranking problems, their non-linearity so far has prevented these models to additionally explain and thus contribute to a better understanding about the nature of the solved problem.”
senselayer-wise relevance propagation = whatever satisfies the constraints; several different algorithms qualify— “It is important to note that the definition is not given as an algorithm or a solution of an objective with a distinct minimum. Instead, it is given as a set of constraints that the solution should satisfy. Thus, different algorithms with different resulting solutions may be admissible under these constraints.”
senseR_d < 0 = evidence AGAINST the structure being present; R_d > 0 = evidence FOR it— “The qualitative interpretation is that R d < 0 contributes evidence against the presence of a structure which is to be classified while R d > 0 contributes evidence for its presence.”
senserelevance at the output layer = the prediction f(x) itself— “The above example gives furthermore an intuition about what relevance R is, namely, the local contribution to the prediction function f(x). In that sense the relevance of the output layer is the prediction itself f(x).”
senseheatmap = relevances mapped to a colour space - named, then explicitly put out of scope— “In terms of subsequent visualization, which however will not be the scope of this paper, the resulting relevances R d for each input pixel x (d) can be mapped to a color space and visualized in that way as a conventional heatmap.”

limitationthe propagation is greedy - computed one layer at a time - and some relevance may hit a dead end in the lower layers and be distributed randomly— “A first aspect that needs to be investigated is the greediness of the layer-wise relevance propagation procedure, in the sense that it is computed one layer at a time, and its potential impact on the quality of heatmaps: While computationally advantageous, some of the backpropagated relevance might encounter a dead-end in the lower layers and…
limitationthe proposed propagation rules are heuristic: whether relevance proportional to weighted neuron activations can be analytically justified is open— “Another open question relates to the heuristic nature of the proposed instances of relevance propagation, in particular, whether the distributed relevance messages being proportional to the weighted neuron activations can be analytically justified.”
limitationthere is no way to evaluate the quality of a heatmap beyond looking at it— “Finally, it is not clear how to evaluate the quality of a heatmap beyond simple visual assessment.”
limitationno universal metric for quantifying heatmap quality exists; the paper defers it to future work— “Future work will need to explore the many domain- and data-specific degrees of freedom in the heatmapping process in order to ultimately propose a universal metric for quantification.”
limitationa decomposition satisfying the conservation equation is neither unique nor guaranteed to be meaningful - the constraint set does not pin down an answer— “As we will show now, a decomposition satisfying Eq (2) per se is neither unique, nor it is guaranteed that it yields a meaningful interpretation of the classifier prediction.”
limitationthe Taylor approach needs a second point besides the input, and finding it is called a technical difficulty in the paper's own words— “One technical difficulty is to find a root point x 0.”
limitationwhere several minimal-norm roots exist the method must average or integrate over them rather than pick one
limitationa competing method is specific to convolutional architectures, which the paper contrasts with its own range of applicability
