mechanisman ontology can never close: no cultural artefact is finally contextualisable, and the vocabularies are uneven across subtopics — “Complex ontologies will never be completely exhaustive since no cultural artifact or experience could be fully and finally contextualized and vocabularies are not equally accurate and detailed for every subtopic.”
mechanismthe target moves: meanings shift over the life of the project doing the modelling — “In addition, contexts and meanings shift over time, even during the timeframe of a research project.”
mechanismthe cost of manual tagging is what forces institutions to publish shallow metadata, which then constrains what anyone can ask of the data — “This leads institutions to publish only a subset of the available information, exposing minimal metadata (Doerr et al. 2010), which might prevent researchers from using the data and constrains them to conventional research methods.”
mechanismdeep modelling is not durable either - the British Museum modelled its whole collection in CIDOC CRM and then could not keep it accessible — “The British Museum, on the other hand, structured its entire collection using CIDOC CRM, but was unable to preserve its database in a consistent and accessible manner…”
