N6-methyladenosine modification of circNSUN2term facilitates cytoplasmic export and stabilizes HMGA2term to promote colorectal liver metastasisterm·key Abstract Circular RNAs (circRNAsterm) have been implicated in cancer progression through largely unknown mechanisms. Herein, we identify an N 6 -methyladenosine (m 6 A) modified circRNAterm, circNSUN2term, frequently upregulated in tumor tissues and serum samples from colorectal carcinomaterm·key (CRC) patients with liver metastasisterm·key (LM) and predicts poorer patient survival. The upregulated expression of circNSUN2term promotes LM in PDXterm·key metastasis models in vivo and accelerates cancer cells invasion in vitro. Importantly, N 6 -methyladenosine modification of circNSUN2term increases export to the cytoplasm. By forming a circNSUN2term/IGF2BP2term·key/ HMGA2term RNA-protein ternary complex in the cytoplasm, circNSUN2term enhances the stability of HMGA2term mRNA to promote CRC metastasis progression. Clinically, the upregulated expressions of circNSUN2term and HMGA2term are more prevalent in LM tissues than in primary CRC tissues. These findings elucidate that N 6 -methyladenosine modification of circNSUN2term modulates cytoplasmic export and stabilizes HMGA2term to promote CRC LM, and suggest that circNSUN2term could represent a critical prognostic marker and/or therapeutic target for the disease.

Introduction Colorectal carcinomaterm·key (CRC) ranks third in terms of incidence and remains the second leading cause of cancer-related mortality worldwide 1 . Clinically, metastasis is the overwhelming cause of death in patients with CRC, and the liver is the most frequent distant metastatic site. Approximately 15–25% of patients with CRC are diagnosed with liver metastasisterm·key (LM), which leads to poor prognosis beyond 5 years 2 . This poor long-term survival outlook highlights the need to understand the mechanisms of LM of CRC to further improve disease control. It is known that LM of CRC follows an ordered and hierarchical pattern, which comprises many steps: proliferation of the primary tumor, transfer to the circulating blood, adhesion to the liver sinusoids, and proliferation into the liver 3 . Although many previous reports have documented that an abundance of molecular abnormalities, in either coding proteins or noncoding RNAs, are involved and play important roles in the pathogenic process of LM of CRC 4 – 6 , the precise molecular mechanisms remain largely unclear.

N 6 -methyladenosine (m 6 A) was the first identified mammalian messenger RNAs (mRNAs) modification and remains the most abundant modification known in eukaryotic mRNAs and noncoding RNAs (ncRNAs) 7 , 8 . Circular RNAs (circRNAsterm) present a new class of small noncoding RNAs with a covalently single-stranded loop configuration that are produced from direct back-splicing or exon skipping of precursor mRNA (pre-mRNA) 9 . In the past two decades, circRNAsterm were considered to be byproducts of splicing errors with little functional potential 10 . Recently, with the improvement of RNA sequencing and computational approaches, a vast majority of circRNAsterm have been identified, and often exhibit tissue- or developmental stage-specific expression 9 , 11 , 12 . CircRNAsterm exhibit patterns of m 6 A modifications that are distinct from those of mRNAs 13 ; however, the contributions of m 6 A modification for circRNAsterm metabolism have not been fully elucidated.

Dysregulated expressions of circRNAsterm have been identified in distinct pathological processes, including the pathogenesis of liver 14 , bladder 15 , lung 16 , and oral 17 cancers. CircRNAsterm can function as miRNA sponges, modulating the activity of miRNAs on related target genes 14 , 15 or regulate gene expression at both the transcription and splicing levels 18 , 19 ; or can be translated, function as encoded protein 20 , 21 . These findings, taken together, suggest that circRNAsterm play functional roles in fundamental processes and serve as potential clinical molecular markers, thereby providing new insights into the treatment of human diseases, such as cancer. To date, however, the discovery of critical circRNAterm abnormalities, as well as their functions and/or underlying mechanisms, in the LM process of CRC, is still lacking.

In the present study, we demonstrate that a specific circRNAterm, circNSUN2term, mapping to the chromosome 5p15 amplicon in CRC, is frequently upregulated in CRC patients with LM and predicts poorer disease survival. By using a patient-derived xenograft (PDXterm·key) CRC model, we find that higher expression of circNSUN2term potently promotes CRC LM. We further reveal that circNSUN2term is exported by YTH domain-containing protein 1 (YTHDC1term) from the nucleus to the cytoplasm in an m 6 A methylation-dependent manner. Importantly, increased cytoplasmic circNSUN2term substantially enhances the stability of high mobility group AT-hook 2 ( HMGA2term ) mRNA through interaction with an RNA-binding protein (RBP), Insulin-Like Growth Factor 2 mRNA-Binding Protein 2 (IGF2BP2term·key), which consequently leads to the aggressive nature of CRC cells. Clinically, the upregulated circNSUN2term and HMGA2term mRNAs are more prevalent in LM tissues than that in primary CRC tissues from the same patient. Our findings suggest that circNSUN2term functions as a critical predictor for LM of CRC patients and/or a potential therapeutic target against CRC.

Discussion In this study, we first demonstrated that circNSUN2term, which maps to the 5p15 amplicon in CRCs, is an important circRNAterm that promotes LM in CRC. We showed that circNSUN2term is frequently upregulated in CRC patients with LM and predicts poorer patient survival. By using a PDXterm·key model, we found that higher expression of circNSUN2term promotes LM in CRCs. We revealed that the nuclear export of circNSUN2term is mediated by YTHDC1term in an m 6 A methylation-dependent manner. Importantly, increased cytoplasmic expression of circNSUN2term enhances the stability of HMGA2term mRNA by forming a circNSUN2term/IGF2BP2term·key/ HMGA2term RNA−protein ternary complex, which consequently leads to the LM of CRC. Clinically, the expressions of circNSUN2term, HMGA2term and CXCR4term·key are significantly associated with advanced T status and occur more frequently in LM compared to PCs.

While m 6 A is recognized as an abundant cotranscriptional modification in mRNAs and ncRNAs 7 , 8 , including circRNAsterm 13 , and is implicated in numerous aspects of post-transcriptional mRNA metabolism 38 , 46 , 47 , little is known about the effects of m 6 A modification on cellular circRNAsterm biology. The biogenesis of circRNAsterm has been widely studied from different aspects. Generally, circRNAsterm are regarded as cotranscriptional products resulting from canonical linear mRNA splicing, which occurs in the nucleus 9 . However, the majority of circRNAsterm have been found localized in the cytoplasm. Therefore, it is critical to investigate the underlying mechanisms that regulate the export of circRNAsterm from the nucleus to the cytoplasm. Recent findings have demonstrated that Drosophila Hel25E and its human homologs, UAP56/URH49, regulate circRNAsterm localization and control the efficiency of nuclear export by measuring the lengths of mature circRNAsterm 48 . Our study provides the first evidence that the export of circNSUN2term from the nucleus to the cytoplasm is dependent on m 6 A modification and is mediated through the recruitment of YTHDC1term. Moreover, although m 6 A modification has been reported as widespread in circRNAsterm and can be recognized by YTHDF1 and YTHDF2term, m 6 A-modified circRNAsterm exhibit less stability when regulated by YTHDF2term 13 . On the other hand, YTHDC1term is shown to interact with splicing factors SRSF1, SRSF3, SRSF7, SRSF7 and SRSF10, regulating the splicing of mRNA 38 . Additionally, YTHDC1term facilitates mRNA binding to both SRSF3 and the canonical export receptor NXF1, mediating export and metabolism of m 6 A-modified mRNAs 37 . We identified that circNSUN2term is interacted with YTHDC1term, SRSF3 and NXF1 from mass spectrometry (Fig. 4a , PeptideAtlas Dataset ID: PASS01424), suggesting that the export of circNSUN2term from the nucleus is through YTHDC1-dependent export. Herein, our results strongly suggest that YTHDC1term exhibits a distinct function compared with YTHDF2term in promoting the cytoplasmic export of m 6 A-modified circNSUN2term and supporting an emerging paradigm of m 6 A as a potential selective signal for the metabolism of mammalian circular RNAs.

So far, there are three well-studied mechanisms by which circRNAsterm exert their biological functions. The first is that nuclear retained circular RNAs can regulate gene expression at both the transcription and splicing levels 14 , 15 . The second is that circRNAsterm can be translated and function as encoded proteins 18 , 19 . The third, which has been widely demonstrated, is that circRNAsterm can act as sponges for miRNAs through their binding sites to modulate the activity of miRNAs on other target genes 20 , 21 . Herein, we provide a potent mechanism by which circNSUN2term can enhance the stability of HMGA2term mRNA by forming a circNSUN2term/IGF2BP2term·key/ HMGA2term RNA-protein ternary complex. Therefore, from the aspect of circRNAterm regulation, we discovered a distinctive function by which circRNAsterm can enhance mRNA stabilization through interacting with RBPs. Interestingly, for IGF2BP2term·key, we identified a regulatory mechanism of mRNA stabilization in which IGF2BP2term·key enhances the stabilization of HMGA2term in an m 6 A modification-independent manner, providing an important role for circRNAsterm in RNA metabolism.

Genomic copy number aberrations are believed to be the driver of tumor carcinogenesis through amplification of oncogenes, inactivation of tumor suppressor genes, or more subtle gene dosage changes. In particular, copy number gains of 5p15.31, 8q24.21, 8q24.3 and 13q12.13, as well as losses of 5q21 and 18q21.1, have been identified as frequently occurring in a substantial fraction of CRC tissues 22 – 26 . To date, studies of these CRC susceptibility loci have mainly focused on a limited number of encoding genes 22 , 49 – 52 . Thus, to further reveal the potential regulatory mechanisms under these genomic loci, studies on the pathological functions of small noncoding RNAs are required. CircRNAsterm are a novel type of noncoding small RNAsterm that form a covalently closed loop structure 9 . During the past 20 years, a large number of exonic and intronic circRNAsterm have been identified among eukaryotes, 11 , 53 indicating that circRNAsterm are not simply aberrant splicing byproducts but rather have multiple potential biological functions. Our study identified that circNSUN2term, arising from the 5p15.31 amplicon, is frequently upregulated in CRCs with LM and is related to poorer patient OS.

Although dysregulation of circRNAsterm has been reported in CRC tissues previously 54 , no prior study has investigated their functions in LM of CRC or explored their feasibility as invasive biomarkers in serum of CRC patients. This is the first study to explore the clinical relevance of circNSUN2term expression in CRC patients with LM and matched serum specimens. We observed a significant increase of circNSUN2term expression in tumor tissues and matched serum from CRC patients with LM. Our data from clinical specimens implies that circNSUN2term may serve as a diagnostic and/or prognostic marker of CRC patients with LM.

Another unique strength of our study is that we were able to substantiate our results by using human clinical CRC specimens implanted into an in vivo metastasis PDXterm·key model. We identified that high expression of circNSUN2term in CRC cells dramatically promoted LM of CRC in the PDXterm·key model. We recognize that although our liver and/or systemic metastasis mouse PDXterm·key models may not reproduce the complexity of metastatic CRC in human beings, our results from this animal model strongly support the clinical data and in vitro functions of circNSUN2term in promoting LM of CRC.

In conclusion, we provide the first line of comprehensive evidence that circNSUN2term is an important oncogenic circRNAterm as well as a diagnostic/prognostic biomarker for CRCs with LM. CircNSUN2term exerts a critical role in stabilizing HMGA2term mRNA by forming a circNSUN2term/IGF2BP2term·key/ HMGA2term ternary complex to promote CRC cell aggressiveness (Fig. 7h ). Importantly, our findings may offer a potential therapeutic target, circNSUN2term, to broaden the treatment options for human CRC, especially the disease with LM.
