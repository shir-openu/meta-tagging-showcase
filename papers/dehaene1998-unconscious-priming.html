senseTHE INVISIBLE PRIME APPLIES THE TASK INSTRUCTIONS, WHICH IS A STRONGER CLAIM THAN SEMANTIC ACTIVATION. `The interference between prime and target can be attributed to a COVERT, PRIME-INDUCED ACTIVATION OF MOTOR CORTEX, a response bias that must be overcome in incongruent trials. This indicates that the prime was UNCONSCIOUSLY PROCESSED ACCORDING TO TASK INSTRUCTIONS, ALL THE WAY DOWN TO THE MOTOR SYSTEM.` **The unseen numeral was compared to 5 and a hand was prepared.** Most masked-priming records in this corpus argue about whether MEANING was reached; this one argues that a RULE was executed on something nobody saw— “We then showed that the interference between prime and target can be attributed to a covert, prime-induced activation of motor cortex, a response bias that must be overcome in incongruent trials. This indicates that the prime was unconsciously processed according to task instructions, all the way down to the motor system”
senseAND THE CONTROL ANSWERS THE OBJECTION THAT WEAKENED AN EARLIER RESULT IN THIS SAME CORPUS: `the ENTIRE RESPONSE TIME DISTRIBUTION was shifted by ~24 ms in incongruent trials compared with congruent trials. Thus, THERE WAS NO EVIDENCE THAT THE EFFECT WAS FOUND IN ONLY A SMALL PROPORTION OF SUBJECTS OR A SMALL NUMBER OF TRIALS with a distinct distribution of response times.` buchner2003 - tagged three records ago - reports that Driver & Baylis's cross-modal effect `was observed only for a small subgroup of 14 (out of 24) participants`, which `leaves some uncertainty as to the reality` of it. **This paper pre-empts exactly that, with a distributional argument rather than an average**, and reports that all 12 subjects showed the effect— “Furthermore, the entire response time distribution was shifted by ,24 ms in incongruent trials compared with congruent trials. Thus, there was no evidence that the effect was found in only a small proportion of subjects or a small number of trials with a distinct distribution of response times”
senseTHE WHOLE PROCESSING CHAIN IS PLACED OUTSIDE AWARENESS IN ONE SENTENCE: `A stream of PERCEPTUAL, SEMANTIC AND MOTOR processes can therefore occur WITHOUT AWARENESS.` Three stages, named in order, and the claim is that none of them requires consciousness. **That is the sentence the rest of this corpus's masked-priming records are arguing with** - Holcomb2005 asks whether the N400 stage is automatic, Huang2018 restricts affective primacy to face stimuli, Kouider2008 asks about levels of processing. Here all three levels are claimed at once, from a single 43 ms numeral— “A stream of perceptual, semantic and motor processes can therefore occur without awareness”
controlthe number-comparison task performed twice by every subject, in counterbalanced order — held — rules out an order effect between the two runs, and between-subject differences, at once - each subject does both and the order is balanced across them— “Subjects performed the number-comparison task twice in counterbalanced order.”
