mechanismCortical perceptual binding regions lose their representations as delay grows, presumably through interference from incoming sensory input, shifting the load onto the hippocampus— “However, as the delay increases, the cortical regions that support these perceptual bindings are no longer able to maintaining those representations, presumably because of interference from other sensory input.”
