transferfrom peace research (Galtung and Ruge's study of foreign news): the taxonomy of news values used throughout journalism studies originates in a 1965 peace-research paper on the structure of foreign news, and is here re-tested rather than assumed— “One of the most inﬂuential and frequently cited of these is a paper by Johan Galtung and Mari Ruge (1965), “The Structure of Foreign News”, published more than 50 years ago.”
transferfrom content analysis (empirical media research): the criteria are put to the test by an empirical content analysis of published news, importing a quantitative method into a literature that had treated the list as given— “Just as that study put Galtung and Ruge’s criteria to the test with an empirical content analysis of published news, this new study explores the extent to which Harcup and O’Neill’s revised list of news values remains relevant given the challenges (and opportunities) faced by journalism today, including the emergence of social media.”
