Exclusivityterm: Stories generated by, or available ﬁrst to, the news organisation as a result of interviews, letters, investigations, surveys, polls, and so on. . Bad newsterm: Stories with particularly negative overtones such as death, injury, defeat and loss (of a job, for example). . Conﬂictterm: Stories concerning conﬂictterm such as controversies, arguments, splits, strikes, ﬁghts, insurrections and warfare. . Surprise: Stories that have an element of surprise, contrast and/or the unusual about them. . Audio-visuals: Stories that have arresting photographs, video, audio and/or which can be illustrated with infographics. . Shareabilityterm: Stories that are thought likely to generate sharing and comments via Facebook, Twitter and other forms of social media. . Entertainmentterm: Soft stories concerning sex, showbusiness, sport, lighter human interest, animals, or offering opportunities for humorous treatment, witty headlines or lists. . Magnitudeterm: Stories perceived as sufﬁciently signiﬁcant in the large numbers of people involved or in potential impact, or involving a degree of extreme behaviour or extreme occurrence. . Celebrity: Stories concerning people who are already famous. . Good news: Stories with particularly positive overtones such as recoveries, breakthroughs, cures, wins and celebrations. .
