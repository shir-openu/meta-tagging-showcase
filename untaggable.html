Key finding. Every untaggable paper here failed at text extraction (garbled source layer), not at faceting. On clean text the method faceted 525/525. So this 2.6% measures digitisation/sourcing quality, not tagging-method coverage. Characteristic signal so far: untaggable papers skew older (median year 2000.5 vs 2014 for tagged) and paywalled (36% personal-copy vs 7% among tagged) — i.e. this correlates with age & access, not reliability. The reliability signal lives elsewhere: in the facet profile of papers that tag successfully (bold claim + no stated limitations + untested/failed replication) — a separate scan.
