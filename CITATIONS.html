How to read a doubtful one

A citation is usually a name and a year — “Devine (1989, Experiment 2)” — rather than a title. Every one of the relations below names a year and none names a title, which is why a shared surname and year is sometimes not enough to tell two papers apart; where that happens the edge says so instead of choosing quietly. Relations to work outside this corpus are not shown at all: this page is only the part where both ends are here.
