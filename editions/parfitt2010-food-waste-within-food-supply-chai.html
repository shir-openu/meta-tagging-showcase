sensesfood waste - THE PAPER ENUMERATES THREE COMPETING DEFINITIONS AND SAYS WHICH IT USES, which is rare enough to be the reason this record is worth holding. (1) FAO 1981: wholesome edible material intended for human consumption, discarded, lost, degraded or eaten by pests anywhere in the chain. (2) Stuart 2009: as (1) PLUS edible material fed to animals or diverted as a processing by-product. (3) Smil 2004: as (1) and (2) PLUS OVER-NUTRITION - the gap between energy consumed and energy needed. The paper adopts the first two and states that the second is only usable where nutritional data on animal feed exist

sensesfood waste vs food LOSS - NOT SYNONYMS AND THE DIFFERENCE IS WHERE THE REMEDY GOES. `Food loss` is used post-harvest and names a decrease in quantity or quality; `food waste` is used at the consumer end and relates to behaviour.
