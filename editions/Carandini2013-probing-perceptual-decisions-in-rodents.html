concept_edgesSignal Detection Theory —contrasted-with→ Drift Diffusion Model
differs in: THE AXIS IS WHETHER THE DECISION VARIABLE EVOLVES WITH ACCUMULATING EVIDENCE. 'An increasingly popular ALTERNATIVE TO SDT, the Drift Diffusion Model POSTULATES A DECISION VARIABLE THAT DRIFTS in one direction or the other depending on THE ACCUMULATING EVIDENCE.' Both models produce a choice from noisy evidence; only one gives the decision a trajectory in time, which is what makes reaction times predictable and is the whole of the difference.
