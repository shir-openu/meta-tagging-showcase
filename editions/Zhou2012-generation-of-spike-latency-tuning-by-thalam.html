Because inhibition is more broadly tuned than excitation, the excitation/inhibition ratio falls at the receptive field edges, which further prolongs the already long integration time there. concept_edgestuning —contrasted-with→ spike
differs in: THE AXIS IS HOW MANY TRIALS THE TUNING NEEDS TO EXIST. 'A RATHER SMOOTH TUNING CURVE OF FSL APPEARED TO BE ESTABLISHED WITH A SINGLE TRIAL, WHEREAS THAT OF SPIKE RATE RELIED ON AVERAGING OF MULTIPLE TRIALS.'
