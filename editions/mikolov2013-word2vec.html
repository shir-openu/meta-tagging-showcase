limitationsThe benchmark's question count is combinatorial: questions are formed by pairing items from short hand-made lists, so the number of questions is far larger than the number of independent facts being tested.
“The questions in each category were created in two steps: first, a list of similar word pairs was created manually. Then, a large list of questions is formed by connecting two word pairs. For example, we made a list of 68 large American cities and the states they belong to, and formed about 2.5K questions by picking two word pairs at random”
what this limits: the size of this test set as a measure of evidence: a list of 68 hand-listed American cities and their states was expanded into about 2,500 questions by pairing entries at random, so questions within a category are not independent of one another
