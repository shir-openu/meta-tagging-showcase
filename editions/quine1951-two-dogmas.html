THE BOUNDARY BETWEEN THESE TWO IS NOT DRAWABLE - the NEGATION of `distinct-from`, and no type we have can express it. All eight types assert that a relation HOLDS; not one can say a relation FAILS, so a paper whose contribution is that a distinction cannot be drawn has no home, and the nearest available move records the distinction as holding. That is exactly what the retracted quine1951[14] did: the paper's DENIAL of the boundary quoted as evidence FOR the boundary. SECOND ATTESTATION, in another field and with no shared vocabulary: Kumar2016 concedes in an fMRI methods paragraph that 'the regressors for the maintenance and encoding phases may be correlated' and 'the activity observed in the maintenance phase may thus be contaminated' - the authors warning they may not reliably tell the two apart, recorded in our corpus as `encoding --distinct-from--> maintenance`. Strip the subject matter and the two papers assert the same relation: THESE TWO CANNOT RELIABLY BE TOLD APART, one argued in philosophy in 1951 and one conceded in a methods note. Neither cites the other and they share no words. Kumar2016 is fenced and is cited here, not touched. Two attestations START A COUNT, they do not license a ninth type.
