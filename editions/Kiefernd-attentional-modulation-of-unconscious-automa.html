senses**THE DEFINITIONAL CLAIM IS QUOTED HERE VERBATIM AND THEN CONTRADICTED BY THE SAME PAPER.** `In classical theories of attentional control, INDEPENDENCE OF CAPACITY-LIMITED ATTENTION is a DEFINING FEATURE of automatic processes (Posner & Snyder, 1975).` If independence from attention is what makes a process automatic, and the process turns out not to be independent of attention, then either it is not automatic or the definition is wrong. **The paper takes the second option and keeps the word** - which is precisely the move this corpus exists to make visible, and it is made in an introduction without comment
“In classical theories of attentional control, independence of capacity-limited attention is a defining feature of automatic processes”

senses**THE RESULT, AND `PREREQUISITE` IS A STRONGER WORD THAN `MODULATES`:** `The present results show that TEMPORAL ATTENTION TO THE PRIME IS A PREREQUISITE for obtaining masked N400 priming effects.` Not amplified, not enhanced - required. Without attention in the prime's time window there is no masked N400 priming at all. **neely1977 built a design to separate the automatic component from the attentional one; this says the separation does not hold at the boundary**
