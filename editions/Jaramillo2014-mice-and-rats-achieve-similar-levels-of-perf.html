Where this record's text came from. faceted 2026-08 by reading the locally held text (xml); every evidence string verified as an exact substring of that text before merging. claim_type is 'null-result' because the contribution is the absence of a species difference in the outcome measures that matter for using mice as a model; the one positive difference found runs the other way and is explicitly hedged — 'On average, rats learned the task faster than mice, although some mice were as fast as the average rat.' A 'method' tag was the alternative, since the paper doubles as a paradigm guide ('Our results demonstrate that mice are an appropriate model for the study of the neural mechanisms underlying adaptive decision-making, and suggest they might be suitable for other cognitive tasks as well.'), but the evidence base is a comparison of two cohorts, not a new technique.
