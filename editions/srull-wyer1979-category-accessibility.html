sensesFREQUENCY IS A DOSE, AND THE PAPER SAYS SO AS A GENERAL LAW RATHER THAN A RESULT: `The accessibility of these concepts, and therefore the likelihood that they are subsequently used, INCREASES WITH THE NUMBER OF TIMES that instances of them have been activated IN THE PAST.` Not `in this experiment` - in the past. **The claim is that ordinary life is continuously setting accessibility**, and the laboratory manipulation is only a controlled instance of something always running. That is a much larger claim than the experiment tests, and it is the one the field inherited
“The accessibility of these concepts, and therefore the likelihood that they are subsequently used, increases with the number of times that instances of them have been activated in the past”
