sensesTHE DELAYED EFFECT IS BIGGER THAN THE IMMEDIATE ONE, AND THE EXPLANATION IS CORRECTION RATHER THAN ACTIVATION: `It may be that an AWARENESS that one's categorizations are BIASED accounts of the stimulus information can function as a `DISCOUNTING CUE` ... that ATTENUATES the direct impact of the categorizations.` The activation does not grow; the correction fades. **So awareness here REDUCES the measured effect, and an immediate test UNDERSTATES it** - the opposite sign to Holcomb2005, where prime visibility PREDICTED the size of semantic priming. Two literatures with awareness pushing the measurement in opposite directions, which is a reason to record the direction and not only the presence of an awareness check
