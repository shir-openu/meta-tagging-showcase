senses**ONE NAME, TWO COMPONENTS - AND THE THIRD RECORD TODAY TO MAKE THAT MOVE:** `It is concluded that normal repetition effects consist of TWO COMPONENTS: a very brief LEXICAL effect that is INDEPENDENT of frequency and a long-term EPISODIC effect that is SENSITIVE to frequency.` tulving-schacter1990 splits priming into PRS and semantic memory; was2019 splits long-term semantic priming into strengthening and memory-for-operations; this splits the repetition effect into lexical and episodic. **Three decades, three different pairs, and every one splits a term the field was using as if it named one thing.** They are not the same distinction - each is drawn on the dimension its own experiments could separate - which is what makes the pattern worth more than any of them
“It is concluded that normal repetition effects consist of two components: a very brief lexical effect that is independent of frequency and a long-term episodic effect that is sensitive to frequency”
senses[0]0.95the tagger's words
senses**AND MASKING IS A DISSECTION INSTRUMENT HERE, NOT A WAY OF HIDING A STIMULUS:** `the masking is assumed to MINIMIZE THE INFLUENCE OF ANY POSSIBLE EPISODIC TRACE of the prime.` Every other masked-priming record in this corpus masks to make the prime UNREPORTABLE - dehaene1998, marcel1983, Finkbeiner2011, hesselmann2016, and all the argument about whether the subject saw it. **Here masking SUBTRACTS A COMPONENT so the other can be measured alone**, and the evidence that it worked is that frequency attenuation disappears. A reader who knows masked priming as an awareness paradigm will misread this design

direction on the measure: increases · strength of the relation: 0.9 — **`IS A PRODUCT OF` IS THE PAPER'S OWN VERB AND THE CLAIM IS ABOUT THE TASK.** Episodic memory is recruited BY THE LEXICAL DECISION PROCESS, and that recruitment produces the frequency sensitivity. Weight 0.9: it is the abstract's stated hypothesis and the masking experiments are built to test it - remove the episodic trace and the attenuation goes. **The direction is testable and was tested**, which is why this is 0.9 rather than the 0.7 a bare attribution would get.

concept_edgesepisodic effect —part-of→ repetition effects
“It is concluded that normal repetition effects consist of two components: a very brief lexical effect that is independent of frequency and a long-term episodic effect that is sensitive to frequency”
strength of the relation: 0.95 — **THE COMPONENT STRUCTURE, AND `part-of` IS EXACTLY RIGHT FOR ONCE.** The quote says repetition effects CONSIST OF two components and names both with their frequency signatures. The episodic half is the one masking removes. I first ran this from `episodic trace` at weight 0.9, discounting for the fact that the quote says `episodic EFFECT` - and the guard refused it. **The right answer was a second node, not a lower weight.** `episodic trace` is what is STORED and masking removes; `episodic effect` is what is MEASURED and frequency modulates. The paper uses both for this half and never says they are the same. A WEIGHT CANNOT EXPRESS A TERM MISMATCH; A NODE CAN.
