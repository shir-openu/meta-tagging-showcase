sensesSUBLIMINAL versus PRECONSCIOUS - a distinction between two states that can look identical in a behavioural report and differ in WHAT WOULD HAVE HAPPENED. Subliminal information `cannot be brought into consciousness, in spite of all efforts of focused attention`; preconscious information `potentially carries enough activation for conscious access` and is merely buffered for want of attention. **A counterfactual criterion**, which this corpus has very few of - the two are told apart by what a different allocation of attention would have done
