RETRACTED: THE NOTE AND THE TYPE SAY OPPOSITE THINGS. The edge is a `contrasted-with` at weight 0.5. The span: 'THE PROPORTIONS AND EFFECT SIZES WERE SIMILAR AMONG NEOCORTICAL AND CA1 PYR (p=0.97, chi-square test; and p=0.91, Mann-Whitney U-test'. Two tests, two p-values within a hair of 1, and the word `similar`. The edge's own note reads 'and the point is that they did NOT differ - proportions and effect sizes were statistically indistinguishable between the two'. WHEN A TAGGER HAS TO WRITE `AND THE POINT IS THAT THEY DID NOT DIFFER` BESIDE AN EDGE ASSERTING A DIFFERENCE, THE VOCABULARY HAS FAILED AND THE TAGGER HAS NOTICED. THE NULL RESULT HAD NOWHERE TO GO. The schema has `contrasted-with` and `distinct-from` for a difference and `same-as` for an identity, and NOTHING FOR 'THE DIFFERENCE WAS LOOKED FOR AND NOT FOUND'. So the nearest positive type was chosen, the truth was written into `note`, and `weight` was lowered - which is this project's own thesis committed inside its own layer: the missing vocabulary did not produce a gap in the data, IT PRODUCED A VALUE IN THE WRONG FIELD. A join reads the type; it does not read the note, and it cannot distinguish a low weight meaning 'weak evidence' from one meaning 'the opposite of what I said'. Q-388. — strength of the relation: 0.5 — the record marks this weak — and the point is that they did NOT differ - proportions and effect sizes were statistically indistinguishable between the two
