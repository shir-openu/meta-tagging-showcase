concept_edgesRETRACTED (TWO EFFECTS OF A COMMON CAUSE, TAGGED AS CAUSING EACH OTHER - a class I have not named before) · hippocampus —causes→ behavioral performance
RETRACTED: TWO EFFECTS OF A COMMON CAUSE, TAGGED AS CAUSING EACH OTHER - a class I have not named before. The edge is `hippocampus --causes--> behavioral performance`. The span is a chain of PREDICTIONS: 'we PREDICTED that subjects who could not maintain a sustained focus of attention ... would rely more on LTM, and, THEREFORE, hippocampus WOULD BE ACTIVATED to a greater extent in these subjects. These subjects, however, WOULD PERFORM POORLY on the task BECAUSE recalling the tone from LTM would entail proactive interference'. The upstream cause is failure to sustain attention, which produces LTM reliance; LTM reliance produces BOTH greater hippocampal activation AND poor performance. Hippocampal activation is a MARKER of the same upstream process, not a cause of the performance. Reading the two consequences as one causing the other is the classic confound, and here the paper itself lays the structure out. WHAT THE SPAN DOES SAY (Q-309): housed below as the common-cause structure, since no type can record 'these two share a cause'. — SIGN NEGATIVE, AND PREDICTED IN ADVANCE FROM THE RIVAL'S MODEL: more hippocampal activity should mean worse performance if it reflects falling back on long-term memory
