response priming subjective brightness local luminance contrast lightness constancy The feedforward sweeps triggered in turn by flankers and targets still carry only local contrast information, so motor activation is driven by raw local contrast while conscious brightness is settled later by recurrent interactions. Conscious lightness and brightness are computed later, by recurrent processing that trails the initial feedforward wave. Response priming accesses a stimulus representation that precedes lightness constancy, i.e. one that has not yet discounted illumination.
