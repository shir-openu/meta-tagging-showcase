The span says the opposite in as many words: 'all possible NRF models are a SUBSET of all possible DNet models' - NRF is inside DNet, DNet is the more general class. So the edge fails twice over - wrong direction for the subsumption, and unscoped for the equivalence. WHAT THE SPAN DOES SAY (Q-309), now recorded as two edges that split the two claims apart: `NRF model --is-a--> DNet` for the general subsumption, and `DNet --same-as--> NRF model` carrying `holds_when` for the equivalence that holds at one point of the parameter space.
