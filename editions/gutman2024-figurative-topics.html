The rule against a class of language is stated in that class of language AND THE SHARPER POINT IS THAT THE HOMONYMY DOES THE WORK, NOT THE METAPHOR: the influence is NOT ONLY evident through the carpenter - RATHER IT IS THE MORE SUBTLE HOMONYM SENSES OF THE WORDS UPRIGHT AND RULE THAT TRULY REVEAL THE PERVASIVE POWER OF THE FIGURATIVE. Two words carrying two senses each, unnoticed by the speakers, are what the argument turns on A CLASS OF METAPHOR IS DEFINED BY WHAT WOULD BE IMPOSSIBLE WITHOUT IT: the word is catalogued as part of the ORIENTATIONAL class of metaphors, which RELY ON OUR MOST BASIC SENSE OF SPACE AND PHYSICAL CONCEPTS, so much so that WITHOUT WHICH WE COULD NOT FUNCTION IN THE WORLD - COULD NOT REASON OR COMMUNICATE. The category's members are identified by the cost of their absence A METHOD'S OUTPUT AND THE CONCEPT IT IS MEANT TO MEASURE ARE REPORTED NOT TO CORRESPOND: after comparing a computational reading with a thematic one, the conclusion quoted is WE CANNOT FIND A CLEAR OR CONSTANT RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN AN LDA-TOPIC AND A THEME. The instrument produces something, and it is not the thing the word names THE METHOD IS SAID TO WORK ON ONE TEXT TYPE AND FAIL ON ANOTHER, AND THE REASON GIVEN IS CONTEXT-EXCLUSIVITY OF VOCABULARY: topic modelling applied to poetry DOES NOT EXTRACT CLEAR AND DEFINED THEMES AS IT DOES WHEN APPLIED TO SCIENTIFIC TEXTS, because IN SCIENTIFIC TEXTS THERE ARE WORDS THAT ARE USED EXCLUSIVELY IN VERY SPECIFIC CONTEXTS - and THE LINGUISTIC USES IN POETRY WORK IN THE EXACT OPPOSITE WAY. **The property that makes the method work is a property of the vocabulary, not of the algorithm** THE NAMING STEP IS IDENTIFIED AS THE MANUAL AND SUBJECTIVE ONE: in topic modelling, DISCERNING THE TITLES of the groups of words REMAINS A MANUAL TASK THAT REQUIRES ACTIVE AND OFTEN SUBJECTIVE INTERPRETATION. The algorithm delivers groups; a person supplies the words that say what the groups are A NAMED DEFECT OF AUTOMATIC GROUPING IS CITED, AND IT IS A DISAGREEMENT BETWEEN MACHINE AND HUMAN CATEGORIES: a list of words treated as one topic MAY INCLUDE WORDS THAT HUMAN INTERPRETERS RECOGNIZE AS BELONGING TO DISTINCT TOPICS - a problem with its own name, WORD INTRUSION. The defect is defined by the mismatch, and the mismatch is what the name records THE RIGHT RELIABILITY MEASURE IS NAMED, ITS PURPOSE STATED, AND IT IS EXPLICITLY NOT DONE, WITH THE REASON: given the SAME set of topics and instances, TWO INTERPRETERS MIGHT STILL CHOOSE DIFFERENT TOPICS for a word - INTER-ANNOTATORS AGREEMENT is the method by which that agreement can be measured, PROVIDING A STATUS OF CONSENSUS, and the procedure REQUIRES CERTAIN RESOURCES AND HENCE WILL BE IMPLEMENTED later. **The measure that would qualify the result is named and deferred in the same breath** THE IMPROVEMENTS PROPOSED ARE PROPERTIES OF THE SETUP RATHER THAN OF THE ANALYSIS: the recommendations begin with REDUCING THE NUMBER OF TOPICS AND ENLARGING THE DATASET, which will allow the algorithm to have MORE DATA AND LESS AMBIGUITY REGARDING TOPICS - so the ambiguity is treated as a quantity the design can lower rather than a feature of the object metaphor literary criticism system-or-tool-building
