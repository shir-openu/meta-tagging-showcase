consciousness qualia hard problem subjective experience easy problems THE FIELD'S PROBLEMS ARE DIVIDED BY THE METHOD THAT ADDRESSES THEM, NOT BY SUBJECT MATTER: the easy problems are DEFINED as those directly susceptible to the standard methods of cognitive science - explanation in terms of computational or neural mechanisms - and the hard ones as those that seem to RESIST those methods. The dividing principle is tractability by an instrument THE CENTRAL TERM IS DECLARED AMBIGUOUS BEFORE ANYTHING IS CLAIMED ABOUT IT: consciousness is said to be an ambiguous term referring to MANY DIFFERENT PHENOMENA, each needing explanation and some easier than others - so the paper's first move is to deny that its subject is one thing ONE WORD IS SHOWN CARRYING SEVERAL OPERATIONAL SENSES AT ONCE, each attaching to a different test: a state is called conscious when it is VERBALLY REPORTABLE, or when it is INTERNALLY ACCESSIBLE; a system is conscious OF information when it can react on that basis, or attends to it, or integrates it into control of behaviour; an action is conscious when DELIBERATE; an organism is conscious when AWAKE A VOCABULARY SPLIT IS PROPOSED AND THE COST OF NOT ADOPTING IT IS NAMED: reserve the word for the phenomena of experience and use the LESS LOADED term `awareness` for the straightforward ones - and the stated consequence of the present situation is that those who talk about consciousness are FREQUENTLY TALKING PAST EACH OTHER A BAIT-AND-SWITCH IS ANATOMISED AS A RECURRING GENRE IN THE LITERATURE, not as a mistake by any one author: the theory delivered TURNS OUT TO BE a theory of one of the more straightforward phenomena - reportability, introspective access, OR WHATEVER - after which the author declares the problem tractable after all, leaving the reader FEELING LIKE THE VICTIM OF A BAIT-AND-SWITCH while the hard problem remains untouched A TEST IS GIVEN FOR WHETHER A TERM IS EXHAUSTED BY A FUNCTION, AND IT IS A TEST ON THE RESIDUAL QUESTION: someone who says the verbal-report function has been explained BUT NOT reportability is making a TRIVIAL CONCEPTUAL MISTAKE, because ALL IT COULD POSSIBLY TAKE to explain reportability is an explanation of how the relevant function is performed - and the same is said to go for the others AND THE SAME TEST IS RUN ON THE CONTRAST CASES AND COMES OUT THE OTHER WAY: after every cognitive and behavioural function in the vicinity has been explained there MAY STILL REMAIN A FURTHER UNANSWERED QUESTION - why is the performance of these functions accompanied by experience - and the paper states flatly that THERE IS NO ANALOGOUS FURTHER QUESTION in the explanation of genes, or of life, or of learning. One test, several terms, opposite verdicts THE METHODS ARE SAID TO BE FITTED TO A CLASS OF PROBLEM BY DESIGN, WHICH IS WHY A RESIDUE REMAINS: the usual methods were developed PRECISELY to explain the performance of cognitive functions and do a good job of it - and as they stand they are ONLY equipped for that, so on the remaining problem the standard approach HAS NOTHING TO SAY. The gap is attributed to the instrument's design, not to insufficient effort A THEORY IS SHOWN TO BE UNABLE TO EXPLAIN THE VERY LINK IT RELIES ON: the account GAINS ITS PURCHASE by assuming a connection between binding and experience, AND SO CAN DO NOTHING TO EXPLAIN THAT LINK - with the converse stated as the diagnostic, that if the link were understood the neurophysiological detail would be ICING ON THE CAKE A RESEARCHER'S OWN DEFINITION OF HIS TERM, IN HIS OWN WORDS: he sets the really difficult aspects aside as possibly having NO SCIENTIFIC SOLUTION, proposes to focus on things that are EASIER TO STUDY, describes someone noticing a cappuccino and reaching for it - and closes THAT'S WHAT I CALL AWARENESS. The term is given its scope by an example of responding in a meaningful manner ELIMINATION BY REDEFINITION IS NAMED AS A MOVE: some deny the phenomenon outright, and OTHERS ACHIEVE THE SAME EFFECT by allowing that experience exists ONLY IF the word is equated with something like the capacity to discriminate and report. The denial is performed by the definition rather than by an argument A CRITERION IS GIVEN FOR WHEN A TERM MAY LEGITIMATELY BE RETIRED, AND IT TURNS ON THE TERM'S ROLE RATHER THAN ITS TRUTH: the vital spirit was put forward AS AN EXPLANATORY POSIT, to explain certain functions, AND COULD THEREFORE BE DISCARDED once those functions were explained without it - whereas an EXPLANANDUM IN ITS OWN RIGHT is not a candidate for that kind of elimination THE OBVIOUS PRECEDENT IS RAISED AND THE DISANALOGY IS LOCATED PRECISELY IN WHAT WAS EVER IN DOUBT: vitalist scepticism was doubt about whether physical mechanisms COULD PERFORM THE FUNCTIONS - while the conceptual claim that explanation of functions IS WHAT IS NEEDED was IMPLICITLY ACCEPTED by both sides. The historical parallel is disarmed by separating the empirical bet from the conceptual premise it never touched A FALLACY ABOUT HOW TWO TERMS COME TO BE LINKED IS NAMED AND GIVEN A LAW: the attractiveness of quantum theories of consciousness is attributed to a LAW OF MINIMIZATION OF MYSTERY - consciousness is mysterious and quantum mechanics is mysterious, SO MAYBE THE TWO MYSTERIES HAVE A COMMON SOURCE. The proposed connection is between the two terms' obscurity, not between their contents A CLASS OF THEORY IS SHOWN TO RUN THE EXPLANATION BACKWARDS: these theories ASSUME THE EXISTENCE of consciousness and USE IT IN THE EXPLANATION of quantum processes. The term appears in the explanans where it was wanted in the explanandum A TEMPTING INDUCTION FROM THE HISTORY OF SCIENCE IS NAMED AND BLOCKED BY SORTING ITS CASES: it is tempting to note that puzzling phenomena have always eventually yielded to physical explanation - but EACH of those was a problem about the observable behaviour of physical objects, coming down to structures and functions, and therefore always the KIND OF THING a physical account might explain. The induction fails because its instances share a property the new case lacks experience reductive explanation functional explanation subjective aspect cognitive science awareness the hard problem experience (subjective aspect) function (broad sense) 'something it is like' nonreductive explanation conceptual-analysis
