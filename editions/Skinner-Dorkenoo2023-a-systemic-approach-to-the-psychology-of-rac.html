The review carries a real critique of the field ('this approach largely ignores the systemic nature of racial biases and the ways in which racial biases are shaped by the broader cultural systems in which people live'), and one could defensibly tag it 'critique'; I chose 'mechanism' because the substantive body of the article is a causal account of how five systemic factors produce individual bias and how individual bias reproduces the systems, with the critique serving as framing for that account. Caste is the only prior work the review names in a sentence that also states a relation to its own argument — everything else, including the nested-levels models it adopts (refs 17-20), is cited numerically, so those were not recorded as relations. Two evidence-quality signals in the paper that the facets cannot hold: it twice flags supporting studies as preprints that have not undergone peer review, and it explicitly notes that the effect of exposure to cultural products on racial attitudes has never been experimentally tested. The intervention agenda (racial socialization in white families, bias awareness, historical education) is a fifth of the article and has no facet. 2026-08-12 schema-0.3 pass: every quote verified
