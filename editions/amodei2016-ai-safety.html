We have no type for it: `causes` asserts production, and `operationalised-as` says the authors CHOSE a measure, whereas a proxy claim says THIS STANDS IN FOR WHAT WE ACTUALLY CARE ABOUT AND WE KNOW IT IS NOT THE SAME THING. Measured at 8% of papers. THE CLEAREST PROXY CLAIM IN THE CORPUS, because it names the gap out loud: 'Avoiding side effects can be seen as a proxy for THE THING WE REALLY CARE ABOUT: avoiding negative externalities.'
