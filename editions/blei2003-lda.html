limitationsDocuments as well as words are assumed exchangeable, which the authors note is less often stated.
“All of these methods are based on the “bag-of-words” assumption—that the order of words in a document can be neglected. content_tagsLDA is a generative probabilistic (three-level hierarchical Bayesian) model for text corpora: each document is a finite mixture over latent topics, each topic a distribution over words — giving an explicit topic-based representation of documents.
“latent Dirichlet allocation (LDA), a generative probabilistic model for collections of discrete data such as text corpora. LDA is a three-level hierarchical Bayesian model, in which each item of a collection is modeled as a finite mixture over an underlying set of topics.”
