THE INDEXING / PROXY RELATION - Q-299. `from` STANDS IN FOR `to`. We have no type for it: `causes` asserts production, and `operationalised-as` says the authors CHOSE a measure, whereas a proxy claim says THIS STANDS IN FOR WHAT WE ACTUALLY CARE ABOUT AND WE KNOW IT IS NOT THE SAME THING. Measured at 8% of papers. A measurement substitution stated plainly: 'we use the “follow” relationship as a proxy for THE ACTUAL CONTENT CONSUMPTION'. The word `actual` is doing the same work as amodei2016's 'really care about' - the authors marking the distance between what they measured and what they meant.
