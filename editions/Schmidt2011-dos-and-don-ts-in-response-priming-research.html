sensesA DESIGN FLAW THAT MANUFACTURES THE CORRELATION IT IS USED TO SHOW: varying the prime's duration while leaving the prime-target ISI constant confounds prime duration with SOA and `creates a SPURIOUS CORRELATION between priming and prime visibility by two mechanisms` - a stronger prime gives stronger priming, and a longer SOA gives stronger priming. The fix is to leave the prime intact and control visibility by varying the MASK. A confound that produces apparent evidence for a link between priming and awareness out of the timing alone
“Unless variation of the prime is of theoretical interest, it is therefore crucial to leave the prime intact and control its visibility by variation of mask attributes only (e.g., its stimulus contrast).
