mechanismsResidual activation (the one-locus account): using a construction leaves its combinatorial node and the lemma linked to it partly activated, so the same structure is cheaper to produce for a while. The lexical boost falls out of the same machinery - a repeated verb re-activates both the lemma AND the strengthened link to the node - which is what makes it ONE locus.

sensesstructural priming - THE LINGUISTICS SENSE, and it shares almost nothing with the social-psychology sense this corpus is otherwise full of except the word. The prime is a SENTENCE, the primed thing is its STRUCTURE, the response is another sentence, and the effect is defined disjunctively: to repeat OR BETTER PROCESS a current sentence because of its structural similarity to a previously experienced one. No concept is activated, no behaviour is influenced, no attitude is reached
