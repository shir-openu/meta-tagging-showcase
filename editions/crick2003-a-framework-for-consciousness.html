framework qualia NCC Attention makes an object conscious by biasing the competition between rival neuronal coalitions while those coalitions are still forming, rather than by acting on an already-formed percept. Positive feedback loops iteratively re-excite a neuron, driving its activity past the consciousness threshold and then holding it there for the duration of a snapshot. Feature detectors are built by neurons detecting recurring correlations in their inputs and modifying their synapses so those inputs drive them more easily — the brain is thereby detecting apparent causation. Because motion is coded as a constant firing rate held fixed within one snapshot, a change of motion has no explicit representation, which is why acceleration is hard to perceive. neural correlate of consciousness (NCC) private (of consciousness) projective field explicit representation essential node zombie modes net-wave coalitions of neurons snapshots (of conscious awareness) binding penumbra theoretical-framework
