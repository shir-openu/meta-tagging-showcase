framework qualia NCC Attention makes an object conscious by biasing the competition between rival neuronal coalitions while those coalitions are still forming, rather than by acting on an already-formed percept. Positive feedback loops iteratively re-excite a neuron, driving its activity past the consciousness threshold and then holding it there for the duration of a snapshot. Feature detectors are built by neurons detecting recurring correlations in their inputs and modifying their synapses so those inputs drive them more easily — the brain is thereby detecting apparent causation.
