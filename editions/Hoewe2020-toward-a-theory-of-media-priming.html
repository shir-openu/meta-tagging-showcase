media priming political priming System 1 System 2 agenda setting prime awareness learning A prime spreads activation through an associative memory network, and because only a few activated nodes reach conscious awareness the resulting judgment cannot be traced back to its source. Media priming works by activating nodes or networks of nodes in associative memory outside the media consumer's immediate awareness. Awareness cancels the effect: once people notice the prime or deliberate about the source of their decision they correct for it, which is why the effect requires unawareness.
