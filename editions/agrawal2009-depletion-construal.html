sensesDEPLETION WORKS THROUGH A SELF-ASSESSMENT, NOT THROUGH A RESOURCE: `At low-level construals, individuals rely on RESOURCE ACCESSIBILITY CUES (e.g., feelings of tiredness) to determine self-control. Hence, they exert less self-control ONLY WHEN THEY ASSESS THEMSELVES AS DEPLETED.` The person reads their own tiredness as evidence about how much control they have left, and acts on the reading. **That makes the depletion effect a judgement about oneself rather than a fact about oneself** - and it predicts that anything which changes what you consult will change the effect, which is exactly what the construal manipulation does

sensesAND THE CONTROL THAT TURNS THAT FROM AN INTERPRETATION INTO A RESULT: `DESPITE EXPERIENCING SIMILAR LEVELS OF TIREDNESS as those at lower construal levels, the self-control exhibited by participants at higher levels of construal was NOT ADVERSELY AFFECTED by their feelings of tiredness.` **The resource state is held constant and the effect still disappears.** Same tiredness, different use of it.
