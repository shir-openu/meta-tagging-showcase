Two different claims travel under one theory's name AND THE TARGET IS THE THEORY AS INTERPRETED AND APPLIED, NOT THE FORMALISM: the paper describes classes of problems in which preferences SYSTEMATICALLY VIOLATE the axioms, and concludes that utility theory AS IT IS COMMONLY INTERPRETED AND APPLIED is not an adequate DESCRIPTIVE model. The qualifier is doing the work - what fails is a use of the theory A PHENOMENON IS NAMED AT THE MOMENT IT IS INTRODUCED, AND THE NAME CARRIES THE MECHANISM: people underweight outcomes that are MERELY PROBABLE compared with outcomes obtained with CERTAINTY, and THIS TENDENCY, CALLED THE CERTAINTY EFFECT, is what contributes to risk aversion for sure gains and risk seeking for sure losses THE SECOND NAMED TENDENCY IS EXPLICITLY A FACT ABOUT DESCRIPTION: people DISCARD COMPONENTS SHARED BY ALL PROSPECTS under consideration, and THIS TENDENCY, CALLED THE ISOLATION EFFECT, LEADS TO INCONSISTENT PREFERENCES WHEN THE SAME CHOICE IS PRESENTED IN DIFFERENT FORMS. The inconsistency is produced by the form, not by the choice A FORMAL OPERATIONALISATION IS REPORTED AS FAILING TO CAPTURE THE INTUITIVE NOTION IT STANDS FOR: the aversion to probabilistic insurance is called A RATHER PUZZLING CONSEQUENCE of the risk aversion hypothesis, and the conclusion drawn is that THE INTUITIVE NOTION OF RISK IS NOT ADEQUATELY CAPTURED BY THE ASSUMED CONCAVITY of the utility function for wealth. The formalism and the word have come apart, and the paper says which is at fault THE CONTRAST CLASS IS DISSOLVED INTO A MATTER OF DEGREE BEFORE ANY NEW TERM IS OFFERED: the aversion to probabilistic insurance is called PARTICULARLY INTRIGUING because ALL INSURANCE IS, IN A SENSE, PROBABILISTIC - even the most avid buyer REMAINS VULNERABLE to many risks his policies do not cover. What looked like two kinds of thing is shown to be one kind at two settings THE THESIS IS STATED IN ONE SENTENCE AND THE DEMONSTRATIONS ARE PROMISED: two prospects that are EQUIVALENT IN PROBABILITIES AND OUTCOMES COULD HAVE DIFFERENT VALUES DEPENDING ON THEIR FORMULATION. Everything else in the section is evidence for that one claim about description THE MECHANISM IS THAT ONE OBJECT ADMITS MORE THAN ONE DECOMPOSITION: to simplify a choice people disregard components the alternatives SHARE and focus on those that DISTINGUISH them - which may produce inconsistent preferences BECAUSE A PAIR OF PROSPECTS CAN BE DECOMPOSED INTO COMMON AND DISTINCTIVE COMPONENTS IN MORE THAN ONE WAY, and different decompositions sometimes lead to different preferences THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN TWO FORMULATIONS IS LOCATED IN THE REPRESENTATION ITSELF: the ESSENTIAL DIFFERENCE between the two decision trees is IN THE LOCATION OF THE DECISION NODE - in the standard form the decision maker faces a choice between two RISKY prospects, and in the sequential form between a RISKY AND A RISKLESS one. The same underlying gamble, two diagrams, and the contrast that drives the effect is a feature of the diagram THE EFFECT IS PRESENTED AS FALSIFYING A FOUNDATIONAL SUPPOSITION RATHER THAN AS A CURIOSITY: the reversal of preferences due to the dependency among events is PARTICULARLY SIGNIFICANT BECAUSE IT VIOLATES THE BASIC SUPPOSITION of a decision-theoretical analysis, THAT CHOICES BETWEEN PROSPECTS ARE DETERMINED SOLELY BY THE PROBABILITIES OF FINAL STATES WHICH REPRESENTATION IS NATURAL IS ITSELF A PROPERTY OF THE PROBLEM, NOT A FREE CHOICE: it is EASY TO THINK OF decision problems MOST NATURALLY REPRESENTED in one of the forms rather than the other - a choice between two risky ventures is likely to be viewed in the standard form, and the paper then gives a problem that is MOST LIKELY TO BE REPRESENTED in the sequential form THE THEORY PUTS A RE-DESCRIPTION STEP BEFORE THE EVALUATION STEP AND MODELS IT SEPARATELY: two phases are distinguished, an early phase of EDITING and a subsequent phase of EVALUATION, and the function of the editing phase is TO ORGANIZE AND REFORMULATE THE OPTIONS so as to simplify subsequent choice. A formal theory of how a problem is restated before it is answered THE REFERENCE POINT THAT DECIDES WHAT COUNTS AS A GAIN IS ITSELF SET BY THE FORMULATION: gains and losses are DEFINED RELATIVE TO SOME NEUTRAL REFERENCE POINT, usually the current asset position - but THE LOCATION OF THE REFERENCE POINT, AND THE CONSEQUENT CODING OF OUTCOMES AS GAINS OR LOSSES, CAN BE AFFECTED BY THE FORMULATION of the offered prospects and by the decision maker's expectations AND THE CONCLUSION IS STATED AS A PROPERTY OF PREFERENCE ORDERS: MANY ANOMALIES OF PREFERENCE RESULT FROM THE EDITING OF PROSPECTS, and MORE GENERALLY THE PREFERENCE ORDER BETWEEN PROSPECTS NEED NOT BE INVARIANT ACROSS CONTEXTS, BECAUSE THE SAME OFFERED PROSPECT COULD BE EDITED IN DIFFERENT WAYS DEPENDING ON THE CONTEXT IN WHICH IT APPEARS. The order is a property of the description as much as of the object prospect utility value gains losses decision probabilities risk certainty value function (prospect theory) certainty effect framing of outcomes as gains vs losses (reference point) risk attitude (aversion vs seeking) / subjective value Bayesian sample-size statistical-significance variance behavioural-experiment
