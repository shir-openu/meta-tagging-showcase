The control is designed to be uninformative about performance and informative about cause A RIVAL EXPLANATION OF THE FAILURE IS ELIMINATED BY A SEPARATE MEASUREMENT: the network preferred its own output to the target by log-likelihood, so the errors are not search failures in the decoder - the model is not failing to FIND the right answer, it does not prefer it THE SUFFICIENCY OF THE TRAINING DATA IS ARGUED SEPARATELY FROM THE RESULT AND MARKED AS IMPORTANT: the authors state that the training data of both experiments provide enough evidence to learn the composition rules that would afford the correct generalisations, and then spell out, per experiment, what was demonstrated during training THE DEFLATIONARY READING OF THE RESULT IS STATED BY THE AUTHORS THEMSELVES AND THEN ANSWERED: the finding can be seen as a trivial restatement of the principle that train and test should come from the same distribution - and the reply is that human learners DO generalise to data governed by rules they have learned, so the triviality does not cover the case AN ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATION OF A NULL IS ELIMINATED BY GRADED DOSING RATHER THAN ARGUMENT: composed examples of the new primitive are stepped 8 -> 16 -> 32 and accuracy climbs 38.3 -> 77.8 -> 88.4, appearing GRADUALLY instead of switching on, which rules out the reading that the network had simply lacked evidence THE CAPACITY IS OPERATIONALISED AS A BEHAVIOURAL SIGNATURE, NOT AS AN ACCURACY: what is expected of a systematically compositional device is ALL-OR-NOTHING rule-based behaviour, so a network that generalises powerfully but needs more positive examples for it to emerge fails the definition WHILE SUCCEEDING AT THE TASK THE AUTHORS NAME THEIR OWN TEST'S POSSIBLE UNFAIRNESS BEFORE ANYONE ELSE CAN: they say they tested systematicity in its purest form, then concede the setup is arguably too opaque for a computational model which could lack evidence that the two actions are the same sort of thing - and the concession is what motivates the follow-up experiment THE CONTROL ITEM IS MATCHED ON THE TEST AND DELIBERATELY UNMATCHED ON THE TRAINING: a real adjective seen in 80 different constructions is tested on exactly the same eight constructions as the invented one and scores 8 of 8 against 1 of 8 - so the comparison isolates breadth of training context while holding the test fixed THE POSITIVE HALF OF A NEGATIVE RESULT IS INSISTED ON RATHER THAN CONCEDED: the authors explicitly stress that the high zero-shot accuracy is an important positive result in controlled experiments, and add that understanding the mechanisms behind it is future work -
