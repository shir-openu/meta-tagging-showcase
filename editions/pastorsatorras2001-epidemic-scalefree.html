unhoused_relationsEuclidean lattice → epidemic threshold
A NONZERO EPIDEMIC THRESHOLD IS A PROPERTY OF THE LOCAL-CONNECTIVITY REGIME, NOT SOMETHING THE LATTICE PRODUCES. The lattice does not increase, reduce or eliminate a threshold; models with local connectivity simply HAVE one, and that is the standard result this paper is written against. None of the eight types says `X holds in regime Y`, and none of the four effect directions says a quantity EXISTS - all four describe a change in it.
