The method is Patch-seq, i.e. whole-cell recording combined with single-cell RNA-sequencing and biocytin morphological reconstruction; the controlled method vocabulary has no combined term, so 'electrophysiology' records the core measurement and the transcriptomic and morphological arms are not captured by that facet. claim_type is 'existence' because the headline claim is that at least 20 cross-modal met-types are present; the paper simultaneously reports a partial null (t-types are not reliably predictable from morpho-electric features, classifier accuracy 57%), which the single claim_type field cannot hold.
