limitations[1]the tagger's words
limitationsTD learning depends critically on temporal representations and breaks down under realistic timing noise.
“Simple variants of TD learning are extremely sensitive to timing noise, with even very small amounts of noise devastating the predictive power of the model, and resulting in pronounced prediction errors at the time of reward delivery, even in thoroughly learned tasks.”
what this limits: any application of TD learning under realistic timing: SIMPLE VARIANTS OF TD LEARNING ARE EXTREMELY SENSITIVE TO TIMING NOISE, WITH EVEN VERY SMALL AMOUNTS OF NOISE DEVASTATING THE PREDICTIVE POWER OF THE MODEL
