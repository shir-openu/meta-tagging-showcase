senses[0]0.95the tagger's words
senses`DIFFERENT TEMPORAL LAWS` - the general form of the claim, and it is a statement about SHAPES rather than about magnitudes: conscious perception and unconscious behavioural effects of the same masked stimuli do not merely differ in size, they are governed by different functions of time. Seventh record this sitting where a time course is the load-bearing variable, and the first where two time courses ARE the finding
“Over a wide range of time courses, we show that conscious perception and unconscious behavioral effects of masked visual stimuli obey different temporal laws”

sensesSUBLIMINAL IN WHICH RESPECT - a precision most records in this corpus do not make: `prime stimuli were NOT subliminal in the sense that their presence or absence could not be detected, but the CRITICAL PRIME FEATURE remained perfectly invisible`. Something was plainly there to be seen; the attribute that did the priming was not. `Subliminal` is a predicate of a FEATURE here, not of a stimulus, and the difference decides what the effect is evidence for
