The asymmetry that makes the weaker notion weaker is supplied by the reading of the outcome, not by the mathematics THE CRITERION IS PRESENTED IN TERMS OF WHERE IT PUTS THE COST: it is described as SHIFTING THE BURDEN OF UNCERTAINTY from the protected class to the decision maker, and thereby INCENTIVISING the collection of better features - ones depending more directly on the target than on the protected attribute - and of data allowing better prediction for all groups THE PAPER PROVES THE LIMIT OF ITS OWN ENTIRE FAMILY OF DEFINITIONS: a NON-IDENTIFIABILITY result shows that different dependency structures, WITH POSSIBLY DIFFERENT INTUITIVE NOTIONS OF FAIRNESS, cannot be separated by ANY oblivious notion or test - not merely by theirs. The contribution is a proof about what the class of measures cannot see TWO SITUATIONS THAT DIFFER IN EXACTLY THE WAY THE CONCEPT IS SUPPOSED TO TRACK ARE SHOWN TO BE INDISTINGUISHABLE: the authors construct two dependency structures which can have FUNDAMENTALLY DIFFERENT INTERPRETATIONS FROM THE POINT OF VIEW OF FAIRNESS and yet be indistinguishable from their joint distribution - so no oblivious test can resolve which applies ACCESS TO THE FEATURES DOES NOT RESCUE IT - THE PAPER CLOSES ITS OWN LOOP: without ATTACHING MEANING to the features or making CAUSAL ASSUMPTIONS about them, no further insight into the two scores is gained.
