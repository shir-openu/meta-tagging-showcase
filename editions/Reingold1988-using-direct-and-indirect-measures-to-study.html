concept_edgesindirect measure —distinct-from→ direct measures
Quotation withheld — this record is not cleared for redistribution. The sentence is in the paper at the link above.
differs in: WHETHER THE TWO ARE MEASURED UNDER COMPARABLE CONDITIONS. Avant and Thieman (1985) claimed an indirect measure was MORE sensitive than a direct one; 'CONTRARY TO THIS CLAIM, WHEN DIRECT AND INDIRECT INDEXES ARE MEASURED UNDER COMPARABLE CONDITIONS', the indirect measure is LESS sensitive. The ordering of the two instruments reverses on the comparability of the conditions, not on the instruments · strength of the relation: 0.85 — THE PUBLISHED CLAIM AND ITS REVERSAL USE THE SAME TWO MEASURES. What changed is that they were put on comparable footing - the same lesson as measuring candidates within one run rather than across runs.
