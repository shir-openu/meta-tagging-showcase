senses**AND THE CRITIQUE IT ANSWERS IS AN ATTACK ON THE MEASUREMENT, NOT ON THE EFFECT:** `In a recent critique of subliminal priming, Pratte and Rouder (2009) suggested that previous claims of subliminal priming may have been due to A FAILURE TO CONTROL THE TASK DIFFICULTY between the experiment proper and the prime-classification task.` **If the certifying task is simply harder, chance performance on it certifies nothing.** That is the strongest form of methodological criticism - it does not dispute a result, it disputes what the result is evidence OF - and a corpus that records priming findings without recording this cannot represent why the literature is contested
