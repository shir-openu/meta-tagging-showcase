Only ten participants in the main fMRI experiment, but the paper never flags sample size as a limitation, so it is not recorded as one — the three limitations listed are the ones the article states about itself. The paper also runs extensive internal control analyses (ROI erosion at 2/4/10 mm, inaudibility of the tactile stimuli, air-conduction headphone control, visual-cortex negative control) which function as robustness checks rather than as stated limitations.
