Insufficient pooling: many neurons coding low-level features may fire to both prime and target, yet not enough of their activity converges to drive the sparser, more complex intermediate representation. Receptive-field displacement: mirror reflection moves the prime-activated features outside the receptive fields of the cells that the target activates, so no priming benefit accrues.
