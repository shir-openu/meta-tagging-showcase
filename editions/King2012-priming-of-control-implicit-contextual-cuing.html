sensesTHE MIRROR OF Kiefer2012, tagged earlier tonight. Kiefer argued that top-down configuration GATES unconscious processing - control reaching down into the automatic. This paper argues the other direction: an automatic associative cue TRIGGERS a control state. Between them the boundary is crossed both ways, and neither needs the other to be wrong. What is left of `automatic` after both is the pair of criteria Kiefer kept - not conscious, not deliberately initiated - and nothing about autonomy
