heterosis euheterosis luxuriance adaptive value polygene complexes population cages Natural selection coadapts polygene complexes only within a population, so chromosomes brought together from populations that never interbreed have not been mutually adjusted and their heterozygotes lack heterosis. Heterosis arises from interaction between polygene complexes carried by differently arranged chromosomes, which the inversions protect from being broken up by crossing over in the heterozygote. A second, distinct kind of heterosis arises when deleterious recessive mutants accumulated in the population are sheltered by their normal dominant alleles in the heterozygote.
