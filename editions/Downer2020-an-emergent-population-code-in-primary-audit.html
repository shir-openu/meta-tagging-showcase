population code single-neuron representation psychophysical threshold Because feature tuning co-varies within single neurons, a gain applied to individual cells cannot separate the two features; only re-weighting neurons at the population level recovers a feature-specific signal. Mixed selectivity cancels the average effect of feature-specific gain, which is why single-neuron measures show nothing while the population does. The attentional benefit is carried by higher-order correlations among neurons, i.e. it is an emergent property of the ensemble rather than a sum of single-cell effects.
