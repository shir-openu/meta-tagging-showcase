Rather than turning CARRY INFORMATION into a precise mathematical statement, one just computes correlation functions. **The general term is a substitute for a specific one that is absent** AND THE CONDITIONS UNDER WHICH THE ABSTRACT VOCABULARY BECOMES NECESSARY ARE SPELLED OUT: for most biological systems finding something like an order parameter REMAINS A DISTANT HOPE, SYMMETRIES ARE ABSENT, and EVEN LOCALITY IS NOT MUCH OF A GUIDE - UNDER THESE CONDITIONS, some MORE GENERAL APPROACHES to formalizing the intuition WOULD BE USEFUL. The need for the general term is derived from three specific things the field does not have A DISCIPLINARY TRADITION IS NAMED AS THE REASON FOR A TRADE-OFF, AND THE COST IS STATED IN THE SAME SENTENCE: THROUGHOUT, WE FOLLOW THE PHYSICS TRADITION OF EMPHASIZING THE UNITY OF THEORETICAL IDEAS AT THE EXPENSE OF SOME OF THE BIOLOGICAL COMPLEXITY. One formal object serving as the answer to a question about knowledge and to a question about storage AN AMBIGUITY IN THE UNITS IS NAMED, RESOLVED BY CONVENTION, AND THE NAME IS ATTACHED TO THE CONVENTION: THERE IS SOME AMBIGUITY ABOUT UNITS - the alphabet is a choice and the constant is arbitrary - BUT if a binary alphabet is chosen and the constant set to one, ALL OF THE AMBIGUITY IS RESOLVED, and measured in these units they ARE CALLED BITS. The familiar name names a settled convention, not a quantity THE SAME QUANTITY IS GIVEN TWO DESCRIPTIONS OF OPPOSITE VALENCE AND ONE IS PREFERRED: Shannon's theorem MAKES PRECISE THE VAGUE STATEMENT made in many statistical mechanics classes, that entropy is related to our LACK of information about the microscopic state - and THE MORE POSITIVE VERSION IS THAT THE ENTROPY IS THE INFORMATION WE WOULD GAIN upon learning the full state. **One object, a lack and a gain, and the author says which framing he prefers** A PREDICTION IS RATED BY WHAT IT DID NOT NEED RATHER THAN BY HOW WELL IT FIT: the measured input/output relation agreed REMARKABLY with the theory, ESPECIALLY CONSIDERING THAT THERE ARE NO FREE PARAMETERS - and the result is credited with having INSPIRED THE COMMUNITY TO TAKE THESE IDEAS MORE SERIOUSLY.
