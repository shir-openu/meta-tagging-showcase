unhoused_relationsbandgap → dielectric constant
Quotation withheld — this record is not cleared for redistribution. The sentence is in the paper at the link above.
AN INVERSE TRADE-OFF ACROSS MATERIALS, NOT A CAUSAL EFFECT WITHIN ONE. `any dielectric with a large bandgap will tend to have a low dielectric constant - and vice versa`. Nothing is being done to a bandgap to lower a permittivity; it is a regularity over the space of available materials, and it is the reason a high-k ultrawide-bandgap gate insulator may not exist. `causes ... reduces` would assert a mechanism the paper does not claim, and there is no `trades-off-against` or `correlates-with` type
