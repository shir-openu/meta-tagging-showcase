Its span is 'VAEs GENERALIZE autoencoders, ADDING STOCHASTICITY to the encoder'. Read on `generalize`, a VAE is the broader class and the edge is right. Read on `adding`, a VAE is an autoencoder PLUS machinery - a SPECIALISATION - and the edge is backwards, which is also how the field standardly uses the terms. WITHDRAWN, with the ambiguity recorded, which is the only move that adds no claim the text does not make. — the paper says VAEs GENERALIZE autoencoders, so the plain autoencoder is the special case and the arrow runs that way.
