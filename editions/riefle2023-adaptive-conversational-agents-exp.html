This is an ECIS research-in-progress paper: the design and hypotheses are fully specified but only pilot data (n=37, 30 analysed) are reported, and results are partial - enjoyment and re-use intention were significant only in the 'need for interaction' group, with no significant effects in the cognitive-style group and enjoyment there actually rated worse for the individualized agent. claim_type 'existence' reflects the abstract's confirmed claim; the paper is honest that the effect did not appear across both characteristics.
