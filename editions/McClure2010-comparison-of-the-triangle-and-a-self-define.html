limitationsThe two measures of the same thing disagree in direction: the method with the statistical advantage returns the LOWER estimate of absolute sensory difference, and the paper calls this paradoxical.
“In summary, the SD-AFC showed a statistical advantage over the triangle procedure in these studies. Paradoxically, the method showed lower absolute discrimination as measured by Thurstonian scale values.”
what this limits: choosing a discrimination test on statistical power: the paper reports the SD-AFC as statistically superior to the triangle while returning lower Thurstonian d-prime values for the same products
