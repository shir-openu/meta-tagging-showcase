P3 conscious access task relevance multivariate decoding degenerative research programs phenomenology mdp.erp Conscious access happens because a temporally deep level of the hierarchy infers and integrates lower-level states over a slow enough timescale to contextualise them and drive extended report behaviour. Late ERPs arise because the ERP is the time derivative of the depolarisation variable, so a rapid change in high-level belief produces a large late deflection. Rapid belief change increases the magnitude of the depolarisation derivative, which is what makes a late component appear.
