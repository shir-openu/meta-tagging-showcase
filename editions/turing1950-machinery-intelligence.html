Closeness is asserted; what is claimed for the new question is its clarity, not its identity with the old one THE SUBSTITUTION IS DECLARED AT THE MOMENT IT HAPPENS: after the game is specified, the text states flatly that THESE QUESTIONS REPLACE OUR ORIGINAL - so the swap is a recorded act rather than a drift, and a reader can see exactly which question is being answered from that point on THE WORTH OF THE NEW QUESTION IS ASKED, AND THE REGRESS IS DELIBERATELY CUT SHORT: as well as asking what the answer is, one may ask whether the new question IS A WORTHY ONE TO INVESTIGATE - and that second question is investigated WITHOUT FURTHER ADO, THEREBY CUTTING SHORT AN INFINITE REGRESS. The stopping rule is stated rather than hidden THE TEST'S DESIGN IS JUSTIFIED BY WHAT IT EXCLUDES: the new problem is said to draw A FAIRLY SHARP LINE between the physical and intellectual capacities of a man, and the physical separation of the rooms is the operationalisation of that line - so a condition of the apparatus IS the conceptual distinction IRRELEVANT CAPACITIES ARE EXCLUDED BY THE FORM OF THE TEST RATHER THAN BY A RULE: the machine is not penalised for failing to shine in beauty competitions nor the man for losing a race against an aeroplane, because THE CONDITIONS OF OUR GAME MAKE THESE DISABILITIES IRRELEVANT - and the witnesses may BRAG as they please, since the interrogator cannot demand practical demonstrations A STRONG OBJECTION IS CONCEDED AS STRONG AND THEN NEUTRALISED BY SUFFICIENCY RATHER THAN ANSWERED: machines might do something that OUGHT TO BE DESCRIBED AS THINKING but is very different from what a man does - and the reply is that IF NEVERTHELESS a machine can play the game satisfactorily, WE NEED NOT BE TROUBLED BY THIS OBJECTION. A sufficient condition is offered where a definition was wanted THREE REQUIREMENTS FOR THE DEFINITION ARE LISTED AND THE DIFFICULTY OF MEETING THEM IS ADMITTED: every engineering technique permitted; machines whose operation their own constructors cannot satisfactorily describe still allowed; and men born in the usual manner excluded. The author states outright that IT IS DIFFICULT TO FRAME THE DEFINITIONS so as to satisfy all three A CANDIDATE DEFINITIONAL CLAUSE IS TESTED AGAINST A COUNTEREXAMPLE AND DROPPED: requiring the team of engineers to be all of one sex is proposed and then defeated by the possibility of rearing an individual from a single skin cell - a feat DESERVING OF THE VERY HIGHEST PRAISE which we would still not be inclined to regard as CONSTRUCTING A THINKING MACHINE THE TERM IS THEN NARROWED BY WHAT THE FIELD CURRENTLY FINDS INTERESTING, NOT BY PRINCIPLE: the requirement that every technique be permitted is abandoned, and the stated reason is that PRESENT INTEREST HAS BEEN AROUSED BY a particular kind of machine - so only digital computers are permitted to play. The scope of the word is set by the salience of a technology THE DEFINITION IS DECLARED CONTINGENT ON ITS OWN OUTCOME: the identification of machines with digital computers, LIKE OUR CRITERION FOR THINKING, WILL ONLY BE UNSATISFACTORY IF it turns out that digital computers cannot give a good showing in the game. Both the definition and the criterion are staked on the result they were introduced to adjudicate THE OBVIOUS EXPERIMENT IS DECLINED BECAUSE THE QUESTION IS ABOUT IMAGINABLE MACHINES: asked why not simply run the game with existing computers and compile statistics, the answer is that we are asking neither about ALL digital computers nor about those PRESENTLY AVAILABLE, but WHETHER THERE ARE IMAGINABLE COMPUTERS WHICH WOULD DO WELL CIRCULARITY IS NAMED AND SIDESTEPPED BY DOWNGRADING A DEFINITION TO AN OUTLINE: using the explanation as a definition WOULD PUT US IN DANGER OF CIRCULARITY OF ARGUMENT, so what is given instead is an OUTLINE of the means by which the effect is achieved. The author declines to define and says why THE SUBSTITUTION IS ACKNOWLEDGED AS CONTESTABLE AND THE ORIGINAL IS KEPT ALIVE FOR THAT REASON: the original form of the problem CANNOT ALTOGETHER BE ABANDONED, because OPINIONS WILL DIFFER AS TO THE APPROPRIATENESS OF THE SUBSTITUTION and we must at least listen to what is said on that. The swap is treated as a claim that can be disputed, not as a definition that settles it THE ORIGINAL QUESTION IS DECLARED TOO MEANINGLESS TO DESERVE DISCUSSION - AND THE PREDICTION THAT FOLLOWS IS ABOUT LANGUAGE, NOT ABOUT MACHINES: at the end of the century THE USE OF WORDS AND GENERAL EDUCATED OPINION WILL HAVE ALTERED SO MUCH that one will be able to speak of machines thinking WITHOUT EXPECTING TO BE CONTRADICTED. The question is not answered; it is forecast to dissolve as the usage moves CONJECTURE IS DEFENDED AS A LEGITIMATE PRODUCT PROVIDED IT IS LABELLED AS SUCH: the popular view that scientists proceed inexorably from well-established fact to well-established fact is called QUITE MISTAKEN, and PROVIDED IT IS MADE CLEAR WHICH ARE PROVED FACTS AND WHICH ARE CONJECTURES, NO HARM CAN RESULT - because conjectures suggest useful lines of research machine computer imitation interrogator digital game intelligence learning the imitation game digital computer machine conversational responses (imitation game) interrogator correct-identification rate conceptual-analysis
