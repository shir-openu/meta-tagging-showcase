sensesAN ATTITUDE BECOMES A QUANTITY WITH A NAMED ZERO: `attitudes as EVALUATIVE ASSOCIATIONS WITH OBJECTS. These associations can VARY IN STRENGTH, ranging from NOT EXISTING AT ALL (the case of a NONATTITUDE) to a weak association that is UNLIKELY to be capable of automatic activation to a strong association that CAN be activated automatically.` **An attitude stops being something a person has or lacks and becomes a link with a magnitude.** Everything else in the paper follows: if it is an association, its strength is measurable, manipulable, and predictive of whether it fires unbidden - and `nonattitude` gives the theory a prediction at the bottom of the scale as well as the top

sensesA FIFTH KIND OF BOUNDARY CONDITION - A PROPERTY OF THE STORED REPRESENTATION: `the likelihood of such automatic activation of an attitude appears to DEPEND UPON THE STRENGTH OF THE ASSOCIATION between the attitude object and the evaluation.` The corpus now holds five places a priming effect can be conditional: the OUTCOME SITUATION (ambiguity - four papers), the OBSERVER (prime visibility, Holcomb2005), the STIMULUS (contains a face, Huang2018), the RELATION between primes (conflict, buchner2003), and here **the stored representation itself**. Each needs a different remedy when a replication fails - change the task, screen the subjects, change the materials, redesign the trial structure, or select the items
