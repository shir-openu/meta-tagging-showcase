sensesAND IGNORING IS NOT ENOUGH - IT HAD TO HAVE BEEN COMPETING: `Negative priming was observed ONLY AFTER CONFLICT, but not after nonconflict, primes.` The ignored item must have called for a DIFFERENT response from the attended one. concept_edgesdistractor inhibition —contrasted-with→ episodic retrieval
“If this position is accepted, the observed data pattern is most easily explained within the distractor inhibition (Tipper, 1985; Tipper & Cranston, 1985) and the episodic retrieval accounts of negative priming”
differs in: WHETHER ANYTHING IS SUPPRESSED AT ALL. Inhibition says the ignored item is actively damped and is still damped when it returns. Episodic retrieval says nothing is suppressed - the probe RETRIEVES the earlier display, which carries a tag that no response was to be made, and that tag conflicts with responding now. **Same prediction for the conflict result, opposite claims about what is in the head between the two trials** · strength of the relation: 0.9 — Both accounts survive this paper's data, and the span carries both statements so a reader meets the alternative rather than the winner.
