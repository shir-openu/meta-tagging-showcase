concept_edgesissue framing —causes→ racial attitudes

strength of the relation: 0.9 — **NO `effect_direction`, AND THIS IS THE CLEAREST CASE YET FOR WHY THAT AXIS DOES NOT FIT EVERY EDGE.** The code word does not increase or reduce racial attitudes - it increases how much they PREDICT the policy answer. The vocabulary has no term for `raises the weight of`, and `increases` would be read as `makes people more prejudiced`, which is not what was measured and would be a serious misreading of a paper about race. Weight 0.9: the design supports the causal claim, and the quantity it acts on needs the note.
