strength of the relation: 0.85 — THE PAPER'S GROUPING VARIABLE IS NOT EXOGENOUS, AND THE PAPER IS THE ONE THAT SHOWS IT. The title says strategy DETERMINES where memory is held; silencing the area that holds it makes the animal CHANGE STRATEGY - 'some mice ... apparently flexibly SWITCH TO THE ALTERNATIVE UNPERTURBED STRATEGY', and those switches were 'consistent across days, present also in trials without perturbation and were clearly reflected in corresponding switches between M2- and P-dominated delay maps.' So the arrow runs both ways: strategy selects the site, and removing the site reselects the strategy. Recorded as `causes` in the direction the manipulation establishes, since that is the one an experiment tested.
