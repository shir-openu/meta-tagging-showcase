unhoused_relationspriming versus framing, as this paper draws it: both activate awareness of an issue and framing additionally attributes MEANINGS to it → the same pair as drawn in drouvelis2010 / drouvelis2015 (framing RE-DESCRIBES a logically equivalent decision problem) and as reported unresolved in Hoewe2020 (where Ottati holds that framing IS a form of priming)
Quotation withheld — this record is not cleared for redistribution. The sentence is in the paper at the link above.
THREE PAPERS IN THIS CORPUS SEPARATE `priming` FROM `framing`, IN THREE FIELDS, ON THREE DIFFERENT AXES - AND NONE OF THEM CITES THE OTHERS. Economics (drouvelis, both papers) makes the criterion THE DECISION PROBLEM: framing re-describes a logically equivalent one, priming does not. Media studies (Hoewe2020) makes it AWARENESS, and reports its own field unable to settle whether the two are even distinct, quoting Ottati that `media framing constitutes a specific form of priming`. Strategic communication (this paper) makes it MEANING: the two share the activation of awareness and framing adds the attribution of meanings. **Not a disagreement about a fact - three literatures independently fixing one boundary and not noticing each other.** That is the negative space this project was built to make visible, and the schema has no relation type for `these papers partition the same pair differently`. See Q-730, and Q-632 for the missing types.
