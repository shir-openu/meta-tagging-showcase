direction on the measure: no-change · strength of the relation: 0.2 — the record marks this weak — **WEIGHT 0.2 AND `no-change`, WHICH ARE SAYING DIFFERENT THINGS AND BOTH ARE NEEDED.** `no-change` is the measured direction: the primed and control groups did not differ. The low weight is the STRENGTH OF THE CLAIM, and it is low because the authors immediately name a reason the manipulation might have failed - `different words were used`. A reader must not take this as evidence that priming does not affect misconceptions; it is evidence that THIS prime did not, in a study powered for d = .45. Recording it as an edge at all is the point: the alternative is that the corpus holds only the literature's successes.
