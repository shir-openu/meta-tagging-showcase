senses**THE PAPER'S MOVE IS TO MAKE THE CONFOUND INTO THE DESIGN.** Earlier work could not say how much facilitation came from attention and how much from automatic spreading, because relatedness and expectancy travel together: a prime that is related to the target is also a prime that makes it expected. Neely breaks them apart - `the presence or absence of AUTOMATIC SPREADING ACTIVATION was controlled by whether the prime and the target were semantically related`, while conscious attention `was controlled by variations in the PROBABILITY that the priming cue itself would appear`. Two knobs where the field had one

senses**AND THE CELL THAT PAYS FOR IT IS BODY-heart: A SIGN REVERSAL PRODUCED BY NOTHING BUT DELAY.** The prime is semantically related to the target, but the subject expects a different category - so automatic activation says facilitate and conscious attention says inhibit, in the same trial. `there was an INHIBITION effect at the 2,000 msec SOA, which decreased as the SOA decreased until, at the 250-msec SOA, IT BECAME A FACILITATION EFFECT.` Not a difference in size between conditions - **the same condition changing sign** as the slow mechanism is given less time to act. That is as close to a crossing double dissociation as a priming experiment gets
