senses[3]1.0the tagger's words
senses`solution depth` / `problem depth` - A STIPULATIVE CASCADE THAT MAKES `DEPTH` WELL-DEFINED WHERE THE FIELD'S OWN BOUNDARY IS NOT. Three definitions in one breath: a `solution` is the set of used weights; the `solution depth` is the depth of the deepest CAP in that sequence; and the `problem depth` is `THE SMALLEST DEPTH OF ANY SOLUTION`. THE THIRD IS A MINIMUM OVER SOLUTIONS, NOT A PROPERTY OF A NETWORK - so two networks solving one problem can have different solution depths while the problem depth is a single number about the PROBLEM. This record already carries at `senses[1]` that experts could not agree where shallow ends and deep begins; this is the same author making the quantity precise by fiat instead of waiting for the field.
