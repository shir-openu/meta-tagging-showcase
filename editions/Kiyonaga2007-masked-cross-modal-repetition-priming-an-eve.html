cross-modal priming within-modality priming N250 N400 bimodal interactive-activation model prime duration A visual prime activates whole-word orthographic representations, which pass activation on to the corresponding whole-word phonological representations, so cross-modal transfer travels the slower lexical route and shows up only in the N400.
