senses**AND IT SITS AGAINST dehaene1998 IN THIS CORPUS, WITHOUT CONTRADICTING IT.** dehaene1998 concluded that an unreportable numeral was `unconsciously processed according to task instructions, ALL THE WAY DOWN TO THE MOTOR SYSTEM`. This paper finds no sensorimotor activation from unconscious words at all. **Both can be right, because `reaches the motor system` names two things:** dehaene1998's is a TRAINED RESPONSE MAPPING - a digit compared to 5, a hand prepared, an arbitrary rule applied - and Bottini's is SIMULATION OF MEANING, the claim that understanding `up` re-enacts upward experience. An unconscious stimulus can drive the first and fail the second. **Same shape as Q-821's two senses of `nonconscious`, and the corpus currently indexes both pairs under one heading**
