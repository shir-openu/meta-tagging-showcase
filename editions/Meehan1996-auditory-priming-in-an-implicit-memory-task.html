sensesTHE CONCLUSION IS ABOUT THE INSTRUMENT, NOT ABOUT MEMORY: `the significance of voice in implicit memory is DEPENDENT ON THE LEVEL OF PROCESSING REQUIRED BY THE TASK and the acoustic environment.` Whether voice matters to implicit memory has no answer on its own - it has an answer per task. **So two studies of `auditory priming` that used different tasks are not measuring the same thing**, and a corpus that pools their effect sizes is pooling answers to different questions. The paper was designed to demonstrate exactly this: it swapped the task and the role of voice changed
“This study demonstrates that the significance of voice in implicit memory is dependent on the level of processing required by the task and the acoustic environment”

sensesTHE DOSE-RESPONSE HAS A CROSSING VARIABLE, WHICH IS WHY THE DESIGN NEEDED A SECOND ROUND. Priming appeared `only in the one- and two-speaker conditions` and vanished at three and four - but voice FAMILIARITY falls as the number of speakers rises, so the two are confounded by construction. When familiarity was equated by pre-training, priming came back at three voices. **`Number of speakers` is therefore not the variable it appears to be**, and the paper takes it apart rather than reporting the monotone result it could have
“Pairwise comparisons between the response latencies of nonstudied and studied stimuli at each level of the factor number of speakers showed significant priming only in the one- and two-speaker conditions”
senses[3]0.95the tagger's words
sensesAND THE TASK WAS CHOSEN TO DEFEAT A RESPONSE BIAS, WHICH IS AN ARGUMENT ABOUT MEASUREMENT RATHER THAN ABOUT MEMORY. The introduction's charge against the standard tasks is that requiring participants to report whole words `renders priming dependent on the activation of whole words while focusing performance away from the acoustic component` - so the instrument was producing the word-level answer it kept finding. Phoneme monitoring forces attention below the word, and the result changes. A methodological objection of the same shape as Holcomb2005's against Kiefer, in a different literature
