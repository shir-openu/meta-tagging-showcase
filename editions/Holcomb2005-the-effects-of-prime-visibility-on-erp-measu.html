sensesTHE DISSOCIATION IS MADE OF CORRELATIONS, NOT OF MEANS, AND THAT IS THE DESIGN. One measure (the N400), one design, two kinds of priming, and the question is whether each effect's size tracks how well the individual participant could see the prime: `unlike repetition priming in Experiment 1, for semantic priming in Experiment 2, VISIBILITY OF PRIMES AT 40 MS ... PREDICTED THE N400 EFFECT`. Individual differences are the instrument - a median split on d-prime - so the NULL in Experiment 1 is the informative half, not the weak one.
