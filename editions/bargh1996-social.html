concept_edgesstereotype —causes→ behavior
Quotation withheld — this record is not cleared for redistribution. The sentence is in the paper at the link above.
direction on the measure: increases · strength of the relation: 0.9 — **`increases` MEANS MORE STEREOTYPE-CONSISTENT BEHAVIOUR, NOT MORE OF ANY MEASURED QUANTITY, AND THE DISTINCTION MATTERS HERE.** In Experiment 2 the measurement WENT DOWN - participants primed with `elderly` WALKED MORE SLOWLY. A reader taking `increases` as `the number went up` would have that experiment backwards. The axis is recording consistency with the primed content, which is what the quote says, and the three experiments move three different dependent variables in three directions.
