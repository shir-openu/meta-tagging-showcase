IS-AN-ASPECT-OF / IS-DEFINED-IN-TERMS-OF, AND NONE OF THE EIGHT TYPES HOLDS IT. `liquidity REFERS TO THE SCALE OF the market impact` - the paper gives `liquidity` no content of its own; it is the size parameter of the other quantity. `operationalised-as` is the nearest type and the gate refused it correctly: the cue vocabulary wants `measured / defined as / quantified / computed as`, and `refers to the scale of` is a definitional gloss, not a measurement procedure. `part-of` is wrong because a scale is not a component. `same-as` is wrong because the two are not interchangeable - one is a response, the other is how big that response is. **Filed rather than forced: typing this as `operationalised-as` would put a measurement claim in the corpus that the paper does not make.** Reached 2026-08-26 by terminal-a during the schema 0.3 pass.
