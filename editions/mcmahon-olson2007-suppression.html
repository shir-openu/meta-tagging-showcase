senses**THE CONCLUSION IS A NEGATIVE AND IT FITS IN ONE LINE:** `We conclude that repetition suppression ACCOMPANIES repetition priming but is UNLIKELY TO BE ITS CAUSE.` The neural correlate everyone reaches for is a passenger. **Carlesimo2004, merged into this corpus forty minutes ago, reads its rCBF decreases as `the reduced neural activity needed to reprocess recently seen words`** - suppression explaining priming, taken as read; and Desimone1996 calls suppression `an intrinsic property of visual cortical areas`. Three records, three positions on whether a reduced response is the mechanism or the companion, and none of them assembled on purpose
“We conclude that repetition suppression accompanies repetition priming but is unlikely to be its cause.”
senses[0]0.95the tagger's words
senses**AND THE ARGUMENT IS TURNED FROM A STATISTICAL QUESTION INTO A MECHANICAL ONE, WHICH IS THE ONLY WAY TO RULE OUT A CO-OCCURRING SIGNAL:** `Whether repetition suppression indeed contributes to repetition priming must depend in part on HOW IT AFFECTS THE STIMULUS SELECTIVITY OF NEURONS.` Correlation is granted, not contested. The test is whether suppression has the PROPERTIES a cause would need - because a uniform reduction across all neurons degrades the representation, while priming requires it sharpened. **A correlate can only be dismissed by asking what it would have to be like**

The enforced types offer causes / operationalised-as / distinct-from / contrasted-with / part-of / is-a / same-as / renamed-across-fields, and **none of them expresses `co-occurs with but does not cause`.** `distinct-from` carries the negative half - they are not one thing, and the neural measure is not the behavioural mechanism - and drops the positive half, which is that the correlation is real and granted. Weight 0.95 because the sentence is the paper's stated conclusion and both endpoints are named. **The missing type belongs on Shir's schema list**: a relation for a correlate that has been tested and ruled out as a cause is exactly what a corpus about definitions should be able to record, and it is the second-commonest finding in this literature after the effect itself.
