strength of the relation: 0.95 — **`distinct-from` IS THE CLOSEST THE VOCABULARY GETS AND IT LOSES HALF THE SENTENCE.** The paper says two things at once: they ACCOMPANY each other, and one is NOT the cause of the other. The enforced types offer causes / operationalised-as / distinct-from / contrasted-with / part-of / is-a / same-as / renamed-across-fields, and **none of them expresses `co-occurs with but does not cause`.** `distinct-from` carries the negative half - they are not one thing, and the neural measure is not the behavioural mechanism - and drops the positive half, which is that the correlation is real and granted. Weight 0.95 because the sentence is the paper's stated conclusion and both endpoints are named. **The missing type belongs on Shir's schema list**: a relation for a correlate that has been tested and ruled out as a cause is exactly what a corpus about definitions should be able to record, and it is the second-commonest finding in this literature after the effect itself.
