During ripples the interneurones act not by shunting spikes but by delivering hyperpolarization timed to de-inactivate voltage-dependent ion channels, which then promotes the pyramidal cells' next discharge. O-LM cells go silent during ripples partly because their pyramidal input has a very low resting release probability that only repetitive presynaptic firing raises, and pyramidal cells emit only one or two spikes per ripple.
