(The corpus already contains comparable out-of-list values such as prospective-cohort-study and retrospective-survey.) The study has a second, instrument-building component - a three-step item development, focus group and expert review process producing the 5-item PVE questionnaire - so claim_type could arguably be method; prediction was chosen because the stated primary purpose and the abstract's headline result are both predictive. replication_status is set to replicated on the strength of the paper's own explicit statement that its finding is consistent with Sheldon and Lyubomirsky's (2012) conclusion, i.e. it replicates that effect in a new (exercise) context; the novel part - variety adding variance beyond the three needs - is itself untested.
