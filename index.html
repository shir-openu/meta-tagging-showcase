A field-neutral, verbatim-grounded tag layer for academic literature. Every paper is described with the same small set of faceted tags — claim type, phenomenon (the paper’s subject in one line), claim relations, replication status, and limitations — and every tag is grounded in an exact sentence from the paper's own text. Papers are joined to one another through the concept layer, where 814 terms are carried by two or more papers. The aim: make cross-disciplinary connections queryable and verifiable, and surface candidate links the literature has not yet drawn. This is a proof-of-format on a curated corpus — an existence proof of the procedure, not a claim of completeness. A surfaced cross-field link is a hypothesis to investigate, never a claimed discovery.
